title: Custom Domains rebuilt around Subdomains
date: 2026-11-05T15:00:00+13:00
---
This release rebuilt *Custom Domains* around *Subdomains*. Every address on your *Custom Domain*, including your *Base Domain*, is now a *Subdomain* with its own page, hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings, and can serve several *Sites* at different subpaths or redirect visitors elsewhere. It also added a setup guide for CloudCannon DNS, step-by-step DNS records for external DNS, the *SSL Certificates* page under *Org Settings*, and eight domain emails.

It also addressed several issues, including those affecting new files, file sorting, Bynder transformations, and the *Site Details* form.

## Features & Improvements

* Added *Subdomains*, allowing you to manage each address on your *Custom Domain*, including your *Base Domain*, with its own hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings. A *Site Subdomain* serves one or more *Sites*, and a *Redirect Subdomain* sends visitors to another domain. The *Add a Subdomain* button lives on the *Summary* and *Subdomains* tabs of the *Domain* page. For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/).
  * Adding example.com as a *Site Subdomain* also adds www.example.com as a *Redirect Subdomain* to it, and the reverse.
  * Every *Subdomain* has its own page, with *Sites* (or *Redirect*), *Hosting*, *HTTPS*, and *DNS* sections, and a *Danger zone* where you can change its type or delete it.
  * The hosting options, *CloudCannon Hosting*, *Cloudflare Proxy*, and *Single Origin*, are now set for each *Subdomain* rather than each *Site*, and each is shown with a diagram of the path visitors take to your *Site*. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/).
* Added the *Summary* tab to the *Domain* page, allowing you to see at a glance whether your *Custom Domain* is healthy. It shows how many *Subdomains* need attention and why, your *Fallback Redirect*, whether your nameservers and DNS records are correct, and how many *Subdomains* are below your minimum TLS version. Each section links to the tab where you can fix it. For more information, please read our documentation on [what the Domain page is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/#the-summary-tab).
* Added the *Subdomains* tab to the *Domain* page, replacing the *Network* tab. It shows a table of every *Subdomain* with its hosting, HTTPS, and DNS status, which you can search, sort, and filter by *Attention*, *Type*, *Hosting*, *HTTPS*, and *DNS Record*. The context menu on each row opens the *Subdomain* page or changes its hosting, HTTPS, type, or *Fallback Redirect*, checks its DNS, and shows its DNS history. For more information, please read our documentation on [what the Subdomains tab shows](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/#the-subdomains-tab).
* Added a *Sites* section to the *Subdomain* page, allowing you to manage the *Sites* a *Subdomain* serves, each at its own subpath, such as a marketing *Site* at example.com and a documentation *Site* at example.com/docs/. The section lets you add a *Site*, change its *Subpath*, swap it for another *Site* in one step, or remove it. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/).
  * When you remove the last *Site* from a *Subdomain*, CloudCannon asks whether to *Leave it set up*, *Redirect visitors*, or *Delete the Subdomain*. A *Site Subdomain* with no *Site* shows visitors a 404 page.
* Added *Redirect Subdomains*, allowing you to send every visitor to a *Subdomain* on to another domain with a 301, 302, 307, or 308 status code. All four keep the path and query string. CloudCannon warns you about a redirect to another redirect, and refuses a redirect that would create a loop. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/).
  * A redirect target on one of your own *Base Domains* must already be a *Subdomain*.
  * Deleting a *Subdomain* lists any other *Redirect Subdomains* that point at it, and deletes them only if you choose to.
* Changed the *Fallback Redirect*, the *Subdomain* visitors reach when they go to a subdomain of your *Base Domain* that you haven't configured, such as typo.example.com, allowing you to choose any *Subdomain*, including a *Redirect Subdomain*, and the status code CloudCannon uses. The *Change Fallback* button on the *Summary* and *Subdomains* tabs, and the *Use as Fallback Redirect* option in the context menu for each *Subdomain*, change it. Your first *Subdomain* becomes the *Fallback Redirect*. For more information, please read our documentation on [updating the Fallback Redirect for your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/update-the-fallback-redirect-for-your-custom-domain/).
* Added a setup guide for CloudCannon DNS, allowing you to move your *Custom Domain* to CloudCannon DNS in three steps. The guide asks you to copy your existing DNS records, such as MX and TXT records for email, then shows the two nameservers to set at your domain registrar. After you click the *I've updated my nameservers* button, CloudCannon checks your nameservers every hour for 48 hours, then daily, and emails you when your domain is live. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/).
* Added the DNS records each *Subdomain* needs on external DNS, allowing you to see what to add at your *DNS Provider* and what CloudCannon found instead, with a copy button for each value. After you click the *I've updated it* button, CloudCannon checks the record every hour for seven days. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).
* Added a footer to every DNS table CloudCannon checks, allowing you to see how often CloudCannon checks, when it last checked, and the history of those checks. The footer holds the *Check now* button, which checks again at most once a minute.
* Changed the *DNS* tab of the *Domain* page to show your nameserver status, then the records your *Subdomains* still need. On CloudCannon DNS, a *Fix* button adds, removes, or replaces a record for you. On external DNS, the *DNS* tab also lists the optional wildcard record, which sends visitors to subdomains you haven't configured to your *Fallback Redirect*.
* Changed the HTTPS status to show the *SSL Certificate* of each *Subdomain* rather than each *Site*, allowing you to see whether it is *Active*, *Issuing*, *Renewing*, *Waiting*, *Failed*, *Expired*, or *Missing*, with a *Retry now* button and the last error when issuing fails. For more information, please read our documentation on [resolving SSL Certificate errors](/documentation/developer-articles/resolve-ssl-certificate-errors/).
  * The *HTTPS for [Subdomain]* modal lets you choose between *Let CloudCannon issue and renew it* and *Use an SSL certificate you upload*, and holds the *Redirect http:// visitors to https://* checkbox, which moved from the *Routing* page under *Site Settings* so that it applies to every *Site* on the *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [redirecting HTTP to HTTPS](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#redirect-http-to-https).
  * The *Switch to TXT validation* button in the *HTTPS* section lets CloudCannon issue a *Subdomain*'s *SSL Certificate* before you point its DNS at CloudCannon, so you can move a live website without a certificate warning.
  * Your *Base Domain* has a wildcard *SSL Certificate*, so visitors to subdomains you haven't configured reach your *Fallback Redirect* over HTTPS. On external DNS, the *DNS* tab offers a CNAME record that lets Cloudflare renew it on its own. For more information, please read our documentation on [renewing the wildcard SSL Certificate on external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#renew-the-wildcard-ssl-certificate-on-external-dns).
* Added a *Minimum TLS version* dropdown to the *HTTPS for [Subdomain]* modal, allowing you to raise the minimum TLS version of one *Subdomain* above the minimum for its *Custom Domain*. Raising the minimum under *Domain Settings* lists the *Subdomains* it changes, and lowering it leaves your *Subdomains* as they are unless you check the *Also lower* checkbox. For more information, please read our documentation on [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/).
* Added the *SSL Certificates* page under *Org Settings*, allowing you to upload, review, and remove the *Custom SSL Certificates* your *Organization* uses. Each certificate shows a *Valid*, *Expires soon*, *Expired*, *Removing*, or *Renews automatically* status, the domains it covers, and the *Subdomains* that use it. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom SSL Certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-ssl-certificate/).
  * Removing a certificate that *Subdomains* still use switches them to a certificate CloudCannon issues, keeps HTTPS on while they switch, and then deletes the certificate.
  * CloudCannon emails you 14 days and three days before a *Custom SSL Certificate* expires. If a *Subdomain* still uses it when it expires, CloudCannon switches that *Subdomain* to a certificate CloudCannon issues.
  * Viewing the page needs the `org:settings:certificate:read` permission, and adding or removing a certificate needs the `org:settings:certificate:write` permission.
* Changed the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* to show the *Subdomain* your *Site* is on, allowing you to change its hosting, HTTPS, and DNS without leaving your *Site*. While the DNS for your *Custom Domain* is being set up, the page shows the setup guide instead. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).
* Changed adding a *Custom Domain* to a *Site* to require its *Base Domain* to be connected to your *Organization* first. For more information, please read our documentation on [connecting a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/).
* Changed *Domain Settings* to preview what happens before you switch your *DNS Provider*, and to name everything that stops you disconnecting a *Custom Domain*, including *Sites* that still use it and redirects that point into it from your other domains. For more information, please read our documentation on [disconnecting a Custom Domain from your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/disconnect-a-custom-domain-from-your-organization/).
* Added a way to move a *Custom Domain* from the legacy CloudCannon DNS to the new CloudCannon DNS. The *Move to the new CloudCannon DNS* button copies your DNS records across, and needs permission to edit both the settings and the DNS of the domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [moving from the legacy CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#move-from-the-legacy-cloudcannon-dns).
* Changed CloudCannon DNS to keep a copy of your DNS records if your nameservers stop pointing at CloudCannon, and to restore them if you change your nameservers back.
* Added eight emails about your *Custom Domains*, and rewrote the emails CloudCannon sends when an *SSL Certificate* is failing to issue and when it is active again. They go to everyone with permission to edit the settings of the domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [the emails CloudCannon sends about your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/#domain-emails).
  * CloudCannon emails you when someone connects a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*.
  * On CloudCannon DNS, CloudCannon emails you when your *Custom Domain* is live, when its nameservers still don't match after 48 hours, and when they stop pointing at CloudCannon DNS.
  * On external DNS, CloudCannon emails you when a *Subdomain* is live, when its DNS record still doesn't point at CloudCannon after 48 hours, and when it stops pointing at CloudCannon.
  * CloudCannon emails you before a *Custom SSL Certificate* expires.
* Changed billing for *Custom Domains* to count each *Subdomain* that serves a *Site*. *Redirect Subdomains* and *Subdomains* with no *Site* are free. Adding a *Subdomain* that increases your bill asks you to confirm the change, and CloudCannon emails everyone who can view your billing. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).
  * In a *Client Organization* on an extended *Free Trial*, the trial now ends five days after a *Subdomain* starts serving a *Site*, rather than after a *Custom Domain* is added to a *Site*.
* Changed the *Domains* link in the *App Sidebar* to *Custom Domains*, and the *Connect a Domain* button to *Connect a Custom Domain*, so the area is named for what you connect there.
* Changed the *Card* for each *Custom Domain* in your *Domains Browser* to show its DNS type, how many *Subdomains* and *Sites* it has, and where it is in setting up CloudCannon DNS, allowing you to see which domains still need setting up without opening each one.
* Changed the *Sites Not Resolving* filter in the *Client Organizations Browser* to *Subdomains Not Pointing*, and the domain filters to check the *Subdomains* of each client's *Custom Domains* rather than its *Sites*. The domain checks on the *Health* tab now report *Subdomains* rather than *Sites*.
* Changed every *Base Domain* that was set to use CloudCannon DNS, but whose nameservers never pointed at CloudCannon and were first checked more than 30 days ago, to use external DNS, so the *Domain* page for each one lists the DNS records its *Subdomains* need at the *DNS Provider* it actually uses.
* Added the Hosting Bandwidth, Custom Domains, and Build Time allowances to each plan on the *Update your Subscription Plan* page, alongside *Seats*, allowing you to compare what each *Subscription Plan* includes and what extra capacity costs.
* Updated the CloudCannon API, which is in Public Beta. For more information, please read our [API changelog](/documentation/developer-reference/api/changelog/).

## Fixes

* Fixed an issue where saving a new file left it marked as *New* and counted as an unsaved change.
* Fixed an issue where files appeared in a scrambled order when sorted by anything other than their path.
* Fixed an issue where the Bynder widget offered transformations on an asset even when your Bynder account didn't have permission to use them.
* Fixed an issue where the *Site Details* form under *Site Settings* stayed locked after a failed update. The result of an update now appears at the top of the form.
* Updated dependencies to patch security vulnerabilities.
