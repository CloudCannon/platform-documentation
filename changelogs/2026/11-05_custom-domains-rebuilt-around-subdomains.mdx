title: Custom Domains rebuilt around Subdomains
date: 2026-11-05T15:00:00+13:00
---
This release rebuilt *Custom Domains* around *Subdomains*. Every address on your *Custom Domain*, including your *Base Domain*, is now a *Subdomain* with its own page, hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings, and can serve several *Sites* at different subpaths or redirect visitors elsewhere. Alongside *Subdomains*, it added a setup guide for CloudCannon DNS, step-by-step DNS records for external DNS, and eight domain emails, and rebuilt the *SSL Certificates* page under *Org Settings*.

It also addressed several issues, including those affecting new files, file sorting, and the *Details* page under *Site Settings*.

## Features & Improvements

* Added *Subdomains*, allowing you to manage each address on your *Custom Domain*, including your *Base Domain*, with its own hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings. A *Site Subdomain* serves one or more *Sites*, and a *Redirect Subdomain* sends visitors to another domain. The *Add a Subdomain* button lives on the *Summary* and *Subdomains* tabs of the *Domain* page. For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/).
  * Adding example.com as a *Site Subdomain* also adds www.example.com as a *Redirect Subdomain* to it, and the reverse.
  * Every *Subdomain* has its own page, with *Sites* (or *Redirect*), *Hosting*, *HTTPS*, and *DNS* sections, and a *Danger zone* where you can change its type or delete it.
  * The hosting options, *CloudCannon Hosting*, *Cloudflare Proxy*, and *Single Origin*, are now set for each *Subdomain* rather than each *Site*. The *Hosting* section of each *Subdomain* page shows a diagram of the path visitors take to your *Site* through the option it uses. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/).

* Added the *Summary* tab to the *Domain* page, allowing you to see at a glance whether your *Custom Domain* is healthy. It shows how many *Subdomains* need attention and why, your *Fallback Redirect*, whether your nameservers and DNS records are correct, and how many *Subdomains* are below your minimum TLS version. The *Subdomains*, *DNS*, and *Minimum TLS* sections link to where you can fix each problem. For more information, please read our documentation on [what the Domain page is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/#the-summary-tab).

<comp.DocsImage path="https://cc-dam.imgix.net/documentation/images/change-log/2026-11/CloudCannon-Changelog-Nov2026-Domain-Summary-Tab.png" alt="A screenshot of the Summary tab of the Domain page shows three of five Subdomains needing attention, the Fallback Redirect, the nameserver and DNS Record status, and the minimum TLS version." title="The Summary tab of the Domain page" type="screenshot"/>

* Added the *Subdomains* tab to the *Domain* page, replacing the *Network* tab. It shows a table of every *Subdomain* with its hosting, HTTPS, and DNS status, which you can search, sort, and filter by *Attention*, *Type*, *Hosting*, *HTTPS*, and *DNS Record*. The *Context Menu* on each row opens the *Subdomain* page or changes its hosting, HTTPS, type, or *Fallback Redirect*, checks its DNS, and shows its DNS history. For more information, please read our documentation on [what the Subdomains tab shows](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/#the-subdomains-tab).

<comp.DocsImage path="https://cc-dam.imgix.net/documentation/images/change-log/2026-11/CloudCannon-Changelog-Nov2026-Domain-Subdomains-Tab.png" alt="A screenshot of the Subdomains tab of the Domain page shows a table of four Subdomains with their hosting, HTTPS, and DNS status, and the Fallback Redirect below it." title="The Subdomains tab of the Domain page" type="screenshot"/>

* Added a *Sites* section to the *Subdomain* page, allowing you to manage the *Sites* a *Subdomain* serves, each at its own subpath, such as a marketing *Site* at example.com and a documentation *Site* at example.com/docs/. The section lets you add a *Site*, change its *Subpath*, swap it for another *Site* in one step, or remove it. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/).
  * When you remove the last *Site* from a *Subdomain*, CloudCannon asks whether to *Leave it set up*, *Redirect visitors*, or *Delete the Subdomain*. A *Site Subdomain* with no *Site* shows visitors a 404 page.
* Added a *Redirect* section to the *Subdomain* page of each *Redirect Subdomain*, allowing you to send every visitor to that *Subdomain* on to another domain with a 301, 302, 307, or 308 status code. All four keep the path and query string. CloudCannon warns you about a redirect to another redirect, and refuses a redirect that would create a loop. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/).
  * A redirect target on one of your own *Base Domains* must already be a *Subdomain*.
  * Deleting a *Subdomain* lists any other *Redirect Subdomains* that point at it, and deletes them too unless you uncheck the *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox (*Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* for one).
* Changed the *Fallback Redirect*, the *Subdomain* visitors reach when they go to a subdomain of your *Base Domain* that you haven't configured, such as typo.example.com, allowing you to choose any *Subdomain*, including a *Redirect Subdomain*, and the status code CloudCannon uses. The *Change Fallback* button on the *Summary* and *Subdomains* tabs, and the *Use as Fallback Redirect* option in the *Context Menu* for each *Subdomain*, change it. Your first *Subdomain* becomes the *Fallback Redirect*. For more information, please read our documentation on [updating the Fallback Redirect for your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/update-the-fallback-redirect-for-your-custom-domain/).
* Added a setup guide for CloudCannon DNS, allowing you to move your *Custom Domain* to CloudCannon DNS in three steps. The guide asks you to copy your existing DNS records, such as MX records for email and TXT records for domain verification, then shows the nameservers to set at your domain registrar. After you click the *I've updated my nameservers* button, CloudCannon checks your nameservers every hour for 48 hours, then daily, and emails you when your domain is live. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/).

<comp.DocsImage path="https://cc-dam.imgix.net/documentation/images/change-log/2026-11/CloudCannon-Changelog-Nov2026-CloudCannon-DNS-Setup-Guide.png" alt="A screenshot of the setup guide on the Summary tab shows the three steps to point example.org at CloudCannon DNS, with the nameservers to set at the Domain Registrar." title="The setup guide for CloudCannon DNS" type="screenshot"/>

* Added the DNS records each *Subdomain* needs on external DNS, allowing you to see what to add at your *DNS Provider* and what CloudCannon found instead, with a copy button for each value. After you click the *I've updated it* button, CloudCannon checks the record every hour for seven days. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).
* Added a footer to every DNS table CloudCannon checks, allowing you to see when CloudCannon last checked and check again with the *Check now* button, at most once a minute. On the nameserver table and on CloudCannon DNS record tables, the footer also says how often CloudCannon checks. On the nameserver table and the *DNS* section of each *Subdomain* page, it also shows the history of those checks.
* Changed the *DNS* tab of the *Domain* page to show your nameserver status, then the records your *Subdomains* still need, allowing you to see what your *Custom Domain* still needs before it goes live. On CloudCannon DNS, a *Fix* button adds, removes, or replaces a record for you. On external DNS, the *DNS* tab also lists the optional wildcard record, which sends visitors to subdomains you haven't configured to your *Fallback Redirect*.
* Changed the HTTPS status to show the *SSL Certificate* of each *Subdomain* rather than each *Site*, allowing you to see its status, such as *Active*, *Issuing*, *Renewing*, *Waiting*, *Failed*, *Expired*, or *Missing*, with a *Retry now* button and the last error when issuing fails. For more information, please read our documentation on [resolving SSL Certificate errors](/documentation/developer-articles/resolve-ssl-certificate-errors/).
  * The *HTTPS for [Subdomain]* modal lets you choose between *Let CloudCannon issue and renew it* and *Use an SSL certificate you upload*, and holds the *Redirect http:// visitors to https://* checkbox, which moved from the *Routing* page under *Site Settings* so that it applies to every *Site* on the *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [redirecting HTTP to HTTPS](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#redirect-http-to-https).
  * The *Switch to TXT validation* button in the *HTTPS* section lets CloudCannon issue a *Subdomain*'s *SSL Certificate* before you point its DNS at CloudCannon, which reduces downtime when you switch the DNS of a live website.
  * Your *Base Domain* has a wildcard *SSL Certificate*, so visitors to subdomains you haven't configured reach your *Fallback Redirect* over HTTPS. On external DNS, the *DNS* tab offers a CNAME record that lets Cloudflare renew it on its own. For more information, please read our documentation on [renewing the wildcard SSL Certificate on external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#renew-the-wildcard-ssl-certificate-on-external-dns).

<comp.DocsImage path="https://cc-dam.imgix.net/documentation/images/change-log/2026-11/CloudCannon-Changelog-Nov2026-Subdomain-HTTPS-Modal.png" alt="A screenshot of the HTTPS for blog.example.com modal shows the choice between letting CloudCannon issue the SSL Certificate and uploading one, the Redirect http:// visitors to https:// checkbox, and the Minimum TLS version dropdown." title="The HTTPS for [Subdomain] modal" type="screenshot"/>

* Added a *Minimum TLS version* dropdown to the *HTTPS for [Subdomain]* modal, allowing you to raise the minimum TLS version of one *Subdomain* above the minimum for its *Custom Domain*. Raising the minimum under *Domain Settings* lists the *Subdomains* it changes, and lowering it leaves your *Subdomains* as they are unless you check the *Also lower [Number] subdomains to TLS [Version]* checkbox. For more information, please read our documentation on [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/).
* Changed the *SSL Certificates* page under *Org Settings*, allowing you to remove a *Custom SSL Certificate* that *Subdomains* still use, and to see which *Subdomains* use each certificate. Each certificate shows a *Valid*, *Expires soon*, *Expired*, *Removing*, or *Renews automatically* status and the domains it covers. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom SSL Certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-ssl-certificate/).
  * Removing a certificate that *Subdomains* still use switches them to a certificate CloudCannon issues, keeps HTTPS on while they switch, and then deletes the certificate.
  * If a *Subdomain* still uses a *Custom SSL Certificate* when it expires, CloudCannon switches that *Subdomain* to a certificate CloudCannon issues.
  * Viewing the page needs the `org:settings:certificate:read` permission, and adding or removing a certificate needs the `org:settings:certificate:write` permission.
* Changed the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* to show the *Subdomain* your *Site* is on, allowing you to change its hosting, HTTPS, and DNS without leaving your *Site*. While the DNS for your *Custom Domain* is being set up, the page also shows the setup guide. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).
* Changed adding a *Custom Domain* to a *Site* to require its *Base Domain* to be connected to your *Organization* first, because each *Site* is now served from a *Subdomain* of a connected *Base Domain*. You can connect the *Base Domain* from the same form. For more information, please read our documentation on [connecting a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/).
* Changed *Domain Settings* to preview what happens before you switch your *DNS Provider*, and to name everything that stops you disconnecting a *Custom Domain*, including *Sites* that still use it and redirects that point into it from your other domains. For more information, please read our documentation on [disconnecting a Custom Domain from your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/disconnect-a-custom-domain-from-your-organization/).
* Added the *Move to the new CloudCannon DNS* button, allowing you to move a *Custom Domain* from the legacy CloudCannon DNS to the new CloudCannon DNS without re-entering your DNS records. The button copies your DNS records across, and needs the `base-domain:settings:details:write` and `base-domain:settings:dns:write` permissions. For more information, please read our documentation on [moving from the legacy CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#move-from-the-legacy-cloudcannon-dns).
* Changed CloudCannon DNS to keep a copy of your DNS records if your nameservers stop pointing at CloudCannon, and to restore them if you change your nameservers back, so moving your nameservers away by mistake doesn't lose your records.
* Added eight emails about your *Custom Domains*, and rewrote the emails CloudCannon sends when an *SSL Certificate* is failing to issue and when it is active again. Unless noted below, they go to everyone with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for the domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [what emails CloudCannon sends about your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/#domain-emails).
  * CloudCannon emails you when someone else connects a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*.
  * On CloudCannon DNS, CloudCannon emails you when your *Custom Domain* is live, when its nameservers still don't match after 48 hours, and when they stop pointing at CloudCannon DNS.
  * On external DNS, CloudCannon emails you when a *Subdomain* is live, when its DNS record still doesn't point at CloudCannon after 48 hours, and when it stops pointing at CloudCannon.
  * CloudCannon emails you 14 days and three days before a *Custom SSL Certificate* expires, if it is used by a *Subdomain* on a domain whose settings you can edit.
* Changed billing for *Custom Domains* to count each *Subdomain* that serves a *Site*. *Redirect Subdomains* and *Subdomains* with no *Site* are free. On a *Subscription Plan* that includes a *Custom Domains* allowance, adding a *Subdomain* that takes you over it asks you to confirm the change. While you are over the allowance, CloudCannon emails everyone who can view your billing, except the person who made the change, each time the number of *Subdomains* serving *Sites* goes up or down. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).
  * In a *Client Organization* on an extended *Free Trial*, the trial now ends five days after a *Subdomain* starts serving a *Site*, rather than after a *Custom Domain* is added to a *Site*.
* Changed the *Domains* link in the *App Sidebar* to *Custom Domains*, and the *Connect a Domain* button to *Connect a Custom Domain*, so the area is named for what you connect there.
* Changed the *Card* for each *Custom Domain* in your *Domains Browser* to show its DNS type, how many *Subdomains* and *Sites* it has, and where it is in setting up CloudCannon DNS, allowing you to see which domains still need setting up without opening each one.
* Changed the *Sites Not Resolving* filter in the *Client Organizations Browser* to *Subdomains Not Pointing*, and the domain filters to check the *Subdomains* of each client's *Custom Domains* rather than its *Sites*. The domain checks on the *Health* tab now report *Subdomains* rather than *Sites*.
* Changed every *Base Domain* that was set to use CloudCannon DNS, but whose nameservers never pointed at CloudCannon and were first checked more than 30 days ago, to use external DNS, so the *Domain* page for each one lists the DNS records its *Subdomains* need at the *DNS Provider* it actually uses.
* Added the *Hosting Bandwidth*, *Custom Domains*, and *Build Time* allowances to each *Subscription Plan* on the *Update your Subscription Plan* page, alongside *Seats*, allowing you to compare what each *Subscription Plan* includes and what extra capacity costs.
* Changed "Authenticate" to "Authorize" wherever you give CloudCannon access to a *Git Provider*, a DAM, or a storage provider account, so each label describes what you are doing: granting CloudCannon access to that account. For example, the *Authentication* tab is now the *Authorization* tab, the *Authenticate with GitLab* button is now *Authorize with GitLab*, the *Authenticate a new DAM* button is now *Authorize a new DAM*, and the *Unauthenticate DAM* button is now *Revoke authorization*.
* Updated the CloudCannon API, which is in Public Beta. For more information, please read our [API changelog](/documentation/developer-reference/api/changelog/).

## Fixes

* Fixed an issue where saving a new file left it marked as *New* and counted as an unsaved change.
* Fixed an issue where files in a *Collection* appeared in a scrambled order when sorted by anything other than their path.
* Fixed an issue where the *Details* page under *Site Settings* stayed locked after a failed update. The result of an update now appears at the top of the page.
* Updated dependencies to patch security vulnerabilities.
