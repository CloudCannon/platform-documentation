This release reworked the Partner experience so your *Partner Organization* is now your dashboard, and added the ability to cancel a subscription outright. It also let you create a new *Organization* from inside the app and extended the sign-up email-selection step to GitLab and Bitbucket.

It also addressed several issues, including those affecting the *App Sidebar*, the *Organization* home greeting, the *Data Editor*, the save modal, credit card checkout, and access key signature verification.

## Features & Improvements

* Improved the appearance and functionality of the *Partner Organization Home* page, including two new links in the *App Sidebar*: the *Client Organizations Browser* and the *Rewards* page.
  * The *App Sidebar* now shows *Home*, *Clients*, and *Rewards*, followed by a *Your Org* section with your *Sites*, *Projects*, *Domains*, and *Org Settings*.
  * You can now access the *Client Organizations Browser* by clicking *Clients* in the *App Sidebar*, which lists every *Client Organization* linked to your *Partner Organization* with search and filters. Members of the *Owners Permission Group* can create a client from this page with the *New Client* button.
  * You no longer need to navigate to the *Organizations Browser* to find your *Client Organizations*.
  * Moved *Partner Rewards* page from the account level into the *Partner Organization* the points are associated with. You can now access the *Partner Rewards* page by clicking *Rewards* in the *App Sidebar*, which shows your *Partner Points*, including a breakdown of where those points come from, *Partner Badge* downloads, tier, and benefits.
  * Added education modals to the *Partner Organization Home* page, the *Client Organizations Browser*, and the *Partner Rewards* page, which introduce each area on your first visit and can be reopened from the information button.

* Added the *Switch Organization Menu*, which you can access by clicking on your *Organization* name at the top of the *App Sidebar*.
  * When you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, an arrow will appear next to your *Organization* name at the top of the *App Sidebar*.
  * You no longer need to navigate to the *Organizations Browser* to open your *Client Organizations* or other *Organizations* you are a member of.
  * If you are a member of many *Organizations*, CloudCannon will show a *View more* button in the *Switch Organization Menu,* which opens the *Organizations* modal.

* Added the *Create new Organization* option to the *Account Menu*.
* Updated the cancellation flow in CloudCannon to no longer require downgrading to the *Personal Plan*. To cancel your subscription, you now end billing immediately and enter a billing cancelled state which removes the credit cards attached to the *Organization*.

* Added the ability to cancel your subscription from the *Subscription* section of your *Organization Settings*, allowing you to end billing immediately instead of downgrading to a free plan. Cancelling ends the subscription right away and removes the credit cards attached to the *Organization*.
* Extended the email-selection step during sign-up to GitLab and Bitbucket, allowing you to choose which of your account email addresses to register with. This step was previously available only when registering through GitHub.
* Redesigned the loading states shown while creating, copying, or connecting a *Site*.
* Added distinct messaging in your *Organization Settings* for when an *Organization* has no credit cards versus when it has cards but no default card set.
* Renamed the *Sites List Logo* setting to *Sites Browser Logo* under *Organization* branding, and renamed the *Transfer* heading under *Site Settings* to *Transfer Site*.
* Updated and expanded the CloudCannon REST API reference documentation, including schemas for *Permission Groups*, group members and permissions, API keys, analytics, and avatars.

## Fixes

* Fixed an issue where *Partner Rewards* points were visible to all members of a *Partner Organization* rather than only members of the *Owners* group.
* Fixed an issue where the *Organization* home greeting showed "Welcome back" to first-time visitors, based on their *Site* and client count rather than their sign-in history.
* Fixed an issue where nested panels in the *Data Editor* were misaligned and did not scroll correctly at some widths.
* Fixed an issue where opening the save modal could crash when a *Collection's* create path was misconfigured.
* Fixed an issue where a confirmation message covered the submit button after you added a credit card during checkout.
* Fixed an issue where a credit card added through the billing prompt was not set as the default card.
* Fixed an issue where the *Subscription* settings did not always reflect the latest state after a plan change.
* Fixed an issue where API requests signed with an access key could fail signature verification because of query parameter ordering or host casing.
* Improved the reliability of file metadata loading in the *Visual Editor* and *Data Editor* by retrying interrupted downloads.
* Updated dependencies to patch security vulnerabilities.
