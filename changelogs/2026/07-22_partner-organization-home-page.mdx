This release reworked the *Organization Home* page for *Partner Organizations* and added *Clients* and *Rewards* to the *App Sidebar*. It also added the *Switch Organization Menu* when you click on your *Organization* name at the top of the *App Sidebar*, removed plan downgrading from the cancellation flow, and added Bitbucket and GitLab email selection to account creation.

It also addressed several issues, including those affecting *Partner Rewards* visibility, the *Organization Home* greeting, the *Save Changes* modal, the *Subscription* page, and credit card handling.

## Features & Improvements

* Improved the appearance and functionality of the *Partner Organization Home* page, including two new links in the *App Sidebar*: the *Client Organizations Browser* and the *Rewards* page.
  * The *App Sidebar* now shows *Home*, *Clients*, and *Rewards*, followed by a *Your Org* section with your *Sites*, *Projects*, *Domains*, and *Org Settings*.
  * You can now access the *Client Organizations Browser* by clicking *Clients* in the *App Sidebar*, which lists every *Client Organization* linked to your *Partner Organization* with search and filters. Members of the *Owners Permission Group* can create a client from this page with the *New Client* button.
  * You no longer need to navigate to the *Organizations Browser* to find your *Client Organizations*.
  * Moved *Partner Rewards* page into the *Partner Organization* the points are associated with. You can now access the *Partner Rewards* page by clicking *Rewards* in the *App Sidebar*, which shows your *Partner Points*, including a breakdown of where those points come from, *Partner Badge* downloads, tier, and benefits.
  * Added education modals to the *Partner Organization Home* page, the *Client Organizations Browser*, and the *Partner Rewards* page, which introduce each area on your first visit and can be reopened from the information button.

* Added the *Switch Organization Menu*, which you can access by clicking on your *Organization* name at the top of the *App Sidebar*.
  * When you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, an arrow will appear next to your *Organization* name at the top of the *App Sidebar*.
  * You no longer need to navigate to the *Organizations Browser* to open your *Client Organizations* or other *Organizations* you are a member of.
  * If you are a member of many *Organizations*, CloudCannon will show a *View more* button in the *Switch Organization Menu,* which opens the *Organizations* modal.

* Added the *Create new Organization* option to the *Account Menu*.
* Updated the cancellation flow in CloudCannon to no longer require downgrading your subscription plan. To cancel your subscription, you will end billing immediately and enter a billing canceled state, which removes the credit cards attached to the *Organization*.
* You can now choose which email to use when signing up with GitLab or Bitbucket. This was previously available only when registering through GitHub.
* Redesigned the create pages when creating a *Site, Project,* or *Domain*.

## Fixes

* Improved the clarity of several field headings, including renaming the *Sites List Logo* under *Organization* branding to *Sites Browser Logo*, and the *Transfer* page under *Site Settings* to *Transfer Site*.
* Added distinct messaging in your *Org Settings* for when an *Organization* has no credit cards versus when it has cards but no default card set.
* Fixed an issue where *Partner Rewards* points were visible to all members of a *Partner Organization* rather than only members of the *Owners* group.
* Fixed an issue where the *Organization* home greeting showed "Welcome back" to first-time visitors, based on their *Site* and *Client Organization* count rather than their sign-in history.
* Fixed an issue where nested panels in the *Data Editor* were misaligned and did not scroll correctly at some widths.
* Fixed an issue where, when the create path for a *Collection* was misconfigured, you could not open the *Save Changes* modal.
* Fixed an issue where, in some cases, the *Subscription* page under *Org Settings* did not reflect the latest state after a plan change.
* Fixed an issue where, when adding a credit card during checkout, a confirmation message could cover the *Submit* button.
* Fixed an issue where, when prompted to add a payment method to your *Organization*, CloudCannon did not make that credit card your default payment method.
* Improved the reliability of file metadata loading in the *Visual Editor* and *Data Editor* by retrying interrupted downloads.
* Updated dependencies to patch security vulnerabilities.
