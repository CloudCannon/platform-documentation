This release provides options to control your site build:

* Jekyll [Environments](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-your-command-line-options/)

* Speed improvements to [301 Redirects](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-custom-domain/) and authentication [Custom Routes](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-authenticated-routes/)
* General bug fixes
