If the only <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/owners.yml">Owner</comp.GlossaryTerm> of an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> has left or become unreachable, you may need to recover access before anyone can manage the *Organization* again. How you recover access depends on whether you can still sign in to the original *Owner*'s account.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  This article covers recovering an *Organization* when its only *Owner* is gone. If you're locked out of your own account, such as after losing your multi-factor authentication device, read [Generate new recovery codes](/documentation/user-articles/generate-new-recovery-codes/).
</comp.Notice>

The best protection is to avoid this situation altogether: keep more than one person in the *Owners* *Permission Group*, so ownership never depends on a single account. To set this up before an *Owner* leaves, read [Transfer ownership of an Organization](/documentation/user-articles/transfer-ownership-of-an-organization/).

## Reset the password on the Owner's account

*Organizations* are often created with a shared company email address, such as `example@yourcompany.com`. If you can still receive email at that address, you can reset the password and sign in. To reset the password:

1. Go to the <a href="https://app.cloudcannon.com/users/password/new">forgotten password form</a>.
2. Enter the email address on the *Owner*'s account, then follow the reset link CloudCannon sends you.
3. Sign in, then add another person to the *Owners* *Permission Group* so more than one person can manage the *Organization*. If they aren't a member yet, [invite them to your Organization](/documentation/user-articles/add-a-team-member-to-your-organization/) first, then [add them to the Owners Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/add-a-team-member-to-a-permission-group/).

## Ask CloudCannon to transfer ownership

If you can't sign in to the original *Owner*'s account or receive its email, <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">contact our support team</a> to request an ownership transfer. Have ready:

* your *Organization* name
* the email address on the *Owner*'s account
* proof of your connection to the *Organization*, such as an invoice number
