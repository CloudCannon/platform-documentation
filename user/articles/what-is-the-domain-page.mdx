<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:details:read` permission, can view the *Domain* page for a *Custom Domain*.
</comp.Notice>

The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm> is where you manage a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> connected to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. From this page, you can see whether every <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> on your *Custom Domain* is working, add *Subdomains*, change the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">Fallback Redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm>, manage DNS, and change the settings of the domain.

You can open the *Domain* page by clicking the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm> to open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then clicking the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your domain. The *Domain* page opens on the *Summary* tab.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Summary" alt="A screenshot of the Summary tab on the Domain page shows the Subdomains section with the Add a Subdomain button, a row saying 3 of 6 Subdomains need attention with the Fix Subdomains button, the Fallback Redirect row, and the View all Subdomains link, beside the DNS section, which shows Using CloudCannon DNS with correct nameservers and DNS Records for three Subdomains that do not match, and the Minimum TLS section set to 1.2." title="The Summary tab on the Domain page" type="screenshot"/>

The *Domain* page has up to five tabs, but which tabs you see depends on your permissions:

* **Summary** — The state of the *Subdomains*, DNS, and minimum TLS version of your *Custom Domain*, and the *Custom Domain* setup guide while your *Custom Domain* or one of its *Subdomains* is still being set up. This is the default tab.
* **Subdomains** — Every *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain*, what it serves, and how visitors reach it, along with your *Fallback Redirect*.
* **DNS** — The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-provider.yml">DNS Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm> and nameserver status of your *Custom Domain*, the records *Subdomains* still need and, if it uses <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-records.yml">DNS Records</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
* **Sites** — The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> that use your *Custom Domain*.
* **Domain Settings** — The *Details*, *TLS*, and *Danger* sections, where you choose your *DNS Provider*, set the minimum TLS version, and disconnect the domain.

## The Summary tab

The *Summary* tab shows the state of your *Custom Domain* in three sections, each a heading above a *Card*: *Subdomains* on the left, and *DNS* and *Minimum TLS* on the right.

### The Custom Domain setup guide

The *Custom Domain* setup guide appears at the top of the *Summary* tab while your *Custom Domain*, or one of its *Subdomains*, is still being set up. It shows each step, what to do next, and when your domain or *Subdomain* goes live. What it asks you to do depends on your *DNS Provider*. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#point-your-domain-at-cloudcannon-dns), [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#follow-the-setup-guide), and [migrating from the legacy CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/migrate-from-the-legacy-cloudcannon-dns/).

### Subdomains

The *Subdomains* *Card* shows:

* **Add a Subdomain** — The *Add a Subdomain* button beside the *Subdomains* heading adds a *Subdomain* that serves a *Site* or redirects visitors.
* **Attention** — How many of your *Subdomains* need attention because their DNS Records don't point at CloudCannon, they have no *Site* yet, or they have an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> problem. Click the *Fix Subdomains* button to show only those *Subdomains* in the *Subdomains Browser*.
* **Fallback Redirect** — Which *Subdomain* CloudCannon sends visitors to when they open a subdomain you haven't configured, or that you have no *Fallback Redirect* and those visitors see a 404 page. Click the *Change Fallback* button to choose another *Subdomain*.

Click the *View all Subdomains* link below the *Card* to open the *Subdomains* tab. If your *Custom Domain* has no *Subdomains* yet, the *Card* says so and shows an *Add subdomain* button in place of these rows and the link.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Summary-Subdomains-Empty-Element" alt="A screenshot of the Subdomains section on the Summary tab shows No subdomains on example.org yet, a sentence about adding one to serve a site or redirect visitors, and the Add subdomain button." title="The Subdomains section before you add a Subdomain" type="ui-snippet"/>

For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/) and [updating the Fallback Redirect for your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/update-the-fallback-redirect-for-your-custom-domain/).

### DNS

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Viewing the *DNS* *Card* also requires the `base-domain:settings:dns:read` permission.
</comp.Notice>

The *DNS* *Card* has two rows. The first is titled with your *DNS Provider* (*Using CloudCannon DNS*, *Using Legacy CloudCannon DNS*, or *Using External DNS*) and shows the state of your nameservers:

* **Nameservers are correct** — Your domain uses *CloudCannon DNS*. The row also says when CloudCannon last checked.
* **Please update the nameservers at your Domain Registrar** — Set the CloudCannon nameservers at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then click the *I've updated my nameservers* button in the setup guide.
* **Checking your nameservers** — You marked the nameservers as updated, and CloudCannon is checking for them. This could take 48 hours.
* **Nameservers do not match** — CloudCannon still can't see the CloudCannon nameservers at your domain registrar.
* **Nameservers have changed** — The nameservers of your domain no longer point at *CloudCannon DNS*.
* **Creating the DNS zone** — CloudCannon is creating the DNS zone for your domain. This takes a moment.
* **Could not create the DNS zone** — CloudCannon couldn't create the DNS zone. Click the *Retry zone creation* button in the setup guide.
* **Please migrate to the new CloudCannon DNS** — Your domain uses the legacy *CloudCannon DNS*.
* **Managed by your DNS Provider** — Your domain uses <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>. The row also names the nameservers CloudCannon found.

The second row, *Required DNS Records*, says whether the DNS Records of your *Subdomains* point at CloudCannon:

* **DNS Records are correct.** or **The DNS Record is correct.** — Every record points at CloudCannon.
* **DNS Records for [Number] Subdomains do not match. Action required.**, **The DNS Record does not match. Action required.**, or **A DNS Record has changed. Action required.** — One or more records don't point at CloudCannon. Open the *DNS* tab to see which.
* **Please add the DNS Record at your DNS Provider.** — A *Subdomain* you added on external DNS still needs its record.
* **Checking your DNS Records. This usually takes less than an hour.** — CloudCannon is checking records you marked as updated.
* **Waiting for the CloudCannon nameservers.** — Your domain uses *CloudCannon DNS*, and its nameservers don't point at CloudCannon yet.
* **CloudCannon has not checked the DNS Record yet.** — CloudCannon hasn't checked a record yet.
* **No DNS Records are required yet.** — Your *Custom Domain* has no *Subdomains*.

Click the *View all DNS Records* link below the *Card* to open the *DNS* tab, where you can check your nameservers and records again or change the *DNS Provider*.

### Minimum TLS

The *Minimum TLS* *Card* shows the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, the lowest version any *Subdomain* accepts, and whether every *Subdomain* meets it. A *Subdomain* can require a higher version. If any *Subdomains* are below the minimum, the *Card* says how many, such as *Five Subdomains are below this minimum. Action required.* Click the *View TLS settings* link below the *Card* to open the *TLS* section under *Domain Settings*. For more information, please read our documentation on [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/).

## The Subdomains tab

The *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page shows the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomains-browser.yml">Subdomains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm> and the *Fallback Redirect* section.

### The Subdomains Browser

The *Subdomains Browser* lists every *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain*. For each *Subdomain*, the *Subdomains Browser* shows:

* **Subdomain** — The address of the *Subdomain*, with a line below it that says which *Site* it serves and at what path, that it serves multiple *Sites*, where it redirects visitors, or that it has no *Site* yet and visitors see a 404 page. CloudCannon labels the *Subdomain* that is your *Fallback Redirect* with *Current Fallback*.
* **Hosting** — How visitors reach the *Subdomain*: *CloudCannon Hosting*, *Cloudflare Proxy*, or *Single Origin*.
* **HTTPS** — The status of the *Subdomain's* certificate, such as *Active*, *Issuing*, *Renewing*, *Expiring*, *Expired*, *Failed*, *DNS changed*, or *Missing*. Hover over a status to see more detail, and open the *Subdomain* page for the full explanation.
* **DNS** — Whether the *Subdomain's* record points at CloudCannon: *Configured*, *Not pointing at CloudCannon*, *No DNS Record*, *Not checked yet*, or *Checking* while CloudCannon looks for a record you recently added or changed.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Subdomains-Tab" alt="A screenshot of the Subdomains tab on the Domain page shows the Name sort dropdown, the Add Filter button, the Subdomains Browser with Subdomain, Hosting, HTTPS, and DNS columns, and the Fallback Redirect section." title="The Subdomains tab on the Domain page" type="screenshot"/>

Click the sort dropdown to the left of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/filter-bar.yml">Filter Bar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which shows the current sort (e.g., *Name*), to sort the *Subdomains Browser* by:

* **Name** — *Subdomains* with fewer levels first (e.g., `example.com` before `www.example.com`), then alphabetically. This is the default.
* **Name (Z-A)** — The reverse of *Name*.
* **Needs attention first** — *Subdomains* that don't reach CloudCannon, have no *Site* yet, or have a certificate problem, first.
* **Recently added** — The newest *Subdomains* first.

Type in the *Filter Bar* to find a *Subdomain* by name, or click the *Add Filter* button to show only the *Subdomains* that match one or more values:

* **Attention** — *Needs attention* or *Does not need attention*.
* **Type** — *Site* or *Redirect*.
* **Hosting** — *CloudCannon Hosting*, *Cloudflare Proxy*, or *Single Origin*.
* **HTTPS** — *Active*, *Issuing*, *Renewing*, *Expired*, *Failed*, or *Missing*.
* **DNS Record** — *Configured*, *Not pointing at CloudCannon*, *No DNS Record*, or *Not checked yet*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Subdomains-Tab-Filter-DNS-Dropdown" alt="A screenshot of the Subdomains tab shows the Add Filter menu with Attention, Type, Hosting, HTTPS, and DNS Record filters, the DNS Record filter open with Configured, Not pointing at CloudCannon, No DNS Record, and Not checked yet, and Subdomains with HTTPS statuses such as Active, Waiting, and Expired." title="The filters on the Subdomains tab" type="screenshot"/>

Click a *Subdomain* in the *Subdomains Browser* to open its [Subdomain page](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-the-subdomain-page/), where you can manage its *Sites* or its redirect, and its hosting, HTTPS, and DNS. Each *Subdomain* in the *Subdomains Browser* also has a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm>, with the following options:

* *Open Subdomain page*
* *Visit live Subdomain*
* *Change redirect target* (for a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm>)
* *Configure hosting*
* *Configure HTTPS*
* *Check DNS now*
* *View DNS history*
* *Use as Fallback Redirect*
* *Change type to Site* or *Change type to Redirect*
* *Delete Subdomain*

For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/), [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/), [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/), and [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/).

### The Fallback Redirect

Below the *Subdomains Browser*, the *Fallback Redirect* section shows where CloudCannon sends visitors who open a subdomain you haven't configured, such as a misspelled address. It shows which *Subdomain* you redirect them to and the type of redirect.

By default, the *Fallback Redirect* is the first *Subdomain* you added. Click the *Change Fallback* button to choose a different *Subdomain*. Until you add your first *Subdomain*, the section explains that visitors see a 404 page, and has no *Change Fallback* button.

For more information, please read our documentation on [updating the Fallback Redirect for your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/update-the-fallback-redirect-for-your-custom-domain/).

## The DNS tab

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Viewing the *DNS* tab also requires the `base-domain:settings:dns:read` permission.
</comp.Notice>

The *DNS* tab shows:

* **Nameservers** — Your *DNS Provider* and the nameservers CloudCannon found, with a *Check now* button. On *CloudCannon DNS* or the legacy *CloudCannon DNS*, the same setup guide as the *Summary* tab appears here too.
* **Required records** — The *Subdomains* whose DNS Record still needs a change, with a *Fix* button on *CloudCannon DNS* or an *I've updated it* button on external DNS.
* **DNS records** — On *CloudCannon DNS*, every record CloudCannon serves for your domain, with an *Add record* button.
* **Wildcard records** — On external DNS, the optional wildcard record, which catches subdomains you haven't added, and the TXT Records for the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/w/wildcard-certificate.yml">wildcard SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/), [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/), [managing your DNS Records on CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-dns-records-on-cloudcannon-dns/), and [adding an auto-generated SSL Certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/).

## The Sites tab

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Viewing the *Sites* tab also requires the `base-domain:settings:details:read` or `base-domain:delete:write` permission.
</comp.Notice>

The *Sites* tab lists the *Sites* in your *Organization* that are served on your *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

## The Domain Settings tab

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Viewing the *Details* and *TLS* sections also requires the `base-domain:settings:details:read` permission, and viewing the *Danger* section requires the `base-domain:delete:write` permission.
</comp.Notice>

The *Domain Settings* tab has three sections:

* **Details** — The *Base Domain* and its *DNS Provider*, which you can switch with the *Switch to CloudCannon DNS* or *Switch to External DNS* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/) and [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).
* **TLS** — The minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/).
* **Danger** — The *Disconnect [Base Domain]* section, where you disconnect the *Custom Domain* from your *Organization*. For more information, please read our documentation on [disconnecting a Custom Domain from your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/disconnect-a-custom-domain-from-your-organization/).

## Why the Domain page might look different

### Permissions

Tabs and sections you don't have permission for are hidden. If you open a tab you don't have permission for, the *Domain* page shows a *Permission denied* page.

Some controls stay visible but are disabled, and hovering over one says which settings you don't have permission to edit:

* **Without `base-domain:settings:details:write`** — The *Add a Subdomain*, *Change Fallback*, *I've updated it*, and *Check now* buttons for *Subdomain* records, the *Minimum TLS version* field and the *Update minimum TLS* button, and every option in the *Context Menu* for a *Subdomain* except *Open Subdomain page*, *Visit live Subdomain*, and *View DNS history*.
* **Without `base-domain:settings:dns:write`** — The *Add record*, *Fix*, *I've updated my nameservers*, and *Retry zone creation* buttons, and the *DNS Provider* options and the *Switch to [DNS Provider]* button in the *Details* section. The *Context Menu* for a record in the *DNS records* table has only the *View* option.

The *Change type to Redirect* and *Delete Subdomain* options also need the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for every *Site* the *Subdomain* serves.

Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/) hold all of these permissions, so they see every tab and can use every control. Members of the *Technical Editors*, *Editors*, and *Billing* groups hold none of them. If you need access, ask someone who manages <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> in your *Organization* for the permission.

### Subscription and Seats

If your *Organization's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> is inactive, or your *Organization* doesn't have enough <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the *Domain* page shows *Custom Domains unavailable* in place of its tabs. If you have the `org:billing:read` permission, the page says how to get access again, and a *View Subscription* link opens the *Subscription* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Otherwise, the page asks you to contact your Organization Owner.
