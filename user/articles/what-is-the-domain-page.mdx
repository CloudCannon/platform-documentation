<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:details:read` permission, can view the *Domain* page for a *Custom Domain*.
</comp.Notice>

The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm> is where you manage a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> connected to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. From this page, you can see whether every <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> on your *Custom Domain* is working, add *Subdomains*, change the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">Fallback Redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm>, manage DNS, and change the settings of the domain.

You can open the *Domain* page by clicking the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm> to open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then clicking the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your domain. The *Domain* page opens on the *Summary* tab.

The *Domain* page has up to five tabs:

* **Summary** — The state of the *Subdomains*, DNS, and minimum TLS version of your *Custom Domain*, and a setup guide while DNS isn't set up yet. This is the default tab.
* **Subdomains** — Every *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain*, what it serves, and how visitors reach it, along with your *Fallback Redirect*.
* **DNS** — The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-provider.yml">DNS Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm> and nameserver status of your *Custom Domain*, the records *Subdomains* still need and, if it uses <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-records.yml">DNS Records</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
* **Sites** — The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> that use your *Custom Domain*.
* **Domain Settings** — The *Details*, *TLS*, and *Danger* sections, where you choose your *DNS Provider*, set the minimum TLS version, and disconnect the domain.

Which tabs you see depends on your permissions.

## The Summary tab

The *Summary* tab shows the state of your *Custom Domain* in three sections, each a heading above a *Card*: *Subdomains* on the left, and *DNS* and *Minimum TLS* on the right.

### The setup guide

While your *Custom Domain* or a new *Subdomain* isn't live yet, a setup guide appears at the top of the *Summary* tab, above the *Subdomains* section. It shows each step and what to do next, depending on whether your *Custom Domain* uses *CloudCannon DNS* or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>:

* **CloudCannon DNS** — The guide first prompts you to copy your existing DNS Records, such as MX Records for email and TXT Records for domain verification. It then shows the nameservers to set at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#point-your-domain-at-cloudcannon-dns).
* **External DNS** — The guide names each new *Subdomain* that still needs its records at your *DNS Provider*, or the first two and how many more if there are more than three, with an *Open the DNS tab* button that opens the *DNS* tab, where the records are listed. The TXT Records for the optional wildcard <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> of your domain are on the *DNS* tab. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#follow-the-setup-guide).

CloudCannon refreshes the guide every minute while the page is open. You don't need to keep it open, because CloudCannon emails everyone who can edit the settings of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> when the domain goes live on *CloudCannon DNS*, or when a *Subdomain* you marked as updated goes live. If your domain or *Subdomain* goes live while you have the *Summary* tab open, the guide shows that it is live until you click the *Dismiss* button.

The *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* shows the same guide for the *Subdomain* that hosts a *Site*.

### Subdomains

The *Subdomains* *Card* shows:

* **Add a Subdomain** — The *Add a Subdomain* button beside the *Subdomains* heading adds a *Subdomain* that serves a *Site* or redirects visitors.
* **Explainer** — A sentence below the heading says that each *Subdomain* can serve one or more *Sites*, or redirect visitors to another domain.
* **Attention** — *No Subdomains need attention*, or *[Number] of [Total] Subdomains need attention* with a count of the *Subdomains* whose DNS Records don't point at CloudCannon, that have no *Site* yet, or that have an *SSL Certificate* problem. When any *Subdomain* needs attention, click the *Fix Subdomains* button to open the *Subdomains* tab with only those *Subdomains* listed. To list every *Subdomain* again, remove the *Subdomain needs attention* filter.
* **Fallback Redirect** — *Fallback Redirect: [Subdomain]*, the *Subdomain* to which CloudCannon sends visitors when they open a subdomain you haven't configured, or *No Fallback Redirect* if visitors see a 404 page. Click the *Change Fallback* button to choose another *Subdomain*.

Click the *View all Subdomains* link below the *Card* to open the *Subdomains* tab. If your *Custom Domain* has no *Subdomains* yet, the *Card* shows *No subdomains on [Base Domain] yet* and an *Add subdomain* button in place of these rows and the link.

For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/) and [updating the Fallback Redirect for your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/update-the-fallback-redirect-for-your-custom-domain/).

### DNS

The *DNS* *Card* shows:

* **DNS Provider** — The row is titled with the *DNS Provider*, *Using CloudCannon DNS*, *Using Legacy CloudCannon DNS*, or *Using External DNS*, and shows the state of your nameservers underneath: *Nameservers are correct* with when CloudCannon last checked, *Please update the nameservers at your Domain Registrar*, *Checking your nameservers*, *Nameservers do not match*, *Nameservers have changed*, *Creating the DNS zone*, *Could not create the DNS zone*, *Please migrate to the new CloudCannon DNS*, or *Managed by your DNS Provider*. If CloudCannon couldn't complete the latest nameserver check, the row also shows *Could not check* with how long ago the check failed.
* **Required DNS Records** — Whether the DNS Records of your *Subdomains* point at CloudCannon: *DNS Records are correct.*, or how many *Subdomains* have a record that doesn't match, such as *DNS Records for two Subdomains do not match. Action required.* If only one *Subdomain* is affected, the row describes that *Subdomain's* record instead, such as *The DNS Record does not match. Action required.*, *A DNS Record has changed. Action required.*, or *Please add the DNS Record at your DNS Provider.* Until CloudCannon has checked a record, the row shows *CloudCannon has not checked the DNS Record yet.* While CloudCannon checks records you marked as updated, the row shows *Checking your DNS Records. This usually takes less than an hour.* While your nameservers don't point at *CloudCannon DNS* yet, the row shows *Waiting for the CloudCannon nameservers.*, and with no *Subdomains*, *No DNS Records are required yet.*

Click the *View all DNS Records* link below the *Card* to open the *DNS* tab, where you can check your nameservers and records again or change the *DNS Provider*.

### Minimum TLS

The *Minimum TLS* *Card* shows the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, the lowest version any *Subdomain* accepts, and whether every *Subdomain* meets it. A *Subdomain* can require a higher version. If any *Subdomains* are below the minimum, the *Card* says how many, such as *Two Subdomains are below this minimum. Action required.* Click the *View TLS settings* link below the *Card* to open the *TLS* section under *Domain Settings*. For more information, please read our documentation on [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/).

## The Subdomains tab

The *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page lists every *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain*. For each *Subdomain*, the list shows:

* **Subdomain** — The address of the *Subdomain*, with what it serves below it: *Serves the Site [Site Name].*, *Serves the Site [Site Name] at the path [Subpath].*, *Serves multiple Sites.*, *Redirects visitors to [Target].*, or *No Site yet. Visitors will see a 404 page.* If the *Subdomain* is your *Fallback Redirect* and has no *Site*, it shows *No Site yet. Visitors to this Subdomain and to unconfigured Subdomains will see a 404 page.* instead. CloudCannon labels the *Subdomain* that is your *Fallback Redirect* with *Current Fallback*.
* **Hosting** — How visitors reach the *Subdomain*: *CloudCannon Hosting*, *Cloudflare Proxy*, or *Single Origin*.
* **HTTPS** — The status of the *Subdomain's* certificate, such as *Active*, *Issuing*, *Renewing*, *Expiring*, *Expired*, *Failed*, *DNS changed*, or *Missing*. Hover over a status to see more detail, and open the *Subdomain* page for the full explanation.
* **DNS** — Whether the *Subdomain's* record points at CloudCannon: *Configured*, *Not pointing at CloudCannon*, *No DNS Record*, *Not checked yet*, or *Checking* while CloudCannon looks for the record of a *Subdomain* that, in the last 48 hours, you added, changed the hosting of, or marked as updated, or whose domain changed *DNS Provider*.

Click a *Subdomain* in the list to open its page, where you can manage its *Sites* or its redirect, and its hosting, HTTPS, and DNS. Each *Subdomain* in the list also has a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm>, with the *Open Subdomain page*, *Visit live Subdomain*, *Change redirect target* (for a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm>), *Configure hosting*, *Configure HTTPS*, *Check DNS now*, *View DNS history*, *Use as Fallback Redirect*, *Change type to Site* or *Change type to Redirect*, and *Delete Subdomain* options. On your current *Fallback Redirect*, the *Use as Fallback Redirect* option reads *Current Fallback Redirect* and is disabled. Below the list, the *Fallback Redirect* section shows your current *Fallback Redirect*.

### The sort dropdown and Filter Bar

Click the sort dropdown to the left of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/filter-bar.yml">Filter Bar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which shows the current sort (e.g., *Name*), to sort the list by:

* **Name** — *Subdomains* with fewer levels first (e.g., `example.com` before `www.example.com`), then alphabetically. This is the default.
* **Name (Z-A)** — The reverse of *Name*.
* **Needs attention first** — *Subdomains* that don't reach CloudCannon, have no *Site* yet, or have a certificate problem, first.
* **Recently added** — The newest *Subdomains* first.

Type in the *Filter Bar* to find a *Subdomain* by name, or click the *Add Filter* button to show only the *Subdomains* that match one or more values:

* **Attention** — *Needs attention* or *Does not need attention*.
* **Type** — *Site* or *Redirect*.
* **Hosting** — *CloudCannon Hosting*, *Cloudflare Proxy*, or *Single Origin*.
* **HTTPS** — *Active*, *Issuing*, *Renewing*, *Expired*, *Failed*, or *Missing*.
* **DNS Record** — *Configured*, *Not pointing at CloudCannon*, *No DNS Record*, or *Not checked yet*.

For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/), [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/), [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/), and [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/).

## The DNS tab

The *DNS* tab shows:

* **Nameservers** — On *CloudCannon DNS*, the *CloudCannon DNS* heading, a *Configure CloudCannon DNS* link, and a sentence ending in a *change the DNS Provider* link to the *Details* section. Until your domain is live, the same setup guide as the *Summary* tab follows, with a table that lists each nameserver your domain needs under *Required*, the nameservers CloudCannon *Found*, and a *Status* for each nameserver. Once your domain is live, the guide stays on the *DNS* tab as *[Base Domain] uses CloudCannon DNS*, and the table collapses into a closed *Nameservers* row inside it that says the nameservers match. On the legacy *CloudCannon DNS*, the same heading has a *Configure CloudCannon DNS* link, then the *Move [Base Domain] to the new CloudCannon DNS* setup guide with the *Move to the new CloudCannon DNS* button. On external DNS, the *External DNS nameservers* table lists the nameservers CloudCannon found.
* **Required records** — The *Subdomains* whose DNS Record still needs a change, in a table with *Subdomain*, *Record*, and *Status* columns. The *Record* column shows the record each *Subdomain* needs, then any records CloudCannon found at that name. Each row also has the *Fix* button on *CloudCannon DNS*, or the *I've updated it* button on external DNS. When every record is connected, a notice says there is nothing to add. On *CloudCannon DNS*, this section is hidden until your nameservers point at *CloudCannon DNS*.
* **DNS records** — On *CloudCannon DNS*, a *Configure CloudCannon DNS* link and a table of every record *CloudCannon DNS* serves for your domain, with a *Filter* field and an *Add record* button. Click the *Context Menu* button for a record to edit or delete it.

Each table that CloudCannon checks has a footer that says when CloudCannon last checked, with a *Check now* button that checks every record in the table again. On external DNS, the footers of the record tables also say *Check now checks every record once.* For a minute after a check, the *Check now* button is disabled. The footer of the nameservers table also has a *View nameserver check history* icon. The *Copy* icon next to a nameserver or record value copies it, then turns into a checkmark and shows *Copied!*.

On external DNS, the tab also shows one warning notice that lists each *Subdomain* that still isn't pointing at CloudCannon or whose DNS Record changed, and the *Wildcard record (optional)* table, for a record that catches subdomains you haven't added. It also shows the status of the optional <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/w/wildcard-certificate.yml">wildcard SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your *Base Domain*: the TXT Records for its first issue under *Wildcard certificate records (optional)*, the TXT Records a renewal needs under *Wildcard certificate records*, and, under *Automatic renewal record (optional)*, a CNAME Record you can add once so the certificate renews by itself, without TXT Records. Once you have added the CNAME Record, the tab says that the certificate renews by itself through the CNAME Record you added.

## The Sites tab

The *Sites* tab lists the *Sites* in your *Organization* that are served on your *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

## The Domain Settings tab

The *Domain Settings* tab has three sections:

* **Details** — The *Base Domain* and its *DNS Provider*. To switch, select the other *DNS Provider* option, then click the *Switch to CloudCannon DNS* or *Switch to External DNS* button. Until you select the other option, the button reads *Update domain* and is disabled. Selecting the *CloudCannon DNS* option shows *What happens when you switch*, and selecting the *External DNS* option shows a warning that the records in your current DNS zone stop being used once you switch. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/) and [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).
* **TLS** — The minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/).
* **Danger** — The *Disconnect [Base Domain]* section, where you disconnect the *Custom Domain* from your *Organization*. For more information, please read our documentation on [disconnecting a Custom Domain from your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/disconnect-a-custom-domain-from-your-organization/).

## What changes what you see

### Permissions

The tabs and sections of the *Domain* page that you don't have permission for are hidden:

* **Summary** and **Subdomains** — Need the `base-domain:details:read` permission, or a permission that shows the *Domain Settings* tab.
* **DNS** — Needs the `base-domain:settings:dns:read` permission. Without it, the *DNS* *Card* on the *Summary* tab is also hidden, and the setup guide on external DNS shows an *Open the Subdomains tab* or *Open the Subdomain page* button in place of the *Open the DNS tab* button.
* **Sites** and **Domain Settings** — Need the `base-domain:settings:details:read` or `base-domain:delete:write` permission. On the *Domain Settings* tab, the *Details* and *TLS* sections need `base-domain:settings:details:read`, and the *Danger* section needs `base-domain:delete:write`. Without `base-domain:settings:details:read`, the *View TLS settings* link on the *Summary* tab and the *change the DNS Provider* links on the *DNS* tab are also hidden.

If you open a tab you don't have permission for, or have none of these permissions, the *Domain* page shows a *Permission denied* page.

Other controls stay visible but are disabled, and hovering over one says which settings you don't have permission to edit:

* **Without `base-domain:settings:details:write`** — The *Add a Subdomain*, *Change Fallback*, *I've updated it*, and *Check now* buttons for *Subdomain* records, the *Minimum TLS version* field and the *Update minimum TLS* button, and every option in the *Context Menu* for a *Subdomain* except *Open Subdomain page*, *Visit live Subdomain*, and *View DNS history*.
* **Without `base-domain:settings:dns:write`** — The *Add record*, *Fix*, *I've updated my nameservers*, and *Retry zone creation* buttons, and the *DNS Provider* options and the *Switch to [DNS Provider]* button in the *Details* section. The *Context Menu* for a record in the *DNS records* table has only the *View* option.

The *Change type to Redirect* and *Delete Subdomain* options also need the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for every *Site* the *Subdomain* serves.

Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/) hold all of these permissions, so they see every tab and can use every control. Members of the *Technical Editors*, *Editors*, and *Billing* groups hold none of them. If you need access, ask someone who manages <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> in your *Organization* for the permission.

### Subscription and Seats

If your *Organization's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> is inactive, or your *Organization* doesn't have enough <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the *Domain* page shows *Custom Domains unavailable* in place of its tabs. If you have the `org:billing:read` permission, the page says how to get access again, such as reactivating your *Subscription*, or removing <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization-member.yml">Organization Members</comp.GlossaryTerm>, turning off <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/guest-access.yml">Guest Access</comp.GlossaryTerm> on a *Site*, or upgrading your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and a *View Subscription* link opens the *Subscription* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Otherwise, the page asks you to contact your Organization Owner.
