<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> and <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can manage <em>Add-Ons</em> and <em>Overages</em>.</p>
</comp.Notice>

Turning <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm> on or off controls both your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm> limits. When enabled, CloudCannon automatically extends these resources if your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> exceeds the limits in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. At the end of each month, CloudCannon will invoice you for the amount of *Overages* you used over your *Subscription Plan* limit in 1-hour or 100 GB bundles, rounded up.

For more information on the available *Overages* and what happens when you reach a limit, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).

To enable or disable *Overages*:

1. Navigate to the *Subscription* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the *Review your Subscription Plan* button in the billing summary. CloudCannon will open the *Your Subscription Plan* page.
3. Turn the *Overages* setting on or off.
4. Click the *Enable Overages* or *Disable Overages* button to save your change.

Your *Overage* settings will update immediately.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Your-Subscription-Plan-Annual-Standard-Plan" alt="A screenshot of the Your Subscription Plan page shows the Overages toggle, plan resources, and estimated bill." title="The Your Subscription Plan page" type="screenshot"/>

You can check what your *Subscription Plan* includes and how much you have used for each resource in the *Plan resources* section of this page.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  When *Overages* are disabled, your *Sites* go offline if you reach your bandwidth limit, and CloudCannon stops running builds once you reach your build time limit. You can still save your changes, but they will not build. Review your usage before you disable *Overages*.
</comp.Notice>
