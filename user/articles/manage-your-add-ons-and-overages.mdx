<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can manage Add-Ons and Overages.</p>
</comp.Notice>

When <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm> are enabled, CloudCannon automatically extends your account resources as your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> exceeds the limits included in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> Plan, and the extra usage appears on your bill.

For more information on the available <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm> and what happens when you reach a limit, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).

This article covers turning *Overages* on or off, which controls your *Hosting Bandwidth* and *Build Time* limits. To check what your Plan includes and how much you have used for each resource, see the *Plan resources* section on the *Your Subscription Plan* page, opened in the steps below.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Your-Subscription-Plan-Annual-Standard-Plan" alt="A screenshot of the Your Subscription Plan page shows the Hosting add-ons toggle, plan resources, and estimated bill." title="The Your Subscription Plan page" type="screenshot"/>

To enable or disable *Overages*:

1. Click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Subscription* page.
2. Click *Review your billing plan* in the billing summary to open the *Your Subscription Plan* page.
3. Turn the *Hosting add-ons* setting on or off.
4. Click *Enable add-ons* or *Disable add-ons* to save your change.

Your *Overage* settings will update immediately.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  When *Overages* are disabled, your *Sites* go offline if you reach your bandwidth limit, and you cannot save build-triggering changes once you reach your build time limit. Review your usage before you disable *Overages*.
</comp.Notice>
