A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/testing-domain.yml">Testing Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a free *.cloudvent.net* URL that CloudCannon assigns to every <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Each *Testing Domain* uses a randomly generated adjective and noun separated by a hyphen (e.g., grey-grouse.cloudvent.net or brave-submarine.cloudvent.net).

*Testing Domains* are useful for seeing your content live after a successful [Site build](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-site-build/). If you use a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/publishing-workflow.yml">publishing workflow</comp.GlossaryTerm>, each *Site* in the workflow has its own *Testing Domain*, so you can see the content of each branch. For more information, please read our documentation on [setting up a staging workflow](/documentation/developer-guides/staging-workflow-guide/).

You can [view your Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/view-your-testing-domain/) any time after your first successful *Site* build.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Header-Tools-Post-Build" alt="A screenshot of the Site Header shows a link to the Testing Domain, which appears after your first successful Site build." title="The Site Header" type="ui-snippet"/>

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  To stop serving content on your *Testing Domain*, you can [set a password](/documentation/developer-articles/enable-password-authentication/) to prevent unauthorized visitors, or switch your *Site* to *Headless Mode*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Headless Mode is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-headless-mode/). If neither suits your *Site*, contact our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>, and include the *Site* name and its *Testing Domain*.
</comp.Notice>

You can use your CloudCannon *Testing Domain* as your primary domain; however, we don't recommend doing this for two reasons.

One, you cannot change the automatically generated domain name without creating a new *Site* on CloudCannon. The automatically generated domain may not be right for you or your *Site*. You can generate a new one by creating a new *Site* with the same files, but this can be inefficient.

Two, by default, CloudCannon automatically de-indexes *Testing Domains* so that they do not appear in search engines. CloudCannon does this by serving the following *robots.txt* file on all *Testing Domains*, even if your *Site* has its own *robots.txt* file, and by adding an `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` header to every page.

<comp.CodeBlock language="plaintext" source="robots.txt">
```

User-agent: *
Disallow: /
noindex: /

```
</comp.CodeBlock>

On a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>, CloudCannon serves your *Site's* own *robots.txt* file, or a default file if your *Site* doesn't have one. For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Custom Domain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-custom-domain/#the-default-robots-txt-file).

## Custom Testing Domains

<comp.Notice info_type="pricing">
  **Subscription Plan required**

  *Custom Testing Domains* are available on our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">Team or Enterprise Plan</a>, and for members of our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">Partner Program</a>.
</comp.Notice>

A *Custom Testing Domain* lets you choose the domain name for your `.cloudvent.net` URL. Choosing your *Testing Domain* improves your branding if you are sharing the URL outside of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> (e.g., sending a test Site to a client).

For access to this feature, please contact our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>. We'll set you up with a unique handle for your *Organization*. This handle will become the suffix for all *Testing Domains* (e.g., `prefix-company-name.cloudvent.net`).

Whenever you create a new *Site*, the *Create a Site* page will ask you to enter a unique prefix in the *Custom Testing Domain Prefix* field, which is prepended to your *Organization's* suffix.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Custom-Testing-Domain-Prefix" alt="A screenshot of the Custom Testing Domain Prefix field on the Create a Site page shows that the prefix is prepended to -company-name.cloudvent.net." title="Custom Testing Domain Prefix field in Site creation" type="ui-snippet"/>

Take care when adding a *Custom Testing Domain* to a new *Site*. Each full domain can only be used once, so you can't reuse that combination of prefix and suffix.
