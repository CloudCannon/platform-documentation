A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> is the billing Plan attached to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Your *Subscription* determines which features are available, how many resources your *Organization* can use, and how you are billed.

Each *Organization* has its own *Subscription*, so if you belong to more than one *Organization*, each is billed separately.

<comp.Notice info_type="pricing">
  For current plans, features, and pricing, see the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">CloudCannon pricing page</a>.
</comp.Notice>

## Plans

CloudCannon offers a range of Plans, designed for teams of different sizes and requirements:

* **Standard** — For small teams who need collaboration features and custom domains.
* **Team** — For larger teams who need higher resource limits, more *Team Members*, and additional hosting bandwidth.
* **Enterprise** — For organizations with custom requirements, including dedicated support, custom billing arrangements, and advanced security features such as mandatory SSO.

Each Plan includes a set amount of *Team Members*, bandwidth, custom domains, and build time.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  You can try CloudCannon for free before committing to a paid plan, without adding a payment method. Your trial starts on the Standard Plan, and you can upgrade it to the Team Plan. For more information, including how long the trial lasts, please read our documentation on [what CloudCannon's free trial is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-cloudcannons-free-trial/).
</comp.Notice>

Paid plans are billed monthly or yearly. Yearly billing is discounted compared to monthly billing. You can switch between billing periods at any time. For more information, please read our documentation on [updating your billing period](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-billing-period/).

If you need more than your Plan includes, you can extend your limits with <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">add-ons</comp.GlossaryTerm>. For more information, please read our documentation on [what add-ons and overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).

If you try to use a feature that is not included in your current plan, CloudCannon prompts you to upgrade and shows which Plan includes that feature. For more information, please read our documentation on [updating your Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-subscription-plan/).

## Managing your Subscription

You manage your *Subscription* on the *Subscription* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. It shows your current plan, estimated bill, billing period, and renewal date, and it's where you update your payment method.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Subscription-Annual-Standard-Plan" alt="A screenshot of the Subscription page shows the billing summary, including the current plan, estimated bill, and billing details." title="The Subscription page in Org Settings" type="screenshot"/>

Members of the *Owners* or *Billing* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/) can view and manage your *Subscription*. On [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/), billing access comes from the `org:billing:read` permission to view and the `org:billing:write` permission to make changes; viewing plan pricing also requires `org:billing:write`. If you don't see the *Subscription* page or the plan options, ask an *Owner* to update your permissions.

If your *Organization* is on an Enterprise Plan, billing is arranged directly with the CloudCannon team. Self-serve plan changes and payment method management aren't available, and the *Subscription* page shows your plan details and directs you to contact CloudCannon to make changes. For questions about Enterprise billing, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>.
