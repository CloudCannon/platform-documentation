<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* [Default Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/) can view your *Organization* as another *Team Member*.
</comp.Notice>

The [Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-permission-groups/) a [Team Member](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-team-member/) belongs to determine what they can see and do in your [Organization](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-an-organization/). *View as* loads CloudCannon with the exact permission set of another *Team Member*, so you can confirm what they can access, either before or after you change their *Permissions*.

*View as* only changes which resources you can open and edit in CloudCannon. It does not sign you in as that *Team Member*, and any changes you make while viewing as them are still logged under your name. This mode applies to one *Organization* at a time. Navigating away from the *Organization*, or logging out, ends it automatically.

If you manage a *Client Organization* through the CloudCannon Partner programme, members of that *Organization's* *Partner Permission Group* can also view it as another *Team Member*.

## Start viewing as a Team Member

To view your *Organization* as another *Team Member*:

1. Navigate to the *Team* page under *Org Settings*.
2. Under the *Members* tab, click the more options icon (three vertical dots) on the card for the *Team Member* you want to view as.
3. Select *View as [Team Member Name]*. CloudCannon will open the *View as [Team Member Name]* modal.
4. Click the *View as [Team Member Name]* button.

CloudCannon will reload and display your *Organization* with that *Team Member's* permissions, with a *Viewing as* banner pinned to the top of the page.

## Stop viewing as a Team Member

You can return to your regular view at any time. To stop viewing as a *Team Member*:

1. Click the *Stop* button in the *Viewing as* banner at the top of the page.

CloudCannon will reload and return you to your regular view.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Viewing-As-Banner" alt="A screenshot of the Viewing as banner shows the name of the Team Member being viewed and a Stop button." title="The Viewing as banner" type="ui-snippet"/>

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  You can also stop viewing as a *Team Member* from the account menu in the *App Sidebar* by selecting *Stop viewing as [Team Member Name]*.
</comp.Notice>
