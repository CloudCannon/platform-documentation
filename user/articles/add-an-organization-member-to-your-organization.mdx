<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `group:member:write` permission, can add *Organization Members* to your *Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

To collaborate with someone in CloudCannon, invite them to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> as a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization-member.yml">Organization Member</comp.GlossaryTerm>. When you invite them, you assign them to one or more <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which control what they can access.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Only members of the [Owners Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/#owners) and the [Partner Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/#partner) can add *Organization Members* to the *Owners Group*.
</comp.Notice>

To add an *Organization Member* to your *Organization*:

1. Navigate to the *People* page under *Org Settings*.
2. Under the *Members* tab, click the *Add members* button on the right. CloudCannon will open the *Invite Organization Members* modal.
3. In the *Email address* field, enter the email address of the person you want to add to your *Organization*.
4. In the *Groups* field, select the *Permission Group(s)* to which you want to add this *Organization Member*. You must select at least one.
5. Click the *Add member(s)* button.

CloudCannon will send an email to your pending *Organization Members*, inviting them to your *Organization*. If someone does not have a CloudCannon account, this email will prompt them to create one. While you wait for them to accept the invitation, their name will appear in the list of *Pending Members* under the *Members* tab of the *People* page.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Adding a new *Organization Member* may cause your *Organization* to exceed the number of *Organization Members* included in your <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">Subscription Plan</a>. If adding an *Organization Member* takes your *Organization* over that number, CloudCannon shows a *Confirm Billing Change* modal before the invite goes out.

  <comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-People-Confirm-Billing-Change-Modal" title="The Confirm Billing Change modal" alt="A screenshot of the Confirm Billing Change modal shows a message that adding a new Organization Member will increase your monthly bill once they accept the invitation, with Cancel and Accept Billing Increase buttons." type="screenshot" />
  
  Accepting that modal adds a seat to your *Subscription Plan*. Your bill increases once the new *Organization Member* accepts their invite, with the first month prorated. Turning on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/guest-access.yml">Guest Access</comp.GlossaryTerm> for a *Site* takes a seat in the same way, however many *Guests* use that *Site's* password. Anyone who can add *Organization Members* can accept this change, so it does not require separate billing permissions.
</comp.Notice>

If you need to re-send or cancel an invitation to a pending member, select the *Re-send invitation* or *Cancel invitation* options from the *Context Menu* at the top right of the member card.

You can paste a list of email addresses into the *Invite Organization Members* modal to quickly add multiple members to your *Organization*. When you enter multiple *Organization Members* and multiple *Permission Groups*, CloudCannon will add all members to all *Groups*.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  If your *Organization* is a *Client Organization* on an extended <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> through the CloudCannon Partner Program, CloudCannon shows a *Confirm End of Trial* modal before the invite goes out. Accepting it shortens that *Free Trial* to five days once the new *Organization Member* accepts their invitation, so you or your client need to add a payment method within those five days. For more information, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).
</comp.Notice>
