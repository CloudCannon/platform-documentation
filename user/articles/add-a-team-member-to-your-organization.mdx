<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `group:member:write` permission, can add *Team Members* to your *Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

To collaborate with someone in CloudCannon, invite them to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> as a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Member</comp.GlossaryTerm>. When you invite them, you assign them to one or more *Permission Groups*, which control what they can access.

To add a *Team Member* to your *Organization*:

1. Navigate to the *Team* page under *Org Settings*.
2. Under the *Members* tab, click the *Add members* button on the right. CloudCannon will open the *Invite team members to your Organization* modal.
3. Enter the email address of the person you want to add to your *Organization*.
4. Select the *Permission Group(s)* to which you want to add this *Team Member*. You must select at least one.
5. Click the *Add member(s)* button.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Team-Members-Tab" title="The Team page" alt="A screenshot of the Team page shows Team Members and the Add members button." type="screenshot" />

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Team-Invite-Modal" title="The Invite team members modal" alt="A screenshot of the Invite team members to your Organization modal shows fields for entering email addresses and selecting Permission Groups." type="screenshot" />

CloudCannon will send an email to your pending *Team Members*, inviting them to your *Organization*. If someone does not have a CloudCannon account, this email will prompt them to create one. While you wait for them to accept the invitation, their name will appear in the list of *Pending Members* under the *Members* tab of the *Team* page.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Adding a new *Team Member* may cause your *Organization* to exceed the number of *Team Members* included in your <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">Subscription Plan</a>. If adding a *Team Member* takes your *Organization* over that number, CloudCannon shows a *Confirm Billing Change* modal before the invite goes out.

  <comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Team-Confirm-Billing-Change-Modal" title="The Confirm Billing Change modal" alt="A screenshot of the Confirm Billing Change modal shows a message that adding a new Team Member will increase your monthly bill once they accept the invite, with Cancel and Accept Billing Increase buttons." type="screenshot" />
  
  Accepting that modal adds a seat to your *Subscription Plan*. Your bill increases once the new *Team Member* accepts their invite, with the first month prorated. *Client Sharing* invites count as a user in the same way. Anyone who can add *Team Members* can accept this change, so it does not require separate billing permissions.
</comp.Notice>

If you need to re-send or cancel an invitation to a pending member, select the *Re-send invitation* or *Cancel invitation* options from the *Context Menu* at the top right of the member card.

You can paste a list of email addresses into the *Invite team members to your Organization* modal to quickly add multiple members to your *Organization*. When you enter multiple *Team Members* and multiple *Permission Groups*, CloudCannon will add all members to all *Groups*.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  If your *Organization* is a *Client Organization* on an extended free trial through the CloudCannon [Partner Program](/documentation/developer-articles/cloudcannon-partner-program/), adding a *Team Member* shortens that trial to three days once they accept.
</comp.Notice>
