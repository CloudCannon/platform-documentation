<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can manage payment methods.</p>
</comp.Notice>

CloudCannon accepts credit card payments on all paid plans. You can add cards, switch which card is charged, and remove cards from the *Subscription* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

If a card is declined, CloudCannon retries the payment several times over the following weeks. For what happens to your *Sites* during that time, read the documentation on [how CloudCannon handles failed payments](/documentation/user-articles/how-cloudcannon-handles-failed-payments/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Enterprise *Organizations* may pay by invoice instead of credit card. If your *Organization* is on an Enterprise Plan, the CloudCannon team manages your billing. Contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> for Enterprise payment questions.
</comp.Notice>

## Add your first credit card

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Subscription-Add-Credit-Card-Modal" title="The Add your Credit Card modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add your Credit Card modal in CloudCannon, showing fields for the name on the card, additional information, and address." type="screenshot" />

To add your first credit card:

1. Click *Org Settings* in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Subscription* page.
2. Under *Billing Details*, click *Add Credit Card* to open the *Add your Credit Card* modal.
3. Enter your card details and verify they are correct.
4. If your bank requires it, enter any *Additional info* needed to process payments.
5. Click *Add Credit Card*.

CloudCannon verifies your card and uses it for your *Organization*'s payments.

## Add another credit card

To add another credit card:

1. Click *Org Settings* in the *App Sidebar*, then open the *Subscription* page.
2. Under *Billing Details*, click *Change Credit Card* to open the *Manage Credit Cards* modal.
3. Click *+ Add Credit Card* to open the *Add your Credit Card* modal.
4. Enter your card details and verify they are correct.
5. If your bank requires it, enter any *Additional info* needed to process payments.
6. Click *Add Credit Card*.

## Switch which card CloudCannon charges

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Manage-Credit-Cards-Modal-Element" title="The Manage Credit Cards modal" alt="A screenshot of the Manage Credit Cards modal in CloudCannon, showing a saved card with Use Card and Remove options and an Add Credit Card button." type="screenshot" />

To change which card CloudCannon charges:

1. Click *Org Settings* in the *App Sidebar*, then open the *Subscription* page.
2. Under *Billing Details*, click *Change Credit Card* to open the *Manage Credit Cards* modal.
3. Click *Use Card* next to the card you want CloudCannon to charge.

CloudCannon charges this card for your *Organization*'s payments going forward.
