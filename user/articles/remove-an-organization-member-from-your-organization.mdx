<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `group:member:write` permission, can remove *Organization Members* from your *Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

When someone no longer needs access to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, you can remove them as a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization-member.yml">Organization Member</comp.GlossaryTerm>. This revokes their access and removes them from every *Permission Group* they belong to.

To leave an *Organization* yourself rather than remove someone else, please read our documentation on [joining or leaving an Organization](/documentation/user-articles/join-or-leave-an-organization/).

To remove an *Organization Member* from your *Organization*:

1. Navigate to the *People* page under *Org Settings*.
2. Under the *Members* tab, click the *Organization Member* you want to remove from this *Organization*.
3. Click the *Remove from Organization* button in the top right of the *Organization Member* page.
4. Click the *Remove from Organization* button in the confirmation modal to confirm.

CloudCannon will remove the *Organization Member* from your *Organization*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-People-Remove-Member-Modal" title="The Remove from Organization confirmation modal" alt="A screenshot of the Remove from Organization confirmation modal shows a message that a current Organization Member must invite the removed Organization Member to rejoin, with Cancel and Remove from Organization buttons." type="screenshot" />

Alternatively, you can remove an *Organization Member* from the *People* page by clicking the *Context Menu* in the top right of a member card and selecting *Remove from Organization*.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> includes a set number of *Organization Members*, but you can pay for extra seats if you need to invite more. Removing an *Organization Member* will only lower your bill if they used an additional seat. Unlike adding an *Organization Member*, removing one does not show a billing confirmation modal.
</comp.Notice>

You can remove multiple people from your *Organization* at once by deleting a *Permission Group*. This only works if:

* You have *Custom Permission Groups*, AND
* All the *Organization Members* you want to remove from your *Organization* are only members of a single *Permission Group*.

For more information, please read our documentation on [what Custom Permission Groups are](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/).

To check that a removal worked, you can export an *Access Review* and look for the person's email address. For more information, please read our documentation on [exporting your Access Review](/documentation/user-articles/export-your-access-review/).
