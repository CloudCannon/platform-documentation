<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can update billing contact information.</p>
</comp.Notice>

You can add billing contact information to your invoices and set an email address for CloudCannon to send your invoices to. By default, your invoices have no billing contact information.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  CloudCannon does not post physical invoices, even if you add an address to your billing contact information.
</comp.Notice>

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Billing-Contact-Information-Modal" title="The Billing contact information modal" alt="A screenshot of the Billing contact information modal in CloudCannon, showing fields for billing name, email, and address." type="screenshot" />

To add billing contact information:

1. Navigate to the *Subscription* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Under *Billing Details*, click the *Update contact info* button. CloudCannon will open the *Billing contact information* modal.
3. Enter the contact details you want on your invoices. If you add an email address, CloudCannon emails your invoices to it automatically.
4. Click the *Update contact information* button.

CloudCannon will update your billing contact information.

The *Billing Details* section of the *Subscription* page will shows the email address your invoices are sent to and when your next invoice is due.
