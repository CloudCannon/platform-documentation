<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Only members of the *Owners* *Permission Group* can add or remove other *Owners*.
</comp.Notice>

CloudCannon doesn't have a single "transfer ownership" button. Instead, you transfer ownership by adding the new person to the *Owners* *Permission Group*, and then removing yourself once they're in place.

## Transfer ownership

To transfer ownership of your *Organization*:

1. Make sure the person is already a member of your *Organization*. If they aren't, [invite them](/documentation/user-articles/add-a-team-member-to-your-organization/) first.
2. [Add them to the Owners Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/add-a-team-member-to-a-permission-group/). They now have full *Owner* access.
3. To fully hand over the *Organization*, [remove yourself](/documentation/user-articles/join-or-leave-an-organization/) once the new *Owner* is in place.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  An *Organization* should always have at least one *Owner*. Don't remove yourself until the new *Owner* is in place. Leaving is hard to reverse: to regain access, the new *Owner* would need to invite you back. If you're locked out, read [Recover access to your Organization](/documentation/user-articles/recover-access-to-your-organization/).
</comp.Notice>

You don't have to remove yourself. If you want to share ownership, you can leave the new person in the *Owners* group alongside you, so the *Organization* has more than one *Owner*.
