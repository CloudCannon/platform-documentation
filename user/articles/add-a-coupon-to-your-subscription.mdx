<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can add a coupon to a subscription.</p>
</comp.Notice>

If you have a coupon, you can apply it to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> for a discount on your billing Plan. Once applied, the discount appears on your billing summary.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Each *Organization* can have one active coupon at a time. Applying a new coupon replaces any existing coupon on your *Subscription*.
</comp.Notice>

To add a coupon to your *Subscription*:

1. Click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Subscription* page.
2. Click *Review your billing plan* in the billing summary to open the *Your Subscription Plan* page.
3. Click *Add a coupon* in the billing summary to open the *Add a coupon* modal.
4. Enter your coupon code in the *Coupon Code* field.
5. Click *Apply Coupon*. If your *Organization* already has a coupon, this button reads *Replace Coupon* instead.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Subscription-Add-Coupon-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add a coupon modal with a Coupon Code input field and an Apply Coupon button." title="The Add a coupon modal" type="screenshot"/>

CloudCannon validates your coupon and applies the discount to your *Subscription*. The discount appears as a line item on your billing summary.

If your coupon code is not valid, CloudCannon shows an error message. Verify the code and try again. If the problem continues, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Enterprise *Organizations* have custom billing arrangements. If you are on an Enterprise Plan, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> for coupon questions.
</comp.Notice>
