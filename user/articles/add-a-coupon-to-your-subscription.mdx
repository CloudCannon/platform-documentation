<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can add a coupon to a subscription.</p>
</comp.Notice>

A coupon applies a discount to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm>, reducing what you pay for your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Each coupon sets its own discount amount and how long the discount lasts. Once you apply a coupon, the discount appears on your billing summary and on your invoices.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Each *Organization* can have one active coupon at a time. CloudCannon has no option to remove a coupon on its own. To change your coupon, apply a different one, which replaces the existing coupon on your *Subscription*.
</comp.Notice>

To add a coupon to your *Subscription*:

1. Navigate to the *Subscription* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the *Review your Subscription Plan* button in the billing summary. CloudCannon will open the *Your Subscription Plan* page.
3. Click the *Add a coupon* icon in the billing summary. CloudCannon will open the *Add a coupon* modal.
4. Enter your coupon code in the *Coupon Code* field.
5. Click the *Apply Coupon* button. If your *Organization* already has a coupon, this button reads *Replace Coupon* instead.

CloudCannon validates your coupon and applies the discount to your *Subscription*. The discount appears as a line item on your billing summary.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Subscription-Add-Coupon-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add a coupon modal with a Coupon Code input field and an Apply Coupon button." title="The Add a coupon modal" type="screenshot"/>

If your coupon code is not valid, CloudCannon shows an error message. Verify the code and try again. If the problem continues, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with your coupon code and the name of your *Organization*.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Enterprise *Organizations* have custom billing arrangements organized directly with the CloudCannon team. If your *Organization* is on the *Enterprise Plan*, contact our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>.
</comp.Notice>
