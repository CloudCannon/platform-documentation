CloudCannon runs your form submissions through a spam filter. Submissions flagged as spam are hidden from your *Sent* category.

To mark a spam submission as not spam:

1. Navigate to the *Spam* category tab in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inbox</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Select the *Mark as not spam* option in that submission's *Context Menu*.

CloudCannon will move the submission to *Sent*.

To mark a submission as spam:

1. Select the *Mark as spam* option in that submission's *Context Menu*.

CloudCannon will move the submission to *Spam*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Inbox-Spam" alt="A screenshot of the Inbox shows the Spam category with a context menu open on a submission, providing options to manage the submission." title="The Spam category with context menu" type="screenshot" />

Marking submissions as spam or not spam helps the spam filter improve over time.

## Stop spam reaching a target

A target forwards a copy of each submission to an email address or an external service, and each target decides for itself what happens to spam. By default spam reaches your targets along with everything else, so you can filter it where it arrives, and two *Spam protection* checkboxes hold it back instead. An *Email* target always holds spam back and cannot be changed.

A held-back submission still stays in your *Inbox*, so nothing is lost. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a target to an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-target-to-an-inbox/).
