<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:details:read` permission, can view the *Subdomain* page.
</comp.Notice>

The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain-page.yml">Subdomain page</comp.GlossaryTerm> shows one <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>, including the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> it serves or where it redirects visitors, and its hosting, HTTPS, and DNS. To open it, click on a *Subdomain* on the *Subdomains* tab of the [Domain page](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/), or select the *Open Subdomain page* option in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm>. The page opens inside the *Subdomains* tab, and the link at the top takes you back to the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomains-browser.yml">Subdomains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

At the top, the page shows the address of the *Subdomain*, an icon that opens the *Subdomain* in a new browser tab, and a *Current Fallback* label if the *Subdomain* is your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">Fallback Redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Site" alt="A screenshot of the Subdomain page for example.com shows the Subdomains link, the Current Fallback label, the Sites section with two Sites served at / and /docs/ and the Add a Site button, and the Hosting section with a diagram from Visitors to the CloudCannon Origin marked Reaching CloudCannon." title="The Subdomain page" type="screenshot"/>

All the information on a *Subdomain* page is repeated in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site-settings.yml">Site Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> of any *Site* it serves. You can find this under the *Custom Domain* page.

## Sites

For a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites*, the *Sites* section lists each *Site* with the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subpath.yml">Subpath</comp.GlossaryTerm> it's served at, its repository and branch, and its build and sync status. Click a *Site* to open its *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*. The *Context Menu* for each *Site* has the *Open Site*, *Swap for another Site*, *Change Subpath*, and *Remove from [Subdomain]* options. Click the *Add a Site* button to serve another *Site* on the *Subdomain* at a different *Subpath*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Sites-Element" alt="A screenshot of the Sites section on the Subdomain page for example.com shows Marketing site at / and Documentation at /docs/, each with its Git Repository and branch, Built and Synced statuses, and Context Menu, and the Add a Site button." title="The Sites section of the Subdomain page" type="ui-snippet"/>

If the *Subdomain* has no *Site* yet, the section says that visitors see a 404 page until you add one.

For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/).

## Redirect

For a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the *Redirect* section shows where CloudCannon sends visitors and the type of redirect, such as a 301 permanent redirect that keeps the path and query string. Click the *Change Target* button to choose where visitors go.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Redirect-Element" alt="A screenshot of the Redirect section on the Subdomain page shows www.example.com redirecting to example.com with a 301 permanent redirect that keeps the path and query string, and the Change Target button." title="The Redirect section on the Subdomain page" type="ui-snippet"/>

For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/).

## Hosting

The *Hosting* section shows how visitors reach the *Subdomain*, as a diagram from *Visitors* to the *CloudCannon Origin*, directly or through the *CloudCannon CDN* or *Your Cloudflare*, depending on its hosting option. Each connection shows whether it is working. Click a connection's status to see the details, with buttons such as *View check history*. Click the *Configure hosting* button to change the hosting option.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Hosting-Diagram-CDN-Element" alt="A screenshot of the Hosting section on the Subdomain page shows the diagram from Visitors through the CloudCannon CDN to the CloudCannon Origin, marked Reaching CloudCannon and Connected securely, with the details for the first connection open and the Configure hosting button." title="The Hosting section on the Subdomain page" type="ui-snippet"/>

For more information, please read our documentation on [what the hosting options for a Subdomain are](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-the-hosting-options-for-a-subdomain/) and [changing the hosting option for a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/#change-the-hosting-option-for-a-subdomain).

## HTTPS

The *HTTPS* section shows the status of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Subdomain*, such as *HTTPS is on*, and whether visitors who open its `http://` address go to `https://`. If the certificate has a problem, the section says what's wrong, with a *Retry now* or *See the last error* button where they apply. Click the *Configure HTTPS* button to choose how the *Subdomain* gets its certificate.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-HTTPS-Active-Element" alt="A screenshot of the HTTPS section on the Subdomain page for blog.example.com shows HTTPS is on, the next renewal date, a note that anyone who opens http://blog.example.com lands on https://blog.example.com, and the Configure HTTPS button." title="The HTTPS section of the Subdomain page" type="ui-snippet"/>

For more information, please read our documentation on [changing how a Subdomain gets its certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/#change-how-a-subdomain-gets-its-certificate) and [resolving SSL Certificate errors](/documentation/developer-articles/resolve-ssl-certificate-errors/).

## DNS

The *DNS* section shows whether the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-records.yml">DNS Record</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Subdomain* points at CloudCannon, with the record it needs and the records CloudCannon found. If the record needs a change, click the *Fix* button on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or the *I've updated it* button on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm> once you've changed it. Click the *Check now* button to check the record again.

For more information, please read our documentation on [checking the DNS for a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/#check-the-dns-for-a-subdomain).

## Danger zone

The *Danger zone* section has two buttons:

* **Change Subdomain Type** — Changes a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site-subdomain.yml">Site Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> to a *Redirect Subdomain*, or a *Redirect Subdomain* to a *Site Subdomain*. A *Subdomain* that changes to a redirect stops serving its *Sites*.
* **Delete Subdomain** — Stops serving the *Subdomain* and deletes its *SSL Certificate*, after you type the address of the *Subdomain* to confirm.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Danger-Zone-Element" alt="A screenshot of the Danger zone section on the Subdomain page for example.com shows the Change Subdomain type to Redirect row, noting that CloudCannon will stop serving 2 of your Sites there, with the Change Subdomain Type button, and the Delete Subdomain row with the Delete Subdomain button." title="The Danger zone section of the Subdomain page" type="ui-snippet"/>

For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/#delete-a-subdomain) and [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/).

## Why the Subdomain page might look different

### Permissions

The *Subdomain* page opens inside the *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page, so you can open it whenever you can see that tab. For which permissions show each tab, please read our documentation on [what the Domain page is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/#permissions).

Every control on the *Subdomain* page stays visible, but the ones you don't have permission for are disabled, and hovering over one says why:

* **Without `base-domain:settings:details:write`** — You can't add a *Site*, change the redirect, configure hosting or HTTPS, retry HTTPS, check DNS, change the type of the *Subdomain*, or delete it.
* **Without `base-domain:settings:dns:write`** — You can't use the *Fix* button.
* **Without `site:settings:hosting:write`** — You can't swap or remove a *Site*, or change its *Subpath*, unless you have this permission for that *Site*. Changing the type of the *Subdomain* to a redirect, or deleting it, needs this permission for every *Site* it serves.

On the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*, the *Subdomain settings* only appear if you have the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission and the `site:details:read` permission for that *Site*. Otherwise, the page says *You do not have permission to edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].* Without the `base-domain:details:read` permission, the *Subdomain settings* heading and this message don't appear at all.

Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/) hold all of these permissions. Members of the *Technical Editors*, *Editors*, and *Billing* groups hold none of the `base-domain` permissions or `site:settings:hosting:write`. If you need access, ask someone who manages <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the permission.

### Subscription and Seats

If your *Organization's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> is inactive, or your *Organization* doesn't have enough <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the *Domain* page shows *Custom Domains unavailable* in place of its tabs, so the *Subdomain* page doesn't open. The *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* shows a *Billing issue* page instead of the *Subdomain settings*, followed only by the *Danger zone* section with the *Remove custom domain* button, so you can still remove the *Custom Domain* from the *Site*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what the Domain page is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/#subscription-and-seats).
