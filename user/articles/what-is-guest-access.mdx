Guest Access allows you to invite someone from outside your [Organization](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-an-organization/) (called a Guest) to view, edit, and publish content for a specific Site. Guests do not need to have a CloudCannon account. Instead, they log into CloudCannon using a site-specific password.

Guests will log in using a [password you set for your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/enable-a-guest-password-for-a-site/) and a dedicated Guest Login Page. By default, the URL for the Login Page is the Site domain with the suffix "/update" (e.g., www.example.com/update). CloudCannon will use your [.cloudvent.net test domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/) if your Site does not have a custom domain. If you want to change the URL subpath, you can [configure the URL for the Login Page](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-the-guest-login-page/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  **Guest Access and Headless Mode**

  [Headless Mode](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-headless-mode/) disables building and hosting for your Site, so [Testing Domains](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/) are not available. CloudCannon will forward Guests trying to login to a headless Site using the .cloudvent.net link to the appropriate app.cloudcannon.com/update link.
</comp.Notice>

Once a Guest has logged in to your Site, they can access a simple version of the CloudCannon Site Dashboard. You can [configure the links in the *Site Navigation* of the Guest Dashboard](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-the-guest-dashboard-support-links/) to support your Guests.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Guest-Dashboard-Summary-Tab" alt="A screenshot of the Guest Dashboard shows a simplified Site Navigation and recent activity on the Summary tab." title="The Guest Dashboard" type="screenshot"/>

Each *Site* with *Guest Access* enabled uses one *Seat* in your *Organization*, no matter how many people use the *Site's* password. Each *Subscription Plan* includes a number of *Organization Members*, and you can extend that limit with paid *Add-Ons* at any time.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Although an unlimited number of people can use a Guest Access password to log in to a Site, we do not recommend giving a Guest Access password to multiple people. If multiple people share a password, you cannot determine which Guest was responsible for which changes.
</comp.Notice>

There is one [Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-permission-groups/) per Site for Guest Access, scoped to the Site you want to share.

<comp.Notice info_type="pricing">
  **Customizing the permissions in the *Guest Permission Group* is available on our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">Team or Enterprise Plan</a>.** *Guest Access* itself is available on all *Subscription Plans*.
</comp.Notice>

You can see all Sites with Guest Access enabled on the *Guests* tab of the *People* page under *Org Settings*. To manage the *Guest Access* settings for a specific *Site*, click its *Card* or navigate to the *Guest Access* page in your *Site Settings*.

The *Guests* tab is visible to members of the *Owners*, *Partner*, and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `org:settings:details:read` permission. The *Members* and *Groups* tabs are visible to everyone in your *Organization*, so some *Organization Members* see two tabs on this page and others see three.
