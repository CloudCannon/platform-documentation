<comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm> are extra capacity you can add to extend the limits included in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> Plan. Every Plan includes a set amount of team members, bandwidth, custom domains, and build time. If your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> needs more than your Plan provides, *Add-Ons* increase those limits without moving to a higher plan.

## Available Add-Ons

CloudCannon offers *Add-Ons* for each kind of plan resource:

* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm> — Additional team members for your *Organization*, including *Client Sharing* seats.
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm> — Additional bandwidth for your *Sites*, added in fixed-size packages.
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm> — Additional domains or subdomains for your *Sites*.
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm> — Additional build time for your *Sites*.

The cost and bundle size of each *Add-On* depend on your Plan. For current pricing, see the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">CloudCannon pricing page</a>.

*Add-On* charges appear as separate line items on your bill. For more information on reading your bill, read the documentation on [accessing and downloading your invoices](/documentation/user-articles/access-and-download-your-invoices/).

## How Overages work

<comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm> control what happens when your *Organization* goes over a Plan's included limits.

When *Overages* are enabled, CloudCannon automatically extends your account resources as you exceed your Plan's limits, and the extra usage appears on your bill.

When *Overages* are disabled, your *Organization* is held to the limits included in your Plan:

* **Bandwidth limit reached** — Your *Sites* are taken offline until the next billing period or until you enable *Overages*.
* **Build time limit reached** — You cannot save changes that trigger a build until the next billing period or until you enable *Overages*.

To turn *Overages* on or off, read the documentation on [managing your Add-Ons and Overages](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-add-ons-and-overages/).

## Track your Hosting Bandwidth and Build Time

CloudCannon shows usage graphs for *Hosting Bandwidth* and *Build Time* so you can monitor consumption against your plan limits. The graphs show cumulative usage over the current billing period alongside your Plan's included limit, so you can see when you are approaching an *Overage*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Dashboard-Usage-Tab" alt="A screenshot of the Usage tab on the Site Dashboard shows the Bandwidth and Build Time graphs and tables." title="The Site Dashboard Usage tab" type="screenshot"/>

You can find usage graphs in two places:

* The *Usage* tab on your *Organization Home* page shows *Hosting Bandwidth* and *Build Time* for every *Site* in your *Organization*. Use the graph for an overview of usage trends, or use the table to see which *Site* or URL is affecting your bill the most.
* The *Usage* tab on your *Site Dashboard* shows *Hosting Bandwidth* and *Build Time* for a single *Site*. The *Bandwidth* table shows which URLs or files affect your bill the most, and the *Build Time* graph shows that *Site*'s build time over time.

Checking these graphs regularly helps you anticipate when your *Organization* might exceed its plan limits and decide whether to add capacity, enable *Overages*, or change your Plan.
