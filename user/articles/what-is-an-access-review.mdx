An <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/access-review.yml">Access Review</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/csv.yml">CSV</comp.GlossaryTerm> file listing everyone with access to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, how they sign in to CloudCannon, and when they were last active. It lets you confirm who currently has access and what kind, and produce evidence for a security audit. Members of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/owners.yml">Owners Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or the *Partner Permission Group* in a *Client Organization*, can export one. For more information, please read our documentation on [exporting your Access Review](/documentation/user-articles/export-your-access-review/).

## What the Access Review covers

An *Access Review* covers everyone with a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm> membership in your *Organization*, including <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm> who access your content through <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site-sharing.yml">Site Sharing</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and anyone you have invited who has not accepted yet. It does not cover people who reach a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> through <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-sharing.yml">Client Sharing</comp.GlossaryTerm>, who have no CloudCannon account to list, or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/api-key.yml">API Keys</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which belong to your *Organization* rather than to a person.

An *Access Review* contains one row for each *Permission Group* membership, not one row for each person. A *Team Member* who belongs to five *Permission Groups* appears on five rows, one per *Group*, with the same email address each time. Rows for pending *Team Members* are listed after current ones, and contain less information.

<comp.OptionsTable>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Email Address" type_markdown="`string`">
    The email address on the *Team Member's* CloudCannon account or, for a pending member, the address you sent the invitation to.
  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="User Name" type_markdown="`string`">
    The name on the *Team Member's* CloudCannon account. A name is optional, so this is empty for pending members, who have no account yet, and for anyone who has not set one.
  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Access Type" type_markdown="`string`">
    `Organization` for someone with access to your whole *Organization*, or `Site` for someone with access to a single *Site* through *Site Sharing*.
  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Site Sharing Site ID" type_markdown="`string`">
    The numeric ID of the shared *Site*, the same ID that appears in that *Site's* CloudCannon URL. Empty on every row where Access Type is `Organization`.
  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Site Sharing Site Name" type_markdown="`string`">
    The name of the shared *Site*. Empty on every row where Access Type is `Organization`.
  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Permission Group Name" type_markdown="`string`">
    The *Permission Group* whose membership this row records. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Permission Groups are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-permission-groups/).
  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Date Invited" type_markdown="`string`">
    When you sent the invitation. Empty on every current member row, where the equivalent date is in the Date Accepted column.
  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Date Accepted" type_markdown="`string`">
    When the person accepted their invitation and joined this *Permission Group*. Empty on every pending member row, where the invitation date is in the Date Invited column.
  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="SSO Login" type_markdown="`boolean`">
    `true` when the *Team Member* signs in to CloudCannon with <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/sso.yml">SSO</comp.GlossaryTerm> rather than a password. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding SSO/SAML authentication](/documentation/developer-articles/add-sso-saml-authentication/).
  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="MFA Enabled" type_markdown="`boolean`">
    `true` when the *Team Member* has turned on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/m/multi-factor-authentication.yml">multi-factor authentication</comp.GlossaryTerm> for their account. For more information, please read our documentation on [enforcing multi-factor authentication](/documentation/developer-articles/enforce-multi-factor-authentication/).
  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Last Active" type_markdown="`string`">
    The most recent date CloudCannon recorded activity for the *Team Member* in this *Organization*. Empty when there is no recorded activity, and on every pending member row.
  </comp.OptionsRow>
</comp.OptionsTable>

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Read the SSO Login and MFA Enabled columns on current member rows only. Both columns describe a CloudCannon account, and a pending member has not created one yet, so both columns read `false` on every pending member row, whichever way that person signs in once they join.
</comp.Notice>

The SSO Login and MFA Enabled columns describe the account as it stands when you run the export, not any particular sign-in. A `true` value in MFA Enabled means the *Team Member* has multi-factor authentication turned on now, not that they used it the last time they signed in. To see the method and date of individual sign-ins, please read our documentation on [what Login Sessions are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-login-sessions/).

## If the Last Active column is empty

An empty Last Active cell means CloudCannon has no recorded activity for that *Team Member* in this *Organization*. Pending members have no activity to record, so their cells are always empty.

If Last Active is empty on every row, including rows for *Team Members* you know are active, treat the column as unavailable rather than as evidence that nobody has used your *Organization*. <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">Contact our support team</a> with your *Organization* name and the date and time you ran the export.
