Occasionally a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> you own ends up connected to a CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> you no longer control, such as after a team dispute or a lapsed agency relationship. If you can't reach the *Organization* currently using the domain, CloudCannon support can help you recover it once you prove you own the domain.

A *Site* stores two things that matter here:

* **Files** cannot be transferred between *Organizations*, but you should still have your own copy in your *Git Repository* or storage provider.
* **The Custom Domain** can be transferred once you prove you own it.

## When to contact support

Contact our support team if a *Custom Domain* you own is connected to an *Organization* you can't reach or no longer control. Have ready:

* the domain name
* proof that you control the domain, typically by adding a unique TXT DNS record to the root domain at your DNS provider

## Recreate your Site

Once the domain is transferred, set up your *Site* again in your own *Organization* by [creating the Site](/documentation/developer-guides/getting-started-with-cloudcannon/) from your files and [connecting the domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/). If the previous *Organization* was paying for the same *Sites*, ask support about the cost.
