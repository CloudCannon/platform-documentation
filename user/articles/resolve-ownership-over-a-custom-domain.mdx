Occasionally a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> you own ends up connected to a CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> you no longer control, such as after a team dispute or a lapsed agency relationship. If you can still reach the other *Organization*, the quickest fix is for them to transfer the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> to you, which moves the domain and its SSL certificate at the same time. For more information, please read our documentation on [transferring a Site between Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/transfer-a-site-between-organizations/).

If you can't reach the *Organization* currently using the domain, CloudCannon support can help you recover the domain once you prove you own it.

A *Site* stores two things that matter here:

* Files aren't part of a domain recovery, but you should still have your own copy in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm> or storage provider.
* The *Custom Domain* can be transferred once you prove you own it.

## When to contact support

Contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> if a *Custom Domain* you own is connected to an *Organization* you can't reach or no longer control. Have ready:

* the domain name
* access to your domain registrar, and to your DNS provider if you manage your domain's DNS yourself

Support will tell you how to prove you control the domain. The method depends on how your domain's DNS is set up: if CloudCannon manages your domain's DNS, you prove control through your registrar rather than through CloudCannon.

## Recreate your Site

Once the domain is transferred, recreate your *Site* in your own *Organization* from your files, then connect the domain. This is a developer task, so you may need a developer to help. For more information, please read our documentation on [getting started with CloudCannon](/documentation/developer-guides/getting-started-with-cloudcannon/) and [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/). If the previous *Organization* was paying for the same *Sites*, ask support about the cost.
