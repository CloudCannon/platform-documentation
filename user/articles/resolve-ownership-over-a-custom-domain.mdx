Occasionally a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> you own ends up connected to a CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> you no longer control, such as after a team dispute or a lapsed agency relationship. When you try to connect a domain like this, CloudCannon shows an error that the domain *is already connected to another CloudCannon Organization*.

If you can still reach the other *Organization*, you have two options:

* **Transfer the Site** — They transfer a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> on the domain to you. This moves the *Custom Domain*, every *Site* on it, and their SSL certificates to your *Organization* at the same time. If the transfer would increase your bill, CloudCannon asks you to accept the billing increase before you can accept the transfer. For more information, please read our documentation on [transferring a Site between Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/transfer-a-site-between-organizations/).
* **Disconnect the domain** — They remove the *Custom Domain* from their *Sites* and disconnect it from their *Organization*, so you can connect it to yours. For more information, please read our documentation on [disconnecting a Custom Domain from your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/disconnect-a-custom-domain-from-your-organization/).

If you can't reach the *Organization* currently using the domain, CloudCannon's support team can help you recover the domain once you prove you own it.

If you use a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm> or storage provider, your website files are safely stored there. Losing access to a *Custom Domain* will not affect that. For this reason, we highly recommend connecting a *Git Provider* when you create your *Site*.

## When to contact support

Contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> if a *Custom Domain* you own is connected to an *Organization* you can't reach or no longer control. Have ready:

* the domain name
* the name of your CloudCannon *Organization*, and the name of the *Organization* the domain is connected to, if you know it
* access to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and to your DNS provider if you use <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>

## Recreate your Site

Once CloudCannon's support team transfers the domain to an *Organization* you control, a developer can recreate your *Site* from your files and re-host it on the domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [getting started with CloudCannon](/documentation/developer-guides/getting-started-with-cloudcannon/) and [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).
