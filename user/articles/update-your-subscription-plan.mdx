<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can change the Subscription Plan of an *Organization*.</p>
</comp.Notice>

You can upgrade or downgrade your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> at any time. For what each Plan includes, see the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">CloudCannon pricing page</a>.

When you change Plans partway through a billing period, CloudCannon prorates the difference, and any adjustment appears on your next invoice.

To upgrade or downgrade your *Subscription*:

1. Click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Subscription* page.
2. Click *Review your billing plan* in the billing summary to open the plan options.
3. On the *Update your plan* page, choose *Monthly* or *Yearly* with the billing period toggle.
4. Select the Plan you want.
5. On the *Your Subscription Plan* page, review the *Plan resources* and *Estimated monthly bill* or *Estimated annual bill*.
6. Click *Complete checkout*.

Your new plan applies immediately.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Update-Your-Plan-Monthly-Comparison" alt="A screenshot of the Update your plan page shows the available CloudCannon Plans and their resources." title="The Update your plan page" type="screenshot"/>

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Downgrading reduces the features and resource limits available to your *Organization*. If your current usage is above the new plan's limits (<comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm>), the billing summary flags each one with a link to resolve it, such as *Manage team*, *Manage domains*, or *Enable add-ons*. Resolve the flagged resources before you complete checkout.
</comp.Notice>

## Legacy Plans

If your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> is on a legacy plan that is no longer available for new subscriptions, you can keep using it. If you change to a different plan, you cannot return to the legacy plan.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Changing away from a legacy plan is a one-way action. Make sure the new plan meets your needs before you confirm.
</comp.Notice>

## Enterprise Plans

Enterprise *Organizations* have custom billing arrangements managed by the CloudCannon team. Self-serve plan changes are not available on Enterprise Plans. To change your Enterprise Plan, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>.
