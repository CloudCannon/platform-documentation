<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can change the *Subscription Plan* of an *Organization*.</p>
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> defines a set of features available to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the monthly usage limits for resources, and the price you pay for access to those features and resources, monthly or annually. You can upgrade or downgrade your *Subscription Plan* at any time. For what each *Subscription Plan* includes, please see the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">CloudCannon pricing page</a>.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  When you change *Subscription Plans* partway through a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/billing-period.yml">Billing Period</comp.GlossaryTerm>, CloudCannon prorates the difference, and any adjustment appears on your next invoice.
</comp.Notice>

To upgrade or downgrade your *Subscription Plan*:

1. Navigate to the *Subscription* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click *Review your Subscription Plan* in the billing summary. CloudCannon will open the *Your Subscription Plan* page.
3. Click *Change your Subscription Plan*. CloudCannon will open the *Update your Subscription Plan* page.
4. Use the *Billing Period* toggle at the top of the page to choose *Monthly* or *Yearly* billing.
5. Click the *Select* button under the *Subscription Plan* you want. CloudCannon will open the *Your Subscription Plan* page.
6. Review the *Plan resources* and *Estimated bill*.
7. Click *Complete checkout*.

CloudCannon will apply the updated *Subscription Plan* to your *Organization* immediately.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Downgrading your *Subscription Plan* will reduce the number of features and the resource limits available to your *Organization*. If your current resource usage puts you above the new *Subscription Plan's* allocation of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm>, CloudCannon will warn you before you can update your *Subscription Plan*. You must reduce your usage, or enable [Add-Ons and Overages](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/) before you can complete checkout.
</comp.Notice>

## Legacy Subscription Plans

CloudCannon supports several legacy *Subscription Plans* that are no longer available to new customers, enabling backwards compatibility for older *Organizations*. If your *Organization* uses one of these legacy *Subscription Plans*, you will not be able to return to it if you update your *Subscription Plan*. Please make sure your new *Subscription Plan* meets your needs before you update.

## Enterprise Plans

If your *Organization* is on an *Enterprise Plan*, your billing is arranged directly with your CloudCannon account manager. Self-serve *Subscription Plan* changes and payment method management aren't available. For questions about Enterprise billing, please contact our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with your *Organization* name and the change you would like to make.
