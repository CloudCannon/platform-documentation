<comp.Notice info_type="pricing">
  **Custom branding is available on our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">Standard, Team, and Enterprise Plans</a>.**
</comp.Notice>

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `org:settings:details:write` permission, can brand an *Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

You can customize your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>'s branding in CloudCannon by setting a brand color and adding a badge and logo. You manage these from the *Branding* page of *Org Settings*, which you open from the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. To rename your *Organization*, please read our documentation on [renaming your Organization](/documentation/user-articles/rename-your-organization/).

## Set your brand color

Your brand color sets the accent color used across CloudCannon. To set your brand color:

1. Navigate to the *Branding* page under *Org Settings*.
2. Choose a color in the *Brand Color* field.
3. Click *Update branding* to confirm.

CloudCannon will save and apply your new brand color.

## Add your brand assets

You can add two brand assets on the *Branding* page. Each must be a PNG, JPEG, or SVG:

* *Organization Badge* — appears when CloudCannon displays your *Organization*, such as in the *Organizations Browser*.
* *Sites Browser Logo* — adds custom branding to your *Sites Browser*.

To add a brand asset:

1. Navigate to the *Branding* page under *Org Settings*.
2. Click *Change Badge* or *Change Logo* and upload your image.
3. Click *Update branding* to confirm.

CloudCannon will show your *Organization Badge* in the *Organizations Browser* and your *Sites Browser Logo* in the *Sites Browser*.

## Brand a Client Organization

If you manage <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm> through the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/developer-articles/cloudcannon-partner-program/), you can brand each one with your client's own color and logo, so their editing experience feels like theirs rather than yours. Apply the same steps above from within the *Client Organization*. As with your own *Organization*, this requires that *Client Organization* to be on a plan that includes custom branding. To rename a *Client Organization*, please read our documentation on [renaming your Organization](/documentation/user-articles/rename-your-organization/) and follow the steps from within the *Client Organization*.
