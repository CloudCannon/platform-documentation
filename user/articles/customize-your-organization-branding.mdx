<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  To rename or brand an *Organization*, your [Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-permission-groups/) needs the `org:settings:details:write` permission.
</comp.Notice>

You can brand your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> in CloudCannon by renaming it, setting a brand color, and adding a badge and logo. You manage your *Organization*'s name from the *Details* tab, and its brand color, badge, and logo from the *Branding* tab, both under *Org Settings* in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

## Rename your Organization

Your *Organization*'s name appears across CloudCannon, including in the *Switch Organization* menu in the *App Sidebar*. To rename your *Organization*:

1. Navigate to the *Details* tab in *Org Settings*.
2. Update the name in the *Organization Name* field.
3. Click *Update Organization* to confirm.

CloudCannon will save the new name and update it everywhere your *Organization* appears.

## Set your brand color

Your brand color defines the color of the *App Sidebar* and many other UI elements in CloudCannon. To set your brand color:

1. Navigate to the *Branding* tab in *Org Settings*.
2. Choose a color in the *Brand Color* field.
3. Click *Update branding* to confirm.

CloudCannon will save and apply your new brand color.

## Add your brand assets

You can add two brand assets on the *Branding* tab. Each must be a PNG, JPEG, or SVG:

* *Organization Badge* — appears when CloudCannon displays your *Organization* in a list, such as the *Organizations* list.
* *Sites List Logo* — adds custom branding to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> list.

To add a brand asset:

1. Navigate to the *Branding* tab in *Org Settings*.
2. Click *Change Badge* or *Change Logo* and upload your image.
3. Click *Update branding* to confirm.

CloudCannon will show your *Organization Badge* and *Sites List Logo* in the *Organizations* and *Sites* lists.

## Brand a Client Organization

If you manage *Client Organizations* through the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/developer-articles/cloudcannon-partner-program/), you can brand each one with your client's own name, color, and logo, so their editing experience feels like theirs rather than yours. Apply the same steps above from within the *Client Organization*.
