<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:read</code> permission, can view invoices.</p>
</comp.Notice>

CloudCannon keeps a record of your invoices in the app. Each invoice lists your base plan charge, any *Add-Ons* (such as extra team members, bandwidth, custom domains, or build time), credits from plan changes, and any discounts applied to your *Organization*.

You can also [add billing contact information](/documentation/user-articles/add-billing-contact-information/) to your invoices and have CloudCannon email them to an address automatically.

To access and download your invoices:

1. Click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Invoices* page. CloudCannon lists your invoices with the most recent first.
2. Click *View more* for the invoice you want. CloudCannon opens that invoice on a *Stripe* page in a new tab.
3. On the *Stripe* page, download the invoice or the receipt as a PDF. The invoice shows the due date; the receipt shows the date the payment was made.

  Enterprise *Organizations* use custom billing, and the CloudCannon team sends invoices to you directly. If your *Organization* is on an Enterprise Plan, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> for invoice questions.
