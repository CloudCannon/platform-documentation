<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can cancel a Subscription.</p>
</comp.Notice>

You can cancel your paid <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> at any time. Canceling ends your paid *Subscription* and stops further charges. It does not delete your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or files, but you lose access to your paid plan's features. After you cancel, your bill drops to zero and you receive no further invoices for the paid plan.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  When you cancel, you can no longer edit your *Sites*, your hosted domains (including <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/testing-domain.yml">Testing Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm>) become unavailable, and any saved credit cards are removed from your *Organization*. Your *Sites* and files are not deleted.
</comp.Notice>

To cancel your *Subscription*:

1. Click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Subscription* page.
2. Expand the *Cancel subscription* section.
3. Click *Cancel subscription*.
4. In the *Cancel subscription* modal, review what you lose, then click *Cancel subscription* to confirm.

Once you confirm, CloudCannon cancels your *Subscription*, your estimated bill becomes zero, and you see the message *Your CloudCannon subscription has been canceled.*

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Enterprise *Organizations* have custom billing arrangements. If you are on an Enterprise Plan, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> to cancel.
</comp.Notice>

## Resubscribe

Canceling does not lock you out. To start paying again, return to the *Subscription* page and select a Plan. For more information, read the documentation on [updating your Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-subscription-plan/).

## Canceling is not the same as deleting

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Canceling your *Subscription* keeps your *Organization*, *Sites*, and files. Deleting your *Organization* removes it along with its *Sites* and is not reversible.
</comp.Notice>

If your goal is to stop paying but keep your content, cancel your *Subscription* using the steps above. If you want to remove the *Organization* entirely, read the documentation on deleting an Organization.

Canceling is also different from having your *Subscription* put on hold. CloudCannon puts your *Subscription* on hold automatically when it cannot collect payment after several attempts, rather than when you choose to cancel. If your *Subscription* stays on hold for a prolonged period, CloudCannon cancels it automatically. For more information, read the documentation on [how CloudCannon handles failed payments](/documentation/user-articles/how-cloudcannon-handles-failed-payments/).

For any issues with billing, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>.
