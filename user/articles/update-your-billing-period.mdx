<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can change the billing period.</p>
</comp.Notice>

Paid <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> Plans are billed monthly or yearly:

* **Monthly** — Your card is charged at the start of each month for the month ahead. Monthly billing suits you if you want to change Plans often.
* **Yearly** — Your card is charged once a year for the year ahead. The yearly price works out cheaper per month than paying monthly.

You can switch between billing periods at any time. When you change your billing period partway through a billing period, CloudCannon prorates the difference, and any adjustment appears on your next invoice.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Update-Your-Plan-Monthly-Comparison" alt="A screenshot of the Update your plan page shows the billing period toggle and the available Plans." title="The Update your plan page" type="screenshot"/>

To update your billing period:

1. Click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Subscription* page.
2. Click *View plans* in the billing summary to open the *Update your plan* page.
3. On the *Update your plan* page, choose *Monthly* or *Yearly* with the billing period toggle.
4. On your current plan, click *Switch to annual pricing* or *Switch to monthly pricing*.
5. On the *Your Subscription Plan* page, review the *Plan resources* and *Estimated monthly bill* or *Estimated annual bill*.
6. Click *Complete checkout*.

Your billing period changes immediately, and CloudCannon prorates the difference on your next invoice.
