<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can change the <em>Billing Period</em> of an <em>Organization</em>.</p>
</comp.Notice>

Your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/billing-period.yml">Billing Period</comp.GlossaryTerm> defines how often CloudCannon bills your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> for its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Changing your *Billing Period* affects when and how much you are charged, but not the features included in your *Subscription Plan*.

Paid *Subscription Plans* are billed monthly or yearly:

* **Monthly** — Your card is charged at the start of each month for the month ahead.
* **Yearly** — Your card is charged once a year for the year ahead. The yearly price works out cheaper per month than paying monthly.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Even if you select *Yearly* as your *Billing Period*, CloudCannon will still send invoices monthly for *Add-Ons* and *Overages*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).
</comp.Notice>

You can switch between *Billing Periods* at any time. When you change your *Billing Period* partway through a *Billing Period*, CloudCannon prorates the difference, and any adjustment appears on your next invoice.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Update-Your-Plan-Monthly-Comparison" alt="A screenshot of the Update your Subscription Plan page shows the Billing Period toggle and the available Plans." title="The Update your Subscription Plan page" type="screenshot"/>

To update your *Billing Period*:

1. Navigate to the *Subscription* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click *Review your Subscription Plan* in the billing summary. CloudCannon will open the *Your Subscription Plan* page.
3. Click *Change your Subscription Plan*. CloudCannon will open the *Update your Subscription Plan* page.
4. Use the *Billing Period* toggle at the top of the page to choose *Monthly* or *Yearly* billing.
5. On your current *Subscription Plan*, click *Switch to annual pricing* or *Switch to monthly pricing*. CloudCannon will open the *Your Subscription Plan* page.
6. Review the *Plan resources* and *Estimated bill*.
7. Click *Complete checkout*.

CloudCannon will update your *Billing Period* immediately.

## Enterprise Plans

If your *Organization* is on an *Enterprise Plan*, your billing is arranged directly with your CloudCannon account manager. Self-serve *Billing Period* changes aren't available. For questions about Enterprise billing, please contact our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with your *Organization* name and the change you would like to make.
