<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> or <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can cancel the <em>Subscription</em> of an <em>Organization</em>.</p>
</comp.Notice>

You can cancel your paid <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> at any time. Canceling ends your paid *Subscription* and stops further charges. It does not delete your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or files, but you lose access to the features included in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. After you cancel, your bill drops to zero and you receive no further invoices.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  When you cancel, you can no longer edit your *Sites*, your hosted domains (including <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/testing-domain.yml">Testing Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm>) become unavailable, and any saved credit cards are removed from your *Organization*. Your *Sites* and files are not deleted.
</comp.Notice>

To cancel your *Subscription*:

1. Navigate to the *Subscription* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Expand the *Cancel Subscription* section.
3. Click *Cancel Subscription*.
4. In the *Cancel Subscription* modal, review what you lose, then click *Cancel Subscription* to confirm.

Once you confirm, CloudCannon will cancel your *Subscription* and stop further charges.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Enterprise *Organizations* have custom billing arrangements. If you are on an *Enterprise Plan*, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> to cancel.
</comp.Notice>

## Resubscribe

You can start a new *Subscription* at any time. To get access to CloudCannon features again, return to the *Subscription* page and select a *Subscription Plan*. For more information, read the documentation on [updating your Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-subscription-plan/).

## Canceling is not the same as deleting

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Canceling your *Subscription* removes CloudCannon features but keeps your *Organization*, *Sites*, and files. Deleting your *Organization* removes all your data from CloudCannon's servers and is not reversible.
</comp.Notice>

If your goal is to stop paying but keep your content, cancel your *Subscription* using the steps above. If you want to remove the *Organization* entirely, read the documentation on [deleting an Organization](/documentation/user-articles/delete-an-organization/).

Canceling is also different from having your *Subscription* put on hold. CloudCannon puts your *Subscription* on hold automatically when it cannot collect payment after several attempts, rather than when you choose to cancel. If your *Subscription* stays on hold for a prolonged period, CloudCannon cancels it automatically. For more information, read the documentation on [how CloudCannon handles failed payments](/documentation/user-articles/how-cloudcannon-handles-failed-payments/).

If you are having any issue with billing, please contact our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>.
