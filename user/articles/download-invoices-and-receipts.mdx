<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> and <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:read</code> permission, can view invoices.</p>
</comp.Notice>

CloudCannon keeps a copy of each invoice on the *Invoices* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Each invoice shows the date your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> payment was due, and its receipt shows the date the payment was made. You can view or download them at any time.

To download an invoice or receipt:

1. Navigate to the *Invoices* page under *Org Settings*.
2. Click the *View more* button for the invoice you want. CloudCannon opens that invoice on a Stripe page in a new tab.
3. On the Stripe page, click the *Download invoice* or *Download receipt* button.

Your browser will download the invoice or the receipt as a PDF.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Invoices-Page" title="The Invoices page" alt="A screenshot of the Invoices page shows a list of invoices, each with a View more button." type="screenshot" />

<comp.DocsImage path="assets/external_screenshots/CloudCannon-Documentation-Stripe-Invoice-Page.png" alt="A screenshot of the Stripe invoice page showing the invoice total, invoice number, payment date, and buttons to download the invoice or receipt." title="The Stripe invoice page" type="screenshot"/>

A recently issued invoice may show a *Pending* button until it is finalized. The *View more* button appears once the invoice is ready.

For a breakdown of what each charge means, please read our documentation on [understanding your CloudCannon invoice](/documentation/user-articles/understand-your-cloudcannon-invoice/).

To have CloudCannon email your invoices to you automatically, please read our documentation on [adding billing contact information](/documentation/user-articles/add-billing-contact-information/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Enterprise *Organizations* use custom billing, and the CloudCannon team sends invoices to you directly. If your *Organization* is on the *Enterprise Plan*, contact us via <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support</a>, Slack, or email.
</comp.Notice>
