<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:details:read` permission, can view *Custom Domains* in the *Domains Browser*.
</comp.Notice>

The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm> is the page that lists the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm> connected to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. To open it, click the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

## Custom Domain Cards

The *Domains Browser* shows a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for each *Custom Domain*. Each *Card* shows:

* The name of the *Custom Domain*.
* Its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-provider.yml">DNS Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm>, *CloudCannon DNS* or *External DNS*.
* How many <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomains</comp.GlossaryTerm> are on the *Custom Domain*, and how many <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> it serves.
* For a *Custom Domain* on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, a status for its DNS setup.

Click a *Card* to open the [Domain page](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/) for that *Custom Domain*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domains-Browser-Card-External-DNS-Element" alt="A screenshot of a Custom Domain Card in the Domains Browser shows example.com with External DNS, 3 Subdomains, and 1 Site, and no DNS setup status." title="A Custom Domain Card on external DNS" type="ui-snippet"/>

### DNS setup statuses

A *Custom Domain* on *CloudCannon DNS* shows one of these statuses on its *Card*:

* **Creating DNS zone...** — CloudCannon is creating the DNS zone for your domain.
* **Adding initial DNS records...** — CloudCannon is adding the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-records.yml">DNS Records</comp.GlossaryTerm> that point your domain at CloudCannon.
* **Failed to create DNS zone** — CloudCannon couldn't create the DNS zone. Open the *Domain* page and click the *Retry zone creation* button on the *Summary* tab. The button needs the `base-domain:settings:dns:write` permission.
* **Waiting for nameservers...** — CloudCannon is waiting for the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/n/nameserver.yml">nameservers</comp.GlossaryTerm> at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm> to change to the CloudCannon nameservers.
* **Nameservers not detected** — CloudCannon still can't see the CloudCannon nameservers at your domain registrar 48 hours after you said you updated them.
* **CloudCannon DNS configured** — Your domain uses *CloudCannon DNS*.
* **Nameservers have changed** — The nameservers of your domain no longer point at *CloudCannon DNS*.
* **Migrate to new CloudCannon DNS** — Your domain uses the legacy *CloudCannon DNS*. For more information, please read our documentation on [migrating from the legacy CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/migrate-from-the-legacy-cloudcannon-dns/).

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domains-Browser-Card-Waiting-Element" alt="A screenshot of a Custom Domain Card in the Domains Browser shows example.com with the Waiting for nameservers... status under its name, CloudCannon DNS, 3 Subdomains, and 1 Site." title="A Custom Domain Card waiting for nameservers" type="ui-snippet"/>

A *Custom Domain* on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm> shows no status. For the steps to finish setting up each *DNS Provider*, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/) and [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).

## The Filter Bar

The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/filter-bar.yml">Filter Bar</comp.GlossaryTerm> above the *Cards* narrows the list. Type in the *Filter Bar* to search by name, or click the *Add Filter* button to filter by:

* **DNS Provider** — *CloudCannon DNS* or *External DNS*.
* **DNS Check** — Whether CloudCannon's latest nameserver check for the domain is *Failing* or *Passing*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domains-Browser-Filter-External-DNS" alt="A screenshot of the Domains Browser shows the Domain uses External DNS filter in the Filter Bar and a single Card for myownpersonaldomain.com on External DNS with 0 Subdomains and 0 Sites." title="The Domains Browser filtered to External DNS" type="screenshot"/>

If no *Custom Domain* matches, the *Domains Browser* shows *No Custom Domains match your search terms or filters.*

To show the *Custom Domains* as a list instead of *Cards*, click the *Change view* button and select the *List* option.

## Connect a Custom Domain

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Connecting a *Custom Domain* also requires the `base-domain:create` permission.
</comp.Notice>

Click the *Connect a Custom Domain* button to open the *Connect a Custom Domain* page. If you can't view any *Custom Domain* yet, such as when your *Organization* hasn't connected one, the *Custom Domains* link opens the *Connect a Custom Domain* page directly, instead of the *Domains Browser*. For more information, please read our documentation on [connecting a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/).

## Why the Domains Browser might look different

### Permissions

The *Domains Browser* only lists the *Custom Domains* you have the `base-domain:details:read` permission for. Members of the *Technical Editors*, *Editors*, and *Billing* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/) don't hold it.

The *Custom Domains* link appears in the *App Sidebar* only if you can view at least one *Custom Domain*, or you have the `base-domain:create` permission. Without the `base-domain:create` permission, the *Connect a Custom Domain* button doesn't appear. If you can't view any *Custom Domain* and don't have the `base-domain:create` permission, opening the *Domains Browser* shows a *Permission denied* page that says *You do not have permission to connect new Custom Domains.*

If you need access to a *Custom Domain*, ask someone who manages <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> in your *Organization* for the permission.

### Subscription and Seats

If your *Organization's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> is inactive, or your *Organization* doesn't have enough <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the *Domains Browser* still lists your *Custom Domains*. Opening one shows *Custom Domains unavailable* in place of the tabs of the *Domain* page, and the *Connect a Custom Domain* button opens a *Billing issue* page. For more information, please read our documentation on [what the Domain page is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/#subscription-and-seats).
