CloudCannon offers a 21-day <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> of the Standard Plan when you create a new <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. This trial allows you to explore CloudCannon's features and determine if it is the right fit for you, without entering any payment details.

During the trial, your *Organization* has all Standard Plan features, including:

* [Team Members](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-team-member/) and [Client Sharing](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-client-sharing/)
* [Custom Domains](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-custom-domain/)
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* All Standard Plan editing and collaboration features

If you need more features, you can upgrade to the Team Plan *Free Trial* at any time. Upgrading does not extend your trial period.

You can track how many days remain using the trial countdown in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

## Start your trial

The trial is available once per *Organization*.

When you [create a CloudCannon account](/documentation/user-articles/create-a-cloudcannon-account/) for the first time, CloudCannon automatically creates your first *Organization* on a Standard Plan *Free Trial*.

If you already have an account and create another Organization, the new *Organization* starts on the Personal Plan. When you use a Standard Plan feature in that *Organization*, CloudCannon shows the *Update your plan* window with a Standard Plan trial offer. Click *Start my Free Trial* in that offer to begin your trial.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Plans-Standard-Trial-Offer-Element" alt="A screenshot shows the Try our Standard plan for 21 days offer with a Start my Free Trial button." title="The Standard Plan trial offer" type="ui-snippet"/>

## What happens when the trial ends

If you add your payment details before the *Free Trial* ends, CloudCannon starts your paid *Subscription* when your trial period is over. You keep all your plan features, and your card is charged monthly or yearly, depending on the billing period you chose. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your payment methods](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-payment-methods/). For a step-by-step walkthrough of starting a trial and setting up billing, please read our guide on [managing your CloudCannon subscription](/documentation/user-guides/manage-your-cloudcannon-subscription/).

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Subscription-Trial-Reminder-Element" alt="A screenshot of the Subscription page shows a banner with the number of days remaining on the trial and a prompt to add a credit card." title="The trial reminder banner" type="ui-snippet"/>

If you do not add a payment method before the trial ends, CloudCannon pauses your *Subscription*. Your *Organization*, *Sites*, and files remain intact. To resume, add a payment method to continue on a paid plan, or [cancel your Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/cancel-your-subscription-plan/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  You can always access your code and content, even when your *Subscription* is *Paused* or *Canceled*.
</comp.Notice>

## Extending your trial

If you need more time to evaluate CloudCannon, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> to ask about a trial extension. If you manage *Client Organizations* through the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">Partner Program</a>, different trial terms may apply.
