To activate a paid <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, add a credit card to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon accepts credit card payments on the *Standard Plan* and *Team Plan*; the *Enterprise Plan* uses custom billing arranged with our team.

## Add your card

Navigate to the *Subscription* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Under *Billing Details*, click *Add Credit Card* to open the *Add your Credit Card* modal.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Add-Credit-Card-Modal-Element" title="The Add your Credit Card modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add your Credit Card modal in CloudCannon, showing fields for the name on the card, additional information, and address." type="screenshot" />

Enter your card details and verify they are correct. If your bank requires it, add any *Additional info* needed to process payments, then click *Add Credit Card*.

CloudCannon verifies your card and uses it for your *Organization*'s payments.

## Complete checkout

Back on the *Your Subscription Plan* page, review the *Plan resources* and *Estimated bill*, then click *Complete checkout* to start your paid subscription.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Your-Subscription-Plan-Monthly-Team-Checkout" title="The Your Subscription Plan checkout" alt="A screenshot of the Your Subscription Plan page in CloudCannon, showing the Plan resources, Estimated bill, and the Complete checkout button." type="screenshot" />

For more detail, read the documentation on [managing your payment methods](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-payment-methods/). If a payment ever fails, read the documentation on [what happens when a payment fails](/documentation/user-articles/what-happens-when-a-payment-fails/).
