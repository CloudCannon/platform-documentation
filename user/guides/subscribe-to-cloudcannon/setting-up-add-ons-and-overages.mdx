If your team needs more than your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> includes, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm> extend your limits without moving to a higher *Subscription Plan*. You can add capacity for team members, bandwidth, custom domains, and build time.

## Decide how Overages work

<comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm> control what happens when you reach a *Subscription Plan* limit:

* **Overages on** — CloudCannon automatically extends your resources as you exceed your limits, and the extra usage appears on your bill.
* **Overages off** — your *Organization* is held to your *Subscription Plan's* limits. If you reach your bandwidth limit, your *Sites* go offline until the next billing period, until you turn *Overages* on, or until you upgrade to a higher *Subscription Plan*.

## Turn Overages on or off

Click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm> and open the *Subscription* page. In the billing summary, click *Review your Subscription Plan* to open the *Your Subscription Plan* page.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Your-Subscription-Plan-Add-Ons-Element" title="The Overages toggle" alt="A screenshot of the Overages setting on the Your Subscription Plan page in CloudCannon, with a toggle to turn Overages on or off." type="screenshot" />

Turn *Overages* on or off, then click *Enable Overages* or *Disable Overages* to save your change.

For more detail, including how to track your usage, read the documentation on [Add-Ons and Overages](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/) and [managing your Add-Ons and Overages](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-add-ons-and-overages/).
