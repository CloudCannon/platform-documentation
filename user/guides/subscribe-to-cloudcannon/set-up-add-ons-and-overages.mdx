If the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> you chose doesn't have the right amount of resources, you can extend it with <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm>. *Add-Ons* allow you to add extra <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm> or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm> to your *Organization*. *Overages* allow you to go beyond your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm> limits and pay for what you use at the end of each month.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  *Overages* are relevant if you build and host your website through CloudCannon, rather than through a third-party service.
</comp.Notice>

## Using Add-Ons

There is no button to purchase *Add-Ons*. Instead, CloudCannon automatically adds them to your *Subscription Plan* when you add a *Team Member* or connect a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*.

You won't be charged for *Add-Ons* until you go over the amount in your *Subscription Plan*. When adding either of these resources would put you over your *Subscription Plan's* limits, CloudCannon shows the *Confirm Billing Change* modal.

We'll cover how to add *Team Members* and *Custom Domains* in a future guide.

## How Overages work

You can control whether *Overages* are enabled or disabled for your *Organization*, which encompasses both *Build Time* and *Hosting Bandwidth*. By default, *Overages* are disabled for all *Organizations*.

When *Overages* are enabled, CloudCannon will automatically extend your resources as you exceed your limits. At the end of each month, CloudCannon will invoice you for the amount of *Overages* you used over your *Subscription Plan* limit in 1-hour or 100 GB bundles, rounded up.

When *Overages* are disabled, and you reach the resource limit in your *Subscription Plan*, CloudCannon will prevent your *Organization* from using more:

* When you reach your *Build Time* limit, CloudCannon stops running *Site* builds for the remainder of the month or until you enable *Overages*. You can still save your changes, but they will not build.
* When you reach your *Hosting Bandwidth* limit, CloudCannon will stop serving the *Sites* on your *Organization* to website visitors for the remainder of the month or until you enable *Overages*. Instead, visitors to your website will see a hosting error message.

<comp.DocsImage path="https://cc-dam.imgix.net/documentation/images/billing/2026-07/CloudCannon-Documentation-Website-Unavailable-Hosting-Bandwidth-Limit.png" alt="A screenshot of the unavailable page a visitor sees shows the heading This website is currently unavailable and a message that the website has reached its hosting bandwidth limit, asking them to contact the site's owner." title="The unavailable page visitors see when a Site reaches its Hosting Bandwidth limit" type="screenshot"/>

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  **How much Hosting Bandwidth do I need?**

  *Hosting Bandwidth* is the amount of data transferred from your website's servers at CloudCannon to your website visitor. The amount of data each webpage needs is measured in megabytes (MB), and mostly depends on how many image/media files are on the page and how optimized your website is. For example, a 4 MB webpage with several images or media files will serve 25,000 page views with 100 GB of *Hosting Bandwidth*. Most optimized webpages are smaller than this, so 100 GB will go further.
</comp.Notice>

## Turn Overages on or off

Even if you don't need *Overages*, we recommend enabling them to prevent your *Sites* from going down in the case that you do reach your limits.

Choosing your *Subscription Plan* took you to the *Your Subscription Plan* page, which is also where you set *Overages*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Your-Subscription-Plan-Monthly-Team-Checkout" title="The Overages setting on the Your Subscription Plan page" alt="A screenshot of the Your Subscription Plan page in CloudCannon shows the Overages setting near the top of the page, with a toggle to turn Overages on or off." type="screenshot" />

Turn *Overages* on or off. You don't need to save this now. CloudCannon applies your choice when you complete checkout in the next step. To change *Overages* after you have subscribed, use the *Enable Overages* or *Disable Overages* button that appears on this page.

## Track your usage

After you subscribe, you can keep track of how much *Hosting Bandwidth* and *Build Time* you use each month from the *Usage* tab on your *Organization Home* page or *Site Dashboard*. There's no need to check it during checkout; it's somewhere to return to over time. Watching these graphs helps you anticipate when your *Organization* or *Site* might reach its *Subscription Plan* limits, and decide whether to enable *Overages* or change your *Subscription Plan*.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Several CloudCannon *Subscription Plans* have unlimited *Build Time*. However, CloudCannon may monitor usage with regard to the fair use of CloudCannon infrastructure, in line with our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/terms/">Terms of Service</a>.
</comp.Notice>
