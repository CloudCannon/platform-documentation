Your first invoice arrives when your CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> starts billing. When that happens depends on whether you subscribed before or after your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> ended:

* **During your Free Trial** — CloudCannon doesn't charge you until the trial ends, so your first invoice is generated once your *Free Trial* is over. Until then, you keep full access but haven't been charged.
* **After your Free Trial has ended** — Your billing starts immediately, so CloudCannon generates your first invoice right away.

The *Subscription* page shows your current <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, your *Estimated bill*, and your renewal date and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/billing-period.yml">Billing Period</comp.GlossaryTerm>. This is the first place to look to confirm what you are paying and when.

## Your invoices

CloudCannon keeps a record of your invoices. To find them, click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Invoices* page.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Invoices-Page" title="The Invoices page" alt="A screenshot of the Invoices page in CloudCannon, showing a list of past invoices grouped by year, each with a date, amount, and View more button." type="screenshot" />

A recently issued invoice shows a *Pending* button until it is finalized, after which the *View more* button appears. Click *View more* on an invoice to open it on a Stripe page, where you can download the invoice or receipt as a PDF. The invoice records the date your payment was due, and its matching receipt records the date it was paid.

Your invoice total is the cost of your *Subscription Plan*, plus any <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm>, minus any credits and discounts, plus any applicable tax. CloudCannon breaks this into line items:

* Your *Subscription Plan* and *Add-Ons* are billed in advance for the coming *Billing Period*, and appear as "[name] Subscription Plan", "Team Member", or "Custom Domain".
* *Overages* are billed at the end of each month, even on a yearly *Billing Period*, and appear as "[amount] x 100 GB Hosting Bandwidth Bundle" or "[amount] x 1 Hour Build Time Bundle".
* Credits from downgrading or removing an *Add-On* partway through a *Billing Period*, and discounts from a coupon, appear as negative line items that lower your total.

## Billing contact details

You can add billing contact details to your invoices and have CloudCannon send them to an email address automatically. On the *Subscription* page, under *Billing Details*, click *Update contact info*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Billing-Contact-Information-Modal" title="The Billing contact information modal" alt="A screenshot of the Billing contact information modal in CloudCannon, showing fields for billing name, email, and address." type="screenshot" />

In the *Billing contact information* modal, enter the details you want on your invoices, then click the *Update contact information* button. If you added an email address, CloudCannon begins emailing your invoices to you automatically, and the *Billing Details* section then shows the address they are sent to and when your next invoice is due. CloudCannon only emails your invoices, so adding an address does not mean you will receive a physical copy.
