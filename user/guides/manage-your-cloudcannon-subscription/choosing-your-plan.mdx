Your first decision is which Plan fits your team. Each <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> Plan includes a set amount of team members, bandwidth, custom domains, and build time.

## Compare the plans

CloudCannon offers a Personal Plan for individuals and small projects, and paid Standard, Team, and Enterprise Plans for growing teams. Rather than duplicating pricing here, see the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">pricing page</a> for what each Plan includes and how much it costs.

If you are not ready to commit, you can try the Standard Plan free for a limited time. For more information, read the documentation on [CloudCannon's free trial](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-cloudcannons-free-trial/).

## Select a plan

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Update-Your-Plan-Monthly-Comparison" title="The Update your plan page" alt="A screenshot of the Update your plan page in CloudCannon, showing the Monthly and Yearly billing toggle and the Personal, Standard, and Team plan comparison." type="screenshot" />

To open the plan options:

1. Click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Subscription* page.
2. Click *View plans* in the billing summary to open the *Update your plan* page.
3. Choose *Monthly* or *Yearly* with the billing period toggle. Yearly billing is discounted compared to monthly.
4. Select the Plan you want.

Selecting a Plan takes you to the *Your Subscription Plan* page, where you confirm your choice. In the next step, we add a payment method to activate your subscription.

For more detail on changing Plans later, read the documentation on [updating your Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-subscription-plan/) and [updating your billing period](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-billing-period/).
