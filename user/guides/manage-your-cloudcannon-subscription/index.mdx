Welcome to the Manage your CloudCannon subscription guide!

This guide is for anyone setting up a paid CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the first time. Whether you own an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> and are setting up billing for your team, or you have been asked to manage the *Subscription*, we walk you through it step by step. No prior CloudCannon billing knowledge is assumed.

If you are already on a paid plan and need to make a specific change, such as updating your card or switching Plans, our [billing articles](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-subscription-plan/) cover individual tasks in detail.

## What we cover

In this guide, we walk through:

* Choosing a Subscription Plan for your team
* Adding a payment method to activate your subscription
* Setting up Add-Ons to extend your Plan's resource limits
* Understanding your billing summary and invoices

By the end, you have a fully configured paid subscription and know where to find your billing information.

## Before you begin

This guide assumes you already have:

* **A CloudCannon account** — If you do not have one yet, you can <a href="https://app.cloudcannon.com/register" target="_blank" rel="noopener">create a free account</a>.
* **An Organization** — An *Organization* is your team's workspace in CloudCannon, and every account starts with one. If you need another, you can create an Organization. Your *Organization* is where your *Sites*, team members, and subscription live.
* **Billing permissions** — To manage billing, you need to be in the *Owners* or *Billing* [Default Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/). If you created the *Organization*, you are already an *Owner*. Otherwise, ask an *Owner* to add you to the *Billing* group.

## A quick orientation

All billing settings live under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm>. To get there, click *Org Settings* in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Subscription* page to see your billing summary, payment methods, and plan details.

CloudCannon offers a Free Plan and a free trial of the Standard Plan, so you can explore before committing to a paid subscription. For a full comparison of Plans and pricing, see the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">pricing page</a>.

In the next step, we choose a Subscription Plan for your *Organization*.
