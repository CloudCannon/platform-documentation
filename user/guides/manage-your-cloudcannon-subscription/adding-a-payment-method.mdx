To activate a paid plan, add a credit card to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon accepts credit card payments on all paid plans.

## Add your card

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Add-Credit-Card-Modal-Element" title="The Add your Credit Card modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add your Credit Card modal in CloudCannon, showing fields for the name on the card, additional information, and address." type="screenshot" />

1. Click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Subscription* page.
2. Under *Billing Details*, click *Add Credit Card* to open the *Add your Credit Card* modal.
3. Enter your card details and verify they are correct.
4. If your bank requires it, enter any *Additional information* needed to process payments.
5. Click *Add Credit Card*.

CloudCannon verifies your card and uses it for your *Organization*'s payments.

## Complete checkout

Back on the *Your Subscription Plan* page, review the *Plan resources* and *Estimated bill*, then click *Complete checkout* to start your paid subscription.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Your-Subscription-Plan-Monthly-Team-Checkout" title="The Your Subscription Plan checkout" alt="A screenshot of the Your Subscription Plan page in CloudCannon, showing the plan resources, estimated bill, and the Complete checkout button." type="screenshot" />

For more detail, read the documentation on [managing your payment methods](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-payment-methods/). If a payment ever fails, read the documentation on [how CloudCannon handles failed payments](/documentation/user-articles/how-cloudcannon-handles-failed-payments/).
