With your subscription active, here is where to find your billing information.

## Your billing summary

The *Subscription* page shows your current plan, your estimated bill, your billing period, and your renewal date. This is the first place to look to confirm what you are paying and when.

## Your invoices

CloudCannon keeps a record of your invoices. To find them, click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Invoices* page. Click *View more* on an invoice to open it on a *Stripe* page, where you can download the invoice or receipt as a PDF.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Invoices-Page" title="The Invoices page" alt="A screenshot of the Invoices page in CloudCannon, showing a list of past invoices grouped by year, each with a date, amount, and View more link." type="screenshot" />

For more detail, read the documentation on [accessing and downloading your invoices](/documentation/user-articles/access-and-download-your-invoices/).

## Billing contact details

You can add billing contact details to your invoices and have CloudCannon email them to an address automatically. On the *Subscription* page, under *Billing Details*, click *Update contact info*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Billing-Contact-Information-Modal" title="The Billing contact information modal" alt="A screenshot of the Billing contact information modal in CloudCannon, showing fields for billing name, email, and address." type="screenshot" />

For more detail, read the documentation on [adding billing contact information](/documentation/user-articles/add-billing-contact-information/).

That's it! You now have a paid subscription and know where to manage it. In the next step, we point you to related resources.
