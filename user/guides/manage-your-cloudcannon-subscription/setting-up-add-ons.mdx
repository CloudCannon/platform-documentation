If your team needs more than your Plan includes, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm> extend your limits without moving to a higher plan. You can add capacity for team members, bandwidth, custom domains, and build time.

## Decide how Overages work

<comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm> control what happens when you reach a plan limit:

* **With Overages on**, CloudCannon automatically extends your resources as you exceed your limits, and the extra usage appears on your bill.
* **With Overages off**, your *Organization* is held to your Plan's limits. If you reach your bandwidth limit, your *Sites* go offline until the next billing period or until you turn *Overages* on.

## Turn Overages on or off

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Your-Subscription-Plan-Add-Ons-Element" title="The Hosting add-ons toggle" alt="A screenshot of the Hosting add-ons setting on the Your Subscription Plan page in CloudCannon, with a toggle to turn overages on or off." type="screenshot" />

1. Click <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then open the *Subscription* page.
2. Click *Review your billing plan* in the billing summary to open the *Your Subscription Plan* page.
3. Turn the *Hosting add-ons* setting on or off.
4. Click *Enable add-ons* or *Disable add-ons* to save your change.

For more detail, including how to track your usage, read the documentation on [Add-Ons and Overages](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/) and [managing your Add-Ons and Overages](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-add-ons-and-overages/).

In the final step, we look at your billing summary and invoices.
