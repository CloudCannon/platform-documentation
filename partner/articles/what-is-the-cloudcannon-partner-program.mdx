The <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">Partner Program</a> is for agencies and freelancers who build and maintain CloudCannon *Sites* for their clients. Becoming a member of the *Partner Program* is free, gives you access to UI for managing all your *Sites*, and provides scaling rewards as you bring more clients onto CloudCannon. When you join the *Partner Program*, your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> becomes a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

* **Partner Organization** — Your *Organization*, which might contain your own agency websites, and summarizes your activity and rewards across your client work.
* **Client Organization** — A separate *Organization* you create for an individual client, managed by your *Partner Organization* but used and paid for by the client, with its own team, billing, and login.

As you take on more client work, you earn <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm> that move you up the partner tiers and unlock more, including a partner discount of up to 20% on any <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your own *Organization* or any <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> you manage, a CloudCannon Experts Directory listing and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/expert-badge.yml">Expert Badge</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your site, priority support with a dedicated Slack channel, and product-roadmap visibility. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Partner Points and Rewards are](/documentation/partner-articles/what-are-partner-points-and-rewards/).

You only pay for the *Subscription Plans* used by your *Partner Organization* and any connected *Client Organizations*. For more information, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).

## Your Partner Organization UI

Your *Partner Organization* adds new UI and functionality.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-Partner-Org" alt="A screenshot of a Partner Organization Home shows Clients and Rewards in the App Sidebar and a Partner Rewards tier summary." title="A Partner Organization Home" type="screenshot"/>

Your *Organization Home* shows all *Unsaved Changes* and *Activity* across *Sites* in your *Partner Organization* and any connected *Client Organizations*, allowing you to keep track of everything from one place. For more information on *Client Organizations*, please read our documentation on [what Client Organizations are](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/).

For *Partner Organizations*, CloudCannon also reorganizes the *App Sidebar*. Clicking the *Clients* link opens the *Client Organizations Browser* and the *Rewards* link opens the *Partner Rewards* page, where you track your *Partner Points*. The *Your Org* section in the *App Sidebar* shows the *Sites*, *Projects*, *Domains*, and *Org Settings* links, specifically for managing your *Partner Organization*. 

## Join the Partner Program

To apply, visit the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> page, which covers eligibility, what's included, and how to sign up. Once you're enrolled, CloudCannon enables the partner features on your *Organization*, with *Rewards* available to its *Owners*.
