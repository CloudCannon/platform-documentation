The <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">Partner Program</a> is for agencies and freelancers who build and maintain CloudCannon *Sites* for their clients. There is no fee to join the *Partner Program*. Membership unlocks CloudCannon UI for managing your client work, and provides rewards that grow as you take on more clients. When you join, your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> becomes a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

* **Partner Organization** — Your *Organization*, which might contain your own agency websites, and summarizes your activity and rewards across your client work.
* **Client Organization** — A separate *Organization* you create for an individual client, managed by your *Partner Organization* but used by the client, with its own team, billing, and login.

As you take on more client work, you earn <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm> that move you up the partner tiers and unlock more, including a partner discount of up to 20% on any <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your own *Organization* or any <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> you manage, a CloudCannon Experts Directory listing and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/expert-badge.yml">Expert Badge</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your site, priority support with a dedicated Slack channel, and product-roadmap visibility. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Partner Points and Rewards are](/documentation/partner-articles/what-are-partner-points-and-rewards/).

Your *Partner Organization* needs a *Subscription Plan* of its own, and every *Client Organization* you create has one as well. The *Team Members* you pay for on your *Partner Organization* can work in every *Client Organization* you manage at no extra cost, no matter how many clients you have. For more information, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  If you don't host your own *Sites* on CloudCannon, and you keep each client in their own *Client Organization*, we recommend the *Lite Plan* for your *Partner Organization*. The *Lite Plan* includes one <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Member</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and additional *Team Members* are charged as an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-On</comp.GlossaryTerm>. It is available only to members of the *Partner Program*, and you can select it from the *Subscription* page under *Org Settings*.
</comp.Notice>

## Your Partner Organization UI

Joining the *Partner Program* changes what you see in your own *Organization*. CloudCannon adds places to manage your clients and track your rewards, and groups the links for your own *Sites* and settings separately.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-Partner-Org" alt="A screenshot of a Partner Organization Home shows Clients and Rewards in the App Sidebar and a Partner Rewards tier summary." title="A Partner Organization Home" type="screenshot"/>

Your *Organization Home* shows the *Unsaved changes* and *Recent activity* for *Sites* in your *Partner Organization*. To review *Health Checks* across every *Client Organization* you manage from one place, use the *Health* tab. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Client Organizations are](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/) and [managing your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/).

<comp.Notice info_type="pricing">
  **The *Health* tab is available through a private Beta.**

  To request access, please contact our friendly <a href="mailto:support@cloudcannon.com?subject=A%20question%20about%20health%20checks">support team</a>. CloudCannon enables the Beta for individual accounts rather than for a whole *Organization*, so everyone on your team who needs the *Health* tab must request access separately.
</comp.Notice>

For *Partner Organizations*, CloudCannon also reorganizes the *App Sidebar*. Clicking the *Clients* link opens the *Client Organizations Browser* and the *Rewards* link opens the *Partner Rewards* page, where you track your *Partner Points*. The *Rewards* link appears only for members of the *Owners Permission Group*. Below these, the *Your Org* section groups the links for managing your *Partner Organization* itself. The *Org Settings* link always appears. The *Sites*, *Projects*, and *Domains* links appear when your *Partner Organization* already has one of those, or when you have permission to create them, so a *Partner Organization* that hosts none of its own *Sites* may show *Org Settings* on its own.

## Join the Partner Program

To apply, visit the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> page, which covers eligibility, what's included, and how to sign up. Once you're enrolled, CloudCannon enables the partner features on your *Organization*, with *Rewards* available to its *Owners*.
