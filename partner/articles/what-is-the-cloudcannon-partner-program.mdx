The <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> is for agencies and freelancers who build and maintain *Sites* for clients. It gives you a single place to manage work across many clients, the option to run each client as a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and rewards that grow as you bring more clients onto CloudCannon.

## Your Partner Organization is your dashboard

When you join the Partner Program, your own *Organization* becomes your Partner *Organization*. Its dashboard, *Organization Home*, is the home base for all your client work: review a summary of your projects, activity, and rewards in one place. Alongside the usual *Sites Browser*, your Partner *Organization* adds new items to the *App Sidebar*:

* *Clients* — opens the *Client Organizations Browser*, where you create and manage separate *Organizations* for individual clients.
* *Rewards* — for *Owners* of your Partner *Organization*, opens the *Partner Rewards* page, where you track the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm> you've earned, see your current tier, and, from the Silver tier up, download your Expert badge.

## Two ways to structure client work

You decide where each client's *Sites* live. You can add a client's *Sites* directly to your Partner *Organization*, or give the client their own [Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/). CloudCannon has no preference between the two, and you can mix them or move a client between them later. For a full comparison, please read our documentation on [structuring your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/).

A key benefit of *Client Organizations* is that your team comes for free: members of your Partner *Organization* can be added to any *Client Organization* at no extra cost. To create one, please read our documentation on [creating a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/); to work across them, please read our documentation on [managing your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/).

## Partner Rewards

The more client work you run on CloudCannon, the more *Partner Points* you earn. Points move you through partner tiers, each unlocking new benefits, and from the Silver tier up you can download an Expert badge to display on your own site. Both client *Sites* in your Partner *Organization* and separate *Client Organizations* count. For details, please read our documentation on [how Partner Points, tiers, and benefits work](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-rewards-and-points/).

## Join the Partner Program

To apply, visit the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> page, which covers eligibility, what's included, and how to sign up. Once you're enrolled, CloudCannon enables the partner features on your *Organization*: your *Organization Home* becomes your Partner *Organization* dashboard, the *Clients* item appears in your *App Sidebar*, and *Rewards* appears alongside it for *Owners* of your Partner *Organization*.

From there, a good order to get started is:

1. Decide how to structure your client work, keeping client *Sites* in your Partner *Organization* or giving each client their own *Client Organization*. For a comparison, please read our documentation on [structuring your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/).
2. Set up your first client. To create a *Client Organization*, please read our documentation on [creating a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/); to keep a client's *Sites* in your own *Organization*, create them from the *Sites Browser* as you would any *Site*.
3. Understand who pays for client work by reading our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/billing-in-the-partner-program/).
4. Track your rewards as you take on clients by reading our documentation on [how Partner Points, tiers, and benefits work](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-rewards-and-points/).
