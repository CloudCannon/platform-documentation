In most cases, *Organizations* are entirely independent from one another. However, a [Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/) is always linked to a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, whose *Owners* can access its content and settings.

## The Partner Permission Group

When you first create a *Client Organization*, CloudCannon automatically adds a *Partner* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/default-permission-groups.yml">Default Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and populates this group with the members of the *Owners Default Permission Group* of the *Partner Organization*. This *Permission Group* mirroring is the fundamental method by which CloudCannon links *Partner Organizations* to their *Client Organizations*, and allows the first to manage the second.

Every time you update the members in your *Partner Organization* *Owners Permission Group*, CloudCannon will also update the membership of all *Partner Permission Groups* across *Client Organizations* they manage. Unlike other *Permission Groups*, you cannot directly edit or delete from the *Client Organization's* *Team* page.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Keep your *Partner Organization's* *Owners* group membership as small as possible to prevent un-needed access to every *Client Organization* you manage.
</comp.Notice>

As a member of the *Partner Permission Group*, you are granted the same permissions as the *Owners Permission Group* in the *Client Organization*. This means you can:

* Create and configure *Sites*, [Projects](/documentation/developer-articles/create-a-project/), and *Custom Domains*.
* Edit, save, and [publish](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-publishing/) content on every *Site*.
* Add *Team Members* to the *Client Organization*, and manage its [Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-permission-groups/).
* Change *Organization* settings, including its [branding](/documentation/user-articles/customize-your-organization-branding/), and create and delete *API Keys*.
* Manage the client's [Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-subscription-plan/), payment methods, and invoices.

You can also take the actions that no other *Permission Group* can be granted, however much access it is given:

* Configure [SAML](/documentation/developer-articles/add-sso-saml-authentication/) on the *Single Sign-On* page under *Org Settings*.
* Set a date after which *Team Members* must use [MFA](/documentation/developer-articles/enforce-multi-factor-authentication/), on the *Multi-Factor Authentication* page under *Org Settings*.
* Export the access review for the *Client Organization* from the *Team* page under *Org Settings*.
* [View](/documentation/user-articles/view-your-organization-as-a-team-member/) the *Client Organization* as one of its *Team Members*.
* Add *Team Members* to, and remove them from, the *Client Organization's* [Owners Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/transfer-ownership-of-an-organization/). This is how you hand a *Client Organization* over to your client.
* [Delete](/documentation/user-articles/delete-an-organization/) the *Client Organization*.

The members of the *Partner Permission Group* are visible to everyone in the *Client Organization* with permission to read *Groups*, and no one can directly edit or delete it.

## Inviting other Partner Organization Members

To [add more Team Members](/documentation/user-articles/add-a-team-member-to-your-organization/) from your *Partner Organization* to the *Client Organization*, you can invite them to any *Custom Permission Group* or *Default Permission Group* on the *Client Organization* the same way you would in your own *Organization*.

Members of your *Partner Organization* added this way do not count towards the number of *Team Members* included in the *Client Organization's* *Subscription Plan*. This way you can add as many people as you need to manage it without increasing the invoice with *Add-Ons*, as long as those *Team Members* remain a member of your *Partner Organization*.

Any actions a *Partner* takes in the *Client Organization*, such as saving changes to files, updating CloudCannon settings, or x, will appear in the *Activity* tabs on the *Site Dashboard* and *Organization Home*, just like any other *Team Member*.

## Client Organization Owners

When you [create a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/), CloudCannon also adds you to the *Owners Permission Group* for the *Client Organization*. That means the initial member of the *Client Organization* will be in the *Partner* and *Owner* groups. As the only member of the *Owner Permission Group*, you [cannot leave](/documentation/user-articles/join-or-leave-an-organization/#leave-an-organization).

When you are ready to hand over control to your client, you can invite them to any of the *Permission Groups* in the *Client Organization*, including the *Owner* group, at which point you can leave the *Owner* group if you want to. Depending on the client relationship, you may want to  manage the *Client Organization* long term. As there is nothing the *Partner* group can't do that the *Owner* group can, it is safe to leave the *Owner Permission Group*.

However, if your client relationship is short term, or a contract is ending, your can detatch the *Client Organization* from your *Partner Organization*, ending the *Organization* management and allowing you client to manage their content, settings, and billing independantly.

For more information on these processes, please read our documentation on [handing over or detatching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).
