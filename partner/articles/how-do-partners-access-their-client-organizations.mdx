Everyone who works on a [Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/) reaches it through a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the same as in any other <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. The *Owners* of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> get access automatically, through the *Partner Permission Group*. You add anyone else on your team yourself.

## The Partner Permission Group

When you first create a *Client Organization*, CloudCannon automatically adds a *Partner* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/default-permission-groups.yml">Default Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and populates this group with the members of the *Owners Default Permission Group* of the *Partner Organization*. The group exists because the *Client Organization* is linked to your *Partner Organization*, and CloudCannon removes it if that link is ever broken.

Every time you update the members in your *Partner Organization's* *Owners Permission Group*, CloudCannon will also update the membership of the *Partner Permission Group* in every *Client Organization* you manage. Unlike other *Permission Groups*, you cannot edit or delete the *Partner* group from the *Client Organization's* *Team* page.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Keep your *Partner Organization's* *Owners* group membership as small as possible to prevent unneeded access to every *Client Organization* you manage.
</comp.Notice>

As a member of the *Partner Permission Group*, you are granted the same permissions as the *Owners Permission Group* in the *Client Organization*. This means you can:

* Create and configure <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm>, [Projects](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-project/), and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
* Edit, save, and [publish](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-publishing/) content on every *Site*.
* Add <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm> to the *Client Organization*, and manage its [Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-permission-groups/).
* Change *Organization* settings, including its [branding](/documentation/user-articles/customize-your-organization-branding/), and create and delete *API Keys*.
* Manage the client's [Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-subscription-plan/), payment methods, and invoices.

You can also take the actions that no other *Permission Group* can be granted, however much access it is given:

* Configure [SAML](/documentation/developer-articles/add-sso-saml-authentication/) on the *Single Sign-On* page under *Org Settings*, available on our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">Enterprise Plan</a>.
* Set a date after which *Team Members* must use [MFA](/documentation/developer-articles/enforce-multi-factor-authentication/), on the *Multi-Factor Authentication* page under *Org Settings*, available on our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">Team or Enterprise Plan</a>.
* Export the access review for the *Client Organization* from the *Members* tab on the *Team* page under *Org Settings*.
* Use [View As mode](/documentation/user-articles/view-your-organization-as-a-team-member/) to see the *Client Organization* as one of its *Team Members*.
* Add *Team Members* to, and remove them from, the *Client Organization's* [Owners Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/#owners). This is how you hand a *Client Organization* over to your client.
* [Delete the Client Organization](/documentation/user-articles/delete-an-organization/).

Everyone in the *Client Organization* who can read *Permission Group* members can see who is in the *Partner Permission Group*, and no one in the *Client Organization* can change it.

## Inviting other Partner Organization members

To [add more Team Members](/documentation/user-articles/add-a-team-member-to-your-organization/) from your *Partner Organization* to the *Client Organization*, you can invite them to any *Default Permission Group* the same way you would in your own *Organization*. The one exception is the *Partner* group, which CloudCannon manages for you. If the *Client Organization* is on our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">Team or Enterprise Plan</a>, you can also invite them to a *Custom Permission Group*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Org-Settings-Team-Invite-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Invite Team Members modal in a Client Organization shows fields for an email address and a Permission Group, above the Add members button." title="The Invite Team Members modal in a Client Organization" type="screenshot"/>

Members of your *Partner Organization* added this way do not count towards the number of *Team Members* included in the *Client Organization's* *Subscription Plan*. This way you can add as many people as you need to manage it without increasing the invoice with <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm>, as long as those *Team Members* remain a member of your *Partner Organization*.

Any actions a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner.yml">Partner</comp.GlossaryTerm> takes in the *Client Organization*, such as saving changes to files, updating CloudCannon settings, or publishing changes to a *Site*, will appear in the *Activity* tabs on the *Site Dashboard* and *Organization Home*, like any other *Team Member*.

## Client Organization Owners

When you [create a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/), CloudCannon also adds you to the *Owners Permission Group* for the *Client Organization*. That means you start out in both the *Partner* and *Owners* groups. While you are the only member of the *Owners* group, you cannot leave it, because every *Organization* must have at least one *Owner*.

When you are ready to hand over control to your client, invite them to any of the *Permission Groups* in the *Client Organization*, including the *Owners* group. Once they are in place, you can step down by selecting the *Leave Group* option from the *Context Menu* on the *Owners* group, on the *My Permissions* page under *Org Settings*. This does not reduce your access, because the *Partner* group already grants everything the *Owners* group does. Depending on the client relationship, you may prefer to stay in both groups and manage the *Client Organization* long term. For more information, please read our documentation on [leaving a Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/manage-my-permissions/#leave-a-permission-group).

Leaving the *Owners* group is not the same as leaving the *Client Organization*. CloudCannon mirrors your *Partner* group membership from your *Partner Organization*, so you cannot remove yourself from a *Client Organization* while you are one of your *Partner Organization's* *Owners*. To end your access, either leave your *Partner Organization's* *Owners* group, which also removes you from every other *Client Organization* you manage, or detach the *Client Organization*.

However, if your client relationship is short term, or a contract is ending, you can detach the *Client Organization* from your *Partner Organization*, ending the *Organization* management and allowing your client to manage their content, settings, and billing independently.

For more information on these processes, please read our documentation on [handing over or detaching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).
