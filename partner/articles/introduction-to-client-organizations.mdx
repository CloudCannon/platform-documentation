A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> that your *Partner Organization* manages on behalf of a client, while the client keeps their own team, billing, and login. Running a client in their own *Client Organization* is one of the two ways to structure client work in the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/).

Articles in this section are useful for the developers and agency owners who create, manage, and hand over *Client Organizations*.

In the Client Organizations section of our Partner documentation, we cover:

* **What a Client Organization is** — Learn how a *Client Organization* keeps a client's team and billing separate from your own.
* **Creating and accessing** — Learn how to create a *Client Organization* and give your team access to it.
* **Managing** — Learn how to find, open, and monitor the clients you run.
* **Handing over and detaching** — Learn how to give your client control, and how to end the relationship.

Let's briefly introduce these topics.

## What a Client Organization is

A *Client Organization* behaves like any other *Organization*, with its own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, team, and settings. The difference is that the *Partner Organization* managing it is automatically allowed access, through a *Partner Permission Group* CloudCannon maintains for you.

For a more in-depth explanation of Client Organizations, please read our documentation:

* [What is a Client Organization?](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/)

## Creating and accessing Client Organizations

You create a *Client Organization* from the *Client Organizations Browser*, choosing the *Subscription Plan* that suits your client. The *Owners* of your *Partner Organization* get access automatically, and you add anyone else on your team yourself.

For a more in-depth explanation of creating and accessing Client Organizations, please read our documentation:

* [Create a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/)
* [How do Partners access their Client Organizations?](/documentation/partner-articles/how-do-partners-access-their-client-organizations/)

## Managing your Client Organizations

Every *Client Organization* you manage appears in the *Client Organizations Browser*, where you can filter for the clients that need attention and open any of them.

For a more in-depth explanation of managing Client Organizations, please read our documentation:

* [Manage your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/)

## Handing over and detaching

Some client relationships run long term and others end on a fixed date. You can give your client control of their *Client Organization* at any time, and detach it from your *Partner Organization* so it runs independently.

For a more in-depth explanation of handing over and detaching, please read our documentation:

* [Hand over or detach a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/)
