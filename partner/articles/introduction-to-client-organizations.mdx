A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> that your Partner *Organization* manages on behalf of a client, while the client keeps their own team, billing, and login. Running a client in their own *Client Organization* is one of the two ways to structure client work in the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/).

Articles in this section are for the developers and agency owners who create, manage, and hand over *Client Organizations*.

In the Client Organizations section of our documentation, we cover:

* What a Client Organization is — how it keeps a client's team and billing separate from your own.
* Creating and managing — how to create a *Client Organization* and work across the ones you run.
* Access and handover — who on your team can reach a *Client Organization*, who owns it, and how to hand it over.

## What a Client Organization is

For what a *Client Organization* is and how your Partner *Organization* manages it, please read our documentation on [what a Client Organization is](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/).

## Creating and managing Client Organizations

To create one, please read our documentation on [creating a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/). To work across the ones you run day to day, please read our documentation on [managing your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/).

## Access and handover

To learn who on your team can reach a *Client Organization*, who owns it, and what the client can see, please read our documentation on [how partner access and permissions work](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-access-and-permissions/). When an engagement ends, please read our documentation on [handing over or detaching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).
