A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> that your *Partner Organization* manages on behalf of a client, while the client keeps their own team, billing, and login. Running a client in their own *Client Organization* is one of the two ways to structure client work in the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/).

Articles in this section are useful for the developers and agency owners who create, manage, and hand over *Client Organizations*.

In the Client Organizations section of our Partner documentation, we cover:

* [What is a Client Organization?](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/) — Learn how a *Client Organization* keeps a client's team and billing separate from your own.
* [Create a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/) — Learn how to create a *Client Organization* and choose the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> that suits your client.
* [How do Partners access their Client Organizations?](/documentation/partner-articles/how-do-partners-access-their-client-organizations/) — Learn who on your team can reach a *Client Organization*, and how to bring in the rest of your agency.
* [Manage your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/) — Learn how to find, open, and monitor the clients you run.
* [Hand over or detach a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/) — Learn how to give your client control, and how to end the relationship.
