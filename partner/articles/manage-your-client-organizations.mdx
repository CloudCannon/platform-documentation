The *Client Organizations Browser* in your Partner *Organization* is where you see and manage every [Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/) you run. Open it by clicking the *Clients* item in the *App Sidebar* of your Partner *Organization*.

## View your Client Organizations

The *Client Organizations Browser* lists each *Client Organization* as a card showing its *Site* and domain counts, its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and whether it has any issues that need attention. Use the filter controls to narrow the list when you manage a large number of clients.

## Open and manage a Client Organization

Click a *Client Organization* to switch into it. From there you manage it like any other *Organization*: create and configure *Sites*, invite team members, and adjust its settings. To switch back to your Partner *Organization*, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-Client-Org" alt="A screenshot of a Client Organization Home shows a Partner Organization card above the current Client Organization card in the App Sidebar." title="A Client Organization Home" type="screenshot"/>

## Your team comes for free

Members of your Partner *Organization* can be added to any *Client Organization* at no extra cost, so you never pay twice for the same seat. When a *Client Organization* is created, CloudCannon adds a *Partner* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm> to it, pre-populated with the *Owners* of your Partner *Organization*. To bring the rest of your team on, add them as members into whichever group suits their role, and they don't count toward the *Client Organization*'s seat limit. For a fuller picture of how this access works, please read our documentation on [how partner access and permissions work](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-access-and-permissions/).

## Add or hand over a Client Organization

* To create a new one, click the *New Client* button and follow the steps in our documentation on [creating a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/).
* To hand a *Client Organization* over to a client, or detach it so it becomes independent, please read our documentation on [handing over or detaching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).
