Managing your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm> in CloudCannon can involve many things: from updating the build settings on a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> and publishing new content, to fixing DNS records or providing people with the CloudCannon permissions they need. Because *Client Organizations* function in the same way as any other <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, these topics are all covered in the [User Articles](/documentation/user-articles/) and [Developer Articles](/documentation/developer-articles/) sections of this documentation.

However, it is important to know how to find and open each *Client Organization*, and determine what needs your attention, especially if you manage several clients.

## Find and open a Client Organization

You can view the *Client Organizations* managed by your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> using the *Client Organizations Browser*. To open this page, click the *Clients* link in your *App Sidebar*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Organization-Browser" alt="A screenshot of the Client Organizations Browser in a Partner Organization shows four Client Organizations as cards, each listing its Sites and domains counts, Subscription Plan, and issue status, with the New Client button above the list and the Clients link in the App Sidebar." title="The Client Organizations Browser" type="screenshot"/>

The *Client Organizations Browser* works in the same way as the *Organizations Browser*, except it lists only the *Client Organizations* managed by your *Partner Organization*. It shows the *Client Organizations* you are a member of, so if you have not been added to any, the *Client Organizations Browser* is empty. Members of the *Owners Permission Group* in your *Partner Organization* see every client, because CloudCannon adds them to the *Partner Permission Group* in each *Client Organization* automatically. For more information, please read our documentation on [how Partners access their Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/how-do-partners-access-their-client-organizations/).

You can use the *Filter Bar* at the top of the *Client Organizations Browser* to narrow your results further. Alongside searching by name, you can filter for the clients that need attention, such as those with failing *Sites*, a payment issue, a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> ending soon, or a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> whose certificate has expired. You can also sort your results using the *Sort by* dropdown.

Each *Client Organization* appears as a *Card*, showing how many *Sites* and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm> it has, its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, how many edits it has had over the last 14 days, its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm> usage so far this month, and whether it has any building or syncing errors. A client whose *Sites* are all healthy shows *No issues* instead.

To open a *Client Organization*, click its *Card* in the *Client Organizations Browser*. CloudCannon opens the *Organization Home* page for that client. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

## Monitor the health of your Client Organizations

<comp.Notice info_type="pricing">
  **This feature is available through a private Beta.**

  Health checks across your *Client Organizations* are currently available through a private Beta. To request access, please contact our friendly <a href="mailto:support@cloudcannon.com?subject=A%20question%20about%20health%20checks">support team</a>.

  CloudCannon enables the Beta for individual accounts rather than for a whole *Organization*, so everyone on your team who needs the *Issues* tab must request it separately.
</comp.Notice>

The *Issues* tab on your *Partner Organization's* *Organization Home* page shows the results of several health checks across your own *Organization* and every *Client Organization* you manage, so you can review them all from one place.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Partner-Organization-Home-Issues-Tab" alt="A screenshot of the Issues tab on a Partner Organization's Organization Home page shows health checks grouped under Danger, Warning, and Passed." title="The Issues tab in a Partner Organization" type="screenshot"/>

CloudCannon lists your health checks under *Danger*, *Warning*, *Informational*, and *Passed*, and only shows a heading when at least one check falls under it. Click on each *Health Check Card* to expand it and see more details about what it affects. These can be your own *Sites* and *Custom Domains*, under *Your Organization*, or the affected clients, under *Client Organizations*.

The *Client Organizations* group lists each affected client as a *Card*, with a note of what triggered the check, such as "3 failing builds" or when a *Free Trial* ends. A client appears under only one of the payment, *Free Trial*, and inactivity checks at a time, so the same client is never counted twice.

Clicking a client's *Card* opens that *Client Organization's* *Issues* tab, or their *Activity* tab for the *Clients are inactive* check.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Partner-Organization-Home-Issues-Check-Expanded" alt="A screenshot of an expanded health check on the Issues tab shows the affected Sites under Your Organization and the affected clients under Client Organizations." title="An expanded health check in a Partner Organization" type="screenshot"/>

The error counts on the *Cards* in the *Client Organizations Browser* are separate from this, and are available to every *Partner Organization*. What the *Issues* tab adds is the aggregated view across every client you manage.
