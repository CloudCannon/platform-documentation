Managing your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm> in CloudCannon can involve many things: from updating the build settings on a *Site* and publishing new content, to fixing DNS records or providing people with the CloudCannon permissions they need. Because *Client Organizations* function in the same way as any other *Organization*, these topics are all covered in the [User Articles](/documentation/user-articles/) and [Developer Articles](/documentation/developer-articles/) sections of this documentation.

However, it is important know how to find and open each *Client Organization*, and determine what needs your attention, especially if you manage several clients.

## Find and open a Client Organization

You can view all the [Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/) managed by your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> using the *Client Organizations Browser*. To open this page, click on the *Clients* link in your *App Sidebar*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Organization-Browser" alt="A screenshot of the Client Organizations Browser shows four Client Organizations as cards, with the Clients link in the App Sidebar of a Partner Organization." title="The Client Organizations Browser" type="screenshot"/>

The *Client Organizations Browser* works in the same way as the *Organizations Browser*, except it lists only the *Client Organizations* managed by your *Partner Organization*. Additionally, unless you are a member of the *Owner Permission Group* in the *Partner Organization*, you can only see the *Client Organizations* you belong to. If you are not invited to any *Client Organizations*, the *Client Organizations Browser* will be empty. For more information, please read our documentation on [how Partners access their Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/how-do-partners-access-their-client-organizations/).

You can use the *Filter Bar* at the top of the *Client Organizations Browser* to narrow your results further. Alongside searching by name, you can filter for the clients that need attention, such as those with failing *Sites*, a payment issue, a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> ending soon, or a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> whose certificate has expired. You can also sort your results using the *Sort by* dropdown.

Each *Client Organization* appears as a card, showing how many <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm> it has, its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, how many edits it has had over the last 14 days, its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm> usage so far this month, and whether it has any building or syncing errors. A client whose *Sites* are all healthy shows *No issues* instead.

To open a *Client Organization*, click on the *Card* in the *Client Organization Browser*. CloudCannon will open the *Organization Home* page for that client. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

## Monitor the health of your Client Organizations

<comp.Notice info_type="pricing">
  **This feature is available through a private Beta.**

  Health checks across your *Client Organizations* are currently available through a private Beta. To request access, please contact our friendly <a href="mailto:success@cloudcannon.com?subject=A%20question%20about%20health%20checks">support team</a>.
</comp.Notice>

The *Issues* tab on your *Partner Organization*'s *Organization Home* page runs health checks across your own *Organization* and every *Client Organization* you manage, so you can review them all from one place. Checks that apply to both split into two groups: *Your Organization* and *Client Organizations*.

The *Client Organizations* group lists each affected client as a card, with a note of what triggered the check, such as "3 failing builds" or when a *Free Trial* ends. A client appears under only one of the payment, *Free Trial*, and inactivity checks at a time, so the same client is never counted twice.

To review a health check across your clients:

1. Click the *Home* link in the *App Sidebar* of your *Partner Organization*. CloudCannon will open your *Organization Home* page.
2. Open the *Issues* tab. CloudCannon will list your health checks under *Danger*, *Warning*, *Informational*, and *Passed*.
3. Click a failing check to expand it. CloudCannon will show what the check applies to, listing any affected clients under *Client Organizations*.
4. Under *Client Organizations*, click the card for the client you want to review.

CloudCannon will open that *Client Organization*'s *Issues* tab, where you can work through the checks failing for that client. The *Clients are inactive* check opens their *Activity* tab instead, so you can see when they last made a change.

The error counts on the cards in the *Client Organizations Browser* are separate from this, and are available to every *Partner Organization*. What the *Issues* tab adds is the aggregated view across every client you manage.
