A [Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/) is a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> that you manage on behalf of a client. The relationship between you and your client will vary: some may be long term while others have a defined period of work. CloudCannon allows you to hand over control of a *Client Organization* to your client without removing access for members of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or detach the *Client Organization* entirely.

Handing over and detaching are separate actions, and you can do either without the other. Ending an engagement completely usually means both, in that order: hand over control so your client can run the *Organization* themselves, then detach it so your *Partner Organization* no longer manages it.

## Hand over a Client Organization

When you create a *Client Organization*, CloudCannon automatically adds the person who created it to the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/owners.yml">Owners Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You can only add or remove members of the *Owners Permission Group* if you belong to the *Owners Permission Group* or the *Partner Permission Group*.

Members of the *Owners Permission Group* and the *Partner Permission Group* can perform the same actions, allowing you to set up the *Organization*. When you are ready to hand over control to your client, add them to the *Owners Permission Group* so they can run the *Organization* themselves. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Team Member to a Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/add-a-team-member-to-a-permission-group/).

Handing over control does not change your *Partner Organization's* access. Your team still reaches the *Client Organization* through the *Partner Permission Group*, the *Organization* still counts toward your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and members of your *Partner Organization* remain exempt from its seat count.

Removing yourself from the *Owners Permission Group* does not end your access either, because CloudCannon mirrors your *Partner Organization's* *Owners* into the *Partner Permission Group* of every *Client Organization* you manage. To step back from a *Client Organization* entirely, detach it. For more information, please read our documentation on [how Partners access their Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/how-do-partners-access-their-client-organizations/).

## Detach a Client Organization

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Detaching a *Client Organization* isn't a self-serve action. To detach an *Organization*, coordinate with CloudCannon support.
</comp.Notice>

Detaching breaks the link between a *Client Organization* and your *Partner Organization*, leaving the *Client Organization* to run on its own.

When you're ready to detach the *Client Organization* from your *Partner Organization*, contact CloudCannon support. Have the following ready:

* Your *Partner Organization* name.
* The *Client Organization* name.
* Who will take over billing for the *Organization* once it's independent.

Detaching ends the access your team had through the *Partner Organization*. CloudCannon removes the *Partner Permission Group* from the *Client Organization*, so anyone who reached it through that group can no longer open it. Any <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm> you added to the *Client Organization's* other <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> keep their access, including you if you are still in its *Owners Permission Group*. They also stop being exempt from its seat count, and begin counting toward the number of *Team Members* included in its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon updates the *Subscription* as soon as you detach rather than at the next renewal, so if this takes the *Client Organization* over its included *Team Members*, the cost changes from that point.

Detaching does not change which payment method the *Organization* uses. If you were paying for the *Client Organization*, remove your payment method so your client's card is the one charged. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your payment methods](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-payment-methods/).

After it's detached, the *Organization* no longer counts toward your *Partner Points*.

## If you're a client who wants to become independent

If your *Organization* was set up and is managed by a partner, start by contacting that partner: they manage the relationship and can arrange the handover. If the partner is unreachable and you need to take over your own *Organization*, contact CloudCannon support. Have the following ready:

* Your *Organization* name.
* The partner or agency that set up your *Organization*.
* Evidence of your relationship to the *Organization*, such as an invoice or contract.

Once your *Organization* is independent, add a payment method to keep its subscription active. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your payment methods](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-payment-methods/).
