A [Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/) is managed by a Partner *Organization* but used by the client. When an engagement ends, or a client wants to run their own *Organization* independently, the *Client Organization* can be detached from the Partner *Organization* so it stands on its own.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Detaching a *Client Organization* isn't a self-serve action in the app today. To detach an *Organization*, coordinate with CloudCannon support. Once independent, an *Organization* needs its own payment method to keep its subscription active, so if you were covering its billing, the client takes it over when you detach.
</comp.Notice>

## If you're the partner handing a Client Organization over

Because you created the *Client Organization*, you start as its *Owner*. Before you hand it over, add your client to the *Owners* *Permission Group* so they can run the *Organization* themselves, then remove yourself if you're stepping away.

When you're ready to detach the *Client Organization* from your Partner *Organization*, contact CloudCannon support. Have the following ready:

* Your Partner *Organization* name.
* The *Client Organization* name.
* Who will take over billing for the *Organization* once it's independent.

After it's detached, the *Organization* no longer counts toward your Partner *Organization*, and the client becomes responsible for its subscription and payment method.

## If you're a client who wants to become independent

If your *Organization* was set up and is managed by a partner, start by contacting that partner: they manage the relationship and can arrange the handover. If the partner is unreachable and you need to take over your own *Organization*, contact CloudCannon support. Have the following ready:

* Your *Organization* name.
* The partner or agency that set up your *Organization*.
* Evidence of your relationship to the *Organization*, such as an invoice or contract.

Once your *Organization* is independent, add a payment method to keep its subscription active. For the steps, read Manage your payment methods.
