When you onboard your clients to CloudCannon, you can choose who pays for the resources they use. Depending on the business model of your agency, you may prefer to pay for CloudCannon directly or have your clients receive and pay their own invoices. CloudCannon has no preference about who pays for each <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, allowing you to pick the best option for you and your clients.

## Pay for your Partner Organization

Your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> has its own *Subscription Plan*, separate from any *Client Organization* you manage. Joining the *Partner Program* does not change that, so your *Partner Organization* needs a *Subscription Plan* even if you host none of your own *Sites* on CloudCannon.

If you don't host your own *Sites* on CloudCannon, and you keep each client in their own *Client Organization*, we recommend the *Lite Plan* for your *Partner Organization*. It is available only to members of the *Partner Program*, and you can select it from the *Subscription* page under *Org Settings*.

The *Lite Plan* includes one <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Member</comp.GlossaryTerm> and additional *Team Member* are charged as an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-On</comp.GlossaryTerm>. For more information about what each plan includes, please read the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">CloudCannon pricing page</a>.

Paying for those *Team Members* once covers your whole team's client work. Everyone in your *Partner Organization* can work in every *Client Organization* you manage, and never counts toward the *Team Members* included in a client's *Subscription Plan*, however many clients you have. For more information, please read our documentation on [how Partners access their Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/how-do-partners-access-their-client-organizations/).

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-sharing.yml">Client Sharing</comp.GlossaryTerm> draws on the same *Team Members* as people with a CloudCannon account. Each *Site* with *Client Sharing* enabled counts as one *Team Member*, and each [Site Sharing](/documentation/user-articles/share-a-site-with-site-sharing/) member counts as one.

  If you invite someone as a *Team Member* and also turn on *Client Sharing* for them, they count twice and the *Organization* is billed for a seat nobody is using. Pick one method for each person.
</comp.Notice>

## Select who pays for your clients

The [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/) allows you to manage clients in two places: as *Sites* inside your *Partner Organization*, or as a separate [Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/) you can manage.

* Keeping client *Sites* in your *Partner Organization* means the resources they use are bundled into your *Subscription Plan* and billed to your *Partner Organization*'s payment method on your single invoice. If you pay for the invoice, you can choose how to incorporate it into how you already bill the client, for example alongside web maintenance or other services.
* Keeping client *Sites* on a separate *Client Organization* gives them their own *Subscription Plan*, with its own payment method and its own invoices. Your client can pay directly with their own card, or you can add yours and keep covering the cost. You can add members of your *Partner Organization* for no extra cost and they do not contribute to the number of seats in their *Subscription Plan*. CloudCannon will warn you if there are any payment issues.

In both cases, each *Organization* has its own *Subscription Plan* and its own payment method. For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Subscription Plan is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-subscription-plan/) and [managing your payment methods](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-payment-methods/).

Who pays and who receives the invoices are separate choices. CloudCannon emails each invoice to the address in the *Billing Email* field on the *Subscription* page under *Org Settings*, so you can send a *Client Organization's* invoices to your client while your own payment method covers the cost, or the other way around. If that field is empty, CloudCannon does not email invoices for that *Organization* at all. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding billing contact information](/documentation/user-articles/add-billing-contact-information/).

Other than billing, there are several factors that may influence where you choose to keep your clients. For more information, please read our documentation on [best practices for structuring client Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/best-practices-for-structuring-client-sites/).

## Partner discounts and Free Trials

As you rise up the tiers of the *Partner Program*, you unlock a discount of up to 20% off the price of all *Subscription Plans* on your *Partner Organization* and connected *Client Organizations* you manage. This discount is not applied automatically; you add it as a coupon to the *Subscription Plan* for each *Organization* you want to discount. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Partner Points and Rewards are](/documentation/partner-articles/what-are-partner-points-and-rewards/) and [adding a coupon to your Subscription](/documentation/user-articles/add-a-coupon-to-your-subscription/).

Your *Partner Organization* starts on the standard 21-day <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, like any other *Organization*. Any *Client Organization* you create starts on an extended six-month *Free Trial* of the *Subscription Plan* you choose instead, so you can build and hand over the *Sites* without paying until your client is ready to go live. For more information, please read our documentation on [what CloudCannon's Free Trial is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-cloudcannons-free-trial/).

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Four actions shorten a *Client Organization's* extended six-month *Free Trial* to three days:

  * Adding a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> to one of its *Sites*.
  * Turning on *Client Sharing* for one of its *Sites*.
  * Adding one of the client's own *Team Members*.
  * Accepting a transfer that takes the *Client Organization* over the *Custom Domains* or *Team Members* included in its *Subscription Plan*.

  The first three take effect as soon as you add one, whether or not the *Client Organization* is still within the limits of its *Subscription Plan*. Adding members of your *Partner Organization* does none of these, so you can build with your own team and keep the full six months.
</comp.Notice>
