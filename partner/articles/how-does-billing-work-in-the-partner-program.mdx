When you onboard your clients to CloudCannon, you can choose who pays for the resources they use. Depending on the business model of your agency, you may prefer to pay for CloudCannon directly or have your clients receive and pay their own invoices. CloudCannon has no preference about who pays for each <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, allowing you to pick the best option for you and your clients.

## Select who pays for your clients

The [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/) allows you to manage clients in two places: as *Sites* inside your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or as a separate [Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/) you can manage.

* Keeping client *Sites* in your *Partner Organization* means the resources they use are bundled into your *Subscription Plan* and billed to your *Partner Organization*'s payment method on your single invoice. If you pay for the invoice, you can choose how to incorporate it into how you already bill the client, for example alongside web maintenance or other services.
* Keeping client *Sites* on a separate *Client Organization* gives them their own *Subscription Plan*, billed to their payment method. You can add members of your *Partner Organization* for no extra cost and they do not contribute to the number of seats in their *Subscription Plan*. CloudCannon will send them invoices directly and warn you if there are any payment issues.

In both cases, each *Organization* has its own *Subscription Plan* and its own payment method. For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Subscription Plan is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-subscription-plan/) and [managing your payment methods](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-payment-methods/).

Other than billing, there are several factors that may influence where you choose to keep your clients. For more information, please read our documentation on [structuring your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/best-practices-for-structuring-client-sites/).

## Partner discounts and Free Trials

As you rise up the tiers of the *Partner Program*, you unlock a discount of up to 20% off the price of all *Subscription Plans* on your *Partner Organization* and connected *Client Organizations* you manage. This discount is not applied automatically; you add it as a coupon to the *Subscription Plan* for each *Organization* you want to discount. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Partner Points and Rewards are](/documentation/partner-articles/what-are-partner-points-and-rewards/) and [adding a coupon to your Subscription](/documentation/user-articles/add-a-coupon-to-your-subscription/).

Any *Client Organization* you create also starts on an extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> of the *Subscription Plan* you choose, so you can build and hand over the *Sites* without paying until your client is ready to go live. For more information, please read our documentation on [what CloudCannon's Free Trial is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-cloudcannons-free-trial/).
