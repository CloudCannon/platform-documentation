In the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/), how a client's work is billed follows how you structure it. The [two ways to structure client work](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/) map to two common billing models, and you can use either for a given client.

## Who pays for what

Every *Organization* in CloudCannon has its own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> and its own payment method. Which *Organization* a client's *Sites* live in decides whose subscription covers them:

* **Client *Sites* in your Partner *Organization*** — the *Sites* are part of your Partner *Organization*'s subscription, so they're billed to your Partner *Organization*'s payment method on your single invoice, alongside your other work.
* **A separate [Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/)** — it has its own subscription and its own invoice, normally paid by the client on their own payment method.

Because a *Client Organization* is a full, separate *Organization*, it manages its billing the same way any *Organization* does. For the mechanics, please read our documentation on [an introduction to billing](/documentation/user-articles/introduction-to-billing/), [managing your payment methods](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-payment-methods/), and [downloading invoices and receipts](/documentation/user-articles/download-invoices-and-receipts/).

## Your team doesn't add to the bill

Members of your Partner *Organization* can be added to any *Client Organization* at no extra cost, so you never pay twice for the same seat. Your team members don't count toward a *Client Organization*'s seat limit. For how this works, please read our documentation on [managing your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/).

## Client Organizations start on a Free Trial

A new *Client Organization* starts on a six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> of the *Subscription Plan* you choose, so you can build the *Sites* and hand them over without paying until the client is ready to go live. For the steps, please read our documentation on [creating a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/).

## Partner discounts

As your partnership grows, you unlock tier discounts you can apply to your own *Organization* or a *Client Organization* you manage. To see your current tier and discount, please read our documentation on [how Partner Points, tiers, and benefits work](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-rewards-and-points/).

## Choosing who pays

Both models work, and CloudCannon has no preference between them. One difference worth weighing is consolidation: when your Partner *Organization* holds the subscription, the CloudCannon cost sits on your invoice, so you can fold it into how you already bill the client, for example alongside maintenance or other services. When the client pays CloudCannon directly through their own *Client Organization*, that cost stays separate from your invoicing. Which suits a given client is your call.

For how the choice affects ownership and handover later, please read our documentation on [handing over or detaching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).
