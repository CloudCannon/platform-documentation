The <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> is for agencies and freelancers who build and maintain *Sites* for clients. It gives you a single place to manage work across many clients, the option to run each client as a separate *Organization*, and rewards that grow as you bring more clients onto CloudCannon.

## Your Partner Organization is your dashboard

When you join the Partner Program, your own *Organization* becomes your Partner *Organization*. Its dashboard, *Organization Home*, is the home base for all your client work: review a summary of your projects, activity, and rewards in one place. Alongside the usual *Sites Browser*, your Partner *Organization* adds two items to the *App Sidebar*:

* **Clients** — opens the *Client Organizations Browser*, where you create and manage separate *Organizations* for individual clients.
* **Rewards** — opens the *Partner Rewards* page, where you track the *Partner Points* you've earned, see your current tier, and download your partner badge.

## Two ways to structure client work

You decide where each client's *Sites* live. You can add a client's *Sites* directly to your Partner *Organization*, or give the client their own *Client Organization*. CloudCannon has no preference between the two, and you can mix them or move a client between them later. For a full comparison, read [Choosing how to structure your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/).

A key benefit of *Client Organizations* is that your team comes for free: members of your Partner *Organization* can be added to any *Client Organization* at no extra cost. To create one, read [Create a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/); to work across them, read [Manage your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/).

## Partner Rewards

The more client work you run on CloudCannon, the more *Partner Points* you earn. Points move you through partner tiers, each unlocking new benefits, and you can download a partner badge to display on your own site. Both client *Sites* in your Partner *Organization* and separate *Client Organizations* count. For details, read [Partner Rewards and Points](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-rewards-and-points/).

## Join the Partner Program

To apply, visit the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> page, which covers eligibility, what's included, and how to sign up. Once you're enrolled, CloudCannon enables the partner features on your *Organization*: your *Organization Home* becomes your Partner *Organization* dashboard, and the *Clients* and *Rewards* items appear in your *App Sidebar*.

From there, a good order to get started is:

1. **Decide how to structure your client work** — keep client *Sites* in your Partner *Organization*, or give each client their own *Client Organization*. Read [Choosing how to structure your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/).
2. **Set up your first client.** To create a *Client Organization*, read [Create a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/); to keep a client's *Sites* in your own *Organization*, create them from the *Sites Browser* as you would any *Site*.
3. **See how billing works** across the two models. Read [Billing in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/billing-in-the-partner-program/).
4. **Track your rewards** as you bring clients on. Read [Partner Rewards and Points](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-rewards-and-points/).
