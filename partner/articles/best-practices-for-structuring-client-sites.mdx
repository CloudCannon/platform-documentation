The [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/) allows you to manage clients in two places: as <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> inside your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or as a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> you can manage. CloudCannon has no preference between these methods, and each has benefits that suit a different kind of client.

You can use either approach, use both at the same time for clients with different needs, and move a client from one to the other later by transferring their *Sites*.

## Keep client Sites in your Partner Organization

<comp.Mermaid
  alt="A diagram shows one Organization containing a Project of Sites for each client."
  chart={`graph TD
    O["Your Partner Organization"] --> PA["Project connected to Client A's Git Repository"]
    O["Your Partner Organization"] --> PB["Project connected to Client B's Git Repository"]
    PA --> SA["Site"]
    PB --> SB["Site"]
  `}
/>

You can manage as many client *Sites* as you want within your *Partner Organization*, grouping them by which *Git Repository* they connect to using <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/project.yml">Projects</comp.GlossaryTerm>. The benefits of this method include:

* You only need to set up your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> once, and all your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm> have access to all client *Sites* by default.
* All your client *Sites* come under one <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which you control and pay for, so taking on a client doesn't require a new subscription.
* All your client *Sites* use the same pool of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
* You can create a proof-of-concept *Site* within your *Partner Organization* then transfer it to a *Client Organization* later.

You can create *Sites* and *Projects* the same way you would create your own, from the *Sites Browser* or *Projects Browser*. For more information, please read our documentation on [creating a branched Site](/documentation/user-articles/create-a-branched-site/) and [creating a Project](/documentation/developer-articles/create-a-project/).

You can give a client access in a few ways, depending on what they need:

* Add them to your *Organization* as a member. To limit their access to specific *Sites*, scope them with a [Custom Permission Group](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/), available on our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">Team or Enterprise Plan</a>.
* Share individual *Sites* through [Site Sharing](/documentation/user-articles/share-a-site-with-site-sharing/), which they access with their own CloudCannon account.
* Share a single *Site* through [Client Sharing](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-client-sharing/), with no CloudCannon account needed.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Each *Site* you enable *Client Sharing* on counts as one *Team Member* in your *Subscription Plan*, and each *Site Sharing* member counts as one, so factor them into your seat count.
</comp.Notice>

This one-size-fits-all approach is good for smaller clients. However, if you need a more complex setup, or separate billing, a *Client Organization* might be a better approach.

## Give each client their own Client Organization

You can manage as many [Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/) as you need, separating the content that belongs to your client from your *Partner Organization*. *Client Organizations* function similarly to any other *Organization*, except that their connected *Partner Organization* is automatically allowed access. The benefits of this method include:

* Each *Client Organization* has its own *Subscription Plan*, and uses *Build Time*, *Hosting Bandwidth*, and *Custom Domains* from its own pool, so they can pay for the features and resources they need.
* You choose who pays and who receives the invoices. Your client can add their own payment method and have invoices emailed straight to them, or you can keep covering the cost yourself.
* Your clients get their own *Team Members* and ways to manage access to their content, including *Custom Permission Groups*, *Site Sharing*, and *Client Sharing*.
* Your clients get their own *Projects* and *Sites*.
* CloudCannon will alert you to any issues in the *Client Organizations* you manage on the *Issues* tab of your *Organization Home* page, so you can review them all from the same location.
* You can customize the branding of each *Client Organization* with the client's own logo and color, so the editing experience feels like theirs.

<comp.Notice info_type="pricing">
  **The *Issues* tab is available through a private Beta.**

  Health checks across your *Client Organizations* are currently available through a private Beta. To request access, please contact our friendly <a href="mailto:support@cloudcannon.com?subject=A%20question%20about%20health%20checks">support team</a>.

  CloudCannon enables the Beta for individual accounts rather than for a whole *Organization*, so everyone on your team who needs the *Issues* tab must request it separately.
</comp.Notice>

You can create a *Client Organization* from the *Client Organizations Browser*. For more information, please read our documentation on [creating a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/).

When you first create a *Client Organization*, CloudCannon adds a *Partner Permission Group* to it with full access to the *Organization* and its *Sites*. The *Owners* of your *Partner Organization* are added to this group automatically, so they can work in the *Client Organization* right away. To bring more of your team onto a client's content, add them to the *Partner Permission Group* in the *Client Organization*. Members of your *Partner Organization* don't count toward the *Client Organization's* *Team Members* limit, so you can add as many as you need at no extra cost.

This tailored approach is good for clients who need their own billing, their own set of features, or a space you can hand over to them. However, if a client doesn't need a setup of their own or you want to bundle the cost of CloudCannon into an invoice with other services, keeping their *Sites* in your *Partner Organization* might be simpler.

## How to choose

There's no single right answer, and you don't have to use the same approach for every client. Match the setup to what each client needs.

Keep a client's *Sites* in your *Partner Organization* when:

* You manage the work end to end, and only one or two people on the client's side edit content, so a seat or two in your own *Subscription Plan* covers them.
* You want to cover the cost of CloudCannon yourself, because you bill the client as part of a larger package or retainer.
* The engagement is small, or a proof of concept.

Give a client their own *Client Organization* when:

* The client wants to pay CloudCannon directly with their own payment method, or you want their billing kept separate from yours.
* They need a branded space, their own *Team Members*, or to manage access themselves.
* You expect to hand the *Sites* over to them, now or later.
* Their feature or resource needs differ enough to warrant their own *Subscription Plan*.

You don't have to commit permanently, and either approach counts toward your [Partner Rewards](/documentation/partner-articles/what-are-partner-points-and-rewards/).

## Move a client between your Organizations

A common pattern is to start a client in your *Partner Organization* while you build, then move them into their own *Client Organization* when they're ready to go live or take ownership. You can transfer a client's *Site* to their own *Client Organization*. For more information, please read our documentation on [transferring a Site between Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/transfer-a-site-between-organizations/).

You can only transfer a *Site* to an *Organization* you belong to. CloudCannon keeps the *Owners* of your *Partner Organization* in the *Partner Permission Group* of every *Client Organization* you manage, so your clients are always available as a destination. Anyone else on your team needs adding to the *Client Organization* first.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Accepting a transfer that takes the *Client Organization* over the *Custom Domains* or *Team Members* included in its *Subscription Plan* shortens its extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> to three days. Adding members of your *Partner Organization* doesn't, so you can build with your own team and keep the full six months.

  Three other actions shorten the trial as well. For the full list, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).
</comp.Notice>

*Projects* don't move with a *Site*, because they belong to the *Organization* that created them. Neither do *Permission Groups*, so your team reaches the transferred *Sites* through the *Partner Permission Group* in the *Client Organization* instead.

Transfers can't be undone, and the same process runs in the other direction: someone in the *Client Organization* transfers a *Site* back to your *Partner Organization*. That's also how you consolidate a client who no longer needs an *Organization* of their own. To leave the *Sites* where they are and hand your client the *Client Organization* around them, please read our documentation on [handing over or detaching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).
