As a partner in the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/), you organize your client work using two structural layers of CloudCannon: <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/project.yml">Projects</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Knowing how they fit together helps you decide where each client's *Sites* belong. For the general hierarchy, please read our documentation on [what an Organization is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-an-organization/).

## Organizations separate your clients

An *Organization* is the boundary for a team, a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and one invoice. You have two ways to place a client's work:

* Keep the client in your Partner *Organization*, where several clients share your team, *Subscription Plan*, and *Organization Home*.
* Give the client their own *Client Organization*, with a fully separate team, billing, and login.

For a full comparison of these two approaches, including their access and billing tradeoffs, please read our documentation on [structuring your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/).

## Projects group each website's Sites

Within an *Organization*, a *Project* groups the *Sites* that are copies of a single website, such as its production, staging, and branched versions. A client with one website has one *Project*; a client with several websites has several. Use *Projects* to keep each website's *Sites* together, whether the client sits in your Partner *Organization* or in their own *Client Organization*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Project is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-project/).

## Moving client Sites later

You're not locked in. As a client's needs change, for example when you graduate them from your Partner *Organization* to their own *Client Organization*, you can move their *Sites* between *Organizations*. For the steps, please read our documentation on [transferring a Site between Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/transfer-a-site-between-organizations/).
