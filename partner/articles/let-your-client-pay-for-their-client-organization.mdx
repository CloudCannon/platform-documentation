<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `group:member:write` permission, can add *Organization Members* to a *Permission Group*. The *Owners Permission Group* is an exception: only members of the *Owners* and *Partner Default Permission Groups* can add *Organization Members* to it. *Custom Permission Groups* cannot be granted this permission.
</comp.Notice>

When you first create a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, CloudCannon does not ask for a payment method. The *Client Organization* starts on an extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and CloudCannon starts asking for a credit card five days before that trial ends. Five actions shorten the extended *Free Trial* so that it ends five days later, which brings that request forward:

* Adding a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> to a *Site*.
* Turning on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/guest-access.yml">Guest Access</comp.GlossaryTerm> for a *Site*.
* Adding a new *Organization Member* to a *Permission Group*. The *Free Trial* shortens when that person accepts their invitation.
* Sharing a *Site* with someone outside the *Client Organization* using <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site-sharing.yml">Site Sharing</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
* Accepting a transfer that brings a *Custom Domain*, a *Site* with *Guest Access* turned on, or a *Site Sharing* member into the *Client Organization*.

Members of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, people who are already *Organization Members* of the *Client Organization*, and anyone added to the *Partner Permission Group* do not shorten the *Free Trial*. Once five days or fewer remain, these actions no longer change the trial end date.

Separately, if you change the *Client Organization's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> or turn on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm> before adding a credit card, CloudCannon asks for one at that point instead. For more information, please read our documentation on [what CloudCannon's Free Trial is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-cloudcannons-free-trial/) and [Free Trials in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/#partner-discounts-and-free-trials).

When you are ready for your client to start paying for their *Subscription Plan*, you can invite them to the *Client Organization* and give them the *Permissions* to manage its billing. These instructions add your client to *Default Permission Groups*, rather than to a *Custom Permission Group*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Default Permission Groups are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/) and [what Custom Permission Groups are](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/).

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Inviting your client to the *Client Organization* shortens the extended *Free Trial*. CloudCannon shows a *Confirm End of Trial* modal before the invitation goes out, and once your client accepts their invitation, the *Free Trial* ends five days later. Members of your *Partner Organization* are exempt, so inviting your own team does not shorten the trial. Invite your client once you have finished setting up their *Client Organization* and they are ready to begin paying for their <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
</comp.Notice>

How much access your client has depends on which <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> you invite them to. You have three options, from the most access to the least:

* **Owners** — Your client can perform the same actions you can, including managing <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization-member.yml">Organization Members</comp.GlossaryTerm> and deleting the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. This is a handover, and it does not change your *Partner Organization's* access: your team still reaches the *Client Organization* through the *Partner Permission Group*. For more information, please read our documentation on [handing over or detaching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).
* **Billing and one other Permission Group** — With *Billing* permissions, your client can manage the payment details for their *Organization*, and perform any actions allowed by their second *Permission Group*. Normally, this would be *Editors* or *Technical Editors*.
* **Billing on its own** — Your client can manage the payment details for their *Organization*, but cannot edit content or change any *Site* or *Organization* settings. Use this when your client only needs to pay for their *Subscription*.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Members of the *Billing Permission Group* can change the *Subscription Plan*, not only pay for it. Your client can upgrade or downgrade the *Client Organization* once they have this access.
</comp.Notice>

## Invite your client to their Client Organization

To invite your client to their *Client Organization*:

1. In the *Client Organization*, navigate to the *People* page under *Org Settings*.
2. Under the *Members* tab, click the *Add members* button. CloudCannon will open the *Invite Organization Members* modal.
3. In the *Email address* field, enter your client's email address.
4. In the *Groups* field, select the *Permission Groups* for the access level you chose.
5. Click the *Add member(s)* button.

CloudCannon will email your client an invitation to the *Client Organization*. If they do not have a CloudCannon account, CloudCannon will ask them to create one. Once they accept, they can add their own payment method, view invoices, and manage the *Subscription Plan* for their *Client Organization*, along with anything else the *Permission Groups* you selected allow.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  If your client is already an *Organization Member* of the *Client Organization*, add them to the *Billing Permission Group* instead of inviting them again. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding an Organization Member to a Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/add-an-organization-member-to-a-permission-group/).
</comp.Notice>

## What your client pays for

Your client's payment method covers the *Subscription Plan* for their *Client Organization* only. Members of your *Partner Organization* do not use a *Seat* in that *Subscription Plan*, so your team can keep working on the *Client Organization* at no cost to your client. For more information, please read our documentation on [paying for your Partner Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/#pay-for-your-partner-organization).

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Do not turn on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/guest-access.yml">Guest Access</comp.GlossaryTerm> for a client who already has an *Organization Member* account. Each <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> with *Guest Access* enabled uses one <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seat</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the *Subscription Plan*, so the same person uses two *Seats* and the *Client Organization* is billed for an extra *Seat*.
</comp.Notice>

To send invoices to your client while your own payment method covers the cost, or the other way around, please read our documentation on [adding billing contact information](/documentation/user-articles/add-billing-contact-information/) and [managing your payment methods](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-payment-methods/).
