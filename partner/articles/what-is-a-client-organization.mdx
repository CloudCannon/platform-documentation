A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> that a partner in the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/) manages on behalf of a client. The client gets their own team, billing, and login, while your Partner *Organization* keeps the access it needs to build and maintain their *Sites*.

Creating a *Client Organization* is one of two ways to structure client work in the Partner Program; the other is to keep a client's *Sites* in your own Partner *Organization*. For a comparison of the two, please read our documentation on [structuring your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/).

## Separate from your Partner Organization

A *Client Organization* is a full *Organization* in its own right. Like any *Organization*, it has its own team, its own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> and invoice, its own settings, and its own login. This keeps each client isolated from your other clients and from your Partner *Organization*.

Because it's separate, the client can have their own users and log in to work on their *Sites* directly, and they can hold their own billing relationship with CloudCannon.

## Managed by your team

Although a *Client Organization* is separate, your Partner *Organization* manages it. When a *Client Organization* is created, CloudCannon adds a *Partner* *Permission Group* to it that gives your Partner *Organization*'s *Owners* access. You can add the rest of your team to any *Client Organization* you manage, and members of your Partner *Organization* don't count toward its seat limit, so you never pay twice for the same seat. For how this access works, and who owns a new *Client Organization*, please read our documentation on [how partner access and permissions work](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-access-and-permissions/).

## Start on a six-month Free Trial

A new *Client Organization* starts on a six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> of the *Subscription Plan* you choose. This gives you time to build the client's *Sites* and hand them over before billing begins.

## Rewards for your client work

Every active *Client Organization* you manage earns you <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which move you up through partner tiers and unlock benefits. For details, please read our documentation on [how Partner Points, tiers, and benefits work](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-rewards-and-points/).

## Working with Client Organizations

* To create one, please read our documentation on [creating a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/).
* To work across the ones you manage, please read our documentation on [managing your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/).
* To hand one over to a client or detach it, please read our documentation on [handing over or detaching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  *Client Organizations* and *Client Sharing* are different features. A *Client Organization* is a full, separate *Organization* with its own team and billing. *Client Sharing* lets you share a single *Site* with someone who doesn't have a CloudCannon account, using a *Site* password. For occasional edits to one *Site*, please read our documentation on [what Client Sharing is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-client-sharing/).
</comp.Notice>
