A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> that you manage on behalf of a client. Like other *Organizations*, it gets its own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> and invoices, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm>, *Projects*, *Sites*, *Custom Domains*, DAMs, and Inboxes. For everything an *Organization* contains, please read our documentation on [what an Organization is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-an-organization/).

In most cases, *Organizations* are entirely independent from one another. However, a *Client Organization* is always linked to a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, whose *Owners* can access it. For how this access works, and how to give the rest of your team access, please read our documentation on [how Partners access their Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/how-do-partners-access-their-client-organizations/).

You can create a *Client Organization* if you are a member of the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/). *Client Organizations* also benefit from an extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> of the *Subscription Plan* you choose. This gives you time to build the client's *Sites* and hand them over before billing begins. For more information, please read our documentation on [creating a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/) and [best practices for structuring client Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/best-practices-for-structuring-client-sites/).

Four actions shorten that six-month trial to three days. For the full list, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).

You can view the *Client Organizations* managed by your *Partner Organization* by clicking the *Clients* link in your *App Sidebar*, opening the *Client Organizations Browser*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Organization-Browser" alt="A screenshot of the Client Organizations Browser in a Partner Organization shows four Client Organizations as cards, each listing its Sites and domains counts, Subscription Plan, and issue status, with the New Client button above the list and the Clients link in the App Sidebar." title="The Client Organizations Browser" type="screenshot"/>

You can always tell which *Organization* you are working in from the top of the *App Sidebar*. Inside a *Client Organization*, its *Card* sits below a *Card* for your *Partner Organization*, and clicking it opens a *Context Menu* headed *Switch Client*. Inside your *Partner Organization*, you see only your own *Card*, alongside the *Clients* link to the *Client Organizations Browser*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-App-Sidebar-Client-Org-Header-Element" alt="A screenshot of the App Sidebar shows the Switch Client menu open, listing the partner's Client Organizations, with a Partner Organization card above the current Client Organization card." title="The Switch Client menu inside a Client Organization" type="ui-snippet"/>

To move to another *Client Organization*, click the *Card* for your current *Organization* and choose a client from the *Context Menu*. To return to your *Partner Organization*, click its *Card* above. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

Every *Client Organization* also has an *Organization Home* page, showing you a summary of what's going on in the *Organization*, including all the resources it contains. You can open it by clicking the *Home* link in the *App Sidebar* while you are inside that *Client Organization*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-Client-Org" alt="A screenshot of a Client Organization's Organization Home page shows its Summary tab with health checks, unsaved changes, recent activity, and usage, and a Partner Organization card above the Client Organization card in the App Sidebar." title="A Client Organization's Organization Home page" type="screenshot"/>

For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/).
