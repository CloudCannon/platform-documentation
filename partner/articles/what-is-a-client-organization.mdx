A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> that you manage on behalf of a client. Just like other *Organizations*, it gets its own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> and invoices, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm>, *Projects*, *Sites*, *Custom Domains*, DAMs, and Inboxes. For everything an *Organization* contains, please read our documentation on [what an Organization is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-an-organization/).

In most cases, *Organizations* are entirely independent from one another. However, a *Client Organization* is always linked to a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, whose *Owners* can access it. For how this access works, and how to give the rest of your team access, please read our documentation on [how partner access and permissions work](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-access-and-permissions/).

You can create a *Client Organization* if you are a member of the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/). *Client Organizations* also benefit from an extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> of the *Subscription Plan* you choose. This gives you time to build the client's *Sites* and hand them over before billing begins. For more information, please read our documentation on [creating a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/) and [best practices for structuring your client Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/best-practices-for-structuring-client-sites/).

You can view all the *Client Organizations* managed by your *Partner Organization* by clicking the *Clients* link in your *App Sidebar*, opening the *Client Organizations Browser*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Organization-Browser" alt="A screenshot of the Client Organizations Browser shows each Client Organization as a card listing its Sites, domains, and Subscription Plan." title="The Client Organizations Browser" type="screenshot"/>

Every *Client Organization* also has an *Organization Home* page, showing you a summary of what's going on in the *Organization*, including all the resources it contains. You can open it by clicking *Home* in the *App Sidebar*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-Client-Org" alt="A screenshot of a Client Organization's Organization Home page shows a summary of its resources, with a Partner Organization card above the Client Organization card in the App Sidebar." title="A Client Organization's Organization Home page" type="screenshot"/>

For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/).

## Switching between your Partner and Client Organizations

You can always tell which *Organization* you are working in from the top of the *App Sidebar*. Inside a *Client Organization*, its card sits below a card for your *Partner Organization*, and clicking it opens a menu labeled *Switch Client*. Inside your *Partner Organization*, you see only your own card, alongside the *Clients* link to the *Client Organizations Browser*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-App-Sidebar-Client-Org-Header-Element" alt="A screenshot of the App Sidebar shows the Switch Client menu open, listing the partner's Client Organizations, with a Partner Organization card above the current Client Organization card." title="The Switch Client menu inside a Client Organization" type="ui-snippet"/>

To move to another *Client Organization*, click your current *Organization* card and choose from the *Switch Client* menu. To return to your *Partner Organization*, click its card above. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).
