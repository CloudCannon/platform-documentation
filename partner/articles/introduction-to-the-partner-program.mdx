The CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-program.yml">Partner Program</comp.GlossaryTerm> is for agencies and freelancers who design and develop websites for clients through CloudCannon. Joining the *Partner Program* unlocks specialized CloudCannon UI for managing your clients' content, and provides rewards that grow as you bring more clients onto CloudCannon.

Articles in this section are useful for the developers and agency owners who set up and run client work in the *Partner Program*.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Thinking about joining the *Partner Program*? Contact us from the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> page.
</comp.Notice>

In the Partner Program section of our Partner documentation, we cover:

* [What is the CloudCannon Partner Program?](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/) — Learn what the program offers and how your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> works as your dashboard.
* [What are Partner Points and Rewards?](/documentation/partner-articles/what-are-partner-points-and-rewards/) — Learn how <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm>, tiers, and benefits grow with your client work.
* [Best practices for structuring client Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/best-practices-for-structuring-client-sites/) — Learn whether each client belongs in your own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> or a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
* [How does billing work in the Partner Program?](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/) — Learn who pays for client work, and how.
