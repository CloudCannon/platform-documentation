The CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-program.yml">Partner Program</comp.GlossaryTerm> is for agencies and freelancers who design and develop websites through CloudCannon. Joining the *Partner Program* unlocks specialized CloudCannon UI for managing your clients' content, and provides rewards as you bring more clients onto CloudCannon.
