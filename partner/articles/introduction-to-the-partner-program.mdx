The CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-program.yml">Partner Program</comp.GlossaryTerm> is for agencies and freelancers who design and develop websites for clients through CloudCannon. Joining the *Partner Program* unlocks specialized CloudCannon UI for managing your clients' content, and provides rewards that grow as you bring more clients onto CloudCannon.

Articles in this section are useful for the developers and agency owners who set up and run client work in the *Partner Program*.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Thinking about joining the *Partner Program*? Contact us from the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> page.
</comp.Notice>

In the Partner Program section of our Partner documentation, we cover:

* The Partner Program — Learn what the program offers and how your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> works as your dashboard.
* Partner Rewards — Learn how <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm>, tiers, and benefits grow with your client work.
* Billing — Learn who pays for client work, and how.
* Structuring client work — Learn whether each client belongs in your own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> or a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

Let's briefly introduce these topics.

## The Partner Program

When you join, your own *Organization* becomes a *Partner Organization*, the dashboard for all your client work. Its reorganized UI adds a *Client Organizations Browser* for clients you run as separate *Organizations* and a *Partner Rewards* page for tracking your progress, alongside your own *Sites*, *Projects*, and settings.

For a more in-depth explanation of the Partner Program, please read our documentation:

* [What is the CloudCannon Partner Program?](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/)

## Partner Rewards

The more client work you run on CloudCannon, the more *Partner Points* you earn. Points move you up four partner tiers, from Bronze to Platinum, each unlocking more benefits such as discounts, priority support, and recognition.

For a more in-depth explanation of Partner Rewards, please read our documentation:

* [What are Partner Points and Rewards?](/documentation/partner-articles/what-are-partner-points-and-rewards/)

## Billing

You choose who pays for each client's work. You can keep a client's *Sites* on your own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> and invoice, or give them a separate *Client Organization* with its own *Subscription Plan* billed to the client. Members of your *Partner Organization* can join any *Client Organization* at no extra cost.

For a more in-depth explanation of billing in the Partner Program, please read our documentation:

* [How does billing work in the Partner Program?](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/)

## Structuring client work

You decide where each client's *Sites* live: in your own *Partner Organization* alongside your other clients, or in a separate *Client Organization* the client owns. CloudCannon supports both, and you can mix them across clients or move a client from one to the other later.

For a more in-depth explanation of structuring client work, please read our documentation:

* [Best practices for structuring client Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/best-practices-for-structuring-client-sites/)
