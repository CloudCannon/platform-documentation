When you build *Sites* for clients in CloudCannon, you decide where each client's *Sites* live. There are two approaches, and CloudCannon has no preference between them:

* Keep a client's *Sites* in your own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, alongside your other clients.
* Give the client their own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> through the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/).

You can use either approach, combine them across different clients, and move a client from one to the other later by transferring their *Sites*. The right choice depends on what each client needs.

## Keep client Sites in your Organization

<comp.Mermaid
  alt="A diagram shows one Organization containing a Project of Sites for each client."
  chart={`graph TD
    O["Your Organization"] --> PA["Project: Client A"]
    O["Your Organization"] --> PB["Project: Client B"]
    PA --> SA["Site"]
    PB --> SB["Site"]
  `}
/>

Add a client's *Sites* directly inside your own *Organization*, alongside the *Sites* you manage for other clients. You can group a client's *Sites* into a *Project* to keep them together.

* Everything stays in one place: one team, one billing account, and one *Organization Home* dashboard for all your clients.
* Client *Sites* use your *Organization*'s existing <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so taking on a client doesn't require a new subscription.
* Your team members get immediate access, and *Sites* are quick to set up.
* You manage the work; the client doesn't get their own login or invoice.

This approach suits smaller clients or proof-of-concept work. You create these *Sites* the same way you create any *Site*, from the *Sites Browser*.

If a client needs to edit or review a *Site* you keep in your *Organization*, you have a few ways to give them access, each with a different cost and identity:

* *Client Sharing* lets someone edit a single *Site* using a *Site* password, without a CloudCannon account. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Client Sharing is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-client-sharing/).
* *Site Sharing* gives a client with their own CloudCannon account access to a single *Site*. For more information, please read our documentation on [sharing a Site with Site Sharing](/documentation/user-articles/share-a-site-with-site-sharing/).
* A paid seat in your *Organization* makes the client a full team member with access across your *Organization*, subject to their *Permission Group*.

For an overview, please read our documentation on [an introduction to sharing](/documentation/user-articles/introduction-to-sharing/).

## Give each client their own Client Organization

<comp.Mermaid
  alt="A diagram shows a Partner Organization managing separate Client Organizations, each containing its own Sites."
  chart={`graph TD
    P["Your Partner Organization"] -->|Manages| CA["Client Organization A"]
    P -->|Manages| CB["Client Organization B"]
    CA --> SA["Site"]
    CB --> SB["Site"]
  `}
/>

Create a separate *Client Organization* for a client. *Client Organizations* are part of the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/), where your Partner *Organization* manages each *Client Organization* while the client uses it. For a full explanation, please read our documentation on [what a Client Organization is](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/).

* The client gets their own *Organization*, team, and billing, isolated from your other clients.
* Your team comes for free. Members of your Partner *Organization* can be added to any *Client Organization* at no extra cost, so you never pay twice for the same seat.
* You can brand each *Client Organization* with the client's own logo and color, so the editing experience feels like theirs. For more information, please read our documentation on [customizing your Organization branding](/documentation/user-articles/customize-your-organization-branding/).
* The client can have their own users and invoices, and you can hand the *Organization* over to them, or let them manage it themselves, when the time comes.

You create and manage these from the *Client Organizations Browser*. For the steps, please read our documentation on [creating a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/).

## How to choose

There's no single right answer, and you don't have to use the same approach for every client. Match the setup to what each client needs:

* Keep *Sites* in your *Organization* when you want everything in one place and you manage the work end to end.
* Create a *Client Organization* when the client needs their own users, billing, or login, or when you expect to hand the work over to them later.

You can move a client into their own *Client Organization* at any time by [transferring their Sites](/documentation/user-articles/transfer-a-site-between-organizations/), and either approach counts toward your [Partner Rewards](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-rewards-and-points/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  *Client Organizations* and *Client Sharing* are different features. *Client Sharing* lets you share a single *Site* with someone who doesn't have a CloudCannon account, using a *Site* password. A *Client Organization* is a full, separate *Organization* with its own team and billing. For occasional edits to one *Site*, please read our documentation on [what Client Sharing is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-client-sharing/).
</comp.Notice>
