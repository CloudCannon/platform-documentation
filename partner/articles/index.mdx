Welcome to CloudCannon's Partner Documentation. These articles are for web design and development agencies in the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a>.

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner.yml">Partner</comp.GlossaryTerm> is anyone who belongs to a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> in CloudCannon, building and maintaining *Sites* for external clients. Partners are usually <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/developer.yml">Developers</comp.GlossaryTerm> who configure CloudCannon to create a tailored editing experience for their clients (please see our [Developer Articles](/documentation/developer-articles/)), and may also be <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/u/user.yml">Users</comp.GlossaryTerm> who edit content (please see our [User Articles](/documentation/user-articles/)).

As a Partner, you manage your client's code and content in CloudCannon, as well as administrative tasks like access and billing. The articles in this section explain how to structure client work, create and manage <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm>, earn <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and hand a *Client Organization* over when an engagement ends.

If you need any more help with your CloudCannon websites, please don't hesitate to contact our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>.

## The Partner Program

Articles in the [Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/introduction-to-the-partner-program/) section cover the features and benefits of CloudCannon's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-program.yml">Partner Program</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Learn what the *Partner Program* is, how to progress up the tiers and earn rewards, how to structure your clients' content into *Sites* in your own *Organization* or a separate *Client Organization*, and who pays for a client's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. These articles are useful for members of the *Partner Program* and anyone who is considering joining.

## Client Organizations

Articles in the [Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/introduction-to-client-organizations/) section cover what a *Client Organization* is, how to create and manage one, who on your team can reach it and who owns it, and how to hand a *Client Organization* over when an engagement ends. These articles are relevant to anyone who directly manages client content in CloudCannon.
