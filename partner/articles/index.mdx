Welcome to CloudCannon's Partner Documentation. These articles are for web design and development agencies in the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a>, who build and maintain websites for external clients.

The Partner Program lets you manage each client's CloudCannon account while the client keeps their own team, billing, and login. These articles explain how to structure client work, create and manage <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm>, earn <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and hand a *Client Organization* over when an engagement ends.

If you configure CloudCannon itself — *Schemas*, layouts, and `cloudcannon.config.yml` — see our [Developer Articles](/documentation/developer-articles/). For the content-editing experience your clients see, see our [User Articles](/documentation/user-articles/).

## The Partner Program

Start with [an overview of the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/cloudcannon-partner-program/) to see what it offers and how your Partner *Organization* works as your dashboard. From there, read about [structuring your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/) to decide whether each client belongs in your own *Organization* or a separate *Client Organization*, and, when you run many *Sites*, [structuring Sites across Organizations and Projects](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-sites-across-organizations-and-projects/). To understand how your client work is rewarded, read about [how Partner Points, tiers, and benefits work](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-rewards-and-points/).

## Client Organizations

When you're ready to onboard a client, read about [creating a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/) and [managing your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/) day to day. To learn who on your team can reach a *Client Organization*, who owns it, and what the client can see, read about [how partner access and permissions work](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-access-and-permissions/). When an engagement ends, [handing over or detaching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/) covers transferring control to the client.

## Billing

To see who pays for a client's work across the two structuring models, and how partner discounts apply, read about [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/billing-in-the-partner-program/).
