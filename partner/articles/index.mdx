Welcome to CloudCannon's Partner Documentation. These articles are for web design and development agencies in the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a>, who build and maintain websites for external clients.

The Partner Program lets you manage each client's CloudCannon account while the client keeps their own team, billing, and login. These articles explain how to structure client work, create and manage *Client Organizations*, earn *Partner Points*, and hand a *Client Organization* over when an engagement ends.

If you configure CloudCannon itself — schemas, layouts, and `cloudcannon.config.yml` — see our [Developer Articles](/documentation/developer-articles/). For the content-editing experience your clients see, see our [User Articles](/documentation/user-articles/).

## Getting started

Start with [The CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/cloudcannon-partner-program/) for an overview of what the program offers and how *Client Organizations* work. Then read [Choosing how to structure your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/) to decide whether each client belongs in your own *Organization* or in a separate *Client Organization*.

## Client Organizations

When you're ready to onboard a client, [Create a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/) and [Manage your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/) walk through the day-to-day of running client accounts. When an engagement ends, [Hand over or detach a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/) covers transferring control to the client.

## Rewards

The more client work you run on CloudCannon, the more you earn. [Partner Rewards and Points](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-rewards-and-points/) explains how *Partner Points*, tiers, and benefits work.
