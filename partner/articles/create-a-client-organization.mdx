<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Only members of the *Owners* [Default Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/) in your *Partner Organization* can create a *Client Organization*. If you are not in the *Owners* group, the *New Client* button still appears, but is disabled.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> manages while your client keeps their own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm>. For a full explanation, please read our documentation on [what a Client Organization is](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/).

CloudCannon puts a new *Client Organization* on the *Standard Plan* by default, so you only need to change it when another *Subscription Plan* suits your client better. 

Partners can also choose the *Lite Plan*, a low-cost, pay-as-you-go option that does not appear for other *Organizations*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Partner Points and Rewards are](/documentation/partner-articles/what-are-partner-points-and-rewards/).

Before we begin, this article assumes your *Organization* is enrolled in the CloudCannon Partner Program. For more information, please read our documentation on [what the CloudCannon Partner Program is](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  If you are creating a new *Organization* that is not connected to your *Partner Organization*, we recommend [creating a new Organization](/documentation/user-articles/create-a-new-organization/) instead.
</comp.Notice>

When you create a *Client Organization*, use the name of your client's business. To create a new *Client Organization*:

1. In your *Partner Organization*, click the *Clients* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Client Organizations Browser*.
2. Click the *New Client* button. CloudCannon will open the *Create a New Client Organization* page.
3. Enter a name in the *Client Organization Name* field.
4. Under *Subscription Plan*, click the *Change Subscription Plan* button. CloudCannon will open the *Select a plan* modal.
5. Click the *Select* button on the *Subscription Plan* that best suits your client. CloudCannon will close the modal and show your choice on the *Create a New Client Organization* page.
6. Click the *Create Client Organization* button.

CloudCannon will create the *Client Organization*, add you to its *Owners* *Permission Group*, and open its *Organization Home* page, ready for you to add the client's first *Site*. CloudCannon also adds a *Partner* *Permission Group* to the *Client Organization*, holding the *Owners* of your *Partner Organization*. For more information, please read our documentation on [how Partners access their Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/how-do-partners-access-their-client-organizations/).

Alternatively, you can also create a *Client Organization* from your *Organization Home* page, by clicking the *Create a Client* icon on the *Clients* card.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Every *Client Organization* you create starts on an extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so you can take your time developing and pay when your client is ready to go live. For more information, please read our documentation on [what CloudCannon's free trial is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-cloudcannons-free-trial/).
</comp.Notice>
