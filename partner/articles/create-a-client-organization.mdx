<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* [Default Permission Group](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/) in your Partner *Organization* can create a *Client Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

A *Client Organization* is a separate *Organization* your Partner *Organization* manages while your client keeps their own team, billing, and login. For a full explanation, please read our documentation on [what a Client Organization is](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/).

Creating a *Client Organization* is one of two ways to structure client work. To decide whether a *Client Organization* is the right fit, or whether to keep the client's *Sites* in your own *Organization* instead, please read our documentation on [structuring your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/best-practices-for-structuring-client-sites/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  *Client Organizations* and *Client Sharing* are two different features. To share a single *Site* with someone who doesn't have a CloudCannon account, please read our documentation on [what Client Sharing is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-client-sharing/) instead.
</comp.Notice>

To create a *Client Organization*, you need a name for it and a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> to put it on. The *Client Organization* starts on a six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so you can take your time developing and pay when your client is ready to go live.

## Create a Client Organization

To create a *Client Organization*:

1. In your Partner *Organization*, click *Clients* in the *App Sidebar* to open the *Client Organizations Browser*.
2. Click *New Client*.
3. On the *Create a New Client Organization* page, enter a name in the *Client Organization Name* field.
4. Under *Subscription Plan*, click *Change plan* and choose the one that best suits your client.
5. Choose whether new *Sites* should use the Unified Configuration file format (see below).
6. Click *Create Client Organization*.

CloudCannon creates the *Client Organization* and opens a page for you to create its first *Site*.

## Choosing Unified Configuration

When you create a *Client Organization*, you can choose whether its new *Sites* use the Unified Configuration file format:

* If your Partner *Organization* uses website templates that you have not yet migrated to Unified Configuration, leave this option off.
* Otherwise, we recommend turning it on. Unified Configuration lets editors work on *Sites* without waiting for a successful build, and lets you edit data and configuration live across all *Sites* in the *Organization*.

For more information, read our [guide on migrating to Unified Configuration](/documentation/developer-guides/unified-configuration-migration-guide/).
