A *Client Organization* is a separate *Organization* that your Partner *Organization* manages but that your client uses and pays for. Web design and development studios in the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/cloudcannon-partner-program/) commonly use them to give each client their own team, billing, and login.

Creating a *Client Organization* is one of two ways to structure client work. To decide whether a *Client Organization* is the right fit, or whether to keep the client's *Sites* in your own *Organization* instead, read [Choosing how to structure your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  *Client Organizations* and *Client Sharing* are two different features. To share a single *Site* with someone who doesn't have a CloudCannon account, read [What is Client Sharing?](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-client-sharing/) instead.
</comp.Notice>

To create a *Client Organization*, you need a name for it and a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> to put it on. The *Client Organization* starts on a six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so you can take your time developing and pay when your client is ready to go live. If you need longer, contact CloudCannon support.

## Create a Client Organization

1. In your Partner *Organization*, open the *Client Organizations Browser* from the *App Sidebar*.
2. Click *New Client*.
3. On the *Create a New Client Organization* page, enter a name in the *Client Organization Name* field.
4. Under *Subscription Plan*, click *Change plan* and choose the one that best suits your client.
5. Choose whether new *Sites* should use the Unified Configuration file format (see below).
6. Click *Create client organization*.

CloudCannon creates the *Client Organization* and opens a page for you to create its first *Site*.

## Choosing Unified Configuration

When you create a *Client Organization*, you can choose whether its new *Sites* use the Unified Configuration file format:

* If your Partner *Organization* uses website templates that you have **not** yet migrated to Unified Configuration, leave this option off.
* Otherwise, we recommend turning it on. Unified Configuration lets editors work on *Sites* without waiting for a successful build, and lets you edit data and configuration live across all *Sites* in the *Organization*.

For more information, read our [guide on migrating to Unified Configuration](/documentation/developer-guides/unified-configuration-migration-guide/).
