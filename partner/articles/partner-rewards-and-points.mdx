If you're in the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/cloudcannon-partner-program/), your partnership is measured in <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm>. The more client work you run on CloudCannon, the more you earn, and points move you through partner tiers that unlock benefits.

## How you earn points

You earn *Partner Points* for each active client you bring onto CloudCannon. Both ways of structuring client work count toward your total: client *Sites* kept in your Partner *Organization*, and separate [Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-a-client-organization/). The larger a client's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> and the more paid seats they add, the more points that client earns you.

You can also earn extra points that CloudCannon awards directly, for example for contributing to the CloudCannon blog or community, teaming up on co-marketing, or taking part in milestones and campaigns. There's no form to submit these; CloudCannon adds them to your total.

## Partner tiers and benefits

As your points grow, your partnership moves up through four tiers, from Bronze to Silver, Gold, and Platinum. Each tier keeps the benefits of the ones below it and unlocks more.

Bronze is where every partner starts. It includes the partner features themselves: your Partner *Organization* dashboard, the ability to run *Client Organizations* with their own *Partner* *Permission Group*, the extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trials</comp.GlossaryTerm> you get on the *Client Organizations* you create, and access to the *Lite Plan*, a low-cost, pay-as-you-go *Subscription Plan*.

Climbing the higher tiers adds benefits such as:

* A partner discount, available from Silver up and growing with your tier to a maximum of 20%, which you can apply to your own *Organization* or any *Client Organization* you manage.
* Recognition, including a listing in the CloudCannon Expert Directory and an Expert badge for your own site.
* Priority support and a dedicated Slack channel.
* Visibility into CloudCannon's product roadmap and the ability to request features.
* Growth opportunities such as client referrals, co-marketing, and early access to beta features.

For the exact points needed to reach each tier, see the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> page. Your current tier, your running points total, and the points needed to reach the next tier are always shown on the *Partner Rewards* page.

## How your tier changes over time

Your tier follows your *Partner Points* total, and that total reflects your current active client work. As you bring on more clients, or as their *Subscription Plans* and paid seats grow, your points rise. If a *Client Organization* becomes inactive, its points stop counting, so your total can fall as well as rise. The *Partner Rewards* page always shows where you stand.

## Your Expert badge

From the Silver tier up, you can download an Expert badge to display on your own site, showing your CloudCannon partnership.

## Track your rewards

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Partner-Rewards-Page" title="The Partner Rewards page" alt="A screenshot of the Partner Rewards page in CloudCannon, showing the partner points total, the current tier, unlocked benefits, and the next tier." type="screenshot" />

The *Partner Rewards* page in your Partner *Organization* is the home base for everything above. If you're an *Owner* of your Partner *Organization*, open it from *Rewards* in the *App Sidebar*. From there you can:

* See your running points total and a breakdown by client.
* View your current tier and how many points remain until the next one.
* Download your Expert badge, available from the Silver tier up.
* Review the benefits you've unlocked and what's coming next.
