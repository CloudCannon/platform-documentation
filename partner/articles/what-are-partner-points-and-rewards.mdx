Your progression in [CloudCannon's Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/) is measured in <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm>. As you expand your client work on CloudCannon, you will earn more points and move up the tiers of the *Partner Program* to unlock more rewards.

## Earning Partner Points

The easiest way to earn *Partner Points* is by having active clients on CloudCannon. You earn points for every client you onboard and the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> they pay for. The bigger a client's *Subscription Plan* and the more paid seats and other *Add-Ons* they take on, the more points they earn you. You can also earn extra points that CloudCannon awards directly, for example for contributing to the CloudCannon blog or community, teaming up on co-marketing, or taking part in milestones and campaigns.

Clients in an unpaid or non-paying state, such as those on a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, do not contribute to your *Organization's Partner Points*.

You can track your *Partner Points* from the *Partner Rewards* page. If you're an *Owner* of your *Partner Organization*, you can open it from the *Rewards* link in the *App Sidebar*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Partner-Rewards-Page" title="The Partner Rewards page" alt="A screenshot of the Partner Rewards page in CloudCannon, showing the partner points total, the current tier, unlocked benefits, and the next tier." type="screenshot" />

On the *Partner Rewards* page, you can view:

* Your *Partner Points* total and a breakdown by client.
* Your current tier in the *Partner Program* and how many points remain until the next one.
* The benefits you've unlocked and what's coming next.
* Your *Expert Badge*, available for download from the Silver tier up.

To see where your points come from, select the *Your Partner Points* card to open the *Points Breakdown*, which lists each client and the points they earn you, along with any points CloudCannon has awarded you directly.

## Partner Tiers and Rewards

As your *Partner Points* grow, your partnership moves up through four tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum. Each tier keeps the benefits of the ones below it and unlocks more.

Every *Partner Organization* starts in the Bronze tier. This tier includes updated UI for managing clients, the ability to create *Client Organizations* with their own *Partner Permission Group*, an extended six-month *Free Trial* for any *Client Organizations* you create, access to the *Lite Plan*, a low-cost, pay-as-you-go *Subscription Plan*, and an onboarding session to help you get set up.

Climbing the higher tiers adds benefits such as:

* A partner discount, available from Silver up and growing with your tier to a maximum of 20%, which you can apply to your own *Organization* or any *Client Organization* you manage.
* Recognition, including a listing in the CloudCannon Experts Directory and an *Expert Badge* for your own site.
* Priority support and a dedicated Slack channel.
* Visibility into CloudCannon's product roadmap.
* Growth opportunities such as client referrals, co-marketing, and early access to beta features.

For the exact points needed to reach each tier, see the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> page.
