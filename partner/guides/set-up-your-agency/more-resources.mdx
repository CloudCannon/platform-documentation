Congratulations! 🎉

By following each step in this guide, you have chosen a structure for your client work, put your *Partner Organization* on a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> that covers your whole team, set up your first client with their own login, and seen how to move that client between *Organizations* later. Every client after this one follows the same path, and you can make a different structure or plan choice each time.

Thanks for finishing the *Set up your agency* guide! If you have any questions, please reach out to our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> or check out the <a href="https://community.cloudcannon.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CloudCannon Community</a>. We're always here to help.

## Earn more as you take on clients

Every paying client moves you up the tiers of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-program.yml">Partner Program</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which unlock a discount, an Experts Directory listing, priority support, and more. These articles cover how that works and what you unlock.

* [What are Partner Points and Rewards?](/documentation/partner-articles/what-are-partner-points-and-rewards/) — Learn how <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm> are earned and lost, what each tier unlocks, and how to claim your partner discount.
* [What is the CloudCannon Partner Program?](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/) — Learn what changes in the app once you're a partner, and where to find your clients and rewards.

## Run several clients at once

Your first client is the hardest one. These articles cover the decisions that come up as your portfolio grows, and the day-to-day of keeping an eye on all of it.

* [Manage your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/) — Learn how to review, switch between, and keep track of every client you manage.
* [Best practices for structuring client Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/best-practices-for-structuring-client-sites/) — Compare the two structures in more depth, and move a client from one to the other.
* [How do Partners access their Client Organizations?](/documentation/partner-articles/how-do-partners-access-their-client-organizations/) — Learn how the *Partner Permission Group* works, and how to bring the rest of your team onto a client's content.
* [How does billing work in the Partner Program?](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/) — Learn who pays for what across your *Partner Organization* and every client.

## When a client relationship changes

Most partner relationships are ongoing, and there's nothing you need to do to keep one going. When an engagement does change shape, these articles cover your options.

* [Let your client pay for their Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/let-your-client-pay-for-their-client-organization/) — Learn how to hand your client their billing without handing over control of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
* [Hand over or detach a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/) — Learn how to give a client full control, and how to end your management of their *Organization* entirely.

## Build more into your clients' Sites

Your clients' *Sites* are ordinary CloudCannon *Sites*, so every developer guide applies to them. These are the ones agencies reach for most.

* [Getting Started with CloudCannon](/documentation/developer-guides/getting-started-with-cloudcannon/) — Learn how to connect a website, write a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/configuration-files.yml">Configuration File</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and set up editing for a client who doesn't write code.
* [Set up Visual Editing](/documentation/developer-guides/set-up-visual-editing/) — Learn how to let a client edit their pages in a live preview rather than a form.
* [Subscribe to CloudCannon](/documentation/user-guides/subscribe-to-cloudcannon/) — Learn how payment methods, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and invoices work, for any *Organization* you or your clients pay for.
