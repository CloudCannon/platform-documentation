In the third step of this guide, we covered the benefits of storing client *Sites* in a dedicated *Client Organization* versus in your agency's *Organization*. You don't have to use the same approach for every client.

## Create a Client Organization

If you want to store client *Sites* on a *Client Organization*, first you must create one. If you want to store your client *Sites* directly in your agency *Organization*, you can skip this section of the guide.

Click the *Clients* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm> to open your *Client Organizations Browser*, then click the *New Client* button in the top right. 

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  The *New Client* button is disabled if you are not in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/owners.yml">Owners Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which requires an *Owner* to add you to it.
</comp.Notice>

On the *Create a New Client Organization* page, enter the name for the *Client Organization*. We recommend the name of your client's business. By default, CloudCannon will select the *Standard Plan* on a monthly *Billing Period* for your *Client Organization*, but you can update that before you create the *Organization*.

### Choose a Subscription Plan for your Client Organization

Click the *Change Subscription Plan* button to review your *Subscription Plan* and *Billing Period* options.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Create-Client-Organization-Select-Plan-Modal-Element" alt="A screenshot of the Select a plan modal on the Create a New Client Organization page shows the Lite, Standard, Team, and Enterprise Plans side by side, with a Partner Plan label on the Lite Plan and a Monthly and Yearly toggle above them." title="The Select a plan modal" type="ui-snippet"/>

CloudCannon offers the same *Subscription Plans* to a *Client Organization* as to your *Partner Organization*. What differs is the requirements: your agency may only need access to CloudCannon, whereas a client may run their business through their website. The key resources for you to consider are how many *Seats*, *Custom Domains*, *Hosting Bandwidth*, and *Build Time* your client may need.

As with the *Subscription Plan* for your *Partner Organization*, you can pay for your *Subscription Plan* on a *Monthly* or *Yearly Billing Period*, with a discounted price if your choose to pay annually on most CloudCannon *Subscription Plans*. Additionally, you can change your client's *Subscription Plan* or *Billing Period* at any time after you create it. For more information, please read our documentation on [updating your Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-subscription-plan/) and [updating your Billing Period](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-billing-period/).

Whichever you pick, you choose separately whether CloudCannon charge your card or your client's. We'll come back to that when you give your client access.

## Extend a Subscription Plan

Choosing a *Subscription Plan* with the correct amount of resources is important, but clients may not fall cleanly into a single *Subscription Plan*. Whether your client *Sites* are on a *Client Organization* or your own agency *Organization*, you can extend any *Subscription Plan* with <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm>. These can bridge the gap if you need more resources than a *Subscription Plan* provides, but don't need the features of the next one up.

*Add-Ons* allow you to purchase additional *Seats* or *Custom Domains*, while enabling *Overages* removes any *Subscription Plan* limits on *Build Time* or *Hosting Bandwidth*, instead invoicing you for the amount an *Organization* uses each month rather than capping usage.

When you reach the limit on your *Build Time* or *Hosting Bandwidth* usage, CloudCannon will prevent more *Site* builds or take websites offline for the rest of the month. Because *Subscription Plan* resources are used by every *Site* in an *Organization*, reaching your resource limit due to activity on one *Site* will affect all *Sites* on that *Organization*. If you store multiple client *Sites* on your agency *Organization*, that means one client's activity can risk downtime for other client *Sites*.

For this reason, we highly recommend enabling *Overages* even if you don't need more resources than a *Subscription Plan* provides. We'll come back to that in the next step of this guide.

## Create a client Site

Your client's website becomes a *Site* in whichever *Organization* you build it in, so check you are in the right one before you start. A *Site* in a *Client Organization* draws on that client's *Seats*, *Build Time*, and *Hosting Bandwidth*, and bills to whichever card is attached to that *Organization*. A *Site* in your agency *Organization* draws on yours, shared with every other client you keep there.

To build in a *Client Organization*, click the *Clients* link in the *App Sidebar*, then click your client's card in the *Client Organizations Browser*. The *App Sidebar* will show your client's name below your agency's, so you can tell which *Organization* you are working in at a glance. To build in your agency *Organization*, stay where you are.

From either *Organization*, creating a *Site*, connecting a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and setting up editing work the same way as anywhere else in CloudCannon, so we won't repeat them here. On the *Create a Site* page, you choose whether CloudCannon syncs your client's website files from a Git provider, your local storage, or a CloudCannon template.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Create-A-Site-Page" alt="A screenshot of the Create a Site page shows a File source dropdown for choosing a Git provider, local storage, or a CloudCannon template, above the Create Site and start syncing button." title="The Create a Site page" type="screenshot"/>

For all of it, including writing the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/configuration-files.yml">Configuration File</comp.GlossaryTerm> that turns a hand-built HTML website into something your client can edit without touching code, please read our documentation on [getting started with CloudCannon](/documentation/developer-guides/getting-started-with-cloudcannon/). Work through it in the *Organization* you picked, then come back here.
