In the third step of this guide, we covered the benefits of storing client <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> in a dedicated <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> versus in your agency's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You don't have to use the same approach for every client.

## Create a Client Organization

If you want to store client *Sites* on a *Client Organization*, first you must create one. If you want to store your client *Sites* directly in your agency *Organization*, you can skip this section of the guide.

Click the *Clients* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm> to open your *Client Organizations Browser*, then click the *New Client* button in the top right.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Organization-Browser" alt="A screenshot of the Client Organizations Browser in a Partner Organization shows four Client Organizations as cards, each listing its Sites and domains counts, Subscription Plan, and issue status." title="The Client Organizations Browser" type="screenshot"/>

On the *Create a New Client Organization* page, enter a name in the *Client Organization Name* field. We recommend the name of your client's business. By default, CloudCannon will select the *Standard Plan* on a monthly *Billing Period* for your *Client Organization*, but you can update that before you create the *Organization*.

### Choose a Subscription Plan for your Client Organization

Click the *Change Subscription Plan* button to review your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/billing-period.yml">Billing Period</comp.GlossaryTerm> options.

CloudCannon offers the same *Subscription Plans* to a *Client Organization* as to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. What differs is the requirements: your agency may only need access to CloudCannon, whereas a client may run their business through their website. The key resources for you to consider are how many <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm> your client may need, and how much <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm> they might use each month.

As with the *Subscription Plan* for your *Partner Organization*, you can pay for your *Subscription Plan* on a *Monthly* or *Yearly Billing Period*, with a discounted price on most CloudCannon *Subscription Plans* if you choose to pay annually. Additionally, you can change your client's *Subscription Plan* or *Billing Period* at any time after you create it. For more information, please read our documentation on [updating your Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-subscription-plan/) and [updating your Billing Period](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-billing-period/).

Whichever you pick, you choose separately whether CloudCannon charges your card or your client's. We'll come back to that when you give your client access.

Four actions shorten it to three days, including adding a *Custom Domain* and inviting your client. Adding your own team does not. ## Extend a Subscription Plan

Choosing a *Subscription Plan* with the correct amount of resources is important, but clients may not fall cleanly into a single *Subscription Plan*. Whether your client *Sites* are on a *Client Organization* or your own agency *Organization*, you can extend any *Subscription Plan* with <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm>. These can bridge the gap if you need more resources than a *Subscription Plan* provides, but don't need the features of the next one up.

*Add-Ons* allow you to purchase additional *Seats* or *Custom Domains*, while enabling *Overages* removes any *Subscription Plan* limits on *Build Time* or *Hosting Bandwidth*, instead invoicing you for the amount an *Organization* uses each month rather than capping usage.

When you reach the limit on your *Build Time* or *Hosting Bandwidth* usage, CloudCannon will prevent more *Site* builds or take websites offline for the rest of the month. Because *Subscription Plan* resources are used by every *Site* in an *Organization*, reaching your resource limit due to activity on one *Site* will affect all *Sites* on that *Organization*. If you store multiple client *Sites* on your agency *Organization*, that means one client's activity can risk downtime for other client *Sites*.

For this reason, we highly recommend enabling *Overages* even if you don't need more resources than a *Subscription Plan* provides. An *Organization* needs a credit card before you can turn *Overages* on, even if you are on a *Free Trial*, so we'll come back to this in the next step of this guide once you have decided who pays.

## Create a client Site

When you connect a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm> to an *Organization*, either yours or a *Client Organization*, you can create a *Site* from any branch in that *Git Repository*. You can connect as many *Git Repositories* and *Sites* as you need, but they all share the resources included with the *Subscription Plan* on that *Organization*.

In either *Organization*, you can create a *Site* from the *Organization Home* page or the *Sites Browser*. CloudCannon will open the *Create a Site* page. For the full walkthrough, please read our documentation on [getting started with CloudCannon](/documentation/developer-guides/getting-started-with-cloudcannon/).

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Create-A-Site-Page" alt="A screenshot of the Create a Site page shows a File source dropdown for choosing a Git provider, local storage, or a CloudCannon template, above the Create Site and start syncing button." title="The Create a Site page" type="screenshot"/>

By the end of that guide, you will have:

* Connected a *Git Repository* to CloudCannon and created a *Site* from one of its branches.
* Written a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/configuration-files.yml">Configuration File</comp.GlossaryTerm> that turns your client's files into something they can edit without touching code.
* Grouped those files into <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/collection.yml">Collections</comp.GlossaryTerm> so your client can find the content they need.
* Saved your configuration changes back to the *Git Repository*.
* Toured the *Site* your client will log in to.

Work through it inside the *Organization* where you want your client's *Site* to live, then come back here.

In the next step of this guide, we'll give your client a login in the right *Permission Groups*, decide who pays for their *Organization*, and enable *Overages*.
