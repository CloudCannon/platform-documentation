Welcome to the Set up your agency guide! 👋

This guide is for developers and agencies who build and maintain websites for clients. You might be deciding whether CloudCannon fits how you work, or you might have an account already with no clients set up yet. Either way, this guide covers how to structure client work, the benefits and drawbacks of each approach, and how to give a client access to their own content.

In this guide, we'll cover how to:

* Picture your agency on CloudCannon, from your own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> to a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> for each client, or client <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> kept alongside your own
* Join the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-program.yml">Partner Program</comp.GlossaryTerm> and choose a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* Create your first *Client Organization* and choose a *Subscription Plan* for it
* Give your client a login to edit their own content, and decide who pays for it
* Move a client's *Sites* between your own *Organization* and a *Client Organization* when their needs change

By the end of this guide, you will have a structure for your client work, a *Partner Organization* on a *Subscription Plan* that covers your whole team, a first client set up with their own login, and a way to change that structure later.

Before we begin, this guide assumes you build or maintain websites for other people, and that you have website files you can connect to CloudCannon from a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Additionally, this guide will specifically cover the *Standard Plan*, and the *Lite Plan* available through the *Partner Program*, as entry points into CloudCannon, rather than the *Team Plan* or *Enterprise Plan* which provide more features for clients with more requirements.

This guide is about structure and cost across several clients, so it does not cover connecting a single website to CloudCannon or setting up editing for it. If you have not created a *Site* before, please read our documentation on [getting started with CloudCannon](/documentation/developer-guides/getting-started-with-cloudcannon/), which walks through creating an *Organization*, connecting a *Site*, and writing the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/configuration-files.yml">Configuration File</comp.GlossaryTerm> that makes your files editable. We'll point back to it when you add your first client's *Site*.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  You do not need to be a member of the *Partner Program* to try CloudCannon. Any account can create an *Organization*, connect a *Site*, and take advantage of CloudCannon's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>. However, the *Partner Program* provides more options for structuring and billing clients.
</comp.Notice>
