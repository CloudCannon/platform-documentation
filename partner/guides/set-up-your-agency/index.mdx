Welcome to the Set up your agency guide! 👋

This guide is for developers and agencies who build and maintain websites for clients. You might be deciding whether CloudCannon fits how you work, or you might have an account already with no clients set up yet. Either way, this guide covers how to structure client work, what each approach costs, and how to give a client access to their own content.

In this guide, we'll cover how to:

* Choose between a separate <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> for each client and keeping client <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> in your own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* Join the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-program.yml">Partner Program</comp.GlossaryTerm> and choose a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* Create your first *Client Organization* and choose a *Subscription Plan* for it
* Give your client a login to edit their own content, and decide who pays for it

By the end of this guide, you will have a structure for your client work, a *Partner Organization* on a *Subscription Plan* that covers your whole team, and a first client set up with their own login.

Before we begin, this guide assumes you build or maintain websites for other people, and that you have website files you can connect to CloudCannon. Everything else is covered as we go.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  You do not need to be a member of the *Partner Program* to try CloudCannon. Any account can create an *Organization*, connect a *Site*, and take advantage of CloudCannon's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>. The *Partner Program* changes how you structure and pay for work across several clients, which is what this guide is about.
</comp.Notice>

In the first step of this guide, we'll compare the two ways to structure client work on CloudCannon and what each one costs.
