Every <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> needs a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, including the one for your agency. We recommend new agencies start on the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/standard-plan.yml">Standard Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, though you can switch to the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/l/lite-plan.yml">Lite Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> after joining the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-program.yml">Partner Program</comp.GlossaryTerm> if you want to minimize cost.

Two *Subscription Plans* suit an agency getting started:

<comp.OptionsTable>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Lite Plan" type_markdown="$10 USD/month, or $120 USD/year">
    The *Lite Plan* is the cheapest way to set up an agency, and is only available to members of CloudCannon's *Partner Program*. It includes:

    * One <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seat</comp.GlossaryTerm> for <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization-member.yml">Organization Members</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site-sharing.yml">Site Sharing</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/guest-access.yml">Guest Access</comp.GlossaryTerm>
    * One hour of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm> per month
    * 20 GB of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm>
    * One <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>

    The *Lite Plan* also includes <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inboxes</comp.GlossaryTerm> that collect <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/form.yml">Form</comp.GlossaryTerm> submissions and forward them to an email address, custom branding for your *Organization*, and automatic HTTPS, DDoS protection, and global CDN hosting for the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> you build.

    This *Subscription Plan* costs the same as a single *Seat*, with the remaining resources provided for free. You can purchase additional *Seats* for $10 USD/month or *Custom Domains* for $2 USD/month as <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You can also enable <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which remove the *Build Time* and *Hosting Bandwidth* limits and invoice you at the end of each month for what you used above them, at $10 USD/month per additional hour of *Build Time* and $15 USD/month per additional 100 GB of *Hosting Bandwidth*, rounded up to the next full bundle.

    The *Lite Plan* is best suited to agencies who primarily use <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm> rather than keeping client *Sites* in their <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, as the *Build Time* and *Hosting Bandwidth* limits are often not enough to manage several clients. The *Lite Plan* also has no publishing features, so you cannot use <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/pull-request.yml">Pull Requests</comp.GlossaryTerm> or merging between *Sites* in an *Organization* on this *Subscription Plan*. If you want to keep your agency's own website on CloudCannon with a staging copy to review changes, choose the *Standard Plan* instead.

  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Standard Plan" type_markdown="$55 USD/month, or $588 USD/year">
    The *Standard Plan* is CloudCannon's entry point for teams that host and work on *Sites*. It includes:
    
    * Three *Seats* for *Organization Members*, *Site Sharing*, or *Guest Access*
    * Unlimited hours of *Build Time* per month
    * 110 GB of *Hosting Bandwidth*
    * Five *Custom Domains*

    The *Standard Plan* includes all the features available on the *Lite Plan*, as well as publishing between *Sites* with *Pull Requests* and merging, connections to <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dam.yml">Digital Asset Managers</comp.GlossaryTerm>, scheduled builds, password protection for your *Sites*, translation and geolocation workflows, and forwarding *Form* submissions to external providers or automation flows.

    You can purchase additional *Seats* for $10 USD/month or *Custom Domains* for $2 USD/month as *Add-Ons*. Because the *Standard Plan* does not limit *Build Time*, the only *Overage* you can incur is *Hosting Bandwidth*, at $15 USD/month per additional 100 GB, rounded up to the next full bundle.

    The *Standard Plan* is best suited to agencies who want the option to do both, keeping some client *Sites* in their own *Organization* while managing other clients through *Client Organizations*. It has the resources to build and host several *Sites* at once, and the publishing features to move changes from a staging *Site* to a live one.
  </comp.OptionsRow>
</comp.OptionsTable>

You can pay for your *Subscription Plan* on a *Monthly* or *Yearly* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/billing-period.yml">Billing Period</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Most CloudCannon *Subscription Plans*, except for the *Lite Plan*, have a discounted price if you choose to pay annually.

You are not locked into either choice. You can move between *Subscription Plans* whenever your agency's needs change, and CloudCannon will adjust your invoice.

## You will choose a Subscription Plan more than once

The comparison above is the one you make for your agency's own *Organization*. You will make the same comparison again for every client you put in a *Client Organization*, because each one carries its own *Subscription Plan* and its own invoice. What changes is what the *Organization* is being asked to do, and which *Subscription Plans* are worth considering.

For your own *Organization*, the question is whether it seats a team or also hosts websites, and the two *Subscription Plans* above are the entry points. For a client's *Organization*, the question is how often their *Site* rebuilds, whether their content needs an approval step before it goes live, and how many of their own people need access. A client who needs to control precisely what each of their people can reach needs the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-plan.yml">Team Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> for <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-permission-group.yml">Custom Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which is why the comparison for a client is a wider one. We'll come back to it when you add your first client.

When you are finished exploring CloudCannon on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> or signing up for the *Partner Program*, and you have decided which *Subscription Plan* is right for your agency's *Organization*, you can start paying for CloudCannon from your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

If your *Free Trial* ends before you choose, CloudCannon pauses your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> until you add your payment details, and your *Organization* shows a warning telling you how to reinstate it. For more information, please read our documentation on [what CloudCannon's Free Trial is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-cloudcannons-free-trial/).

### Select a Subscription Plan

Click the *Org Settings* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm> and open the *Subscription* page. This page shows your current *Subscription Plan*, your *Billing Period*, and your estimated next invoice.

In the billing summary, click the *Review your Subscription Plan* button. CloudCannon will open the *Your Subscription Plan* page, where you can see the resources included on your current *Subscription Plan* and how much of each you are using.

Click the *Change your Subscription Plan* button to open the *Update your Subscription Plan* page, which compares every *Subscription Plan* available to your *Organization*.

Use the *Billing Period* toggle to choose *Monthly* or *Yearly*, then click the *Select [Plan Name]* button on the *Subscription Plan* you want. While your *Organization* is on its *Free Trial*, every *Subscription Plan* except the *Enterprise Plan* shows a *Select [Plan Name]* button, including the one your trial is on.

### Add-Ons and Overages

When you click a *Select [Plan Name]* button, the *Your Subscription Plan* page opens with the *Overages* toggle switched on for your agency's *Organization*. Before you complete checkout, it helps to know what that toggle does.

Every *Subscription Plan* includes a set amount of most resources, and you can extend it in two ways rather than moving up to the next *Subscription Plan*. *Add-Ons* let you buy more *Seats* or *Custom Domains*. *Overages* remove the *Subscription Plan's* limit on *Build Time* and *Hosting Bandwidth*, and invoice you at the end of each month for what you used above it. On the *Standard Plan*, *Build Time* is unlimited, so *Overages* only extend *Hosting Bandwidth*.

Without *Overages*, an *Organization* that reaches its *Build Time* limit stops building its *Sites*, so changes no longer reach the live websites, and one that reaches its *Hosting Bandwidth* limit stops serving webpages to visitors, until your monthly usage resets. Every *Site* in an *Organization* draws on the same resources, so one *Site* reaching a limit affects all of them. If you keep client *Sites* in your agency's *Organization*, one client's traffic can take your other clients' websites offline.

For this reason, we recommend leaving *Overages* on. Turning them on does not charge you anything by itself, and a charge only appears on your invoice in a month where you go over a limit. If you would rather cap your usage, click the *Overages* toggle to switch it off before you complete checkout.

### Pay for your Subscription Plan

Selecting a *Subscription Plan* does not pay for it. To start your *Subscription*, you must attach a credit card to your *Organization*. Scroll down to the *Payment details* section and click the *Add Credit Card* button to open the *Add your Credit Card* modal.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Your-Subscription-Plan-Add-Credit-Card" alt="A screenshot of the Your Subscription Plan page in CloudCannon, scrolled down to the Payment details section, shows the Add Credit Card button." title="The Payment details section" type="screenshot"/>

Enter your card details and click the *Add Credit Card* button. CloudCannon will confirm your card with your bank and add it to your *Organization*. Some banks also require a billing address to approve a card, so if you run into trouble, expand the *Additional info* section and add yours.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Subscription-Add-Credit-Card-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add your Credit Card modal shows a Name on card field and a collapsed Additional info section, above the Add Credit Card button." title="The Add your Credit Card modal" type="screenshot"/>

Click the *Complete checkout* button. CloudCannon will move your *Organization* onto the *Subscription Plan* you selected, update your resource limits and next invoice to match, and return you to the *Subscription* page. If your *Organization* is still on its *Free Trial*, completing checkout ends the trial straight away and starts billing on the *Subscription Plan* you selected.

For more information, please read our documentation on [updating your Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-subscription-plan/) and [managing your payment methods](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-payment-methods/).
