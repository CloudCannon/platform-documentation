The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cms.yml">Content Management System</comp.GlossaryTerm> you build on decides how your clients access and edit their content, and how much of your code and infrastructure they can reach. It also decides who pays for the tools that run their website, whether the CMS integrates with the development tools your team already uses, and whether you retain ownership of your files.

As a Git-backed CMS, CloudCannon allows you to:

* Give each client as much control over their own content as you want.
* Keep each client away from your code and from your other clients' content.
* Decide how each client pays for editing and hosting, and fold that into your existing invoice structure.
* Let your developers keep building in the tools they already use, while everyone else edits the same files through CloudCannon.
* Retain ownership of your files, which stay in a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm> you control rather than a database CloudCannon owns.

Those outcomes all depend on how your work is organized in CloudCannon. An <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> is your home base, holding your content, settings, and people. As an agency, your *Organization* can hold your own website, your clients' websites, or both, and you connect each one to CloudCannon from a *Git Repository* on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-provider.yml">Git Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-Single-Org" alt="A screenshot of the Organization Home page shows quick links to Sites, Projects, and Domains, alongside a summary of the Organization's usage against its Subscription Plan limits." title="The Organization Home page" type="screenshot"/>

Each *Organization* has a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which determines how much you pay for CloudCannon, what features you have access to, and what resource limits you have. CloudCannon provides a range of *Subscription Plans* designed for teams of different sizes and requirements. All the work you do on an *Organization* is billed under a single invoice.

An *Organization* also contains:

* Any number of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon *Sites* differ from websites, as you can have many *Sites* which are all copies of the same website maintained for different purposes. Copies of the same website, such as its live and draft versions, are grouped into a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/project.yml">Project</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
* A number of [Custom Domains](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-custom-domain/) (e.g., example.com or company.net). You can connect any CloudCannon *Site* to a *Custom Domain* in your *Organization* and host it live on the internet, with options for subdomains, subpaths, authentication, and more.
* A number of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Each *Seat* pays for one way into your *Organization's* content. You can invite [Organization Members](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-team-member/) to view, edit, or publish content, using [Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-permission-groups/) to set what each person can reach. You can also turn on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-sharing.yml">Client Sharing</comp.GlossaryTerm> for a single *Site*, which lets people without CloudCannon accounts log in with a password.
* An amount of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which is the time CloudCannon spends building your *Sites* each month, and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm> which is the data your live websites transfer to visitors' browsers.

We'll set up your agency's *Organization* later in this guide. You can optionally extend it by joining CloudCannon's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-program.yml">Partner Program</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which adds agency-specific UI, a *Subscription Plan* built for smaller clients, and discounts that grow as your client work does.

## The Partner Program

The *Partner Program* is for agencies and freelancers who build and maintain CloudCannon *Sites* for clients. There is no fee to apply, and CloudCannon turns your *Organization* into a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> once you are enrolled. A *Partner Organization* can set up a separate *Organization* for each client, called a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which your agency manages on their behalf. A *Client Organization* is one of the two places your clients' *Sites* can live.

Every *Partner Organization* gets:

* **Client Organizations** — Create a separate *Organization* for any client you manage, keeping each client's team, *Sites*, settings, and *Subscription Plan* independent from your own and from each other. Only members of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/owners.yml">Owners Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm> in your *Partner Organization* can create one.
* **The Partner Permission Group** — Every *Client Organization* you create gets a *Partner Permission Group* with full <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permissions.yml">Permissions</comp.GlossaryTerm>, whose members mirror the *Owners Permission Group* in your *Partner Organization*. You can give anyone else in your agency access by adding them to that *Client Organization's* other *Permission Groups*, and members of your *Partner Organization* never contribute to a client's *Seats*.
* **Extended Free Trials** — Every *Client Organization* you create starts on a six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> rather than the standard 21 days, allowing you to take your time building a client's website before you pay for the *Subscription Plan*. Four actions shorten that trial to three days: adding a *Custom Domain*, turning on *Client Sharing*, adding an *Organization Member* from outside your *Partner Organization*, or accepting a transfer that takes the *Client Organization* over the *Custom Domains* or *Seats* included in its *Subscription Plan*. For more information, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).
* **An extra Subscription Plan** — The *Lite Plan* is a low-cost, pay-as-you-go *Subscription Plan* that only members of the *Partner Program* can select. We'll compare it against the *Standard Plan* further on in this guide.
* **New links in your App Sidebar** — Gain a *Clients* link in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm> that opens the *Client Organizations Browser*, where you can search and filter your *Client Organizations*, and a *Rewards* link that opens the *Partner Rewards* page, where you can keep track of your progress through the *Partner Program*. The *Rewards* link appears only for members of the *Owners Permission Group*. Your *Organization's* own *Sites*, *Projects*, and *Domains* links move down the *App Sidebar*, underneath a new *Your Org* heading, while *Home* stays above your *Clients* link. Each of those links appears only when your *Partner Organization* already has what it points to, or you have permission to create it, so someone without those permissions in a *Partner Organization* that holds none of them sees *Org Settings* on its own, with no *Home* link above.
* **Client Health Checks** — The *Health* tab on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization-home.yml">Organization Home</comp.GlossaryTerm> will also show the result of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/health-check.yml">Health Checks</comp.GlossaryTerm> on your *Client Organizations*, as well as *Sites* within your *Partner Organization*, warning you about failed builds and syncs, unresolved *Custom Domains*, expiring SSL certificates, *Free Trials* that are ending, subscriptions that need attention, and inactive clients.
* **An onboarding session** — Talk through your agency's setup on a call with our team, which you can arrange with our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> once you have joined.

The *Partner Program* has four tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum. As you bring clients on, you earn <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm> and move up through these tiers, which give your agency an increasing discount on *Subscription Plans* for you and your clients. The discount rises from 10% at Silver to 20% at Platinum, and each tier unlocks benefits such as a listing in the CloudCannon Experts Directory, priority support, client referrals, and co-marketing. For the points each tier requires and the full list of benefits, please read our documentation on [what Partner Points and Rewards are](/documentation/partner-articles/what-are-partner-points-and-rewards/).

We'll cover how to join the *Partner Program* once your agency's *Organization* is set up.
