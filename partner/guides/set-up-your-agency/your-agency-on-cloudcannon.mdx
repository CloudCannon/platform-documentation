As a web development agency, choosing a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cms.yml">Content Management System</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a decision about how your clients access and edit their content, and how much of your code and infrastructure they can reach. It also decides who pays for the tools that run their website, whether the CMS integrates with the development tools your team already uses, and whether you retain ownership of your files. You want to understand the benefits and drawbacks of each option, know it will suit the needs of your team and your clients, and feel confident that setting up will be a streamlined process, especially if you are migrating existing clients.

As a Git-backed CMS, CloudCannon allows you to:

* Give each client as much control over their own content as you want.
* Keep each client away from your code and from your other clients' content.
* Decide how each client pays for editing and hosting, and fold that into your existing invoice structure.
* Let your developers keep building in the tools they already use, while everyone else edits the same files through CloudCannon.
* Retain ownership of your files, which stay in a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm> you control rather than a database CloudCannon owns.

Those outcomes all depend on how your work is organized in CloudCannon. An <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> is your home base, holding your content, settings, and people. As an agency, your *Organization* can hold your own website, your clients' websites, or both, and you connect each one to CloudCannon from a repository on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-provider.yml">Git Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-Single-Org" alt="A screenshot of the Organization Home page shows quick links to Sites, Projects, and Domains, alongside a summary of the Organization's usage against its Subscription Plan limits." title="The Organization Home page" type="screenshot"/>

Each *Organization* has a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which determines how much you pay for CloudCannon, what features you have access to, and what resource limits you have. CloudCannon provides a range of *Subscription Plans* designed for teams of different sizes and requirements. All the work you do on an *Organization* is billed under a single invoice.

An *Organization* also contains:

* Any number of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon *Sites* differ from websites, as you can have many *Sites* which are all copies of the same website maintained for different purposes. Copies of the same website, such as its live and draft versions, are grouped into a [Project](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-project/).
* A number of [Custom Domains](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-custom-domain/) (e.g., example.com or company.net). You can connect any CloudCannon *Site* to a *Custom Domain* in your *Organization* and host it live on the internet, with options for subdomains, subpaths, authentication, and more.
* A number of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You can use these *Seats* to invite [Organization Members](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-team-member/) to view, edit, or publish content in your *Organization*, with [Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-permission-groups/) to determine their level of access, or enable <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-sharing.yml">Client Sharing</comp.GlossaryTerm> for a specific *Site* to allow people who don't have CloudCannon accounts to log in with a password.
* An amount of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which set how much you can update your *Sites* each month and how many visitors can view your live websites.

We'll set up your agency's *Organization* later in this guide. You can optionally extend it by joining CloudCannon's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-program.yml">Partner Program</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which adds agency-specific UI, a *Subscription Plan* built for smaller clients, and discounts that grow as your client work does.

## The Partner Program

The *Partner Program* is for agencies and freelancers who build and maintain CloudCannon *Sites* for clients. There is no fee to apply, and CloudCannon turns your *Organization* into a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> once you are enrolled. This is what lets you set up a separate *Organization* for each client, which your agency manages on their behalf. It is one of the two places your clients' *Sites* can live.

Every *Partner Organization* gets:

* **Client Organizations** — Create a separate *Organization* for any client you manage, keeping each client's team, *Sites*, settings, and *Subscription Plan* independent from your own and from each other.
* **The Partner Permission Group** — Every <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> you create gets a *Partner Permission Group* with full *Permissions*, whose members mirror the *Owners Permission Group* in your *Partner Organization*. You can give anyone else in your agency access by adding them to that *Client Organization's* other *Permission Groups*, and members of your *Partner Organization* never contribute to a client's *Seats*.
* **Extended Free Trials** — Every *Client Organization* you create starts on a six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> rather than the standard 21 days, allowing you to take your time building a client's website and only pay for the *Subscription Plan* when you begin inviting clients or host it live.
* **An extra Subscription Plan** — The *Lite Plan* is a low-cost, pay-as-you-go *Subscription Plan* that only members of the *Partner Program* can select. We'll compare it against the *Standard Plan* further on in this guide.
* **New pages in your App Sidebar** — Gain links to the *Client Organizations Browser* and *Partner Rewards* page in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which allow you to search and filter your *Client Organizations* and keep track of your progress through the *Partner Program*. Your *Organization's* other links move down the *App Sidebar*, underneath a new *Your Org* heading, while *Home* stays at the top.
* **Client health in Issues** — The *Issues* tab on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization-home.yml">Organization Home</comp.GlossaryTerm> will also show the result of health checks on your *Client Organizations*, as well as *Sites* within your *Partner Organization*, warning you about failed builds and syncs, unresolved *Custom Domains*, expiring SSL certificates, *Free Trials* that are ending, subscriptions that need attention, and inactive clients.
* **An onboarding session** — Talk through your agency's setup on a call with our team.

<comp.Notice info_type="pricing">
  **The *Issues* tab is available through a private Beta.**

  To request access, please contact our friendly <a href="mailto:support@cloudcannon.com?subject=A%20question%20about%20health%20checks">support team</a>. CloudCannon enables the Beta for individual accounts rather than for a whole *Organization*, so everyone on your team who needs the *Issues* tab must request access separately.
</comp.Notice>

The *Partner Program* has four tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum. As you bring clients on, you earn <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-points.yml">Partner Points</comp.GlossaryTerm> and move up through these tiers, providing your agency with an increasing discount on *Subscription Plans* for you and your clients. The discount rises from 10% at Silver to 20% at Platinum, and each tier unlocks benefits such as a listing in the CloudCannon Experts Directory, priority support, client referrals, and co-marketing.

We'll cover how to join the *Partner Program* once your agency's *Organization* is set up.
