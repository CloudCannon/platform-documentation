Setting your agency up on CloudCannon starts with creating an account and an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Optionally, you can join the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-program.yml">Partner Program</comp.GlossaryTerm> to gain access to <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/l/lite-plan.yml">Lite Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and the other benefits we covered earlier in this guide.

## Create an account

If you already have a CloudCannon account, you can skip this step of the guide.

Open the <a href="https://app.cloudcannon.com/register" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CloudCannon app</a> using your Internet browser. You can create an account using your email, or your GitHub, Bitbucket, or GitLab account. In this guide, we'll create an account using email.

Click on the *Sign up with email* button.

<comp.DocsImage path="assets/onboarding_screenshots/CloudCannon-Documentation-Create-Account-Login-Page.png" alt="A screenshot of the Register page shows the options to sign up with GitHub, Bitbucket, GitLab, or email." title="The CloudCannon Register page" type="screenshot"/>

Enter your email in the *Email Address* field and click the *Send Code* button.

<comp.DocsImage path="assets/onboarding_screenshots/CloudCannon-Documentation-Create-Account-Enter-Email.png" alt="A screenshot of the Signup page shows a field to enter the email address you want to associate with your CloudCannon account." title="The Signup page" type="screenshot"/>

To validate your email address, CloudCannon will send a six-digit confirmation code to the email you provided. This validation email should arrive in your inbox quickly and will expire after 10 minutes.

Enter the code in the *Confirmation Code* field and click the *Confirm my email* button.

<comp.DocsImage path="assets/onboarding_screenshots/CloudCannon-Documentation-Create-Account-Verify-Email.png" alt="A screenshot of the Signup page shows a field for your Confirmation Code to validate your email address." title="The Signup page" type="screenshot"/>

Once you have validated your email address, you can finish adding your account details, such as your first name, last name, and an account password, and whether you are a developer or a content editor. Your password must be at least nine characters long.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Do not share your CloudCannon password with anyone. CloudCannon employees will never ask you for your password.
</comp.Notice>

Click the *Create my account* button to finish creating your account.

<comp.DocsImage path="assets/onboarding_screenshots/CloudCannon-Documentation-Create-Account-Details.png" alt="A screenshot of the Signup page shows fields for your first name, last name, password, and role." title="The Signup page" type="screenshot"/>

## Your Organization

CloudCannon will automatically create an *Organization* for you when you sign up, and add you to the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/owners.yml">Owners Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm>. By default, this *Organization* is named using the first name on your account, so we recommend changing it to the name of your agency. For more information, please read our documentation on [renaming your Organization](/documentation/user-articles/rename-your-organization/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  If you created your account by accepting an invitation to someone else's *Organization*, CloudCannon will add you to theirs instead of creating one for you. To run an agency you need one of your own. You can [create a new Organization](/documentation/user-articles/create-a-new-organization/) at any time.
</comp.Notice>

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-No-Sites" alt="A screenshot of the Organization Home page for a new Organization shows a welcome message above cards offering a demo Site and the option to add your own files to CloudCannon." title="A new Organization Home" type="screenshot"/>

Your new *Organization* starts on a 21-day <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> of the *Standard Plan*, so you can connect a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> and try CloudCannon before you enter any payment details. If you are a member of the *Owners Permission Group*, you can see how many days remain on the *Trial Countdown* in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-App-Sidebar-Trial-Countdown-Element" alt="A screenshot of the App Sidebar shows a trial countdown displaying the number of days remaining." title="The trial countdown in the App Sidebar" type="ui-snippet"/>

Now that you have an *Organization*, you can pause this guide here. Your 21-day *Free Trial* is the time to connect a website of your own and see what editing on CloudCannon feels like before you enter any payment details. You have already created an account and an *Organization*, so pick up our [Getting Started with CloudCannon](/documentation/developer-guides/getting-started-with-cloudcannon/create-a-site/) guide at the *Create a Site* step. Come back here when you are ready to set up a *Site* for a client and pick your *Subscription Plan*.

## Join the Partner Program

There is no fee to join the *Partner Program*, and you can join during the 21-day *Free Trial* on your own *Organization*. That gives you three weeks to create *Client Organizations*, try the benefits, and discuss your setup with our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> before you pay for your agency's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

To apply, visit the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> page, which covers eligibility, what's included, and how to sign up. Once you're enrolled, CloudCannon will turn your *Organization* into a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, every *Client Organization* you create from then on starts on a six-month *Free Trial*, and the *Lite Plan* becomes available when you choose a *Subscription Plan* in the next step.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-Partner-Org" alt="A screenshot of a Partner Organization Home shows Clients and Rewards in the App Sidebar and a Partner Rewards tier summary." title="A Partner Organization Home" type="screenshot"/>

In the next step of this guide, we'll choose a *Subscription Plan* for your agency and start your *Subscription*.
