## Create an account

If you already have a CloudCannon account, you can skip this step of the guide.

Open the <a href="https://app.cloudcannon.com/register" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CloudCannon app</a> using your Internet browser. You can create an account using your email, or your GitHub, Bitbucket, or GitLab account. Click on the *Sign up with email* button.

<comp.DocsImage path="assets/onboarding_screenshots/CloudCannon-Documentation-Create-Account-Login-Page.png" alt="A screenshot of the Register page shows the options to sign up with GitHub, Bitbucket, GitLab, or email." title="The CloudCannon Register page" type="screenshot"/>

Enter your email in the *Email address* field and click *Send Code*.

<comp.DocsImage path="assets/onboarding_screenshots/CloudCannon-Documentation-Create-Account-Enter-Email.png" alt="A screenshot of the Signup page shows a field to enter the email address you want to associate with your CloudCannon account." title="The Signup page" type="screenshot"/>

To validate your email address, CloudCannon will send a six-digit confirmation code to the email you provided. This validation email should arrive in your inbox quickly and will expire after 10 minutes.

Enter the code in the *Confirmation Code* field and click *Continue*.

<comp.DocsImage path="assets/onboarding_screenshots/CloudCannon-Documentation-Create-Account-Verify-Email.png" alt="A screenshot of the Signup page shows a field for your Confirmation Code to validate your email address." title="The Signup page" type="screenshot"/>

Once you have validated your email address, you can finish adding your account details, such as your first name, last name, and an account password, and whether you are a developer or a content editor. Your password must be at least nine characters long.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Do not share your CloudCannon password with anyone. CloudCannon employees will never ask you for your password.
</comp.Notice>

Click *Create my account* to finish creating your account.

<comp.DocsImage path="assets/onboarding_screenshots/CloudCannon-Documentation-Create-Account-Details.png" alt="A screenshot of the Signup page shows fields for your first name, last name, password, and role." title="The Signup page" type="screenshot"/>

## Your Organization

CloudCannon will automatically create an *Organization* for you when you sign up, and add you to the *Owners Permission Group*. By default, this *Organization* is called ??, however we recommend changing the name to that of your agnecy. We'll cover how to do that later in this step of the guide.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  If you created your account by accepting an invitation to someone else's *Organization*, CloudCannon will add you to theirs instead of creating one for you. To run an agency you need an *Organization* of your own, which you can [create](/documentation/user-articles/create-a-new-organization/) at any time.
</comp.Notice>

## Your Subscription Plan

Your new *Organization* starts on a 21-day <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> of the *Standard Plan*, so you can connect a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> and try CloudCannon before you enter any payment details. If you are a member of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/owners.yml">Owners Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm>, you can see how many days remain on the *Trial Countdown* in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. 

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-App-Sidebar-Trial-Countdown-Element" alt="A screenshot of the App Sidebar shows a trial countdown displaying the number of days remaining." title="The trial countdown in the App Sidebar" type="ui-snippet"/>

Every *Organization* needs a *Subscription Plan*, including the one for your agency. We recommend new agencies start on the *Standard Plan*, however you can switch to the *Lite Plan* after joining the *Partner Program* if you want to minimize cost. Which *Subscription Plan* is right for your agency depends on a few factors:

<comp.OptionsTable>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Lite Plan" type_markdown="$10 USD/month, or $120 USD/year">
    The *Lite Plan* is the cheapest way to set up an agency, and is only available to members of CloudCannon's *Partner Program*. It includes:

    * One <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seat</comp.GlossaryTerm> for *Organization Members*, *Site Sharing*, or *Client Sharing*
    * One hour of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm> per month
    * 20 GB of <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm>
    * One <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>

    The *Lite Plan* also includes <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inboxes</comp.GlossaryTerm> that collect <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/form.yml">Form</comp.GlossaryTerm> submissions and forward them to an email address, custom branding for your *Organization*, and automatic HTTPS, DDoS protection, and global CDN hosting for the *Sites* you build.

    The cost of this *Subscription Plan* is the same cost as a single *Seat*, with the remaining resources provided for free. You can purchase additional *Seats* for $10 USD/month or *Custom Domains* for $2 USD/month as <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You can also enable <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which remove the *Build Time* and *Hosting Bandwidth* limits and invoice you at the end of each month for what you used above them, at $10 USD/month per additional hour of *Build Time* and $15 USD/month per additional 100 GB of *Hosting Bandwidth*, rounded up to the next full bundle.

    The *Lite Plan* is best suited to agencies who primarily use *Client Organizations* rather than keeping client *Sites* in their *Partner Organization*, as the *Build Time* and *Hosting Bandwidth* limits are often not enough to manage several clients.

  </comp.OptionsRow>
  <comp.OptionsRow label="Standard Plan" type_markdown="$55 USD/month, or $588 USD/year">
    The *Standard Plan* is CloudCannon's entry point for teams that host and work on *Sites*. It includes:
    
    * Three *Seats* for *Organization Members*, *Site Sharing*, or *Client Sharing*
    * Unlimited hours of *Build Time* per month
    * 110 GB of *Hosting Bandwidth*
    * Five *Custom Domains*

    The *Standard Plan* includes all the features available on the *Lite Plan*, as well as publishing between *Sites* with pull requests and merging, connections to <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dam.yml">Digital Asset Managers</comp.GlossaryTerm>, scheduled builds, password protection for your *Sites*, translation and geolocation workflows, and forwarding *Form* submissions to an external providers or automation flows.

    You can purchase additional *Seats* for $10 USD/month or *Custom Domains* for $2 USD/month as *Add-Ons*. Because the *Standard Plan* does not limit *Build Time*, the only *Overage* you can incur is *Hosting Bandwidth*, at $15 USD/month per additional 100 GB, rounded up to the next full bundle.

    The *Standard Plan* is best suited to agencies who want the option to do both, keeping some client *Sites* in their own *Organization* while managing other clients through *Client Organizations*. It has the resources to build and host several *Sites* at once, and the publishing features to move changes from a staging *Site* to a live one.
  </comp.OptionsRow>
</comp.OptionsTable>

You are not locked into either choice. You can move between *Subscription Plans* whenever your agency's needs change, and CloudCannon will adjust your invoice.

## Join the Partner Program

There is no fee to join the *Partner Program*, and you can join during the 21-day *Free Trial* on your own *Organization*. This allows you to create *Client Organizations*, try the benefits below, and discuss your setup with our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>, before you pay for your agency's *Subscription Plan*.

The *Partner Program* is measured in tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum. As you bring clients on, you earn *Partner Points* and move up through these tiers, providing your agency with an increasing discount on *Subscription Plans* for you and your clients. The discount rises from 10% at Silver to 20% at Platinum, and each tier unlocks benefits such as a listing in the CloudCannon Experts Directory, priority support, client referrals, and co-marketing.

Every member of the *Partner Program* also gets:

* **Client Organizations** — Create a separate *Organization* for any client you manage, keeping each client's team, *Sites*, settings, and *Subscription Plan* independent from your own and from each other.
* **The Partner Permission Group** — Every *Client Organization* you create gets a *Partner Permission Group* with full *Permissions*, whose members mirror the *Owners Permission Group* in your *Partner Organization*. You can give anyone else in your agency access by adding them to that *Client Organization's* other *Permission Groups*, and members of your *Partner Organization* never contribute to a client's *Seats*.
* **The Lite Plan** — Put price-sensitive clients on a low-cost, pay-as-you-go *Subscription Plan* that is not available outside the *Partner Program*.
* **Extended Free Trials** — Every *Client Organization* you create starts on a six-month *Free Trial* rather than the standard 21 days, so you can build a client's website and start paying for CloudCannon when they are ready to go live.
* **New pages in your App Sidebar** — Gain links to the *Client Organizations Browser* and *Partner Rewards* page in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which allow you to search and filter your *Client Organizations* and keep track of your progress through the *Partner Program*. Your *Organization* links move down the *App Sidebar*, underneath a new *Your Org* heading.
* **Client health in Issues** — The *Issues* tab on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization-home.yml">Organization Home</comp.GlossaryTerm> will also show the result of health checks on your *Client Organizations*, as well as *Sites* within your *Partner Organization*, warning you about failed builds and syncs, unresolved *Custom Domains*, expiring SSL certificates, *Free Trials* that are ending, subscriptions that need attention, and inactive clients.
* **An onboarding session** — Talk through your agency's setup on a call with our team.

To apply, visit the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> page, which covers eligibility, what's included, and how to sign up. Once you're enrolled, CloudCannon will turn your *Organization* into a *Partner Organization*.

## Subscribe to CloudCannon

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em> and <em>Billing</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:billing:write</code> permission, can change your <em>Subscription Plan</em>.</p>
</comp.Notice>

When you are finished exploring CloudCannon on your *Free Trial* or signing up for the *Partner Program*, and you have selected the right *Subscription Plan* for your agency's *Organization*, you can subscribe to CloudCannon from your *Org Settings*.

Click the *Org Settings* link in the *App Sidebar*, then open the *Subscription* page. This page shows your current *Subscription Plan*, your *Billing Period*, and your estimated next invoice.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Subscription-Annual-Standard-Plan" alt="A screenshot of the Subscription page shows the billing summary, including the current plan, estimated bill, and billing details." title="The Subscription page in Org Settings" type="screenshot"/>

In the billing summary, click the *Review your Subscription Plan* button. CloudCannon will open the *Your Subscription Plan* page, where you can see the resources included on your current *Subscription Plan* and how much of each you are using.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Your-Subscription-Plan-Annual-Standard-Plan" alt="A screenshot of the Your Subscription Plan page shows the Overages toggle, plan resources, and estimated bill." title="The Your Subscription Plan page" type="screenshot"/>

Click the *Change your Subscription Plan* button to open the *Update your Subscription Plan* page, which compares every *Subscription Plan* available to your *Organization*.

Use the *Billing Period* toggle to choose *Monthly* or *Yearly*, then click the *Select [Plan Name]* button on the *Subscription Plan* you want. CloudCannon will move your *Organization* onto that *Subscription Plan*, and update your resource limits and next invoice to match. For more information, please read our documentation on [updating your Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-subscription-plan/).

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Update-Your-Subscription-Plan-Page-Yearly" alt="A screenshot of the Update your Subscription Plan page shows the Billing Period toggle set to Yearly and a comparison of the available Subscription Plans." title="The Update your Subscription Plan page" type="screenshot"/>

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  The *Lite Plan* appears on the *Update your Subscription Plan* page for members of the *Partner Program* only. Anyone who is not in a *Partner Organization* sees it only while it is already their current *Subscription Plan*, which matters when a client holds their own billing. We'll come back to this when you give your client access.
</comp.Notice>
