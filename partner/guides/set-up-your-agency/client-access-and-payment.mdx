<comp.Notice info_type="pricing">
  ***Custom Permission Groups*, and the *Author* and *Permissions* tabs for *Guest Access*, are available on our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/pricing/">Team or Enterprise Plan</a>.**
</comp.Notice>

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em>, <em>Developers</em>, and <em>Partner</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>group:member:write</code> permission, can invite people to an <em>Organization</em>.</p>
</comp.Notice>

In the third step of this guide, we covered the benefits of accessing a *Site* as a full *Organization Member*, as an *Organization Member* invited via *Site Sharing*, and through a *Guest Password* on a *Site* with *Guest Access* enabled. You don't have to use the same approach for every client, but some methods are better suited to *Sites* stored in a *Client Organization* versus your agency's *Organization*.

Which method suits your client depends on where their *Sites* live and whether they want a CloudCannon account:

* If your client's *Sites* are in a *Client Organization*, invite them as an *Organization Member* of that *Organization*. This is the only method where your client can receive and pay their own invoices.
* If your client only needs one *Site*, use *Site Sharing* to invite them as an *Organization Member* with access scoped to that *Site*, so they never see anything else in the *Organization* that holds it. This works whichever *Organization* the *Site* is in, and is the safest way to give a client access to a *Site* in your agency's *Organization*.
* If your client does not want a CloudCannon account at all, turn on *Guest Access* for their *Site* and give them the *Guest Password*. This works whichever *Organization* the *Site* is in, but everyone who uses that password shares one identity, so you cannot tell who made a change.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  **Only add clients when you are ready to start paying.**

  Inviting a client as an *Organization Member*, or enabling *Guest Access* on a *Site* will shorten the extended <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> on a *Client Organization* from six months to three days. This occurs when they accept their invitation, or immediately in the case of enabling *Guest Access*.
</comp.Notice>

## Invite a client as a full Organization Member

When you invite a client to an *Organization*, you must add them to at least one <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm>. *Permission Groups* determine what an *Organization Member* can see and interact with in CloudCannon.

Every *Organization* has the same five <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/default-permission-groups.yml">Default Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> on every *Subscription Plan*: *Owners*, *Developers*, *Technical Editors*, *Editors*, and *Billing*. A *Client Organization* also has the *Partner Permission Group*, which is where your agency's own access comes from. A person can belong to more than one *Permission Group*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Org-Settings-Team-Groups-Tab" alt="A screenshot of the Groups tab on the People page in a Client Organization shows the Partner, Owners, Developers, Technical Editors, Editors, and Billing groups, each labeled Default Permission Group, beside an Add new Group button." title="The Groups tab in a Client Organization" type="screenshot"/>

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Most clients are well served by the *Default Permission Groups*. If you need more fine-grained control you can use <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-permission-group.yml">Custom Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm>, available on the *Team Plan* and *Enterprise Plan*. This guide does not cover how to create or configure them.
</comp.Notice>

These are the three most common *Permission Groups* combinations you might invite your client to:

* **Owners** — Your client can do everything you can, including managing *Organization Members*, changing *Organization* settings, and deleting the *Organization*. This is a good option for technical clients or *Organization* handovers.
* **Editors and Billing** — Your client can edit and publish content and manage their own billing, but cannot change *Site* or *Organization* settings, or manage their team. This option allows your client to pay for their CloudCannon invoice directly and contribute non-technical changes to their *Site*, without ending your management of the *Organization*.
* **Billing** — Your client can pay and nothing else. This option is for members of your client's team who need to manage payment details but not contribute to content.

Handing over control does not remove your team. Your agency keeps full access through the *Partner Permission Group*, without using a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seat</comp.GlossaryTerm> on your client's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

To invite a client as an *Organization Member*, click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm> and open the *People* page.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Org-Settings-Team-Invite-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Invite Organization Members modal in a Client Organization shows an Email address field and a Group dropdown, above the Add Members button." title="The Invite Organization Members modal in a Client Organization" type="screenshot"/>

On the *Members* tab, click the *Add Members* button to open the *Invite Organization Members* modal. Enter your client's email address in the *Email address* field, choose the *Permission Groups* you decided on in the *Group* dropdown, and click the *Add Member* button to send the invitation. CloudCannon emails your client, and asks them to create an account if they do not have one.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  If you store your client *Sites* in your agency's *Organization*, we do not recommend inviting clients as full *Organization Members* unless you use *Custom Permission Groups*, as they may be allowed to see and interact with other clients' *Sites*. Please use *Site Sharing* instead.
</comp.Notice>

### View CloudCannon using your client's permissions

Once your client has accepted their invitation, you can confirm you picked the right *Permission Groups* using <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/v/view-as.yml">View As</comp.GlossaryTerm> mode, which loads CloudCannon with their exact permissions.

On the *Members* tab, click the context menu icon on your client's card and select *View as [Organization Member Name]*. CloudCannon will explain what the mode does, then reload the app with your client's permissions.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Team-Member-Context-Menu" alt="A screenshot of the Members tab on the People page shows an open context menu beside an Organization Member, listing Copy details, View as that person, and Remove from Organization." title="The context menu on an Organization Member" type="screenshot"/>

While the mode is on, you can explore your *Organization* from the perspective of your client. Links and buttons your client cannot see based on their *Permission Groups* are hidden rather than disabled. If you navigate to a page directly via URL, CloudCannon shows a permission denied message instead.

CloudCannon pins a *Viewing as [Organization Member Name]* banner to the top of the page with a *Stop* button.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Team-View-As-Banner" alt="A screenshot of CloudCannon in viewing mode shows a Viewing as banner pinned to the top of the page with a Stop button beside it." title="The Viewing as banner" type="screenshot"/>

This mode only changes what you can interact with. Any change you make is still recorded under your own name, so use it to check access rather than to work on your client's behalf. Navigating away from the *Organization* or logging out ends *View As* mode, as does the *Stop* button.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  *View As* mode is available to members of the *Owners* and *Partner Permission Groups*, so your agency can use it in every *Client Organization* you manage. You cannot view as yourself.
</comp.Notice>

## Invite a client to one Site as an Organization Member

*Site Sharing* gives your client their own CloudCannon account with access to a single *Site*, so they never see anything else in the *Organization* that holds it. Every *Site* has its own *Site Sharing Permission Groups*, whether it sits in your agency *Organization* or a *Client Organization*.

As with inviting a client as a full *Organization Member*, inviting them using *Site Sharing* requires you to add them to at least one *Permission Group*. CloudCannon provides two *Site Sharing Permission Groups* per *Site*: *[Site Name] Editors* and *[Site Name] Technical Editors*.

These *Permission Groups* contain broadly the same permissions as their *Organization*-wide counterparts, but scoped to a single *Site*.

* **[Site Name] Editors** — Your client edits content, uploads images, and reads form submissions. They cannot open the *Source Editor*, so they never see raw Markdown or YAML and cannot break a build by editing front matter. This suits most clients.
* **[Site Name] Technical Editors** — Everything above, plus the *Source Editor*, site analytics, and control over builds, backups, and *Build Deploys*. Choose this for a client who is comfortable in raw files, or who wants to see their own traffic figures.

Clients invited this way use one *Seat* on the *Subscription Plan* of whichever *Organization* holds the *Site*. You can invite a client to several *Sites* in the same *Organization* without using more than one *Seat*. They cannot receive or pay their own invoices through *Site Sharing*, because they are a member of one *Site* rather than of the *Organization* that bills for it.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Both groups can publish to the live *Site*. If you want to review a client's changes before they go live, that comes from the *Site's* publishing workflow rather than from their *Permission Group*.
</comp.Notice>

To invite a client as an *Organization Member* via *Site Sharing*, click the *Sites* link in the *App Sidebar* and open the *Site* you want to add them to by clicking on it in the *Sites Browser*.

Click on the *Site Settings* link in your *Site Navigation* and navigate to the *Site Sharing* page, which lists everyone who can already reach this *Site* and the *Permission Group* each belongs to.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Site-Sharing" alt="A screenshot of the Site Sharing page under Site Settings shows three Organization Members shared on the Site, listed with the Permission Group each belongs to, beside an Add Members button." title="The Site Sharing page" type="screenshot"/>

Click the *Add Members* button to open the *Invite Organization Members to your Site* modal. Enter your client's email address in the *Email address* field, choose the *Permission Group* you decided on in the *Group* dropdown, and click the *Add Member* button to send the invitation. CloudCannon emails your client, and asks them to create an account if they do not have one.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Site-Sharing-Invite-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the invite modal on the Site Sharing page shows an Email address field and a Group dropdown, above the Add Member button." title="The Site Sharing invite modal" type="screenshot"/>

As described above, you can use *View As* mode to review your client's access once they accept their invitation.

## Enable Guest Access for a Site

*Guest Access* suits a client who edits rarely and does not want another account to manage. Instead of an invitation, you set a *Guest Password* on one *Site*, and anyone with that password reaches a simplified version of CloudCannon for that single *Site*. The simplified CloudCannon UI has the *Dashboard* and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/collection.yml">Collections</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the *Site Navigation*, but no *File Browser* or *Site Settings*. There is also no *App Sidebar*, so the *Organization's* other *Sites* and your other clients stay out of view. Your client sees the *Organization's* name and branding in the header, beside a *Sign Out* link.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Sharing-Dashboard-Summary-Tab" alt="A screenshot of the simplified Site Dashboard a guest sees shows a Site Navigation reduced to Dashboard, Pages, Blog, Data, Services, and Staff Members, beside a Recent activity feed and a Save button." title="The Site Dashboard a guest sees" type="screenshot"/>

By default, anyone who logs into a *Site* with a *Guest Password* will have the same permissions as the *[Site Name] Editors* group from *Site Sharing*: they can edit content, upload images, read form submissions, and publish to the live *Site*. On the *Team Plan* and *Enterprise Plan*, you can edit these permissions to create a custom set.

Each *Site* in your *Organization* with *Guest Access* enabled counts as one *Seat* in your *Subscription Plan*, although multiple people can use the *Guest Password* you create for that *Site*. If multiple people use the same *Guest Password*, you will not be able to determine who performed an action, as they will all share the same *Guest* identity in CloudCannon.

Guests cannot receive or pay their own invoices either, because they have no CloudCannon account to bill. If your client wants to hold their own billing, they need to be an *Organization Member* of a *Client Organization*.

To enable *Guest Access* for a *Site*, click the *Sites* link in the *App Sidebar* and open the *Site* you want to add them to by clicking on it in the *Sites Browser*.

Click on the *Site Settings* link in your *Site Navigation* and navigate to the *Guest Access* page, which displays a *Guest Access is off* card.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Client-Sharing-Page" alt="A screenshot of the Guest Access page in Site Settings shows a Guest Access is off card above a Password field with a strength meter, and a Set password and turn on button." title="The Guest Access page before it is turned on" type="screenshot"/>

Enter a password in the *Password* field and click the *Set password and turn on* button. CloudCannon will turn *Guest Access* on for this *Site*, and the card will change to *Guest Access is on* above the address of the *Guest Login Page* your client uses.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Sharing-Enabled-Password-Tab" alt="A screenshot of the Guest Access page in Site Settings shows a Guest Access is on card above the Guest Login Page address, with Password, Author, Permissions, and Settings tabs below it." title="The Guest Access page once it is turned on" type="screenshot"/>

Four tabs appear once *Guest Access* is on:

* **Password** — Replace the *Guest Password* at any time. CloudCannon stores the password encrypted and never shows it again, so keep your own record of it when you set it. If a client loses theirs, setting a new one is the only way to restore access. A new password stops the old one working for new sign-ins, but it does not sign out anyone who is already in.
* **Author** — Set the Git author name and email recorded against your guests' changes, available on the *Team Plan* and *Enterprise Plan*. The *Git author name* field defaults to `CloudCannon` and the *Git author email* field defaults to `support@cloudcannon.com`, and the committer stays CloudCannon whichever you set. Use this when a client's repository history needs to show who asked for a change.
* **Permissions** — Narrow what guests can reach, down to individual files with *File globs*. The scope is fixed to this *Site*, and editing the resources requires the *Team Plan* or *Enterprise Plan*, the same gate as *Custom Permission Groups*. On other *Subscription Plans* the tab shows the defaults read-only.
* **Settings** — Change the *URL subpath* your guests log in at, which defaults to `update`, and add *Documentation URL* and *Support URL* links to their sidebar so they have somewhere to go before they contact you.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Don't turn on *Guest Access* for someone who is already an *Organization Member*. Each *Site* with *Guest Access* enabled uses one *Seat* on top of the people with accounts, so the same person consumes two *Seats* and the *Organization* pays for one nobody is using. Pick one method for each person.
</comp.Notice>

For more information, please read our documentation on [what Guest Access is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-client-sharing/).

## Choose who pays for your client

Once your client's *Site* is created and they have access, you can choose who pays for their work on CloudCannon: your agency, with the cost folded into your existing billing structure for clients, or the client directly.

Each *Organization* has its own payment method, and members of the *Owners*, *Billing*, and *Partner Default Permission Groups* can edit payment details. If you want your client to pay their invoice directly, you must have set up a *Client Organization* and invited them as a full *Organization Member*.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Members of the *Billing Permission Group* can change the *Subscription Plan* for their *Organization*, not only pay for it, so your client can upgrade or downgrade once they have that access.
</comp.Notice>

Adding a payment method works the same way in every *Organization*, so you can follow these steps yourself or send them to your client to follow in their own *Client Organization*.

Click the *Org Settings* link in the *App Sidebar* and open the *Subscription* page. Scroll to the *Billing Details* section and click the *Add Credit Card* button to open the *Add your Credit Card* modal.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Subscription-Add-Credit-Card-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add your Credit Card modal shows a Name on card field and a collapsed Additional info section, above the Add Credit Card button." title="The Add your Credit Card modal" type="screenshot"/>

Enter the card details and click the *Add Credit Card* button. CloudCannon will confirm the card with the bank and attach it to that *Organization*. Some banks need a billing address before they will approve a card, so if a payment is declined, expand the *Additional info* section in the modal and add the address, city, state, postal code, and country.

Once an *Organization* has a card, the button becomes *Manage Credit Cards*, which is where you replace a card or remove one you no longer use.

To add your agency's card to a *Client Organization*, open that *Organization* first by clicking the *Clients* link in the *App Sidebar* and then your client's card in the *Client Organizations Browser*.

Who pays and who receives the invoices are separate choices. CloudCannon emails invoices to the address in the *Billing Email* field, which you reach by clicking the *Update contact info* button in the *Billing Details* section of the *Subscription* page. This means you can send a client's invoices to them while your card covers the cost, or the other way around. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding billing contact information](/documentation/user-articles/add-billing-contact-information/) and [managing your payment methods](/documentation/user-articles/manage-your-payment-methods/).

### Keep an eye on your client's billing

<comp.Notice info_type="pricing">
  **This feature is available through a private Beta.**

  *Health Checks* and the *Health* tab are currently available through a private Beta. Access is granted per account rather than per *Organization*, so each person at your agency who needs it must be added individually. To request access, please contact our friendly <a href="mailto:success@cloudcannon.com">support team</a>.
</comp.Notice>

Handing a client their own billing does not mean losing sight of it. CloudCannon runs <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/health-check.yml">Health Checks</comp.GlossaryTerm> across every *Client Organization* you manage and reports them on the *Health* tab of your *Partner Organization Home* page, so a client whose payment fails surfaces on your dashboard rather than only on theirs.

Two of those checks concern billing. *Subscriptions need attention* is a *Danger* check that counts *Client Organizations* whose *Subscription* is overdue, unpaid, or paused, any of which can put that *Organization* on hold. *Free Trials are ending soon* is a *Warning* check for clients whose trial ends in the next few days without a *Subscription Plan* chosen.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Partner-Organization-Home-Issues-Tab" alt="A screenshot of the Issues tab on a Partner Organization Home page shows Health Checks grouped under Danger, Warning, and Passed, including Subscriptions need attention listing one Client Organization as past due." title="The Issues tab on Partner Organization Home" type="screenshot"/>

Each check lists the affected clients by name. Click a *Client Organization* to open the *Health* tab on its own *Organization Home* page, where the specific problem and the link to fix it appear. You can also filter for these clients directly in the *Client Organizations Browser*.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  You can resolve these yourself. The *Partner Permission Group* holds full permissions in every *Client Organization* you manage, so your agency's *Owners* can open a client's *Subscription* page and update their payment details, as can anyone from your team you have added to a group with the `org:billing:write` permission there. Whether you should is a separate question: if your client holds their own payment method, replacing it with yours moves the cost onto your agency.
</comp.Notice>

### Remove a client's access

When a relationship with a client ends, how you revoke access depends on the method you used to grant it.

* **A full *Organization Member*** — On the *Members* tab of the *People* page, click the context menu icon on their card and select *Remove from Organization*. CloudCannon will ask you to confirm, and warn you that a current *Organization Member* must invite them to rejoin.
* **A *Site Sharing* recipient** — On the *Site Sharing* page under *Site Settings*, click the context menu icon on their card and select *Remove from Site*. They keep their CloudCannon account and anything else they can reach.
* **A guest** — Click the *Turn Off* button on the *Guest Access* page. *Guest Access* has no way to revoke one person, so this ends it for that *Site* and signs out everyone using the *Guest Password*. Setting a new password instead stops the old one working for new sign-ins, but leaves anyone already signed in where they are. If one person needs to lose access while the rest keep theirs, use *Site Sharing*, where each client has their own account to remove.

Removing someone frees the *Seat* they were using on your *Subscription Plan*.

## Enable Overages to avoid client downtime

In the previous step of this guide, we covered extending a *Subscription Plan* with <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/overage.yml">Overages</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and recommended turning *Overages* on for every *Organization* you manage so that reaching a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm> or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm> limit never takes a client's website offline. Now that you have decided who pays, you can do that.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  An *Organization* needs a credit card attached before you can turn *Overages* on, and a *Free Trial* does not exempt it. If the *Organization* has no payment method yet, CloudCannon will tell you to add one and try again.
</comp.Notice>

To turn *Overages* on for an *Organization*, click the *Org Settings* link in the *App Sidebar* and open the *Subscription* page. If you are enabling *Overages* for a *Client Organization*, open that *Organization* first by clicking the *Clients* link in the *App Sidebar*, then clicking your client's card in the *Client Organizations Browser*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Subscription-Add-Ons-Off-Warning" alt="A screenshot of the Subscription page in Org Settings shows an Overages are disabled warning, explaining that reaching a Build Time or Hosting Bandwidth limit stops changes appearing on your live Site or stops CloudCannon serving webpages, above the Enable Overages and View Subscription Plans buttons." title="The Subscription page with Overages disabled" type="screenshot"/>

While *Overages* are off, CloudCannon shows an *Overages are disabled* warning, telling you that your changes won't appear on your live *Site*, or that CloudCannon will stop serving webpages to visitors, if you reach a limit.

Click the *Enable Overages* button. CloudCannon will open the *Your Subscription Plan* page with the *Overages* toggle already switched on, so you can confirm the change before it takes effect.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Your-Subscription-Plan-Add-Ons-Element" alt="A screenshot of the Overages section on the Your Subscription Plan page shows a toggle beside a description explaining that CloudCannon will automatically extend your Hosting Bandwidth with Overage Bundles when you reach the limit in your Subscription Plan." title="The Overages toggle" type="ui-snippet"/>

Turning *Overages* on does not charge you immediately. Instead, you are enabling CloudCannon to invoice you for usage above your *Subscription Plan* limits each month, so a charge will only appear on your invoice in a month where you go over one. Click the *Enable Overages* button to confirm.

CloudCannon will return you to the *Subscription* page, where the warning is replaced by an *Overages are enabled* confirmation.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Subscription-Add-Ons-On-Element" alt="A screenshot of the Subscription page in Org Settings shows a confirmation reading Overages are enabled beside a verified icon." title="The Overages are enabled confirmation" type="ui-snippet"/>

Turning *Overages* off again takes a different route, because the confirmation carries no button. On the *Subscription* page, click the *Review your Subscription Plan* button, click the *Overages* toggle to switch it off, then click the *Disable Overages* button that appears.
