The structure you chose in the first step of this guide is not permanent. You can move a client's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> between your own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> and a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> at any point, in either direction, by transferring them.

Agencies move clients more often than anyone else on CloudCannon, usually for one of two reasons. You might build a client's website in your own *Organization* where your team already has access, then move it into a *Client Organization* when the client is ready to go live, take over their own billing, or bring their own people in. Or you might move a client the other way, back into your own *Organization*, when they no longer need an *Organization* of their own and you would rather run their *Sites* alongside the rest of your work.

Transferring is the only way to move a *Site* between *Organizations*. Handing a client the *Client Organization* they already have is a different action, which leaves the *Sites* where they are. For more information, please read our documentation on [handing over or detaching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).

## What moves with a Site

A transfer takes the *Site* and everything needed to run it. That includes its SSL certificates, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inboxes</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dam.yml">DAMs</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and any other *Sites* that share those resources, so a live website keeps working on the same domain after it moves.

Two things stay behind. <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/project.yml">Projects</comp.GlossaryTerm> belong to the *Organization* that created them, so a transferred *Site* arrives without the *Project* it sat in and you group it again on the other side. <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> stay behind too, which means access does not carry over: anyone who could reach the *Site* through a *Permission Group* in the sending *Organization* loses it, and the receiving *Organization* grants access through its own groups instead.

For your own team, that usually takes care of itself. CloudCannon keeps the *Owners* of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the *Partner Permission Group* of every *Client Organization* you manage, so they reach a transferred *Site* as soon as it lands. Anyone else on your team needs adding to the destination *Client Organization* first.

## Send the transfer request

Transferring happens in two steps, and the two *Organizations* each do one of them. You send the request from the *Site*, and someone in the receiving *Organization* accepts it.

Navigate to the *Transfer Site* page under *Site Settings*, select the *Organization* you want to send the *Site* to, then click the *Send Transfer Request* button and confirm. You can only send a *Site* to an *Organization* you already belong to, which is why your own *Client Organizations* appear in the list and a client's unrelated *Organization* does not.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Transfer-Site-Page" alt="A screenshot of the Transfer Site page shows a menu for choosing the destination Organization and the Send Transfer Request button." title="The Transfer Site page in Site Settings" type="screenshot"/>

Sending and accepting both need the `org:transfer:write` permission, which the *Owners* and *Developers Permission Groups* have by default. Members of the *Partner Permission Group* have it in every *Client Organization* they manage. If the *Transfer Site* page is unavailable, ask an *Owner* to add you to a group that has it.

## Accept it in the receiving Organization

Navigate to the *Transfer Requests* page under *Org Settings* in the receiving *Organization*, find the incoming request, and click the *Accept* button. CloudCannon will move the *Site* and its resources across.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Transfer-Requests-Incoming" alt="A screenshot of the Transfer Requests page shows an incoming request with an Accept button." title="Incoming requests on the Transfer Requests page" type="screenshot"/>

Accepting moves the cost of the *Site* to the receiving *Organization*, so its bill goes up and its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> has to cover what arrives. CloudCannon asks you to confirm the extra cost and add a payment method if the *Site* brings more *Custom Domains* than that *Subscription Plan* includes, and asks you to upgrade first if it uses a DAM the *Subscription Plan* does not cover. Both need billing access, so a member of the *Billing Permission Group* or the *Owners Permission Group* may need to act before the transfer can complete.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Moving a client into a *Client Organization* during its extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> can cut that trial to three days. Accepting a transfer that takes the *Client Organization* over the *Custom Domains* or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/team-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm> its *Subscription Plan* includes ends the six months, so transfer before you connect a client's domain where you can. Adding members of your *Partner Organization* does not affect the trial.
</comp.Notice>

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Once the receiving *Organization* accepts, you cannot undo a transfer. Moving the *Site* back means someone in that *Organization* sending it to you as a new transfer request, and that is only possible while you still have a way into it. If you are moving a client's *Sites* out as part of ending an engagement, transfer them before you detach the *Client Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

You can cancel a request you sent while it is still waiting, from the *Transfer Requests* page under *Org Settings*. For the full walkthrough, including declining an incoming request, please read our documentation on [transferring a Site between Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/transfer-a-site-between-organizations/).
