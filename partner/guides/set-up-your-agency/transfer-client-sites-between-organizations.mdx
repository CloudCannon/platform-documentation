You can change whether your client <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> are stored in your own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> or a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> at any time by <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/transfer.yml">transferring</comp.GlossaryTerm> *Sites* between *Organizations*.

Agencies move clients more often than anyone else on CloudCannon, usually for one of two reasons:

* You might build a client's website in your own *Organization* where your team already has access, then move it into a *Client Organization* when the client is ready to go live, take over their own billing, or bring their own people in.
* You might move a client website from a *Client Organization* back into your own *Organization*, when they no longer need an *Organization* of their own and you would rather run their *Sites* alongside the rest of your work.

To transfer a *Site* to another *Organization*, you must be an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization-member.yml">Organization Member</comp.GlossaryTerm> of both the sending and receiving *Organization*.

Transferring is the only way to move a *Site* between *Organizations*. Handing a client the *Client Organization* they already have is a different action, which leaves the *Sites* where they are. For more information, please read our documentation on [handing over or detaching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).

## What does transferring a Site move?

When you transfer a *Site*, CloudCannon moves everything that *Site* needs to run as part of the transfer request. This includes:

* SSL certificates
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inboxes</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dam.yml">DAMs</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site-sharing.yml">Site Sharing</comp.GlossaryTerm> <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/guest-access.yml">Guest Access</comp.GlossaryTerm>, including its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/guest-password.yml">Guest Password</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* Any other *Sites* that share the above resources
* Any other *Sites* connected to it through <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site-mounting.yml">Site Mounting</comp.GlossaryTerm>

Accepting a transfer request for a *Site* means accepting the transfer of all the connected resources. *Sites* with *Custom Domains*, *Guest Access* enabled, and members in *Site Sharing Permission Groups* may increase the invoice for that *Organization*.

CloudCannon does not transfer <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/project.yml">Projects</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/default-permission-groups.yml">Default Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-permission-group.yml">Custom Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> of the sending *Organization*. *Site Sharing Permission Groups* move with the *Site*, so their members keep access. Anyone who reaches the *Site* through the sending *Organization's* own *Permission Groups* loses access to it and its resources, unless they are also a member of the receiving *Organization*.

When you transfer between your own *Organization* and a *Client Organization*, the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/owners.yml">Owners</comp.GlossaryTerm> of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> are always members of that *Client Organization's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner.yml">Partner Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so they keep access. Anyone else at your agency needs adding to the *Client Organization* first.

## Send the transfer request

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em>, <em>Developers</em>, and <em>Partner</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:transfer:write</code> permission, can send a transfer request from an <em>Organization</em> and accept one into it.</p>
</comp.Notice>

Transferring a *Site* and its connected resources happens in two phases: the original *Organization* sends a transfer request from the *Site*, and the recipient *Organization* accepts it.

To send a transfer request, start in the *Organization* that holds the *Site*. If the *Site* is in a *Client Organization*, open that *Organization* first by clicking the *Clients* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then your client's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the *Client Organizations Browser*. Open the *Site* and navigate to the *Transfer Site* page under *Site Settings*. Choose the *Organization* you want to send the *Site* to in the *Organization to receive* field, and click the *Send Transfer Request* button. If you are a member of the *Owners Permission Group* in your *Partner Organization*, every *Client Organization* you manage appears in the list, but a client's unrelated *Organization* does not.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Transfer-Site-Page" alt="A screenshot of the Transfer Site page shows a menu for choosing the destination Organization and the Send Transfer Request button." title="The Transfer Site page in Site Settings" type="screenshot"/>

CloudCannon will open a *Please confirm this action* modal. Click the *Confirm* button to send the request.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Transfer-Site-Confirm-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Transfer Site page with a Client Organization chosen in the Organization to receive field shows the Please confirm this action modal, with Cancel and Confirm buttons." title="The Please confirm this action modal" type="screenshot"/>

CloudCannon allows one open request at a time for the same resources. If you try to send a second request for a *Site* that shares resources with one already waiting, CloudCannon will tell you a request already exists.

## Accept the transfer request in the receiving Organization

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em>, <em>Developers</em>, and <em>Partner</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:transfer:write</code> permission, can accept a transfer request into an <em>Organization</em>.</p>
</comp.Notice>

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Once the receiving *Organization* accepts, you cannot undo a transfer. Moving the *Site* back means someone in that *Organization* sending it to you as a new transfer request, for which you must be a member of both *Organizations*. If you are moving a client's *Sites* out as part of ending an engagement, transfer them before you detach the *Client Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

To accept a transfer request, navigate to the *Transfer Requests* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the receiving *Organization*. Find the incoming request and click the *Accept* button. CloudCannon will move the *Site* and its resources across.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Transfer-Requests-Incoming" alt="A screenshot of the Transfer Requests page shows an incoming request with an Accept button." title="Incoming requests on the Transfer Requests page" type="screenshot"/>

If the *Sites*, *Custom Domains*, or SSL certificates in the request have changed since it was sent (for example, someone updated the *Custom Domain*), accepting the request will fail, and CloudCannon will prompt you to have the sender send a new request.

Before anything else, CloudCannon checks that the receiving *Organization's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> covers any DAM the *Site* uses. If it does not, CloudCannon will open an *Upgrade required* modal, and someone with the `org:billing:write` permission needs to upgrade the *Subscription Plan* before you can accept.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Org-Transfer-Requests-Upgrade-Required-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Transfer Requests page in a Client Organization shows the Upgrade required modal, explaining that DAM support is not available on the current Subscription Plan, with Cancel and View Subscription Plans buttons." title="The Upgrade required modal" type="screenshot"/>

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Transferring a *Site* into a *Client Organization* that is on an extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> can cut that trial short so that it ends five days later.

  Accepting a transfer that brings a *Custom Domain*, a *Site* with *Guest Access* turned on, or a *Site Sharing* member who is not already in the *Client Organization* ends the six months, whether or not the *Client Organization* is still within the limits of its *Subscription Plan*. CloudCannon shows a *Confirm End of Trial* modal before the transfer completes, and does not show the *Confirm Billing Change* or *Add your Credit Card* modals for an *Organization* on its extended trial. To keep the full six months while you build, transfer the *Site* before you connect a client's domain. Inviting members of your *Partner Organization* does not affect the trial, but in a transfer your own staff count like anyone else if they are *Site Sharing* members of the *Site* and not yet in the *Client Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Org-Transfer-Requests-Confirm-End-Of-Trial-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Transfer Requests page in a Client Organization on its extended Free Trial shows the Confirm End of Trial modal, explaining that the trial will end in 5 days, with Cancel and End extended trial buttons." title="The Confirm End of Trial modal" type="screenshot"/>

Accepting moves the cost of the *Site* to the receiving *Organization*. For any *Organization* not on an extended *Free Trial*, if accepting the transfer would take that *Organization* over the *Custom Domains* or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm> its *Subscription Plan* includes, CloudCannon will open a *Confirm Billing Change* modal, then an *Add your Credit Card* modal if the *Organization* has no payment method. Anyone who can accept the transfer can confirm the extra cost, but adding a payment method needs the `org:billing:write` permission, which the *Owners*, *Billing*, and *Partner Permission Groups* have by default.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Client-Org-Transfer-Requests-Confirm-Billing-Change-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Transfer Requests page in a Client Organization shows the Confirm Billing Change modal, explaining that the transfer will increase billing, with Cancel and Accept Billing Increase buttons." title="The Confirm Billing Change modal" type="screenshot"/>

Transferring every *Site* out of a *Client Organization* does not close it. The *Client Organization* keeps its *Subscription Plan* and its invoices until you cancel its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Once the *Subscription* is canceled, a member of the *Owners* or *Partner Permission Group* can delete the *Client Organization*. For more information, please read our documentation on [canceling your Subscription](/documentation/user-articles/cancel-your-subscription/) and [deleting an Organization](/documentation/user-articles/delete-an-organization/).

You can cancel a request you sent while it is still waiting, from the *Transfer Requests* page under *Org Settings*. For the full walkthrough, including declining an incoming request, please read our documentation on [transferring a Site between Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/transfer-a-site-between-organizations/).
