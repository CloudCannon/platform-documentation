You can change whether your client <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> are stored in your own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> or a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> at any time by <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/transfer.yml">transferring</comp.GlossaryTerm> *Sites* between *Organizations*.

Agencies move clients more often than anyone else on CloudCannon, usually for one of two reasons:

* You might build a client's website in your own *Organization* where your team already has access, then move it into a *Client Organization* when the client is ready to go live, take over their own billing, or bring their own people in.
* You might move a client website from a *Client Organization* back into your own *Organization*, when they no longer need an *Organization* of their own and you would rather run their *Sites* alongside the rest of your work.

To transfer a *Site* to another *Organization*, you must be an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization-member.yml">Organization Member</comp.GlossaryTerm> of both the sending and receiving *Organization*.

Transferring is the only way to move a *Site* between *Organizations*. Handing a client the *Client Organization* they already have is a different action, which leaves the *Sites* where they are. For more information, please read our documentation on [handing over or detaching a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).

## What does transferring a Site move?

When you transfer a *Site*, CloudCannon also moved everything that *Site* needs to run as part of the transfer request. This includes:

* SSL certificates
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inboxes</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dam.yml">DAMs</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site-sharing.yml">Site Sharing</comp.GlossaryTerm> <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm>
* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/guest-access.yml">Guest Access</comp.GlossaryTerm> passwords continue to work
* Any other *Sites* that share the above resources
* Any other *Sites* connected to it through <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site-mounting.yml">Site Mounting</comp.GlossaryTerm>

Accepting a transfer request for a *Site* means accepting the transfer of all the connected resources. *Sites* with *Custom Domains*, *Guest Access* enabled, and members in *Site Sharing* *Permission Groups* may increase the invoice for that *Organization*.

CloudCannon does not transfer <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/project.yml">Projects</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/default-permission-groups.yml">Default Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> other than *Site Sharing*, or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-permission-groups.yml">Default Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Unless an *Organization Member* is also a member of the recipient *Organization*, they will lose access to the transferred *Site* and its resources.

For web development agencies transfering between their own *Organization* and a *Client Organization*, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/owners.yml">Owners</comp.GlossaryTerm> of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/partner-organization.yml">Partner Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> always have membership in the *Partner Permission Group* of every *Client Organization*, so will always have access. Anyone else that needs access must be added to the *Client Organization* first.

## Send the transfer request

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  <p><strong>Permissions required</strong></p>
  <p>Members of the <em>Owners</em>, <em>Developers</em>, and <em>Partner</em> <a href="/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/">Default Permission Groups</a>, or <a href="/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/">Custom Permission Groups</a> with the <code>org:transfer:write</code> permission, can send a transfer request from an <em>Organization</em> and accept one into it.</p>
</comp.Notice>

Transfering a *Site* and its connected resources happens in two phases: the original *Organization* will send a transfer request from the *Site*, and the recipient *Organization* accepts that transfer.

To initiate a *Transfer Request*, navigate to the *Transfer Site* page under *Site Settings*. Choose the *Organization* you want to send the *Site* to in the *Organization to receive* field, and click the *Send Transfer Request* button. If you are a member of the *Owners Permission Group* in your *Partner Organization*, every *Client Organization* you manage appears in the list, but a client's unrelated *Organization* does not.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Transfer-Site-Page" alt="A screenshot of the Transfer Site page shows a menu for choosing the destination Organization and the Send Transfer Request button." title="The Transfer Site page in Site Settings" type="screenshot"/>

CloudCannon will open a *Please confirm this action* modal. Click the *Confirm* button to send the request.

[docshot]

CloudCannon allows one open request at a time for the same resources. If you try to send a second request for a *Site* that shares resources with one already waiting, CloudCannon will tell you a request already exists.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Once the receiving *Organization* accepts, you cannot undo a transfer. Moving the *Site* back means someone in that *Organization* sending it to you as a new transfer request, for which you must be a member of both *Organizations*. If you are moving a client's *Sites* out as part of ending an engagement, transfer them before you detach the *Client Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

To accept a *Transfer Request*, navigate to the *Transfer Requests* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the receiving *Organization*. Find the incoming request and click the *Accept* button. CloudCannon will move the *Site* and its resources across.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Transfer-Requests-Incoming" alt="A screenshot of the Transfer Requests page shows an incoming request with an Accept button." title="Incoming requests on the Transfer Requests page" type="screenshot"/>

If the *Site's* connected resources have changed after the request was sent (e.g., someone updated the *Custom Domain*), accepting the request will fail, and CloudCannon will prompt you to have the sender send a new request.

Accepting moves the cost of the *Site* to the receiving *Organization*. If accepting the transfer would take that *Organization* over the *Custom Domains* or *Seats* its *Subscription Plan* includes, CloudCannon will open a *Confirm Billing Change* modal, and an *Add your Credit Card* modal if the *Organization* has no payment method. Anyone who can accept the transfer can confirm the extra cost, but adding a payment method needs the `org:billing:write` permission, which the *Owners*, *Billing*, and *Partner Permission Groups* have by default.

[docshot] The Confirm Billing Change modal that opens when accepting a transfer takes the receiving Organization over its Custom Domains or Seats, with the Accept Billing Increase button.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Transfering a *Site* into a *Client Organization* that is on an extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm> can cut that trial short to five days.
  
  Accepting a transfer that brings a *Custom Domain*, a *Site* with *Guest Access* turned on, or a *Site Sharing* member who is not already in the *Client Organization* ends the six months, whether or not the *Client Organization* is still within the limits of its *Subscription Plan*. CloudCannon shows a *Confirm End of Trial* modal before the transfer completes. To keep the full six months while you build, transfer the *Site* before you connect a client's domain. Adding members of your *Partner Organization* does not affect the trial.

  [docshot] The Confirm End of Trial modal that opens when accepting a transfer ends a Client Organization's extended Free Trial, with the End extended trial button.
</comp.Notice>

If the *Site* uses a DAM the *Subscription Plan* does not cover, CloudCannon will open an *Upgrade required* modal instead, and someone with the `org:billing:write` permission needs to upgrade the *Subscription Plan* before you can accept.

[docshot] The Upgrade required modal that opens when accepting a transfer of a Site whose DAM the receiving Subscription Plan does not cover, with the View Subscription Plans button.

Transfering every *Site* out of a *Client Organization* does not close it. The *Client Organization* keeps its *Subscription Plan* and its invoices until you cancel its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Once the *Subscription* is canceled, a member of the *Owners* or *Partner Permission Group* can delete the *Client Organization*. For more information, please read our documentation on [canceling your Subscription](/documentation/user-articles/cancel-your-subscription/) and [deleting an Organization](/documentation/user-articles/delete-an-organization/).

You can cancel a request you sent while it is still waiting, from the *Transfer Requests* page under *Org Settings*. For the full walkthrough, including declining an incoming request, please read our documentation on [transferring a Site between Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/transfer-a-site-between-organizations/).
