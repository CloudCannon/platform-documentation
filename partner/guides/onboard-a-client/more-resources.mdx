Congratulations! 🎉 You have finished the Onboard a client guide.

By following each step, you've taken a client from an empty setup through to a working, billed *Organization*, and you know how to hand it over when the time comes.

## Partner Articles

You can find more resources about the topics we covered under the [Partner Articles](/documentation/partner-articles/) section of our documentation. Here are some specific articles you may find helpful:

### Structure and manage client work

* [Choosing how to structure your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/) — Compare keeping *Sites* in your *Organization* with separate *Client Organizations*.
* [Create a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/) — Set up a new *Client Organization* step by step.
* [Manage your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/) — Work across every *Client Organization* you run.

### Access, billing, and rewards

* [Partner access and permissions](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-access-and-permissions/) — Understand the *Partner* group, who owns a *Client Organization*, and what the client can see.
* [Billing in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/billing-in-the-partner-program/) — See who pays for what across the two structuring models.
* [Partner Rewards and Points](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-rewards-and-points/) — Learn how you earn points and move through partner tiers.

### End the engagement

* [Hand over or detach a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/) — Give the client control, or detach the *Organization* so it runs independently.
