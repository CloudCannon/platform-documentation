How you set the client up follows the structure you chose in the previous step.

## If you're creating a Client Organization

Create the *Client Organization* from the *Client Organizations Browser* in your Partner *Organization*, giving it a name and a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. It starts on a six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so you can build the *Sites* and hand them over without paying until the client is ready to go live.

For the steps, read [Create a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/create-a-client-organization/). To work across all the *Client Organizations* you manage, read [Manage your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/).

## If you're keeping the Sites in your Organization

Create the client's *Sites* from the *Sites Browser* the same way you create any *Site*. You can group them into a *Project* to keep them together, and they use your Partner *Organization*'s existing *Subscription Plan*, so taking on the client doesn't require a new subscription.

With the client set up, the next step is to give your team and the client the right access.
