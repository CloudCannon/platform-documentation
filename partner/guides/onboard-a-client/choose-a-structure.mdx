Your first decision is where the client's *Sites* will live. CloudCannon gives you two ways to structure client work, and you can use either for a given client:

* **Keep the client's *Sites* in your Partner *Organization***, alongside your other clients. This is the simplest setup and suits smaller clients or proof-of-concept work.
* **Give the client their own *Client Organization***, a separate *Organization* your Partner *Organization* manages. This suits clients who need their own users, billing, or login, or who you expect to hand the work over to later.

You don't have to use the same approach for every client, and you can move a client from one to the other later by transferring their *Sites*.

For a full comparison, including how each option affects access and billing, read [Choosing how to structure your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/).

Once you've decided, the next step is to set the client up in CloudCannon.
