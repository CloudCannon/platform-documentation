With the client running, two things happen in the background: billing, and your *Partner Rewards*.

## Billing

Who pays follows the structure you chose:

* Client *Sites* kept in your Partner *Organization* are billed on your Partner *Organization*'s subscription, on your invoice.
* A *Client Organization* has its own subscription and invoice, normally paid by the client.

For a fuller explanation, including the tradeoffs of each model, read [Billing in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/billing-in-the-partner-program/).

## Partner Rewards

Every active client you run on CloudCannon earns you *Partner Points*, whether you keep their *Sites* in your Partner *Organization* or give them a *Client Organization*. Points move you up through partner tiers that unlock benefits such as discounts and an Experts Directory listing.

To see how points are earned and track your tier, read [Partner Rewards and Points](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-rewards-and-points/).
