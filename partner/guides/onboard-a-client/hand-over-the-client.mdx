Onboarding a client isn't the start of a countdown to handover. Most partner relationships are ongoing: you keep managing the client's work for as long as the engagement lasts, billing continues under whichever model you set up, and an active *Client Organization* keeps earning you *Partner Points*. To continue, there's nothing extra to do, just keep managing the client's work as covered in [Manage your Client Organizations](/documentation/partner-articles/manage-your-client-organizations/).

If the relationship does change, you have two options for a *Client Organization*, depending on how far you want to step back.

* **Handover.** Because you created the *Client Organization*, you start as its *Owner*. To give the client control, add them to the *Owners* *Permission Group*, then remove yourself if you're stepping away. The *Client Organization* stays linked to your Partner *Organization*.
* **Detach.** To fully separate the *Client Organization* so it runs independently of your Partner *Organization*, it needs to be detached. This removes the *Partner* group and ends the free-team and partner-billing arrangement, so the *Organization* needs its own payment method. Detaching isn't a self-serve action in the app today, so coordinate it with CloudCannon support.

For the full details of both, read [Hand over or detach a Client Organization](/documentation/partner-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).
