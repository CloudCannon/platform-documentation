With the client set up, decide who needs access: your team, and the client themselves.

## Your team

If you created a *Client Organization*, your Partner *Organization*'s *Owners* already have access through the *Partner* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm>. To bring in other team members, add them to the *Client Organization* the same way you add anyone to an *Organization*. Because they belong to your Partner *Organization*, they don't count toward the *Client Organization*'s seat limit.

For how partner access works, including who owns a new *Client Organization*, read [Partner access and permissions](/documentation/partner-articles/partner-access-and-permissions/).

## The client

How the client edits or reviews depends on your structure:

* In a *Client Organization*, the client gets their own users and login, added like any team member.
* When you keep the client's *Sites* in your own *Organization*, give them access with *Client Sharing*, *Site Sharing*, or a paid seat, depending on what they need. Read [Choosing how to structure your clients' Sites](/documentation/partner-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/) for a comparison of these options.
