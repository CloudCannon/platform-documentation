Welcome to the Onboard a client guide!

This guide is for agencies and freelancers in the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/what-is-the-cloudcannon-partner-program/) who are taking on a new client. It walks through the full lifecycle, from deciding how to structure the client's work through to running an ongoing relationship, and links to the detailed article for each step. Many partner relationships continue indefinitely, so handing a client over is an option, not a required final step.

In this guide, we'll cover how to:

* Choose how to structure a client's *Sites*
* Set up the client in CloudCannon
* Give your team and the client the right access
* Understand billing and track your *Partner Rewards*
* Continue the relationship, or hand the client over if the engagement ends

Before we begin, this guide assumes:

* **Your Organization is in the Partner Program.** If it isn't yet, visit the <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/">CloudCannon Partner Program</a> page to apply. Once you're enrolled, the *Clients* and *Rewards* items appear in your *App Sidebar*.
* **You can create Client Organizations.** Only an *Owner* of your Partner <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> can create a *Client Organization*.

In the first step, we'll look at the two ways to structure a client's work and how to choose between them.
