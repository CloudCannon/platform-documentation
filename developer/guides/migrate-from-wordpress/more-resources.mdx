Congratulations on moving your website from WordPress to CloudCannon! 🎉

Thanks for finishing the *Migrate from WordPress to CloudCannon* guide! If you have any questions, please reach out to our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> or check out the <a href="https://community.cloudcannon.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CloudCannon Community</a>. We're always here to help.

If you contact support about your migration, include your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> names, the SSG you chose, and the inventory files from the planning step: `post-types.csv`, `taxonomies.csv`, and `plugins.csv`.

## Help your team edit in CloudCannon

The people who edited your WordPress site will now edit in CloudCannon. WordPress accounts do not move to CloudCannon, so each person needs to join your *Organization*. These resources help you add your team and show them how to edit.

* [Add an Organization Member to your Organization](/documentation/user-articles/add-an-organization-member-to-your-organization/) — How to invite each person to CloudCannon and choose what they can do.
* [WordPress concepts in CloudCannon](/documentation/developer-guides/migrate-from-wordpress/wordpress-concepts-in-cloudcannon/) — The CloudCannon names for post types, custom fields, the Media Library, roles, and other WordPress concepts.
* [Editing in CloudCannon](/documentation/user-guides/editing-in-cloudcannon/) — How to edit files in CloudCannon's editing interfaces and save changes back to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
* [View your form submissions](/documentation/user-articles/view-your-form-submissions/) — Where to find the submissions from your website's forms, for a *Site* hosted on CloudCannon.

## Configure your Site further

These resources cover CloudCannon configuration beyond what this guide set up.

* [Set up Visual Editing](/documentation/developer-guides/set-up-visual-editing/) — How to add <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/editable-region.yml">Editable Regions</comp.GlossaryTerm> to your templates, so your team can edit content on a preview of each page.
* [Create a schema](/documentation/developer-articles/create-a-schema/) — How to give each new file in a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/collection.yml">Collection</comp.GlossaryTerm> the front matter its post type needs.
* [Populate a Select input](/documentation/developer-articles/populate-a-select-input/) — How to fill select inputs from fixed lists, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dataset.yml">Datasets</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and other *Collections*.
* [Configure custom routing](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-custom-routing/) — Every option for redirects and custom headers on a *Site* hosted on CloudCannon.
