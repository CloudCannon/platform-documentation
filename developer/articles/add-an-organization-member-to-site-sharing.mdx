<comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site-sharing.yml">Site Sharing</comp.GlossaryTerm> allows you to manage which Organization Members have access to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> and control their permissions. You can add existing Organization Members from your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> or invite new users by email.

To add an Organization Member to your *Site*:

1. Navigate to the *Site Sharing* page under *Site Settings* in the *App Sidebar*.
2. Click the *+ Add members* button. CloudCannon will open the *Invite Organization Members to your Site* modal.
3. Enter the name of the Organization Members you want to add to this *Site*. You can also enter the email addresses of new users you want to invite to your *Organization*.
4. Select the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Group</comp.GlossaryTerm> to which you want to add these Organization Members.
5. Click the *Add members* button.

CloudCannon will add the selected Organization Members to your *Site* with the chosen *Permission Group*.
