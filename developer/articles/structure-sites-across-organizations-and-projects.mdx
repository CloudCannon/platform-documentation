When you manage many *Sites* in CloudCannon, you can keep them together in one *Organization* or split them across several. Both work, and the right structure depends on how much you want to share versus separate.

## One Organization with many *Sites*

  alt="A diagram shows one Organization containing two Projects, each holding one or more Sites."
  chart={`graph TD
    O["Organization"] --> P1["Project"]
    O["Organization"] --> P2["Project"]
    P1 --> S1["Site"]
    P1 --> S2["Site"]
    P2 --> S3["Site"]
  `}
/>

An *Organization* can hold any number of *Sites*, and you can group related *Sites* into *Projects* (for example, by repository or publishing workflow). Everything in the *Organization* shares:

* One team and one set of *Permission Groups*.
* One subscription, plan, and invoice.
* One set of *Organization* settings and branding.

This is the simplest structure, and it suits a single team or company that runs many related *Sites*. Use *Projects* to keep those *Sites* organized within the *Organization*.

## Multiple Organizations

<comp.Mermaid
  alt="A diagram shows three separate Organizations, each containing its own Sites."
  chart={`graph TD
    O1["Organization"] --> A1["Site"]
    O1 --> A2["Site"]
    O2["Organization"] --> B1["Site"]
    O3["Organization"] --> C1["Site"]
  `}
/>

Splitting *Sites* across separate *Organizations* gives each one its own team, billing, and settings. Choose this when you need hard separation, such as:

* Different departments, brands, or campaigns that should be billed and administered separately.
* Clients who need their own team and invoices. If you're an agency, see [Choosing how to structure your clients' Sites](/documentation/developer-articles/structure-your-clients-sites/) for the Partner Program options.

The trade-off is more to manage: each *Organization* has its own team, subscription, and settings to maintain.

## What's shared and what's separate

* **Shared within an Organization:** team, *Permission Groups*, subscription and billing, *Organization* settings and branding.
* **Per *Site*:** content, configuration, publishing workflow, and editing interfaces.

## Moving *Sites* later

You're not locked in. You can move a *Site* from one *Organization* to another as your structure changes. For the steps, please read our documentation on transferring a Site between Organizations.
