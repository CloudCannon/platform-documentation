<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:dns:read` and `base-domain:settings:dns:write` permissions, can view and change the DNS of a *Base Domain*.
</comp.Notice>

<comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm> allows you to manage your DNS Records within CloudCannon for each <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> connected to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon adds the records that point your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> and its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomains</comp.GlossaryTerm> at CloudCannon, and the TXT Records for your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL Certificates</comp.GlossaryTerm>, for you.

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/). If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

## Enable CloudCannon DNS

*CloudCannon DNS* is the default <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-provider.yml">DNS Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm> when you connect a *Custom Domain*. If your domain uses <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">External DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, you can switch it to *CloudCannon DNS* on the *Details* section of the *Domain Settings* tab. Switching needs the `base-domain:settings:details:read` permission to open the *Details* section, as well as the `base-domain:settings:dns:write` permission.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Updating your *DNS Provider* can cause downtime for anything connected to your domain, such as your website or custom email address. Please make sure you have a copy of your existing DNS Records before you change providers.

  If you need help updating your DNS, please contact our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>. Include the *Custom Domain*, your current *DNS Provider*, and a copy of your existing DNS Records.
</comp.Notice>

To switch to CloudCannon DNS:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Select the domain for which you want to configure DNS by clicking the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the domain. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Domain Settings* tab. CloudCannon will open the *Details* section.
4. Under *DNS Provider*, select the *CloudCannon DNS* option. CloudCannon will label the provider you are switching from *(current)* and list *What happens when you switch*.
5. Click the *Switch to CloudCannon DNS* button.

CloudCannon will create a DNS zone for your domain and add the records CloudCannon needs.

Nothing changes for visitors until you change the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/n/nameserver.yml">nameservers</comp.GlossaryTerm> at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Before you change them, add your other DNS Records, such as MX Records for email and TXT Records for domain verification, on the *DNS* tab.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Domain-Settings-Tab-Details-Switch-To-CloudCannon-DNS" alt="A screenshot of the Details section on the Domain Settings tab shows CloudCannon DNS selected under DNS Provider, External DNS marked (current), and the What happens when you switch steps." title="The Details section on the Domain Settings tab" type="screenshot" />

You can also open the *Details* section from the *DNS* tab, by clicking the *change the DNS Provider* link below the *External DNS nameservers* heading at the top of the tab.

## Move from the legacy CloudCannon DNS

Some older *Custom Domains* use the legacy CloudCannon DNS, where you can't add or change DNS Records. Moving your domain to the new CloudCannon DNS creates a DNS zone with a copy of its current records, where you can add and edit them, and nothing changes for visitors until you update the nameservers at your domain registrar. For the steps, please read our documentation on [migrating from the legacy CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/migrate-from-the-legacy-cloudcannon-dns/).

## Point your domain at CloudCannon DNS

Once you enable CloudCannon DNS, you need to replace the nameservers at your domain registrar with CloudCannon's nameservers. Until you do, a setup guide at the top of the *Summary* tab of your *Domain* page walks you through each step. The *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* shows the same guide for any <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> on the domain, and notes that the *Site* stays on its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/testing-domain.yml">Testing Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> until your domain goes live. The *DNS* tab shows the same guide below its *CloudCannon DNS* heading, a *Configure CloudCannon DNS* link, and a sentence that says how CloudCannon DNS answers for your domain, ending in a *change the DNS Provider* link.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Custom-Domain-Setup-Guide" alt="A screenshot of the Custom Domain page under Site Settings shows that Careers site goes live at https://careers.example.org/ once careers.example.org points at CloudCannon, and the Point example.org at CloudCannon DNS setup guide with the Update your nameservers step in progress." title="The setup guide on the Custom Domain page" type="screenshot" />

The setup guide shows a three-step tracker (*Update your nameservers*, *Nameservers match*, and *CloudCannon DNS configured*) and a heading for the step you are on. Once you click the *I've updated my nameservers* button, the first step reads *You updated your nameservers*. While CloudCannon checks your nameservers, the second step reads *Checking nameservers*, and if CloudCannon can't detect them, *Nameservers do not match*:

* **Getting [Base Domain] ready** — CloudCannon is creating the DNS zone for your domain. Your nameservers show once the zone is ready, usually in a moment.
* **CloudCannon could not create a DNS zone for [Base Domain]** — CloudCannon couldn't create the DNS zone, and shows Cloudflare's reason under *Error reason*. Click the *Retry zone creation* button to try again.
* **Point [Base Domain] at CloudCannon DNS** — Update the nameservers at your domain registrar.
* **Waiting for CloudCannon nameservers on [Base Domain]** — Your registrar is handing your domain over to CloudCannon DNS.
* **[Base Domain]'s nameservers do not match the CloudCannon nameservers** — CloudCannon still can't see your new nameservers 48 hours after you clicked the *I've updated my nameservers* button.
* **[Base Domain]'s nameservers no longer point at CloudCannon DNS** — Your domain was live on CloudCannon DNS, but its nameservers no longer point at CloudCannon.

If the zone still can't be created after you retry, contact our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>. Include your *Base Domain* and the reason Cloudflare gave in the setup guide.

### Add your existing records

Unless you moved from the legacy CloudCannon DNS, CloudCannon doesn't copy records from your current *DNS Provider*. Records at your current *DNS Provider* stop working once you switch nameservers, so add them to CloudCannon DNS first. These usually include MX Records for email and TXT Records for domain verification.

The first step of the setup guide, *1. Copy your existing DNS Records*, has an *Open the DNS tab* button on the *Summary* tab, which opens the *DNS* tab. On the *DNS* tab, the same step has a *Scroll to DNS Records* button, which scrolls down to the *DNS records* section. For the steps to add a record, please read our documentation on [adding DNS Records](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-dns-records-on-cloudcannon-dns/#add-dns-records).

### Update your nameservers

The second step of the setup guide, *2. Update your nameservers*, shows a table with one row for each nameserver. It lists each nameserver your domain needs under *Required*, the nameserver CloudCannon *Found* at your registrar, and a *Status* for each. A nameserver that matches shows *Detected*. The check passes only when your registrar lists exactly CloudCannon's nameservers. If your registrar lists a nameserver that CloudCannon DNS doesn't use, its row shows *Remove*.

Configuring your nameservers requires you to use the services provided by your domain registrar. For support, please contact your domain registrar.

CloudCannon DNS doesn't support DNSSEC. If DNSSEC is turned on for your domain at your registrar, turn it off before you update your nameservers. Otherwise, resolvers that check DNSSEC reject the answers from CloudCannon DNS, and your domain stops resolving for many visitors.

To update your nameservers:

1. Log in to your third-party domain registrar with the account details used when you purchased your domain name. If you don't know who your domain registrar is, click the *WHOIS* link in the setup guide.
2. Click the *Copy* icon next to a nameserver listed under *Required*. The icon will turn into a tick and show *Copied!*.
3. Paste the nameserver at your domain registrar. Repeat steps 2 and 3 until your registrar lists every nameserver under *Required*, and remove any other nameservers.
4. Save your changes at your domain registrar.
5. Click the *I've updated my nameservers* button in the nameservers table.

CloudCannon will check your nameservers every hour, and email you when your domain uses CloudCannon DNS. There's no need to keep the page open. For how long CloudCannon checks hourly, and how to check again immediately, please read the section on [how often CloudCannon checks your nameservers](#how-often-cloudcannon-checks-your-nameservers).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Most registrars hand your domain over to CloudCannon DNS within an hour, but it can take up to 48 hours.
</comp.Notice>

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Summary-Setup-Guide-Spreading" alt="A screenshot of the setup guide on the Summary tab shows the Waiting for CloudCannon nameservers on example.org heading, the Nameservers table with the required and found nameservers each marked Checking, and the Marked as updated and Check now buttons." title="The setup guide while your nameservers propagate" type="screenshot" />

If CloudCannon still can't see your new nameservers 48 hours after you clicked *I've updated my nameservers*, the setup guide lists what usually fixes it under *How can I fix this?*:

* Check that the change was saved at your registrar, not only entered.
* Replace all of the old nameservers. Adding CloudCannon's nameservers alongside them doesn't work.
* If DNSSEC is on at your registrar, turn it off first.

Once you have fixed the problem, click the *I've updated my nameservers* button. CloudCannon will check every hour for another 48 hours.

If CloudCannon still can't see your nameservers after you have checked each of these, contact our friendly <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a>. Include your *Base Domain*, the name of your domain registrar, and the nameservers your registrar lists.

### If your nameservers change

If the nameservers of a live domain stop pointing at CloudCannon, the setup guide shows when CloudCannon noticed the change. As DNS caches expire, visitors stop reaching the DNS Records you store in CloudCannon, and reach the records at the new nameservers instead.

* If you didn't mean to change your nameservers, change them back at your domain registrar, then click the *I've updated my nameservers* button.
* If you moved your DNS on purpose, click the *Open the Domain Settings tab* button in the *Do you now use a different DNS Provider?* notice to open the *Details* section of the *Domain Settings* tab, and switch your domain to External DNS. This button requires the `base-domain:settings:details:read` permission.

Cloudflare deletes your domain's CloudCannon DNS zone if the nameservers stay changed for seven days. CloudCannon keeps a copy of your DNS Records when it notices the change. If you change the nameservers back after Cloudflare deletes the zone, CloudCannon creates the zone again and restores your records. Cloudflare may give the new zone different nameservers, in which case the setup guide shows the nameservers to set at your registrar.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Summary-Setup-Guide-Changed" alt="A screenshot of the setup guide on the Summary tab shows the heading example.org's nameservers no longer point at CloudCannon DNS, the Do you now use a different DNS Provider? notice, and the nameservers marked Changed." title="The setup guide when your nameservers change" type="screenshot" />

The *I've updated my nameservers* and *Retry zone creation* buttons require the `base-domain:settings:dns:write` permission. The *Move to the new CloudCannon DNS* button requires both the `base-domain:settings:dns:write` and `base-domain:settings:details:write` permissions. Without them, the buttons are disabled, and hovering over a disabled button shows *You do not have permission to edit the DNS for [Base Domain].* or *You do not have permission to edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].* Ask someone in your *Organization* who has these permissions.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Summary-Setup-Guide-Read-Only" alt="A screenshot of the setup guide on the Summary tab shows the Point example.org at CloudCannon DNS heading, the Copy your existing DNS Records step with an Open the DNS tab button, and the nameservers marked Update needed." title="The setup guide without permission to edit the DNS" type="screenshot" />

If you can't view the DNS of the *Base Domain*, the *Summary* tab replaces the setup guide with a *CloudCannon DNS is not configured for [Base Domain].* notice. Domains on the legacy CloudCannon DNS show the setup guide instead.

### How often CloudCannon checks your nameservers

CloudCannon checks your nameservers daily. It checks every hour until your domain is live, for up to 48 hours after the latest of these: you connected the domain, you set up CloudCannon DNS for it, or you clicked the *I've updated my nameservers* button. If your domain still isn't live after 48 hours, CloudCannon emails you and goes back to checking daily. Click the *I've updated my nameservers* button again to start another 48 hours of hourly checks.

The footer of the nameservers table says how often CloudCannon checks and when it last checked. Once your domain is live, the *DNS* tab shows this in the closed *Nameservers* row instead, and the footer keeps only its buttons. Click the *Check now* button in the footer to check immediately. For a minute after a check, the button is disabled, and hovering over it shows *Checked less than a minute ago. You can check again in a minute.* If CloudCannon couldn't complete the latest check, the footer shows *Could not check* with how long ago the check failed, and the status stays at the last known result.

### View the nameserver check history

To see every recent check of your nameservers, click the *View nameserver check history* icon in the footer of the nameservers table. CloudCannon will open the *Nameserver checks for [Base Domain]* modal, which lists the nameservers your domain needs and the checks, most recent first. Back-to-back checks with the same result share a row.

Each row shows which *DNS Provider* the nameservers CloudCannon found belong to (*CloudCannon DNS*, *Legacy CloudCannon DNS*, or *Another provider*), *Nothing found*, or *Couldn't check*, along with the nameservers found, any error, and when the checks ran. A *Couldn't check* row doesn't change the status of your nameservers. Click the *Load older results* button to see older checks.

You can also list the checks with the [CloudCannon API](/documentation/developer-reference/api/), at `GET /api/v0/base-domains/{base_domain_uuid}/dns/checks`.

### Emails about your nameservers

CloudCannon will email everyone in your *Organization* who has permission to edit the settings of your *Base Domain* about the nameservers of your domain. This includes members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), members of the *Partner* group in a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and members of [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission.

#### [Base Domain] now uses CloudCannon DNS

CloudCannon sends this email about 15 minutes after your nameservers switch to CloudCannon, if an earlier check found nameservers that didn't match.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Mailer-Dns-Live" alt="A screenshot of the CloudCannon DNS live email shows the headline example.com now uses CloudCannon DNS, a tracker with every step done, each Subdomain on example.com marked Live, and the Open the Subdomains tab and Open the DNS tab buttons." title="The domain is live email" type="screenshot"/>

This email shows a progress tracker with every step done and what is live on up to four *Subdomains* under *Subdomains live on [Base Domain]*. If they apply, it also lists *Subdomains with errors*, *Subdomains without a valid SSL Certificate*, and *Certificates still issuing*, and asks *Do you use email on [Base Domain]?* when CloudCannon found no MX Records.

Under *Next actions*, the email lists what is left to do, such as adding a *Subdomain* or adding the MX Records for your domain on the *DNS* tab. Depending on what is left, its buttons open the *Subdomains* tab or the *DNS* tab of your *Domain* page, add a *Subdomain*, or open your domain in your browser.

To stop receiving this email, click the *Unsubscribe from this list* link at the bottom of the email. The same list includes other emails, such as when a *Site* build fails or a *Site* can't sync. To choose which lists you receive, click the *Manage email preferences* link instead.

#### [Base Domain]'s nameservers do not match the CloudCannon nameservers

CloudCannon sends this email when it still can't see your new nameservers more than 48 hours after you clicked *I've updated my nameservers*. If you didn't click it, CloudCannon counts the 48 hours from when you connected the domain or set up CloudCannon DNS for it, whichever is later, so switching a domain to CloudCannon DNS starts the 48 hours again. CloudCannon only sends the email within seven days of that starting point, and doesn't send it once your domain uses External DNS.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Mailer-Dns-Nameservers-Not-Detected" alt="A screenshot of the nameservers not detected email shows the headline example.com's nameservers do not match the CloudCannon nameservers, the nameservers required and found, the How can I fix this? list, and the Open the Domain page for example.com button." title="The nameservers not detected email" type="screenshot"/>

This email shows the nameservers required, the nameservers your registrar still has, and a *How can I fix this?* list of what usually fixes it, such as removing old nameservers instead of adding the CloudCannon nameservers alongside them, or turning off DNSSEC, which CloudCannon DNS doesn't support.

Under *Next actions*, the email asks you to check that the nameservers at your domain registrar match, then click the *I've updated my nameservers* button on your *Domain* page. Click the *Open the Domain page for [Base Domain]* button to open your *Domain* page. Clicking the *I've updated my nameservers* button makes CloudCannon check every hour again, and CloudCannon will email you when your domain uses CloudCannon DNS.

To stop receiving this email, click the *Unsubscribe from this list* link at the bottom of the email. The same list includes other emails, such as when a *Site* build fails or a *Site* can't sync. To choose which lists you receive, click the *Manage email preferences* link instead.

#### Action required: [Base Domain]'s nameservers no longer point at CloudCannon DNS

CloudCannon sends this email when the nameservers of a live domain change away from CloudCannon.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Mailer-Dns-Nameservers-Changed" alt="A screenshot of the nameservers changed email shows the headline example.com's nameservers no longer point at CloudCannon DNS, the nameservers required and found, when the CloudCannon DNS zone will be deleted, and the Open the Domain page for example.com button." title="The nameservers changed email" type="screenshot"/>

This email shows when CloudCannon detected the change and the nameservers your domain uses now. Until you fix it, visitors and email reach the records stored at the changed nameservers instead of the DNS Records you store in CloudCannon. The email also says when the CloudCannon DNS zone will be deleted if the nameservers stay changed, and that CloudCannon keeps a copy of your DNS Records to restore when you change the nameservers back.

Under *Next actions*, the email asks you to change the nameservers back at your domain registrar if the change was a mistake, or, if you now use another *DNS Provider*, switch your domain to External DNS on the *Domain Settings* tab of your *Domain* page. Click the *Open the Domain page for [Base Domain]* button to open your *Domain* page.

This email has no unsubscribe link.
