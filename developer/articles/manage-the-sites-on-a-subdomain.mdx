<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` and `site:settings:hosting:write` permissions, can manage the *Sites* on a *Subdomain* and delete *Subdomains* that serve *Sites*.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> either serves one or more <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> or redirects visitors to another domain. This article covers the *Sites* already on a *Subdomain*: swapping and removing them, changing the path at which each one is served, and deleting a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites*. For more information on how *Subdomains* work, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/).

A *Subdomain* can serve several *Sites*, each at its own path. For example, `example.com` can serve your marketing *Site* at the root (`/`) and your documentation *Site* at `/docs/`. Every *Site* on a *Subdomain* shares the *Subdomain's* hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Sites-Element" alt="A screenshot of the Sites section of the Subdomain page for example.com shows Marketing site at / and Documentation at /docs/, each built and synced from main, and the Add a Site button." title="Two Sites on one Subdomain" type="ui-snippet"/>

For *Subdomains* that redirect, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/). To change a *Subdomain's* hosting option or certificate, or check its DNS, please read our documentation on [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  You can manage all the *Sites* served on a *Subdomain* from two places: the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain-page.yml">Subdomain page</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or from the *Custom Domain* page under the *Site Settings* for any one of the *Sites* it serves.
</comp.Notice>

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [added a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/) or [added a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/). If you are a member of multiple <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm>, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

## Swap the Site at a path

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Swapping the *Site* at a path also requires the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for the *Site* you want to swap to.
</comp.Notice>

When you swap the *Site* at a path, including at the root (`/`), CloudCannon replaces it with another *Site* in a single step, so the path stays live. Swapping doesn't affect the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/testing-domain.yml">Testing Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> of either *Site*. If the *Site* you swap in already has a *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon moves it from its old *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [moving your Site from another Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/#move-your-site-from-another-subdomain).

To swap the *Site* at a path:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Context Menu* for the *Site* you want to replace.
6. Select the *Swap for another Site* option. CloudCannon will open the *Swap the Site at [Subdomain and path]* modal.
7. Select the *Site* you want to swap in from the *Site* dropdown.
8. Click the *Swap Site* button.

CloudCannon will serve the new *Site* at that path.

## Change the path of a Site

When you change the path of a *Site* (e.g., `example.com/guides/` instead of `example.com/docs/`), CloudCannon starts serving the *Site* at the new path immediately, without rebuilding it.

Because your *Site's* build output needs a folder that matches the new path, visitors to the new path won't find your pages until you rebuild your *Site* with a matching folder. For more information, please read our documentation on [building your Site for a subpath](/documentation/developer-articles/build-your-site-for-a-subpath/).

To change the path of a *Site*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Context Menu* for the *Site* whose path you want to change.
6. Select the *Change Subpath* option. CloudCannon will open the *Change [Site Name]'s Subpath* modal.
7. Enter the new path in the *Subpath* field (e.g., `/guides/`). CloudCannon will update the *Subpaths on [Subdomain] after this change* list, and warn you if existing links to the old path would show a 404 page or another *Site*.
8. Click the *Change Subpath* button.

CloudCannon will serve the *Site* at the new path.

## Remove a Site from a Subdomain

When you remove a *Site* from a *Subdomain*, CloudCannon stops serving that *Site* on the *Subdomain*. This does not affect the *Site's* *Testing Domain*.

If you remove the last *Site* on a *Subdomain*, CloudCannon asks what should happen to the empty *Subdomain*:

* **Leave it set up** — The *Subdomain* stays set up with no *Site*. Its hosting and certificate stay connected, so you can add a *Site* back without waiting for DNS or a new certificate. Until then, visitors see a 404 page. This is the default. Choose this option to take your website down temporarily.
* **Redirect visitors** — The *Subdomain* becomes a redirect that sends every visitor to another domain, keeping the path and query string.
* **Delete the Subdomain** — CloudCannon deletes the *Subdomain* and its certificate, and sends its visitors to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">Fallback Redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

To remove a *Site* from a *Subdomain*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Context Menu* for the *Site* you want to remove.
6. Select the *Remove from [Subdomain]* option. CloudCannon will open the *Remove [Subdomain] from [Site Name]?* modal.
7. If this is the last *Site* on the *Subdomain*, select what happens to it:
   * *Leave it set up* or *Delete the Subdomain* — (Optional.) If the *Other Redirect Subdomains* notice lists *Subdomains* that redirect to this one, uncheck the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* or *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox to keep them. If you leave it checked, CloudCannon deletes them too. The checkbox only covers redirects on domains whose settings you can edit.
   * *Redirect visitors* — CloudCannon will show the *Redirect visitors to* field. Enter the domain to which you want to send visitors. If the destination itself redirects, or CloudCannon can't use it, CloudCannon will show a message below the field.
8. Click the *Remove from [Subdomain]*, *Remove and redirect*, or *Remove and delete* button.

CloudCannon will stop serving the *Site* on the *Subdomain*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Remove-Last-Site-Leave-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Remove blog.example.com from Engineering blog? modal shows the Leave it set up, Redirect visitors, and Delete the Subdomain options, a Delete this other Redirect Subdomain checkbox for news.example.com, and the Remove from blog.example.com button." title="The Remove modal" type="screenshot"/>

If you redirected the *Subdomain*, CloudCannon will also update any <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomains</comp.GlossaryTerm> on the same *Custom Domain* that pointed to the removed *Site*, so they point to the new destination.

## Delete a Subdomain

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Deleting a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites* also requires the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for every *Site* it serves.
</comp.Notice>

When you delete a *Subdomain*, CloudCannon stops serving it and removes its certificate. Any *Sites* on the *Subdomain* keep their *Testing Domains*. If the *Subdomain* was your *Fallback Redirect*, CloudCannon will make another *Subdomain* your *Fallback Redirect*, which you can change on the *Subdomains* tab. Visitors to the deleted *Subdomain* go to your *Fallback Redirect*. If it was your last *Subdomain*, your *Custom Domain* has no *Fallback Redirect*.

To delete a *Subdomain*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Delete Subdomain* button in the *Danger zone* section. CloudCannon will open the *Delete [Subdomain]?* modal.
6. (Optional.) If other *Subdomains* redirect to this one, uncheck the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* or *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox to keep them.
7. Type the *Subdomain* in the *Type [Subdomain] to confirm* field.
8. Click the *Delete Subdomain* button.

CloudCannon will delete the *Subdomain*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Delete-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Delete example.com? modal shows that another Subdomain becomes the Fallback Redirect, the redirect pointing at the Subdomain with a Delete this other Redirect Subdomain checkbox, the Type example.com to confirm field, and the Delete Subdomain button." title="The Delete Subdomain modal" type="screenshot"/>

You can also delete a *Subdomain* from the *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page, using the *Delete Subdomain* option in the *Context Menu* for the *Subdomain*.

## Emails after you change the Sites on a Subdomain

CloudCannon emails everyone else in your *Organization* who has permission to view its billing after some of these actions. CloudCannon doesn't send these emails on the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/enterprise-plan.yml">Enterprise Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

Removing the last *Site* from a *Subdomain*, deleting a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites*, or swapping in a *Site* that was the last *Site* on its old *Subdomain* reduces the number of *Subdomains* serving *Sites* that you are charged for as part of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. If your *Organization* had more of these *Subdomains* than its *Subscription Plan* includes, CloudCannon sends the *[Name] removed an extra Subdomain from [Organization]'s Subscription Plan* email.

For more information about this email, please read our documentation on [the emails after you remove a Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/remove-a-custom-domain-from-your-site/#emails-after-you-remove-a-custom-domain).

## Troubleshoot managing the Sites on a Subdomain

If CloudCannon doesn't make the change you expect, or the *Subdomain* page doesn't show the controls you expect, here are some common causes. If you see an error that isn't listed here, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Organization* and *Site*, the *Subdomain*, and the full error message.

### You do not have permission to edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Delete Subdomain* button, over a disabled option in a *Site's* *Context Menu*, or over the disabled options under the *Previous Subdomain* heading. You don't have the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Ask someone who manages <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> in your *Organization* for this permission.

### You do not have permission to change the hosting of the Site [Site Name] or edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].

This message appears when you hover over a disabled option in a *Site's* *Context Menu*. You don't have either permission the option needs. If you are missing only the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission, the message reads *You do not have permission to change the hosting of the Site [Site Name].* instead. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for the permissions the message names.

### You do not have permission to change the hosting of any Site on this Subdomain.

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Delete Subdomain* button. Deleting a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites* requires the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for each of those *Sites*. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for this permission for each *Site*.

### You can only select a Site if you have permission to change its hosting.

This message appears below the *Site* dropdown, which disables any *Site* whose hosting you can't change. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for that *Site*.

### [Site Name] already uses this Subpath.

Another *Site* on the *Subdomain* is already served at the path you entered in the *Subpath* field. Enter a different path, or [swap the Site at that path](#swap-the-site-at-a-path) instead.
