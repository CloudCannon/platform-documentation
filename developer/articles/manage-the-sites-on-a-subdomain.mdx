<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` and `site:settings:hosting:write` permissions, can manage the *Sites* on a *Subdomain* and delete *Subdomains*.
</comp.Notice>

A *Subdomain* on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> either serves one or more <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> or redirects visitors to another domain. This article covers the *Sites* on a *Subdomain*: adding, swapping, moving, and removing them, changing the path at which each one is served, and deleting a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites*. For more information on how *Subdomains* work, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/).

For *Subdomains* that redirect, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/). To change a *Subdomain's* hosting option or certificate, or check its DNS, please read our documentation on [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/).

Each *Subdomain* has its own page, which you open by clicking the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Subdomain* in the list on the *Subdomains* tab of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm>. The *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* also shows the page for the *Subdomain* that serves that *Site*, under the *Subdomain settings* heading, with the same sections and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm> options. If a change there takes the *Site* off the *Subdomain*, CloudCannon opens the *[Site Name] is no longer on [Subdomain]* modal, with the *Stay here* and *Go to [Subdomain] settings* buttons, or *Go to subdomains* if you deleted the *Subdomain*.

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [added a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/).

## Add a Site to a Subdomain

A *Subdomain* can serve several *Sites*, each at its own path. For example, `example.com` can serve your marketing *Site* at the root (`/`) and your documentation *Site* at `/docs/`. Every *Site* on a *Subdomain* shares the *Subdomain's* hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings.

If the *Site* you add already has a *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon moves the *Site* to the new address. For more information, please read the section on [moving a Site from another Subdomain](#move-a-site-from-another-subdomain).

You can also add a *Custom Domain* from the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

To add a *Site* to a *Subdomain*:

1. Navigate to the page for the *Subdomain*.
2. Click the *Add a Site* button in the *Sites* section. CloudCannon will open the *Add a Site to [Subdomain]* modal.
3. Select the *Site* you want to add from the *Site* dropdown.
4. (Optional.) Enter a path in the *Subpath* field. Leave this field blank to serve the *Site* at the root of the *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Add Site* button.

CloudCannon will serve the *Site* on the *Subdomain* at the path you entered.

## Swap the Site at a path

You can replace the *Site* at a path with another *Site* in one step, so the path is never left without a *Site*. The *Site* you replace keeps its [Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/).

The *Swap for another Site* option is disabled unless you can both change the hosting of the *Site* and edit the settings of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Hover over the option to see which permission you're missing, then ask someone in your *Organization* who has it.

To swap the *Site* at a path:

1. Navigate to the page for the *Subdomain*.
2. Click the *Context Menu* for the *Site* you want to replace.
3. Select the *Swap for another Site* option. CloudCannon will open the *Swap the Site at [Subdomain and path]* modal.
4. Select the *Site* that takes over from the *Site* dropdown.
5. Click the *Swap Site* button.

CloudCannon will serve the new *Site* at that path. *Sites* whose hosting you can't change are disabled in the *Site* dropdown.

## Change the path of a Site

You can change the path at which a *Subdomain* serves a *Site*. Each path on a *Subdomain* is served by one *Site*, so the new path can't be a path another *Site* already serves. Before you save, CloudCannon shows the *Subpaths on [Subdomain] after this change*. If links to the old path would show a 404 page or another *Site*, CloudCannon warns you so you can update those links or keep the old path.

The *Change Subpath* option is disabled unless you can both change the hosting of the *Site* and edit the settings of the *Base Domain*. Hover over the option to see which permission you're missing, then ask someone in your *Organization* who has it.

To change the path of a *Site*:

1. Navigate to the page for the *Subdomain*.
2. Click the *Context Menu* for the *Site* you want to move.
3. Select the *Change Subpath* option. CloudCannon will open the *Change [Site Name]'s Subpath* modal.
4. Enter the new path in the *Subpath* field (e.g., `/guides/`).
5. Review the paths on the *Subdomain* after the change.
6. Click the *Change Subpath* button.

CloudCannon will serve the *Site* at the new path.

Your *Site's* build output needs a folder that matches the new path. For more information, please read our documentation on [building your Site for a path](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/#build-your-site-for-a-path).

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Change-Path-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Change path modal for the Documentation Site shows the new path /guides/, a preview of the paths on example.com after the change, and a warning that links to the old path will be served by the Marketing site instead." title="The Change Subpath modal" type="screenshot"/>

You can also change the path of a *Site* from the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*. For more information, please read our documentation on [reviewing your Site's Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/#review-your-site-s-custom-domain).

## Remove a Site from a Subdomain

When you remove a *Site* from a *Subdomain*, CloudCannon stops serving that *Site* on the *Subdomain*. The *Site* keeps its *Testing Domain*.

The *Remove from [Subdomain]* option is disabled unless you can both change the hosting of the *Site* and edit the settings of the *Base Domain*. Hover over the option to see which permission you're missing, then ask someone in your *Organization* who has it.

If you remove the last *Site* on a *Subdomain*, CloudCannon asks what happens to the *Subdomain*:

* **Leave it set up** — The *Subdomain* stays set up with no *Site*. Its hosting and certificate stay connected, so you can add a *Site* back without waiting for DNS or a new certificate. Until then, visitors see a 404 page. This is the default. Choose this option to take your website down temporarily.
* **Redirect visitors** — The *Subdomain* becomes a redirect that sends every visitor to another domain, keeping the path and query string.
* **Delete the Subdomain** — CloudCannon deletes the *Subdomain* and its certificate, and sends its visitors to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">Fallback Redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

If other *Subdomains* redirect to the *Subdomain* you are emptying, CloudCannon lists them in the *Other Redirect Subdomains* notice and deletes them too, unless you uncheck the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* or *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox. The checkbox appears when you leave the *Subdomain* set up or delete it, and only covers redirects on domains whose settings you can edit.

If you redirect the *Subdomain*, enter its new destination in the *Redirect visitors to* field. If the destination itself redirects, CloudCannon shows *Avoid a double redirect by entering [Destination] instead.* below the field, which doesn't stop you from saving. If other *Redirect Subdomains* on the same *Custom Domain* point at the *Subdomain*, the *Other Redirect Subdomains* notice lists them, because CloudCannon will update them to redirect to the new destination. CloudCannon refuses a destination that would send visitors in a loop (*Cannot select this Subdomain as it would create a redirect loop.*), a destination that is the *Subdomain* itself (*Cannot select this Subdomain as it would redirect to itself.*), and a destination on one of your *Custom Domains* that isn't a *Subdomain* yet (*Cannot select this Subdomain until you configure it on [Custom Domain].*). For how loops form and how to fix one, please read our documentation on [fixing a redirect loop](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/#fix-a-redirect-loop).

To remove a *Site* from a *Subdomain*:

1. Navigate to the page for the *Subdomain*.
2. Click the *Context Menu* for the *Site* you want to remove.
3. Select the *Remove from [Subdomain]* option. CloudCannon will open the *Remove [Subdomain] from [Site Name]?* modal.
4. If this is the last *Site* on the *Subdomain*, select the *Leave it set up*, *Redirect visitors*, or *Delete the Subdomain* option. If you select *Redirect visitors*, enter the domain to which you want to send visitors in the *Redirect visitors to* field.
5. (Optional.) Uncheck the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* or *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox to keep the *Subdomains* that redirect to this one.
6. Click the *Remove from [Subdomain]* button. If you selected *Redirect visitors* or *Delete the Subdomain*, click the *Remove and redirect* or *Remove and delete* button instead.

CloudCannon will stop serving the *Site* on the *Subdomain*.

## Move a Site from another Subdomain

If the *Site* you add to a *Subdomain* already has a *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon moves the *Site* from its old *Subdomain* to the new one. This applies when you add a *Subdomain* with a *Site*, add a *Site* to a *Subdomain*, swap the *Site* at a path, or change a *Redirect Subdomain* to serve a *Site*. The modal shows where the *Site* is now and where it moves to.

If the *Site* is the last *Site* on its old *Subdomain*, the modal also asks what happens to the old *Subdomain*, under the *Previous Subdomain* heading, with similar choices to when you [remove a Site from a Subdomain](#remove-a-site-from-a-subdomain):

* **Leave it set up** — The old *Subdomain* stays set up with no *Site*, and its visitors see a 404 page. This is the default.
* **Redirect visitors** — The old *Subdomain* becomes a redirect. Enter its destination in the *Redirect visitors to* field. Other redirects that pointed at the old *Subdomain* redirect to the new destination instead.
* **Delete the previous Subdomain** — CloudCannon deletes the old *Subdomain* and its certificate.

If you leave the old *Subdomain* set up or delete it, CloudCannon also deletes the *Subdomains* that redirect to it, unless you uncheck the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* or *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox.

Choosing what happens to the old *Subdomain* requires permission to edit the settings of its *Base Domain*. Without that permission, the modal shows what happens to the old *Subdomain*, and CloudCannon leaves it set up.

## Delete a Subdomain

When you delete a *Subdomain*, CloudCannon stops serving it and removes its certificate. Any *Sites* on the *Subdomain* keep their *Testing Domains*. If the *Subdomain* was your *Fallback Redirect*, CloudCannon will make another *Subdomain* your *Fallback Redirect*, which you can change on the *Subdomains* tab. Visitors to the deleted *Subdomain* go to your *Fallback Redirect*.

To delete a *Subdomain*:

1. Navigate to the page for the *Subdomain*.
2. Click the *Delete Subdomain* button in the *Danger zone* section. CloudCannon will open the *Delete [Subdomain]?* modal.
3. (Optional.) Uncheck the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* or *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox to keep the *Subdomains* that redirect to this one.
4. Type the *Subdomain* in the *Type [Subdomain] to confirm* field.
5. Click the *Delete Subdomain* button.

CloudCannon will delete the *Subdomain*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Delete-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Delete example.com? modal shows that another Subdomain becomes the fallback, the redirect pointing at the Subdomain with a Delete this redirect too checkbox, and the confirmation field." title="The Delete Subdomain modal" type="screenshot"/>

You can also delete a *Subdomain* from the *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page, using the *Delete Subdomain* option in the *Context Menu* for the *Subdomain*.
