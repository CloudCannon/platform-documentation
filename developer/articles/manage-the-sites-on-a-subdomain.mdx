<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` and `site:settings:hosting:write` permissions, can manage the *Sites* on a *Subdomain* and delete *Subdomains*.
</comp.Notice>

A *Subdomain* on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> either serves one or more <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> or redirects visitors to another domain. This article covers the *Sites* on a *Subdomain*: adding, swapping, moving, and removing them, changing the path at which each one is served, and deleting a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites*. For more information on how *Subdomains* work, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/).

For *Subdomains* that redirect, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/). To change a *Subdomain's* hosting option or certificate, or check its DNS, please read our documentation on [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/).

Each *Subdomain* has its own page, which you open by clicking the *Subdomain* in the list on the *Subdomains* tab of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [added a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/).

## Add a Site to a Subdomain

A *Subdomain* can serve several *Sites*, each at its own path. For example, `example.com` can serve your marketing *Site* at the root (`/`) and your documentation *Site* at `/docs/`. Every *Site* on a *Subdomain* shares the *Subdomain's* hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings.

If the *Site* you add already has a *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon moves the *Site* to the new address. For more information, please read the section on [moving a Site from another Subdomain](#move-a-site-from-another-subdomain).

You can also add a *Custom Domain* from the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

To add a *Site* to a *Subdomain*:

1. Navigate to the page for the *Subdomain*.
2. Click the *Add site* button. CloudCannon will open the *Add a site to [Subdomain]* modal.
3. Select the *Site* you want to add from the *Site to serve* dropdown.
4. (Optional.) Enter a path in the *Path* field. Leave this field blank to serve the *Site* at the root of the *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Add site* button.

CloudCannon will serve the *Site* on the *Subdomain* at the path you entered.

## Swap the Site at a path

You can replace the *Site* at a path with another *Site* in one step, so the path is never left without a *Site*. The *Site* you replace keeps its [Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/).

The *Swap for another site* option is disabled unless you can both change the hosting of the *Site* and edit the settings of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Hover over the option to see which permission you're missing, then ask someone in your *Organization* who has it.

To swap the *Site* at a path:

1. Navigate to the page for the *Subdomain*.
2. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Site* you want to replace.
3. Select the *Swap for another site* option. CloudCannon will open the *Swap the site at [Subdomain and path]* modal.
4. Select the *Site* that takes over from the *New site* dropdown.
5. Click the *Swap site* button.

CloudCannon will serve the new *Site* at that path. *Sites* whose hosting you can't change are disabled in the *New site* dropdown.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Swap-Site-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Swap the site at example.com/docs/ modal shows the New site dropdown and a note that both sites change in one step, so /docs/ is never left without a site." title="The Swap the site modal" type="screenshot"/>

## Change the path of a Site

You can change the path at which a *Subdomain* serves a *Site*. Each path on a *Subdomain* is served by one *Site*, so the new path can't be a path another *Site* already serves. Before you save, CloudCannon shows the paths on the *Subdomain* after the change. If links to the old path would show a not found page or another *Site*, CloudCannon warns you so you can update those links or keep the old path.

The *Change path* option is disabled unless you can both change the hosting of the *Site* and edit the settings of the *Base Domain*. Hover over the option to see which permission you're missing, then ask someone in your *Organization* who has it.

To change the path of a *Site*:

1. Navigate to the page for the *Subdomain*.
2. Click the *Context Menu* for the *Site* you want to move.
3. Select the *Change path* option. CloudCannon will open the *Change [Site Name]'s path* modal.
4. Enter the new path in the *Path* field (e.g., `/guides/`).
5. Review the paths on the *Subdomain* after the change.
6. Click the *Change path* button.

CloudCannon will serve the *Site* at the new path.

Your *Site's* build output needs a folder that matches the new path. For more information, please read our documentation on [building your Site for a path](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/#build-your-site-for-a-path).

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Change-Path-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Change path modal for the Documentation Site shows the new path /guides/, a preview of the paths on example.com after the change, and a warning that links to the old path will be served by the Marketing site instead." title="The Change path modal" type="screenshot"/>

You can also change the path of a *Site* from the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*. For more information, please read our documentation on [reviewing your Site's Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/#review-your-site-s-custom-domain).

## Remove a Site from a Subdomain

When you remove a *Site* from a *Subdomain*, CloudCannon stops serving that *Site* on the *Subdomain*. The *Site* keeps its *Testing Domain*.

The *Remove from [Subdomain]* option is disabled unless you can both change the hosting of the *Site* and edit the settings of the *Base Domain*. Hover over the option to see which permission you're missing, then ask someone in your *Organization* who has it.

If you remove the last *Site* on a *Subdomain*, CloudCannon asks what happens to the *Subdomain*:

* **Leave it set up** — The *Subdomain* stays set up with no *Site*. Its hosting and certificate stay connected, so you can add a *Site* back without waiting for DNS or a new certificate. Until then, CloudCannon sends visitors to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">fallback redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or shows a not found page if the *Subdomain* is the fallback. This is the default. Choose this option to take your website down temporarily.
* **Redirect** — The *Subdomain* becomes a redirect that sends every visitor to another domain, keeping the path and query string.
* **Delete it** — CloudCannon deletes the *Subdomain* and its certificate.

If other *Subdomains* redirect to the *Subdomain* you are emptying, CloudCannon lists them and deletes them too, unless you uncheck the *Delete this redirect too* or *Delete these redirects too* checkbox. The checkbox appears when you leave the *Subdomain* set up or delete it, and only covers redirects on domains whose settings you can edit. CloudCannon notes the destination of visitors to the redirects that stay.

If you leave the *Subdomain* set up, CloudCannon sends its visitors to your fallback redirect. If your fallback sends visitors back to this *Subdomain*, directly or through other redirects, CloudCannon refuses to leave it set up, because visitors would go around in a loop. This can happen when your fallback is one of the redirects that point at the *Subdomain*, and you uncheck the checkbox to keep it. The modal says what to do instead, for example *Visitors would go round in a loop: blog.example.com -> news.example.com -> blog.example.com. Delete the redirect on news.example.com, or redirect or delete blog.example.com instead.*

If you redirect the *Subdomain*, enter its new destination in the *Send visitors to* field. If the destination itself redirects, CloudCannon warns that visitors are redirected twice, and suggests sending them to the final destination instead. CloudCannon refuses a destination that would send visitors in a loop, a destination that is the *Subdomain* itself (*[Subdomain] cannot redirect to itself.*), and a destination on one of your *Custom Domains* that isn't a *Subdomain* yet (*[Destination] is not set up yet. Add it as a subdomain first.*).

To remove a *Site* from a *Subdomain*:

1. Navigate to the page for the *Subdomain*.
2. Click the *Context Menu* for the *Site* you want to remove.
3. Select the *Remove from [Subdomain]* option. CloudCannon will open the *Remove [Subdomain] from [Site Name]?* modal.
4. If this is the last *Site* on the *Subdomain*, select the *Leave it set up*, *Redirect*, or *Delete it* option. If you select *Redirect*, enter the domain to which you want to send visitors in the *Send visitors to* field.
5. (Optional.) Uncheck the *Delete this redirect too* or *Delete these redirects too* checkbox to keep the *Subdomains* that redirect to this one.
6. Click the *Remove from [Subdomain]* button. If this is the last *Site* on the *Subdomain*, click the *Remove domain*, *Remove and redirect*, or *Remove and delete* button instead.

CloudCannon will stop serving the *Site* on the *Subdomain*.

## Move a Site from another Subdomain

If the *Site* you add to a *Subdomain* already has a *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon moves the *Site* from its old *Subdomain* to the new one. This applies when you add a *Subdomain* with a *Site*, add a *Site* to a *Subdomain*, swap the *Site* at a path, or change a *Redirect Subdomain* to serve a *Site*. The modal shows where the *Site* is now and where it moves to.

If the *Site* is the last *Site* on its old *Subdomain*, the modal also asks what happens to the old *Subdomain*, with the same choices as when you [remove a Site from a Subdomain](#remove-a-site-from-a-subdomain):

* **Leave it set up** — The old *Subdomain* stays set up with no *Site*. This is the default.
* **Redirect** — The old *Subdomain* becomes a redirect. Enter its destination in the *Send visitors to* field. Other redirects that pointed at the old *Subdomain* redirect to the new destination instead.
* **Delete it** — CloudCannon deletes the old *Subdomain* and its certificate.

If you leave the old *Subdomain* set up or delete it, CloudCannon also deletes the *Subdomains* that redirect to it, unless you uncheck the *Delete this redirect too* or *Delete these redirects too* checkbox.

Choosing what happens to the old *Subdomain* requires permission to edit the settings of its *Base Domain*. Without that permission, the modal shows what happens to the old *Subdomain*, and CloudCannon leaves it set up.

## Delete a Subdomain

When you delete a *Subdomain*, CloudCannon stops serving it and removes its certificate. Any *Sites* on the *Subdomain* keep their *Testing Domains*. If the *Subdomain* was your fallback redirect, another *Subdomain* becomes the fallback. You can choose which one under *Fallback* on the *Summary* tab. Visitors to the deleted *Subdomain* go to your fallback redirect.

To delete a *Subdomain*:

1. Navigate to the page for the *Subdomain*.
2. Click the *Delete subdomain* button in the *Danger zone* section. CloudCannon will open the *Delete [Subdomain]?* modal.
3. (Optional.) Uncheck the *Delete this redirect too* or *Delete these redirects too* checkbox to keep the *Subdomains* that redirect to this one.
4. Type the *Subdomain* in the *Type [Subdomain] to confirm* field.
5. Click the *Delete subdomain* button.

CloudCannon will delete the *Subdomain*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Delete-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Delete example.com? modal shows that another Subdomain becomes the fallback, the redirect pointing at the Subdomain with a Delete this redirect too checkbox, and the confirmation field." title="The Delete subdomain modal" type="screenshot"/>

You can also delete a *Subdomain* from the *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page, using the *Delete subdomain* option in the *Context Menu* for the *Subdomain*.
