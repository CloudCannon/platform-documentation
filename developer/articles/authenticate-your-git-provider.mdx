<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `org:settings:git:provider:write` permission, can manage which *Git Providers* are connected to an *Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

For CloudCannon to sync your files from a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm>, you must give it permission to access your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-provider.yml">Git Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon supports [GitHub](/documentation/developer-articles/connecting-a-github-repository-as-your-source/), [GitLab](/documentation/developer-articles/connecting-a-gitlab-respository-as-your-source/), and [Bitbucket](/documentation/developer-articles/connecting-a-bitbucket-respository-as-your-source/). You only need to authenticate each *Git Provider* once per <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You can authenticate several *Git Providers*, but only one account on each *Git Provider*.

CloudCannon also supports [GitHub Enterprise Server](/documentation/developer-articles/connecting-a-github-enterprise-server-repository-as-your-source/) and [Self-hosted GitLab](/documentation/developer-articles/connecting-a-self-hosted-gitlab-repository-as-your-source/). You must configure your server in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> before you can authenticate one of these *Git Providers*.

There are two ways to authenticate a *Git Provider*: when you create a new <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and through your *Org Settings*.

## Authenticate during Site creation

In the *File source* dropdown on the *Create a Site* page, authenticated *Git Providers* have a green shield icon next to their option.

To authenticate a *Git Provider* during *Site* creation:

1. Navigate to the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/sites-browser.yml">Sites Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm> by clicking the *Sites* button in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the *Create a Site* button. CloudCannon will open the *Create a Site* page.
3. Select the *[Provider Name] repository* option for an unauthenticated *Git Provider* in the *File source* dropdown. CloudCannon will show you the *Authorize* button.
4. Click the *Authorize* button.
5. Follow your *Git Provider's* instructions to authenticate access.
6. Return to the *Create a Site* page on CloudCannon and continue following the prompts to create a *Site*.

Your *Git Provider* is now authenticated, and CloudCannon will display all *Git Repositories* available through that *Git Provider* in the *Repository* dropdown.

## Authenticate in your Org Settings

Each *Git Provider* has its own page under the *Files* heading in your *Org Settings*, with an *Authentication* tab and a *Sites* tab. The *GitHub Enterprise Server* and *Self-Hosted GitLab* pages also have a *Settings* tab for your server details. Members who can manage *Git Providers* also see a *Danger Zone* tab once the *Git Provider* is authenticated, or once the server is configured.

GitLab works differently: the *GitLab* page asks you to choose an authentication method first, then shows either the *Authenticate with GitLab* button or a form for a group access token. For more information, please read our documentation on [connecting a GitLab repository as your source](/documentation/developer-articles/connecting-a-gitlab-respository-as-your-source/).

To authenticate GitHub or Bitbucket through your *Org Settings*:

1. Click the *Org Settings* button in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Details* page.
2. Navigate to the *GitHub* or *Bitbucket* page under the *Files* heading, depending on which *Git Provider* you want to authenticate. CloudCannon will open the *Authentication* tab.
3. Click the *Authenticate* button.
4. Follow your *Git Provider's* instructions to authenticate access.

Your *Git Provider* is now authenticated. The *Authentication* tab will show the account that authenticated your *Git Provider*, with an *Open profile* button that links to the account's profile. CloudCannon syncs files as this account, so your *Sites* can only access the *Git Repositories* that this account can access.

## View Sites that sync with a Git Provider

The *Sites* tab on each *Git Provider* page lists every *Site* in your *Organization* that syncs its files from that *Git Provider*. You can use the *Sites* tab to check which *Sites* will be affected before you reconnect or unauthenticate a *Git Provider*.

To view the *Sites* that sync with a *Git Provider*:

1. Click the *Org Settings* button in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Details* page.
2. Navigate to the page for your *Git Provider* under the *Files* heading.
3. Select the *Sites* tab.

CloudCannon will list each *Site* that syncs with that *Git Provider*.

## Reconnect your Git Provider

If your *Git Provider* revokes CloudCannon's access, or CloudCannon cannot fetch information about your connected account, the *Authentication* tab shows an error message and a *Reconnect* button. You can reconnect your *Git Provider* without unauthenticating it first.

To reconnect your *Git Provider*:

1. Click the *Org Settings* button in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Details* page.
2. Navigate to the page for your *Git Provider* under the *Files* heading.
3. Click the *Reconnect* button on the *Authentication* tab.
4. Sign in to your *Git Provider* as the account already connected to your *Organization*.
5. Follow your *Git Provider's* instructions to authenticate access.

CloudCannon will return you to the *Authentication* tab and show your connected account.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-GitHub-Account-Fetch-Error" alt="A screenshot of the Authentication tab on the GitHub page under Org Settings shows an error message and a Reconnect button." title="A GitHub account that needs reconnecting" type="screenshot"/>

If you sign in to your *Git Provider* as a different account, CloudCannon will not reconnect it. To connect a different account, unauthenticate your *Git Provider* first, then authenticate it with the new account.

## Unauthenticate your Git Provider

Unauthenticating a *Git Provider* removes CloudCannon's access to that *Git Provider* and stops syncing for every *Site* that uses it. CloudCannon keeps the files for those *Sites*.

To unauthenticate your *Git Provider*:

1. Click the *Org Settings* button in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Details* page.
2. Navigate to the page for your *Git Provider* under the *Files* heading.
3. Select the *Danger Zone* tab.
4. Click the *Unauthenticate* button.
5. Click the *Confirm* button.

CloudCannon will remove its access to your *Git Provider* and return you to the *Authentication* tab.
