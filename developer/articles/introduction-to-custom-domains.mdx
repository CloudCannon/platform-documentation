A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is the web address you host your website on, purchased through a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. When you host your websites through CloudCannon, you connect your *Custom Domain* to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> and choose which <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subpath.yml">subpath</comp.GlossaryTerm> each <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> is served on. You can manage all the *Custom Domains* connected to your *Organization* through the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>, from the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

Articles in this section are for developers and anyone responsible for the domains, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns.yml">DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL Certificates</comp.GlossaryTerm> of an *Organization*. Routing and external hosting are covered in the [Hosting section](/documentation/developer-articles/introduction-to-hosting/) of our Developer documentation. For the domain CloudCannon provides to each *Site*, please read our documentation on [what a Testing Domain is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/).

Hosting your *Site* through CloudCannon is optional. If another provider hosts your *Site*, you will set up its domain and *SSL Certificate* with that provider, but can still manage your domain's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-records.yml">DNS Records</comp.GlossaryTerm> with <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

In the Custom Domains section of our Developer documentation, we cover:

* How hosting works — Learn how visitors reach your *Site*, from your domain registrar to the *Site* CloudCannon serves
* Custom Domains — Learn how to connect a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization* and add it to your *Sites*
* Subdomains — Learn how to add *Subdomains* to your *Custom Domain*, serve *Sites* on them, and configure their hosting, HTTPS, and DNS
* Redirects — Learn how to redirect a *Subdomain* to another domain, and where CloudCannon sends visitors to subdomains you haven't configured
* DNS — Learn how to configure *CloudCannon DNS* or external DNS, migrate from the legacy *CloudCannon DNS*, and manage your DNS Records on *CloudCannon DNS*
* SSL — Learn how to add an auto-generated or *Custom SSL Certificate*, and resolve *SSL Certificate* errors

Let's briefly introduce these topics.

## Hosting through CloudCannon

When a visitor enters your web address in their internet browser, the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/n/nameserver.yml">nameservers</comp.GlossaryTerm> and DNS Records for your domain send them to CloudCannon, which serves the *Site* on that address. Each address uses one of three hosting options: <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-hosting.yml">CloudCannon Hosting</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudflare-proxy.yml">Cloudflare Proxy</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/single-origin.yml">Single Origin</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

For a more in-depth explanation of how visitors reach your *Site*, please read our documentation:

* [How does hosting work on CloudCannon?](/documentation/developer-articles/how-does-hosting-work-on-cloudcannon/)

## Custom Domains

To manage your *Custom Domains* through CloudCannon, you must connect each one to your *Organization*, choose *CloudCannon DNS* or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and add the *Subdomain* on which you want to host a *Site*. For each *Custom Domain* you connect, CloudCannon creates a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm>, where you can see whether the domain is set up correctly and manage its *Subdomains*, DNS, and settings. You can view all the *Custom Domains* connected to your *Organization* from the *Custom Domain Browser*.

Connecting a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization* is free. Only *Subdomains* that serve a *Site* count toward your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

## Subdomains

A subdomain is a name in front of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> (e.g., www.example.com or docs.example.com). Every address on a *Custom Domain* is a *Subdomain* in CloudCannon, including the *Base Domain* itself (e.g., example.com). After you connect a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*, you choose whether each *Subdomain* serves one or more *Sites*, each at its own subpath, or redirects visitors to another domain.

For a more in-depth explanation of *Subdomains*, please read our documentation:

## Redirects

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> sends every visitor to another domain, keeping the path and query string, and doesn't count toward the *Subdomains* serving *Sites* that your *Subscription Plan* includes. Your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">Fallback Redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm> decides where CloudCannon sends visitors to subdomains you haven't configured.

For a more in-depth explanation of redirects, please read our documentation:

* [Manage your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/)
* [Redirect a domain to another domain](/documentation/developer-articles/redirect-a-domain-to-another-domain/)
* [Update the Fallback Redirect for your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/update-the-fallback-redirect-for-your-custom-domain/)

## DNS

The Domain Name System (DNS) translates a domain name into the address of the server that hosts it. When you connect a *Custom Domain*, you choose its *DNS Provider*. With *CloudCannon DNS*, you set CloudCannon's nameservers at your domain registrar, and CloudCannon adds the records your *Subdomains* need. With external DNS, you keep your current *DNS Provider* and add the records CloudCannon lists for each *Subdomain*.

For a more in-depth explanation of DNS, including how to configure CloudCannon or external DNS, please read our documentation:

## SSL

An *SSL Certificate* lets browsers load your *Site* over HTTPS. CloudCannon issues and renews an *SSL Certificate* for each *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain*, or you can upload your own. You can also set the minimum <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/tls.yml">TLS</comp.GlossaryTerm> version that browsers must use for every *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain* that uses *CloudCannon Hosting* or *Cloudflare Proxy*.
