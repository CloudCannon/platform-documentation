Two different kinds of limit apply to an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inbox</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> caps how many *Inboxes* and targets you can set up, and a rate limit caps how many submissions each *Inbox* accepts. Reaching a plan limit stops you adding more; reaching a rate limit does not lose a submission.

## Inbox and target limits

The number of *Inboxes* you can link to a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and the number of targets you can add to an *Inbox*, depend on your *Subscription Plan*:

* The *Standard Plan* allows one *Inbox* per *Site* and one target per *Inbox*.
* The *Team Plan* allows 10 *Inboxes* per *Site* and five targets per *Inbox*.
* The *Enterprise Plan* allows 100 *Inboxes* per *Site* and 30 targets per *Inbox*.

CloudCannon shows your current usage above each list, for example *Connected inboxes (1 of 10)*, so you can see how much room you have before adding another.

## Submission rate limits

CloudCannon applies two rate limits to incoming submissions:

* Each *Inbox* accepts 2250 submissions per 30-day period. The period starts when the *Inbox* receives its first submission, and a fresh period begins on the next submission after that one expires.
* Each visitor IP address can send 60 submissions per day.

## What happens when a form reaches a limit

A submission that exceeds either rate limit is not lost. CloudCannon records it as an errored submission, so it appears in the *Errored* category of the *Inbox* rather than reaching your targets. You can read it there, and resend it once you have room. For more information, please read our documentation on [resending errored form submissions](/documentation/user-articles/resend-errored-form-submissions/).

A form that submits to an *Inbox* you have unlinked, or to no *Inbox* at all, is a different case: CloudCannon has nowhere to record it. For more information, please read our documentation on [connecting your Site to an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-your-site-to-an-inbox/).

## Raising a limit

Moving to a higher *Subscription Plan* raises the *Inbox* and target limits. For more information, please read our documentation on [updating your Subscription Plan](/documentation/user-articles/update-your-subscription-plan/).

If you need more than the *Enterprise Plan* allows, or a higher submission limit on your current *Subscription Plan*, please <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/contact/">contact our team</a>.
