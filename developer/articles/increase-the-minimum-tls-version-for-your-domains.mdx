<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* or *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, can set the minimum TLS version for a base domain.
</comp.Notice>

You set the minimum TLS version once for your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and CloudCannon shows which of its subdomains meet it. On CloudCannon, the minimum TLS version is either 1.2 by default, or 1.3. For more about the minimum TLS version and how to choose between 1.2 and 1.3, please read our documentation on [SSL certificates](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-an-ssl-certificate/#tls-version).

When you raise the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon also raises every subdomain that is below it. Subdomains update shortly after you save.

CloudCannon never lowers the minimum TLS version of a subdomain. If you lower the minimum for your *Custom Domain* from 1.3 to 1.2, subdomains already at 1.3 stay at 1.3.

The minimum TLS version applies to subdomains whose visitors go through CloudCannon's Cloudflare network: subdomains on *CloudCannon Hosting* and *Cloudflare Proxy*. Visitors reach *Single Origin* subdomains at CloudCannon's origin instead, as they do your *Base Domain* on *CloudCannon Hosting* when your domain uses external DNS, so the minimum TLS version doesn't apply to them, and CloudCannon counts them as meeting your minimum.

## Check which subdomains meet your minimum TLS version

The *Minimum TLS* *Card* on the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page shows the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, which applies to every subdomain. Below the version, the *Card* confirms that all subdomains meet it, or names the subdomains that are below it.

The *TLS* section under the *Domain Settings* tab sets one minimum TLS version for every subdomain of your *Custom Domain*, and shows the same check in more detail. When you select a new version from the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown, CloudCannon lists the subdomains that change to that version under *These subdomains change to TLS [Version]*, along with each subdomain's own minimum TLS version. This lets you see which subdomains will be raised before you save it.

## Set the minimum TLS version for your Custom Domain

To set the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*:

1. Click the *Domains* button in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Identify the domain you want to update and click on the *Domain* card. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page for this *Custom Domain*.
3. Click the *View TLS settings* link below the *Minimum TLS* *Card*. CloudCannon will open the *TLS* section under the *Domain Settings* tab.
4. Select a version number from the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown.
5. Review the subdomains that change to your selected version.
6. Click the *Update minimum TLS* button.

CloudCannon will save the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain* and raise the subdomains below it.
