<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, can set the minimum TLS version for a *Base Domain* and its *Subdomains*.
</comp.Notice>

You set the minimum TLS version once for your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and CloudCannon shows which of its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomains</comp.GlossaryTerm> meet it. On CloudCannon, the minimum TLS version is either 1.2 by default, or 1.3. For more about the minimum TLS version and how to choose between 1.2 and 1.3, please read our documentation on [what an SSL Certificate is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-an-ssl-certificate/#tls-version).

New *Subdomains* start at the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*. When you raise the minimum for your *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon also raises every *Subdomain* below it, and the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/w/wildcard-certificate.yml">wildcard SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> that covers subdomains you haven't set up. *Subdomains* update shortly after you save.

When you lower the minimum for your *Custom Domain*, *Subdomains* keep their own minimum TLS version unless you check the *Also lower [Number] subdomains to TLS [Version]* checkbox. With the checkbox checked, CloudCannon lowers every *Subdomain* above the new minimum, and the wildcard *SSL Certificate*, to the new minimum. Without it, only new *Subdomains* start at the lower version.

CloudCannon saves the minimum TLS version on every *Subdomain*. CloudCannon's Cloudflare network applies it to *Subdomains* on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-hosting.yml">CloudCannon Hosting</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudflare-proxy.yml">Cloudflare Proxy</comp.GlossaryTerm>. <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/single-origin.yml">Single Origin</comp.GlossaryTerm> *Subdomains* count toward the check below like any other *Subdomain*.

## Check which Subdomains meet your minimum TLS version

The *Minimum TLS* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> on the *Summary* tab of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm> shows the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, the lowest version any *Subdomain* accepts. Below the version, the *Card* confirms that all *Subdomains* meet it, or says how many *Subdomains* are below it.

The *TLS* section under the *Domain Settings* tab sets the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, and shows the same check in more detail. When you select a new version from the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown, CloudCannon lists the *Subdomains* that change to that version under *These subdomains change to TLS [Version]* (or *This subdomain changes to TLS [Version]* for one), with each *Subdomain's* own minimum TLS version. Click the *Card* for a *Subdomain* in the list to open its page. If every *Subdomain* already meets the version you select, the section says *All subdomains meet TLS [Version].* instead. After you save, until the change reaches them shortly after, the section lists the *Subdomains* still below the new minimum under *[Number] subdomains are below TLS [Version]*.

## Set the minimum TLS version for your Custom Domain

If you are a member of multiple <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm>, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To set the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain you want to update. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page for this *Custom Domain*.
3. Click the *View TLS settings* link below the *Minimum TLS* *Card*. CloudCannon will open the *TLS* section under the *Domain Settings* tab.
4. Select a version number from the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown. CloudCannon will list the *Subdomains* that change to that version.
5. (Optional.) If you are lowering the minimum, check the *Also lower [Number] subdomains to TLS [Version]* checkbox.
6. Click the *Update minimum TLS* button.

CloudCannon will save the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, raise the *Subdomains* below it, and show *The minimum TLS version for [Base Domain] is now [Version].*

## Raise the minimum TLS version for one Subdomain

You can give one *Subdomain* a higher minimum TLS version than the rest of your *Custom Domain*, such as 1.3 for a *Subdomain* that handles sign-ins while the others stay on 1.2. A *Subdomain's* minimum can't be lower than the minimum for its *Custom Domain*, so the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown only offers versions at or above it.

On the *Subdomain* page for your *Base Domain* (e.g., example.com), the minimum you choose also applies to subdomains you haven't set up, which the wildcard *SSL Certificate* covers.

If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To set the minimum TLS version for one *Subdomain*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Configure HTTPS* button in the *HTTPS* section. CloudCannon will open the *HTTPS for [Subdomain]* modal.
6. Select a version from the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown under *HTTP*.
7. Click the *Save HTTPS* button.

CloudCannon will save the minimum TLS version for the *Subdomain* and apply it shortly after.
