<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, can set the minimum TLS version for a *Base Domain* and its subdomains.
</comp.Notice>

You set the minimum TLS version once for your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and CloudCannon shows which of its subdomains meet it. On CloudCannon, the minimum TLS version is either 1.2 by default, or 1.3. For more about the minimum TLS version and how to choose between 1.2 and 1.3, please read our documentation on [what an SSL certificate is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-an-ssl-certificate/#tls-version).

New subdomains start at the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*. When you raise the minimum for your *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon also raises every subdomain below it, and the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/w/wildcard-certificate.yml">wildcard certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> that covers subdomains you haven't set up. Subdomains update shortly after you save.

When you lower the minimum for your *Custom Domain*, subdomains keep their own minimum TLS version unless you check the *Also lower [Number] subdomains to TLS [Version]* checkbox. With the checkbox checked, CloudCannon lowers every subdomain above the new minimum, and the wildcard certificate, to the new minimum. Without it, only new subdomains start at the lower version.

The minimum TLS version applies to every subdomain, whichever way visitors reach it. CloudCannon's Cloudflare network applies it to subdomains on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-hosting.yml">CloudCannon Hosting</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudflare-proxy.yml">Cloudflare Proxy</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and CloudCannon's origin applies it to <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/single-origin.yml">Single Origin</comp.GlossaryTerm> subdomains, and to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> on *CloudCannon Hosting* when your domain uses <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>. *Single Origin* subdomains count toward the check below like any other subdomain.

## Check which subdomains meet your minimum TLS version

The *Minimum TLS* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> on the *Summary* tab of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm> shows the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, the lowest version any subdomain accepts. Below the version, the *Card* confirms that all subdomains meet it, or names the subdomains that are below it.

The *TLS* section under the *Domain Settings* tab sets the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, and shows the same check in more detail. When you select a new version from the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown, CloudCannon lists the subdomains that change to that version under *These subdomains change to TLS [Version]*, with each subdomain's own minimum TLS version. Click a subdomain in the list to open its page. If every subdomain already meets the version you select, the section says *All subdomains meet TLS [Version].* instead.

## Set the minimum TLS version for your Custom Domain

To set the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*:

1. Click the *Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain you want to update. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page for this *Custom Domain*.
3. Click the *View TLS settings* link below the *Minimum TLS* *Card*. CloudCannon will open the *TLS* section under the *Domain Settings* tab.
4. Select a version number from the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown. CloudCannon will list the subdomains that change to that version.
5. (Optional.) If you are lowering the minimum, check the *Also lower [Number] subdomains to TLS [Version]* checkbox.
6. Click the *Update minimum TLS* button.

CloudCannon will save the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, raise the subdomains below it, and show *The minimum TLS version for [Base Domain] is now [Version].*

## Raise the minimum TLS version for one subdomain

You can give one subdomain a higher minimum TLS version than the rest of your *Custom Domain*, such as 1.3 for a subdomain that handles sign-ins while the others stay on 1.2. A subdomain's minimum can't be lower than the minimum for its *Custom Domain*, so the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown only offers versions at or above it.

On the subdomain page for your *Base Domain* (e.g., example.com), the minimum you choose also applies to subdomains you haven't set up, which the wildcard certificate covers.

To set the minimum TLS version for one subdomain:

1. Click the *Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the subdomain. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of subdomains.
4. Click the subdomain. CloudCannon will open the page for that subdomain.
5. Click the *Configure* button in the *HTTPS* section. CloudCannon will open the *HTTPS for [Subdomain]* modal.
6. Select a version from the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown under *HTTP*.
7. Click the *Save HTTPS* button.

CloudCannon will save the minimum TLS version for the subdomain and apply it shortly after.
