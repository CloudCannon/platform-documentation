Connecting a Bitbucket <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm> to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> allows you to work on your website files locally and sync your changes to CloudCannon. CloudCannon also syncs any file changes you make in CloudCannon back to Bitbucket.

To connect a Bitbucket *Git Repository* and start syncing files:

1. Navigate to the *Syncing* page under *Site Settings*.
2. Select the *Bitbucket repository* option from the provider dropdown.
3. Click the *Authorize* button.

CloudCannon will redirect you to Bitbucket.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Syncing-Select-Bitbucket-No-Authorization" alt="A screenshot of the Syncing page under Site Settings shows the Bitbucket repository option selected and an Authorize button." title="The Syncing page with Bitbucket selected" type="screenshot"/>

To give CloudCannon access to your Bitbucket account:

1. Log in to your Bitbucket account.
2. Grant CloudCannon access to your Bitbucket account.

Bitbucket will redirect you back to the *Syncing* page, where you can select the *Git Repository* to connect. If you don't have a *Git Repository* for this *Site* yet, create one in Bitbucket and refresh the *Syncing* page.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  When you click the *Backup and Sync* button, CloudCannon will replace any existing files on your *Site* with the contents of the selected *Git Repository*. To cancel the process, exit the page instead.
</comp.Notice>

To connect your *Git Repository* and branch:

1. Select your *Git Repository* from the *Repository* dropdown.
2. Choose the *Use an existing branch* option or the *Create a new branch* option under the *Branch Setup* heading.
3. Pick your branch from the *Branch* dropdown. If you are creating a new branch, pick the branch to copy from the *Source branch* dropdown and enter a name in the *New branch name* field.
4. Click the *Backup and Sync* button.

CloudCannon will connect your *Site* to your Bitbucket *Git Repository*. CloudCannon pulls in any changes you push to your *Git Repository*, and automatically commits and pushes any changes you make in CloudCannon.

## Migrate to the new Bitbucket integration

When CloudCannon releases a new version of its Bitbucket integration, the older version stays in use until you migrate. If your *Organization* authenticated Bitbucket with an older version, the *Bitbucket* page under *Org Settings* shows a *New Bitbucket integration available* notice below the page heading, above the tabs. Migrating reauthorizes CloudCannon with your existing Bitbucket account, so your *Sites* keep syncing with the same *Git Repositories*.

To migrate to the new Bitbucket integration:

1. Navigate to the *Bitbucket* page under the *Files* heading in your *Org Settings*.
2. Make sure you are signed in to Bitbucket with the same Bitbucket account shown on the *Authentication* tab.
3. Click the *Upgrade Bitbucket integration* button.
4. Grant CloudCannon access to your Bitbucket account.

CloudCannon will return you to the *Bitbucket* page and remove the *New Bitbucket integration available* notice.
