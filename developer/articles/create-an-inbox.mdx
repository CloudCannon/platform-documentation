<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `org:settings:inbox:write` permission, can create an *Inbox*.
</comp.Notice>

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  If you are still using legacy CloudCannon forms, please read our documentation on [migrating to the new CloudCannon forms](/documentation/developer-articles/legacy-forms-migration-guide/).
</comp.Notice>

An <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inbox</comp.GlossaryTerm> receives the submissions from forms on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> and stores them for you to read. It belongs to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so creating one is the first step in setting up CloudCannon forms. For more information, please read our documentation on [what an Inbox is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-an-inbox/).

Two of the values you set here matter later. The *Inbox* key is what a form names in its `inbox_key` input to reach this *Inbox*, so take note of it; you can change both the name and the key afterwards from the *Inbox* settings. You can also choose a CAPTCHA provider during creation, though it needs keys from that provider and a widget on your form to take effect. For more information, please read our documentation on [reducing spam with a CAPTCHA](/documentation/developer-articles/reduce-spam-with-a-captcha/).

To create an *Inbox*:

1. Navigate to the *Forms* page under *Org Settings*.
2. Click the *Add Inbox* button.
3. Enter a name and a key for your *Inbox*.
4. Set how long CloudCannon stores submissions, and choose a CAPTCHA provider if you want one.
5. Click the *Create inbox* button.
6. Add any targets that should receive submissions, then click the *Finish Inbox* button.

CloudCannon will create your *Inbox* and open it under *Org Settings*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Forms-Create-Inbox-Targets" alt="A screenshot of the second step of Inbox creation shows the connected targets count, an Add target button, and a Finish Inbox button." title="The Inbox targets step" type="screenshot" />

You can skip the targets step and add them later. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a target to an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-target-to-an-inbox/).

A new *Inbox* receives nothing until a *Site* is linked to it and a form names its key. For more information, please read our documentation on [connecting your Site to an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-your-site-to-an-inbox/).

How many *Inboxes* you can link to a *Site* depends on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. For more information, please read our documentation on [how Inbox limits work](/documentation/developer-articles/how-do-inbox-limits-work/).
