Connecting a GitLab <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm> to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> allows you to work on your website files locally and sync your changes to CloudCannon. CloudCannon also syncs any file changes you make in CloudCannon back to GitLab.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  To connect a self-hosted GitLab *Git Repository* instead, please read our documentation on [connecting a self-hosted GitLab repository as your source](/documentation/developer-articles/connecting-a-self-hosted-gitlab-repository-as-your-source/).
</comp.Notice>

To connect a GitLab *Git Repository* and start syncing files:

1. Navigate to the *Syncing* page under *Site Settings*.
2. Select the *GitLab repository* option from the provider dropdown.
3. Click the *Authorize* button.

CloudCannon will open the *Connect to GitLab* modal, where you can choose one of two authentication methods. The same options appear on the *Authentication* tab of the *GitLab* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Syncing-Select-GitLab-No-Authorization" alt="A screenshot of the Syncing page under Site Settings shows the GitLab repository option selected and an Authorize button." title="The Syncing page with GitLab selected" type="screenshot"/>

## Authenticate with your GitLab account

When you authenticate with your GitLab user account, your CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> gets access to the same *Git Repositories* on GitLab to which you have access.

To authenticate with your GitLab user account:

1. Select the *Connect your GitLab account* option under *Authentication method* in the *Connect to GitLab* modal.
2. Click the *Authenticate with GitLab* button. CloudCannon will redirect you to GitLab.
3. Log in to your GitLab account.
4. Authorize CloudCannon to access your GitLab account.

GitLab will redirect you back to CloudCannon, where you can select a *Git Repository* to connect.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-GitLab-Unauthenticated-OAuth" alt="A screenshot of the GitLab page under Org Settings shows the Connect your GitLab account authentication method with an Authenticate with GitLab button." title="The GitLab settings with OAuth authentication" type="screenshot"/>

## Authenticate with a group access token

Alternatively, you can create a group access token in GitLab and save that token in CloudCannon. A group access token gives your CloudCannon *Organization* access to all the resources scoped to that token.

To create and configure a token, please read GitLab's documentation on <a href="https://docs.gitlab.com/ee/user/group/settings/group_access_tokens.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">group access tokens</a>.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  For full functionality in CloudCannon, your group access token should have at least the Maintainer role and the `api`, `read_api`, `read_repository`, and `write_repository` scopes.
</comp.Notice>

To authenticate with a group access token:

1. Select the *Authenticate with a group access token* option under *Authentication method* in the *Connect to GitLab* modal.
2. Paste your token into the *Group Access Token* field.
3. Enter the expiry date of your token in the *Expiry Date* field.
4. Click the *Save and authenticate* button.

CloudCannon will save your token and authenticate GitLab.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-GitLab-Unauthenticated-Token" alt="A screenshot of the GitLab page under Org Settings shows the group access token form with the Group Access Token and Expiry Date fields." title="The GitLab settings with token authentication" type="screenshot"/>

Once you've authenticated with a group access token, the *Authentication* tab on the *GitLab* page under *Org Settings* shows the token's bot account, labeled *Authenticated with access token*, and the date the token expires. CloudCannon will email the members of the *Owners Permission Group* in your *Organization* when the token is about to expire.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-GitLab-Authenticated-Token" alt="A screenshot of the Authentication tab on the GitLab page under Org Settings shows a group access token bot account with its expiry date and an Open profile button." title="GitLab authenticated with a group access token" type="screenshot"/>

## Select your Git Repository and branch

Once you've authenticated, you can choose the GitLab *Git Repository* and branch to connect to your *Site*. Under the *Branch Setup* heading, you can sync from a branch that already exists in your *Git Repository*, or have CloudCannon create a new branch from a source branch. If your *Git Repository* has no branches, CloudCannon will show a link to add one in GitLab.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  When you click the *Backup and Sync* button, CloudCannon will replace any existing files on your *Site* with the contents of the selected *Git Repository*. To cancel the process, exit the page instead.
</comp.Notice>

To connect your *Git Repository* and branch:

1. Select your *Git Repository* from the *Repository* dropdown.
2. Choose the *Use an existing branch* option or the *Create a new branch* option under the *Branch Setup* heading.
3. Pick your branch from the *Branch* dropdown. If you are creating a new branch, pick the branch to copy from the *Source branch* dropdown and enter a name in the *New branch name* field.
4. Click the *Backup and Sync* button.

CloudCannon will connect your *Site* to your GitLab *Git Repository*. CloudCannon pulls in any changes you push to your *Git Repository*, and automatically commits and pushes any changes you make in CloudCannon.
