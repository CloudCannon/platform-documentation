The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm> is where you manage a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> connected to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. From this page, you can see whether every <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> on your *Custom Domain* is working, add *Subdomains*, change the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">fallback redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm>, manage DNS, and change the settings of the domain.

You can open the *Domain* page by clicking the *Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm> to open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then clicking the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your domain. The *Domain* page opens on the *Summary* tab.

The *Domain* page has up to five tabs:

* **Summary** — The state of the *Subdomains*, DNS, and minimum TLS version of your *Custom Domain*, and a setup guide while DNS isn't set up yet. This is the default tab.
* **Subdomains** — Every *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain*, what it serves, and how visitors reach it, along with your fallback redirect.
* **DNS** — The DNS provider and nameserver status of your *Custom Domain*, the records each *Subdomain* needs and, if it uses <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, its DNS records.
* **Sites** — The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> that use your *Custom Domain*.
* **Domain Settings** — The *Details*, *TLS*, and *Danger* sections, where you choose your DNS provider, set the minimum TLS version, and disconnect the domain.

Which tabs you see depends on the permissions you hold for the *Custom Domain*:

* **DNS** — Needs the `base-domain:settings:dns:read` permission.
* **Sites** and **Domain Settings** — Need the `base-domain:settings:details:read` or `base-domain:delete:write` permission. On the *Domain Settings* tab, the *Details* and *TLS* sections need `base-domain:settings:details:read`, and the *Danger* section needs `base-domain:delete:write`.
* **Summary** and **Subdomains** — Shown if you can see the *Domain Settings* tab, or if you have the `base-domain:details:read` permission.

Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/) hold all of these permissions, so they see every tab. If you don't see a tab you need, ask a member of the *Owners* group in your *Organization*.

## The Summary tab

The *Summary* tab shows the state of your *Custom Domain* in three sections, each a heading above a *Card*: *Subdomains* on the left, and *DNS* and *Minimum TLS* on the right. The *DNS* and *Minimum TLS* sections end with a link to the tab or section where you can make changes.

### The setup guide

While your *Custom Domain* or a new *Subdomain* isn't live yet, a setup guide appears at the top of the *Summary* tab, above the *Subdomains* section. It shows each step, what to do next, and the records or nameservers to add, depending on whether your *Custom Domain* uses CloudCannon DNS or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>:

* **CloudCannon DNS** — The guide shows the nameservers to set at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, prompts you to bring over your other records, and warns you if CloudCannon DNS has no mail records for your domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#point-your-domain-at-cloudcannon-dns).
* **External DNS** — The guide lists every record each new *Subdomain* needs at your DNS provider, and the TXT records for the first wildcard certificate of your domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#follow-the-setup-guide).

CloudCannon refreshes the guide every minute while the page is open. You don't need to keep it open, because CloudCannon emails everyone who can edit the settings of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> when the domain goes live on CloudCannon DNS, or when a *Subdomain* you marked as updated goes live. If your domain or *Subdomain* goes live while you have the *Summary* tab open, the guide shows that it is live until you click the *Dismiss* button.

The *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* shows the same guide for the *Subdomain* that hosts a *Site*.

### Subdomains

The *Subdomains* *Card* shows:

* **Add a subdomain** — Click the *Add subdomain* button to add a *Subdomain* that serves a *Site* or redirects visitors.
* **Subdomain count** — The number of *Subdomains* on your *Custom Domain*, how many serve *Sites*, how many have no *Site* yet, and how many redirect. Click the *View subdomains* button to open the *Subdomains* tab.
* **Subdomains that need attention** — The *Subdomains* that don't reach CloudCannon, *Subdomains* with no *Site* yet, and *Subdomains* with certificate problems. Click the *View them* button to open the *Subdomains* tab with only those *Subdomains* listed. To list every *Subdomain* again, remove the *Subdomain needs attention* filter. This row only appears when at least one *Subdomain* needs attention.
* **Fallback** — The *Subdomain* to which CloudCannon sends visitors when they open a subdomain that isn't set up. Click the *Change fallback* button to choose another *Subdomain*.

If your *Custom Domain* has no *Subdomains* yet, the *Card* shows *No subdomains on [Base Domain] yet* and an *Add subdomain* button in place of these rows.

Adding a *Subdomain* or changing the fallback requires permission to edit the settings of the *Base Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/) and [updating the fallback redirect for your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/update-the-fallback-redirect-for-your-custom-domain/).

### DNS

The *DNS* *Card* shows:

* **DNS provider** — *CloudCannon DNS* or *External DNS*. To change it, use the *Change DNS provider* link on the *DNS* tab.
* **Nameservers** — Whether the nameservers for your *Custom Domain* match CloudCannon's nameservers (*Nameservers match*, with when CloudCannon last checked), still need updating at your domain registrar, are propagating, no longer point at CloudCannon DNS, or are managed by another DNS provider. The row can also show *Nameservers not assigned yet*, *Nameservers not assigned: the DNS zone could not be created*, *Nameservers not detected after 48 hours*, or *Nameservers on the legacy CloudCannon DNS*.
* **Required records** — How many *Subdomains* have a DNS record that needs a change before it reaches CloudCannon (*[Number] of [Total] need a change*), or *All [Number] point at CloudCannon*. If only one *Subdomain* isn't connected, the row is labeled with that *Subdomain* and its state instead, such as *[Subdomain] is not pointing at CloudCannon* or *[Subdomain]'s DNS record changed*. While your nameservers don't point at CloudCannon DNS yet, the row shows *Waiting for the nameservers*.

Click the *Check now* button beside the *DNS* heading to check your nameservers and *Subdomains* again. Click the *View DNS* link below the *Card* to open the *DNS* tab. If CloudCannon couldn't complete the latest check, the *Nameservers* row shows *Could not check* with how long ago the check failed.

The *DNS* *Card* only appears if you have permission to view the DNS settings of the *Base Domain*.

### Minimum TLS

The *Minimum TLS* *Card* shows the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, which applies to every *Subdomain*, and whether every *Subdomain* meets it. If any *Subdomains* are below the minimum, the *Card* names them. Click the *View TLS settings* link below the *Card* to open the *TLS* section under *Domain Settings*. The link only appears if you have the `base-domain:settings:details:read` permission. For more information, please read our documentation on [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/).

## The Subdomains tab

The *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page lists every *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain*. For each *Subdomain*, the list shows:

* **Subdomain** — The address of the *Subdomain*, with the *Sites* it serves, the domain it redirects to, or *No site yet. Visitors get the fallback* below it. If the *Subdomain* is your fallback and has no *Site*, it shows *No site yet. Visitors get a not found page* instead. CloudCannon labels the *Subdomain* that is your fallback redirect with *Fallback*.
* **Hosting** — How visitors reach the *Subdomain*: *CloudCannon Hosting*, *Cloudflare Proxy*, or *Single Origin*.
* **HTTPS** — The status of the *Subdomain's* certificate, such as *Active*, *Issuing*, *Renewing*, *Expiring*, *Expired*, *Failed*, or *No certificate*. Hover over a status to see more detail, and open the *Subdomain* page for the full explanation.
* **DNS** — Whether the *Subdomain's* record points at CloudCannon: *Connected*, *Not pointing at CloudCannon*, *No DNS record*, *Not checked yet*, or *Waiting for DNS* while CloudCannon looks for the record of a *Subdomain* that, in the last 48 hours, you added, changed the hosting of, or marked as updated, or whose domain changed DNS provider.

Click a *Subdomain* to open its page, where you can manage its *Sites* or its redirect, and its hosting, HTTPS, and DNS. Each *Subdomain* in the list also has a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm>, with options to open the *Subdomain* page, configure its hosting or HTTPS, check its DNS now or view its check history, use it as the fallback, change it to a redirect or to serve a *Site*, change the target of a redirect, and delete it. Below the list, the *Fallback* section shows your current fallback.

### Sort and filter the Subdomains

Click the sort dropdown to the left of the search field, which shows the current sort (e.g., *Name*), to sort the list by:

* **Name** — *Subdomains* with fewer levels first (e.g., `example.com` before `www.example.com`), then alphabetically. This is the default.
* **Name (Z-A)** — The reverse of *Name*.
* **Needs attention first** — *Subdomains* that don't reach CloudCannon, have no *Site* yet, or have a certificate problem, first.
* **Recently added** — The newest *Subdomains* first.

Click the *Add Filter* button to show only the *Subdomains* that match one or more values:

* **Attention** — *Needs attention* or *Doesn't need attention*.
* **Type** — *Site* or *Redirect*.
* **Hosting** — *CloudCannon Hosting*, *Cloudflare Proxy*, or *Single Origin*.
* **HTTPS** — *Active*, *Issuing*, *Renewing*, *Expired*, *Failed*, or *Missing*. *Missing* matches the *Subdomains* whose status is *No certificate*.
* **DNS** — *Connected*, *Not pointing at CloudCannon*, *No DNS record*, or *Not checked yet*.

You can also type in the search field to find a *Subdomain* by name.

For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/), [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/), [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/), and [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/).

## The DNS tab

The *DNS* tab starts with the DNS provider of your *Custom Domain*, with a *Change DNS provider* link to the *Details* section. The link only appears if you have the `base-domain:settings:details:read` permission. Below that, it shows:

* **Nameservers** — The nameserver status of your *Custom Domain*. On CloudCannon DNS, this is the setup guide, which stays on the *DNS* tab after your domain goes live (*[Base Domain] is live*), with the *Nameservers* table comparing the nameservers CloudCannon found with the nameservers your domain needs. On external DNS, this lists the nameservers CloudCannon found.
* **Required records** — One row for every *Subdomain*, with the record it *Needs*, the records CloudCannon *Found*, and its *Status*. On external DNS, the *Needs* column has a *Copy* button next to each record name and value. For each *Subdomain* that isn't connected, the row also has the *Fix* button on CloudCannon DNS, or the *I've updated it* button on external DNS.
* **DNS records** — On CloudCannon DNS, a table of the DNS records for your domain, with a *Filter* field and an *Add record* button. Click the *Context Menu* button for a record to edit or delete it.

On external DNS, the tab also shows a notice for each *Subdomain* that still isn't pointing at CloudCannon or whose DNS record changed, and an optional wildcard record to catch unknown subdomains. It also shows how the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/w/wildcard-certificate.yml">wildcard certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your *Base Domain* renews: the TXT records to add while a renewal is waiting, and a CNAME record you can add once so the certificate renews by itself, without TXT records. Once you have added the CNAME record, the tab says that the certificate renews by itself through the CNAME record you added.

For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/) and [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).

## The Sites tab

The *Sites* tab lists the *Sites* in your *Organization* that are served on your *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

## The Domain Settings tab

The *Domain Settings* tab has three sections:

* **Details** — The *Base Domain* and its DNS provider. Select the other DNS provider to see what happens when you switch, then click the *Switch to CloudCannon DNS* or *Switch to External DNS* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/) and [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).
* **TLS** — The minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/).
* **Danger** — Disconnect the *Custom Domain* from your *Organization*. For more information, please read our documentation on [disconnecting a Custom Domain from your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/disconnect-a-custom-domain-from-your-organization/).

## Domain emails

CloudCannon emails people in your *Organization* when something about your *Custom Domain* changes or needs action. Who receives each email depends on their permissions for the *Custom Domain* or the *Organization*. Each subject links to the section that describes the email and what to do next.

| Subject | When CloudCannon sends it | Who receives it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Organization]: [Name] added [Base Domain]](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/#organization-name-added-base-domain) | Someone connects a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write`, except the person who connected it |
| [[Base Domain] is live on CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#base-domain-is-live-on-cloudcannon-dns) | The nameservers of your domain point at CloudCannon. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [CloudCannon has not detected [Base Domain]'s new nameservers yet](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#cloudcannon-has-not-detected-base-domain-s-new-nameservers-yet) | On CloudCannon DNS, CloudCannon still can't see your new nameservers 48 hours after you marked them as updated, set up CloudCannon DNS, or connected the domain. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Base Domain]'s nameservers changed](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#base-domain-s-nameservers-changed) | The nameservers of a live domain change away from CloudCannon. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Subdomain] is live](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#subdomain-is-live) | On external DNS, a *Subdomain* you marked as updated in the last seven days points at CloudCannon, and its certificate is active. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Subdomain] is still not pointing at CloudCannon](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#subdomain-is-still-not-pointing-at-cloudcannon) | On external DNS, CloudCannon still can't find the DNS record for a *Subdomain* 48 hours after you marked it as updated, added the *Subdomain*, or changed its hosting or your DNS provider. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Subdomain]'s DNS record changed](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#subdomain-s-dns-record-changed) | The DNS record of a live *Subdomain* stops pointing at CloudCannon. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Action Required] [Subdomain] is failing to generate an SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/resolve-ssl-certificate-errors/#action-required-subdomain-is-failing-to-generate-an-ssl-certificate) | A certificate keeps failing to issue for a *Subdomain* whose DNS is connected. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Subdomain] has a new SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/resolve-ssl-certificate-errors/#subdomain-has-a-new-ssl-certificate) | A certificate issues after CloudCannon sent the failing email for it. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [Action required: [Base Domain] certificate is expiring](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#action-required-base-domain-certificate-is-expiring) | The wildcard certificate for a *Base Domain* on external DNS needs TXT records to renew, and you haven't added the CNAME record that lets it renew by itself. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [Second warning: [Base Domain] certificate is expiring](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#action-required-base-domain-certificate-is-expiring) | The renewal still needs TXT records six days before the wildcard certificate expires, if the first email went out more than ten days before it expired. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [You have added an extra custom domain to [Organization]](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/#you-have-added-an-extra-custom-domain-to-organization) | A *Subdomain* starts serving a *Site* and takes your *Organization* past the *Custom Domains* in your *Subscription Plan*. | Everyone with `org:billing:read` |
| [You have removed an extra custom domain from [Organization]](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/#you-have-removed-an-extra-custom-domain-from-organization) | A *Subdomain* stops serving a *Site* while your *Organization* is over the *Custom Domains* in your *Subscription Plan*. | Everyone with `org:billing:read` |
