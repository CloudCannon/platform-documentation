The *Domain* page is where you manage a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> connected to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>. From this page, you can see whether every subdomain on your *Custom Domain* is working, add subdomains, change the fallback redirect, manage DNS, and change the settings of the domain.

You can open the *Domain* page by clicking the *Domains* button in the *App Sidebar* to open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>, then clicking the *Card* for your domain. The *Domain* page opens on the *Summary* tab.

The *Domain* page has up to five tabs:

* **Summary** — The state of the subdomains, DNS, and minimum TLS version of your *Custom Domain*, and a setup guide while DNS isn't set up yet. This is the default tab.
* **Subdomains** — Every subdomain on your *Custom Domain*, what it serves, and how visitors reach it, along with your fallback redirect.
* **DNS** — The DNS provider and nameserver status of your *Custom Domain*, the records each subdomain needs and, if it uses CloudCannon DNS, its DNS records.
* **Sites** — The *Sites* that use your *Custom Domain*.
* **Domain Settings** — The *Details*, *TLS*, and *Danger* sections, where you choose your DNS provider, set the minimum TLS version, and disconnect the domain.

Which tabs you see depends on the permissions you hold for the *Custom Domain*:

* **DNS** — Needs the `base-domain:settings:dns:read` permission.
* **Sites** and **Domain Settings** — Need the `base-domain:settings:details:read` or `base-domain:delete:write` permission. On the *Domain Settings* tab, the *Details* and *TLS* sections need `base-domain:settings:details:read`, and the *Danger* section needs `base-domain:delete:write`.
* **Summary** and **Subdomains** — Shown if you can see the *Domain Settings* tab, or if you have the `base-domain:details:read` permission.

## The Summary tab

The *Summary* tab shows the state of your *Custom Domain* in three sections, each a heading above a *Card*: *Subdomains* on the left, and *DNS* and *Minimum TLS* on the right. The *DNS* and *Minimum TLS* sections end with a link to the tab or section where you can make changes.

### The setup guide

While your *Custom Domain* or a new subdomain isn't live yet, a setup guide appears at the top of the *Summary* tab, above the *Subdomains* section. It shows each step, what to do next, and the records or nameservers to add:

* **CloudCannon DNS** — The guide shows the nameservers to set at your domain registrar, prompts you to bring over your other records, and warns you if CloudCannon DNS has no mail records for your domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#point-your-domain-at-cloudcannon-dns).
* **External DNS** — The guide lists every record each new subdomain needs at your DNS provider, and the TXT records for the first wildcard certificate of your domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#follow-the-setup-guide).

CloudCannon refreshes the guide every minute while the page is open. You don't need to keep it open, because CloudCannon emails everyone who can edit the settings of the *Base Domain* when the domain goes live on CloudCannon DNS, or when a subdomain you marked as updated goes live. If your domain or subdomain goes live while you have the *Summary* tab open, the guide shows that it is live until you click the *Dismiss* button.

The *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* shows the same guide for the subdomain that hosts a *Site*.

### Subdomains

The *Subdomains* *Card* shows:

* **Add a subdomain** — Click the *Add subdomain* button to add a subdomain that serves a *Site* or redirects visitors.
* **Subdomain count** — The number of subdomains on your *Custom Domain*, how many serve *Sites*, how many have no *Site* yet, and how many redirect. Click the *View subdomains* button to open the *Subdomains* tab.
* **Subdomains that need attention** — The subdomains that don't reach CloudCannon, subdomains with no *Site* yet, and subdomains with certificate problems. Click the *Show them* button to open the *Subdomains* tab with only those subdomains listed. This row only appears when at least one subdomain needs attention.
* **Fallback** — The subdomain to which CloudCannon sends visitors when they open a subdomain that isn't set up. Click the *Change fallback* button to choose another subdomain.

If your *Custom Domain* has no subdomains yet, the *Card* shows *No subdomains on [Base Domain] yet* and an *Add subdomain* button in place of these rows.

Adding a subdomain or changing the fallback requires permission to edit the settings of the *Base Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the subdomains on your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-subdomains-on-your-custom-domain/) and [updating the fallback redirect for your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/update-the-fallback-redirect-for-your-custom-domain/).

### DNS

The *DNS* *Card* shows:

* **DNS provider** — *CloudCannon DNS* or *External DNS*. To change it, use the *Change DNS provider* link on the *DNS* tab.
* **Nameservers** — Whether the nameservers for your *Custom Domain* match CloudCannon's nameservers (*Nameservers match*, with when CloudCannon last checked), still need updating at your domain registrar, are propagating, no longer point at CloudCannon DNS, or are managed by another DNS provider.
* **Records your subdomains need** — How many subdomains have a DNS record that needs a change before it reaches CloudCannon, or *All [Number] point at CloudCannon*. If only one subdomain isn't connected, the row names it and its state, such as a subdomain that still isn't pointing at CloudCannon or whose DNS record changed. While your nameservers don't point at CloudCannon DNS yet, the row shows *Waiting for the nameservers*.

Click the *View DNS* link below the *Card* to open the *DNS* tab. Click the *Check now* button to check your nameservers and subdomains again. If CloudCannon couldn't complete the latest check, the row shows *Could not check* with how long ago the check failed.

The *DNS* *Card* only appears if you have permission to view the DNS settings of the *Base Domain*.

### Minimum TLS

The *Minimum TLS* *Card* shows the minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*, which applies to every subdomain, and whether every subdomain meets it. If any subdomains are below the minimum, the *Card* names them. Click the *View TLS settings* link below the *Card* to open the *TLS* section under *Domain Settings*. For more information, please read our documentation on [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/).

## The Subdomains tab

The *Subdomains* tab lists every subdomain on your *Custom Domain*, with the *Sites* it serves or the domain to which it redirects, its hosting option, its certificate status, and its DNS status. You can sort and filter the list. Click a subdomain to open its page, where you can manage its *Sites*, hosting, HTTPS, and DNS. Below the list, the *Fallback* section shows your current fallback.

For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the subdomains on your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-subdomains-on-your-custom-domain/) and [managing redirect subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-redirect-subdomains/).

## The DNS tab

The *DNS* tab starts with the DNS provider of your *Custom Domain*, with a *Change DNS provider* link to the *Details* section. Below that, it shows:

* **Nameservers** — The nameserver status of your *Custom Domain*. On CloudCannon DNS, this is the setup guide, which stays on the *DNS* tab after your domain goes live (*[Base Domain] is live*), with the *Nameservers* table comparing the nameservers CloudCannon found with the nameservers your domain needs. On external DNS, this lists the nameservers CloudCannon found.
* **Required records** — One row for every subdomain, with the record it *Needs*, the records CloudCannon *Found*, its *Status*, and an action. On CloudCannon DNS, the action is the *Fix* button. On external DNS, it is the *Copy* buttons and the *I've updated it* button.
* **DNS records** — On CloudCannon DNS, a table of the DNS records for your domain, with a *Filter* field and an *Add record* button. Click the *Context Menu* button for a record to edit or delete it.

On external DNS, the tab also shows a notice for each subdomain that still isn't pointing at CloudCannon or whose DNS record changed, and an optional wildcard record to catch unknown subdomains. It also shows how the wildcard certificate for your *Base Domain* renews: the TXT records to add while a renewal is waiting, and a CNAME record you can add once so the certificate renews by itself, without TXT records. Once you have added the CNAME record, the tab says that the certificate renews by itself through the CNAME record you added.

For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/) and [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).

## The Sites tab

The *Sites* tab lists the *Sites* in your *Organization* that are served on your *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

## The Domain Settings tab

The *Domain Settings* tab has three sections:

* **Details** — The *Base Domain* and its DNS provider. Select the other DNS provider to see what happens when you switch, then click the *Switch to CloudCannon DNS* or *Switch to External DNS* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/) and [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).
* **TLS** — The minimum TLS version for your *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/).
* **Danger** — Disconnect the *Custom Domain* from your *Organization*. For more information, please read our documentation on [disconnecting a Custom Domain from your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/disconnect-a-custom-domain-from-your-organization/).

## Domain emails

CloudCannon emails people in your *Organization* when something about your *Custom Domain* changes or needs action. Who receives each email depends on their permissions for the *Custom Domain* or the *Organization*. Each subject links to the section that describes the email and what to do next.

| Subject | When CloudCannon sends it | Who receives it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Organization]: [Name] added [Base Domain]](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/#organization-name-added-base-domain) | Someone connects a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*. | Everyone with `base-domain:details:read`, except the person who connected it |
| [[Base Domain] is live on CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#base-domain-is-live-on-cloudcannon-dns) | The nameservers of your domain point at CloudCannon. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [CloudCannon has not detected [Base Domain]'s new nameservers yet](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#cloudcannon-has-not-detected-base-domain-s-new-nameservers-yet) | On CloudCannon DNS, CloudCannon still can't see your new nameservers 48 hours after you marked them as updated, set up CloudCannon DNS, or connected the domain. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Base Domain]'s nameservers changed](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/#base-domain-s-nameservers-changed) | The nameservers of a live domain change away from CloudCannon. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Subdomain] is live](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#subdomain-is-live) | CloudCannon finds the DNS record for a subdomain on external DNS and issues its certificate. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Subdomain] is still not pointing at CloudCannon](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#subdomain-is-still-not-pointing-at-cloudcannon) | On external DNS, CloudCannon still can't find the DNS record for a subdomain 48 hours after you marked it as updated, added the subdomain, or changed its hosting or your DNS provider. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Subdomain]'s DNS record changed](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#subdomain-s-dns-record-changed) | The DNS record of a live subdomain stops pointing at CloudCannon. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Action Required] [Subdomain] is failing to generate an SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/resolve-ssl-certificate-errors/#action-required-subdomain-is-failing-to-generate-an-ssl-certificate) | A certificate keeps failing to issue for a subdomain whose DNS is connected. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [[Subdomain] has a new SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/resolve-ssl-certificate-errors/#subdomain-has-a-new-ssl-certificate) | A certificate issues after CloudCannon sent the failing email for it. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [Action required: [Base Domain] certificate is expiring](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#action-required-base-domain-certificate-is-expiring) | A renewal of the wildcard certificate for a domain on external DNS needs TXT records. | Everyone with `base-domain:settings:details:write` |
| [You have added an extra custom domain to [Organization]](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/#you-have-added-an-extra-custom-domain-to-organization) | A subdomain starts serving a *Site* and takes your *Organization* past the *Custom Domains* in your *Subscription Plan*. | Everyone with `org:billing:read` |
| [You have removed an extra custom domain from [Organization]](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/#you-have-removed-an-extra-custom-domain-from-organization) | A subdomain stops serving a *Site* while your *Organization* is over the *Custom Domains* in your *Subscription Plan*. | Everyone with `org:billing:read` |
