*Site Building Mode* is the setting that decides whether CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build.yml">builds</comp.GlossaryTerm> and hosts your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Each *Site* is in one of two modes, *Hosted Mode* or *Headless Mode*, and you can change the mode on the *Details* page under *Site Settings*.

The *Site Building Mode* setting only appears for *Sites* that use <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/u/unified-configuration.yml">Unified Configuration</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Other *Sites* are always in *Hosted Mode*. For more information, please read our documentation on [migrating to Unified Configuration](/documentation/developer-guides/unified-configuration-migration-guide/).

## Hosted Mode

In *Hosted Mode*, CloudCannon builds your *Site* and hosts the build output. *Hosted Mode* is the default for new *Sites*, and gives your *Site*:

* A free <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/testing-domain.yml">Testing Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>, where you can preview your built *Site*.
* The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/v/visual-editor.yml">Visual Editor</comp.GlossaryTerm>, where your team can edit content on a preview of the page.
* Preview screenshots of the files in your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/collection.yml">Collection</comp.GlossaryTerm> browsers.
* The option to serve your *Site* on a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

Choose *Hosted Mode* if CloudCannon builds your *Site*, even if another provider serves it to visitors, or if you want your team to edit with the *Visual Editor*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Site build is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-site-build/) and [what a subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/).

## Headless Mode

In *Headless Mode*, CloudCannon doesn't build or host your *Site*. Your team can still edit your source files in CloudCannon, and CloudCannon saves their changes to your files as usual. A *Site* in *Headless Mode* has no *Testing Domain*, *Visual Editor*, or *Collection* screenshots, and can't be served on a *Custom Domain*.

Choose *Headless Mode* if your *Site* is dynamic, such as a *Site* that uses server-side rendering, or if another provider builds and hosts it. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Headless Mode is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-headless-mode/) and [what external hosting is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-external-hosting/).

## Switch Site Building Mode

Changing the mode of a *Site* requires permission to edit its details (the `site:settings:details:write` permission), which members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/) hold. You can also grant it to a [Custom Permission Group](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/). You select the mode on the *Details* page under *Site Settings*, then click the *Update Details* button.

Switching to *Headless Mode* opens the *Switch to Headless Mode?* modal, where you click the *Turn off hosting and builds* button to confirm. If your *Site* has built, switching takes it offline straight away and stops any further builds. CloudCannon won't switch a *Site* that has a *Custom Domain* to *Headless Mode*, so remove the *Custom Domain* from your *Site* first. For more information, please read our documentation on [enabling Headless Mode](/documentation/developer-articles/enable-headless-mode/) and [removing a Custom Domain from your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/remove-a-custom-domain-from-your-site/).

You can switch back to *Hosted Mode* at any time, without a confirmation step. While a *Site* is in *Headless Mode*, the build setup steps on the *Summary* tab of its *Site Dashboard* also show a *Turn off headless mode* button, which switches it back.

## Set the mode for new Sites

New *Sites* use *Hosted Mode*. To create a *Site* in *Headless Mode*, set `mode` to `headless` in the `.cloudcannon/initial-site-settings.json` file in your repository. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring your initial Site settings](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-your-initial-site-settings/).
