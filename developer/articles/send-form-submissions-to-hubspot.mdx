You can forward the submissions an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inbox</comp.GlossaryTerm> receives to a HubSpot form, using a *HubSpot* target.

A *HubSpot* target does not take a webhook URL. CloudCannon builds the endpoint it posts to from your HubSpot *Portal ID* and *Form GUID*, so find both in HubSpot first.

To find your *Portal ID* and *Form GUID*:

1. In HubSpot, open your *Settings*, then select *Account Defaults* under *Account Management*.
2. Copy the account ID listed on that page, which is your *Portal ID*.
3. Open the form that will receive your submissions in the HubSpot form editor.
4. Copy the GUID from the form editor URL, or from the embed code HubSpot gives you for that form.

You now have both of the values a *HubSpot* target needs.

Now add the target in CloudCannon. Select *HubSpot* in the *Choose your target type* dropdown, then enter your values in the *Portal ID* and *Form GUID* fields. For the full steps, and for the *Spam protection* and *Links to attached files* options every target carries, please read our documentation on [adding a target to an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-target-to-an-inbox/).

CloudCannon will post each new submission to that HubSpot form, in the shape the HubSpot Forms API expects.

## Map your form fields to HubSpot fields

HubSpot rejects a submission that carries a field its form does not define, so a *HubSpot* target sends only the fields you map. Each row under the *HubSpot field map* heading pairs one field on your form with the HubSpot field it fills. At least one pair is required, and CloudCannon will not save the target without one.

A row is two empty boxes side by side, with no labels above them. The left box reads *HubSpot field name* until you type in it, and the right box reads *Your form field name*.

To map a field:

1. Type the name of the HubSpot field into the left box in the row.
2. Type the name of the matching field on your form into the right box.
3. Click the *Add field* button to start another row, then repeat for every field you want to send.

CloudCannon will send the fields you map and leave every other field out of the submission it posts. Your *Inbox* still stores every field, including the ones you do not map, so an unmapped field is missing from HubSpot rather than lost.

If HubSpot starts rejecting your submissions, check that each HubSpot field name still matches a field the form defines, and that every field HubSpot requires has a pair in the map.

## Send extra context with each submission

Two checkboxes under the *HubSpot context* heading add information alongside the fields you map.

Turn on the *Send the page the form was submitted from* checkbox to include the page URI and name with each submission, so HubSpot can attribute it.

Turn on the *Send a legitimate interest legal consent* checkbox to record consent against the contact in HubSpot. Only turn this on if your form collects that consent.
