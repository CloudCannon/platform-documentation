<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  *Inboxes* only work for *Sites* hosted through CloudCannon. If you host your *Site* elsewhere, or use CloudCannon in [Headless Mode](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-headless-mode/), your forms will not reach an *Inbox*.
</comp.Notice>

An <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inbox</comp.GlossaryTerm> collects and stores the submissions that visitors send through the forms on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm>. It means you can receive form submissions without running a backend server of your own.

An *Inbox* belongs to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> rather than to one *Site*, and you manage it from the *Forms* page under *Org Settings*. You then link it to the *Sites* whose forms should submit to it, so several *Sites* can share one *Inbox*, and one *Site* can have several. For more information, please read our documentation on [connecting your Site to an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-your-site-to-an-inbox/).

Each linked *Site* shows its *Inboxes* in the *Site Navigation*, where anyone with access can read, filter, and export the submissions it holds. For more information, please read our documentation on [viewing your form submissions](/documentation/user-articles/view-your-form-submissions/).

## How submissions reach an Inbox

A form reaches an *Inbox* by naming its key in an `inbox_key` input. When a visitor submits the form, CloudCannon accepts the POST request, records the submission against that *Inbox*, and redirects the visitor to the form's action. For more information, please read our documentation on [creating a Form](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-form/).

CloudCannon sorts each submission into a category as it arrives, so spam and failures stay separate from the submissions you want to read. A submission that fails a CAPTCHA check, exceeds a rate limit, or trips the `_gotcha` honeypot does not disappear; it lands in the *Spam* or *Errored* category instead.

## What an Inbox does with a submission

Storing the submission is the *Inbox's* main job, and it is the only one that happens by default. Everything else is opt-in:

* **Targets** forward a copy of each submission to an email address or an external service. An *Inbox* with no targets still collects submissions normally. For more information, please read our documentation on [what an Inbox Target is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-an-inbox-target/).
* **A CAPTCHA provider** checks each submission before the *Inbox* accepts it. For more information, please read our documentation on [reducing spam with a CAPTCHA](/documentation/developer-articles/reduce-spam-with-a-captcha/).
* **A submission retention period** decides how long the *Inbox* keeps submissions before deleting them.

How many *Inboxes* you can link to a *Site*, how many targets each can carry, and how many submissions each accepts all depend on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. For more information, please read our documentation on [how Inbox limits work](/documentation/developer-articles/how-do-inbox-limits-work/).

## Disconnecting and deleting

Unlinking an *Inbox* from a *Site* stops that *Site's* forms reaching it and leaves the *Inbox* and its submissions intact. Removing an *Inbox* deletes it from your *Organization* along with every submission it holds, which cannot be undone. For more information, please read our documentation on [unlinking an Inbox from your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/unlink-an-inbox-from-your-site/) and [removing an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/remove-an-inbox/).
