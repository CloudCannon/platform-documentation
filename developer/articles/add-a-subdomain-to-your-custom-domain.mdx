<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, can add *Subdomains*.
</comp.Notice>

Every address you host on a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> in CloudCannon, including the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> itself (e.g., `example.com`). When you add a *Subdomain*, you choose its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain-type.yml">Type</comp.GlossaryTerm>:

* **Site** — The *Subdomain* serves one or more <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm>, each at its own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subpath.yml">subpath</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Once it serves a *Site*, it counts toward the number of *Subdomains* serving *Sites* that your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> includes.
* **Redirect** — The *Subdomain* sends every visitor to another domain, keeping the path and query string.

For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/).

This article covers adding a *Subdomain* from the *Domain* page. You can also add a *Subdomain* while you [add a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/) from *Site Settings*.

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/).

If you are a member of multiple <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm>, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

## Add a Subdomain that serves Sites

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Adding a *Subdomain* that serves a *Site* also requires the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for that *Site*.
</comp.Notice>

To serve a *Site* on the *Subdomain*, the *Site* must be in <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosted-mode.yml">Hosted Mode</comp.GlossaryTerm>. A *Site* in <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/headless-mode.yml">Headless Mode</comp.GlossaryTerm> can't have a *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Site Building Mode is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-site-building-mode/).

To add a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the domain you want to add a *Subdomain* to. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains* on your *Custom Domain*.
4. Click the *Add a Subdomain* button in the top right of the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the *Add a Subdomain* modal.
5. Enter the *Subdomain* name in the *Subdomain* field (e.g., `docs`). Leave this field empty to add the *Base Domain* itself.
6. Select the *Site* option from the *Type* dropdown. CloudCannon will show the *Site* dropdown and the *Subpath* field.
7. (Optional.) Select the *Site* you want the *Subdomain* to serve from the *Site* dropdown.
8. (Optional.) If you selected a *Site*, enter a path in the *Subpath* field to serve the *Site* at that subpath (e.g., `/docs/`). Leave this field empty to serve the *Site* at the root of the *Subdomain*.
9. Click the *Add Subdomain* button.

CloudCannon will add the *Subdomain* to the list on the *Subdomains* tab.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Subdomains-Tab-Add-Subdomain-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add a Subdomain modal shows jobs in the Subdomain field, the Type dropdown set to Site, Jetstream selected in the Site dropdown, and openings in the Subpath field." title="The Add a Subdomain modal for a Site Subdomain" type="screenshot"/>

You can also click the *Add a Subdomain* button on the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.

If you leave the *Site* dropdown empty, CloudCannon configures the *Subdomain* with no *Site* yet, and its visitors see a 404 page until you add a *Site*.

When you add the *Base Domain* (e.g., `example.com`) as a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site-subdomain.yml">Site Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm>, with or without a *Site*, CloudCannon also adds `www.example.com` as a redirect to it, unless `www.example.com` already exists. When you add `www.example.com`, CloudCannon adds `example.com` as a redirect to it in the same way. The *Add a Subdomain* modal shows this redirect below the *Site* dropdown (e.g., *CloudCannon will also add www.example.com as a Redirect Subdomain to example.com.*).

If the *Site* you select already has a *Custom Domain*, the *Add a Subdomain* modal shows where the *Site* moves from and to. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/).

If you select a *Site* and the new *Subdomain* takes your *Organization* over the number of *Subdomains* serving *Sites* that its *Subscription Plan* includes, CloudCannon opens the *Confirm Billing Change* modal. Click the *Accept Billing Increase* button to continue. *Subdomains* with no *Site* yet do not count toward this number, so CloudCannon asks when you add the first *Site* to them instead. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).

When the new *Subdomain* takes your *Organization* over that number, CloudCannon also emails the people who manage its billing. For more information, please read our documentation on [the emails after you add a Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/#emails-after-you-add-a-custom-domain).

In a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> on its extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, serving a *Site* on a *Subdomain* that had no *Sites* ends the trial five days later. This applies on every *Subscription Plan* except the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/enterprise-plan.yml">Enterprise Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon opens the *Confirm End of Trial* modal first. Click the *End extended trial* button to continue. For more information, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).

## Add a Redirect Subdomain

To add a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm>:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain you want to add a *Subdomain* to. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains* on your *Custom Domain*.
4. Click the *Add a Subdomain* button in the top right of the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the *Add a Subdomain* modal.
5. Enter the *Subdomain* name in the *Subdomain* field (e.g., `promo`). Leave this field empty to add the *Base Domain* itself.
6. Select the *Redirect* option from the *Type* dropdown. CloudCannon will show the *Redirect visitors to* field and the *Status code* dropdown.
7. Enter the domain to which you want to send visitors in the *Redirect visitors to* field. Unless you are adding the *Base Domain* itself, CloudCannon fills in your *Base Domain* to start with.
8. (Optional.) Select a status code from the *Status code* dropdown. Leave it on *301 permanent redirect* for a permanent redirect.
9. Click the *Add Subdomain* button.

CloudCannon will add the *Redirect Subdomain* to the list on the *Subdomains* tab.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Subdomains-Tab-Add-Redirect-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add a Subdomain modal shows www in the Subdomain field, the Type dropdown set to Redirect, example.com in the Redirect visitors to field, 301 permanent redirect in the Status code dropdown, and the Add Subdomain button." title="The Add a Subdomain modal for a Redirect Subdomain" type="screenshot"/>

The destination can be a *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain* or any other domain. If the destination is on a *Custom Domain* connected to your *Organization*, it must already be a *Subdomain* there. CloudCannon won't add a redirect to the *Subdomain* itself, or a redirect that sends visitors around in a loop, and shows why below the *Redirect visitors to* field. For how loops form and how to fix one, please read our documentation on [fixing a redirect loop](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/#fix-a-redirect-loop).

*Redirect Subdomains* do not count toward the number of *Subdomains* serving *Sites* that your *Subscription Plan* includes. For more information on status codes, and on redirecting a whole domain to another domain, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/).

## After you add a Subdomain

Every new *Subdomain* starts on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-hosting.yml">CloudCannon Hosting</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Once the *Subdomain's* DNS points at CloudCannon, visitors can reach it, and CloudCannon issues its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm>. If your *Custom Domain* uses external DNS and the *Subdomain* is already live elsewhere, you can get the certificate before you switch DNS, using TXT Records. This isn't available for your *Base Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [issuing the SSL Certificate with TXT validation](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#issue-the-ssl-certificate-with-txt-validation).

If your *Custom Domain* has no <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">Fallback Redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm> yet, the new *Subdomain* becomes your *Fallback Redirect*, whichever *Type* you chose. If it is a *Site Subdomain* with no *Site* yet, its visitors, and visitors to subdomains you haven't set up, see a 404 page until you add a *Site*.

If your *Custom Domain* uses <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the wildcard record CloudCannon adds for your *Base Domain* covers new *Subdomains* one level below it (e.g., `docs.example.com`) on *CloudCannon Hosting*. A *Subdomain* nested more than one level deep (e.g., `api.docs.example.com`) needs its own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-records.yml">DNS Record</comp.GlossaryTerm>, which CloudCannon doesn't add when you add the *Subdomain*. To add it, click the *Fix* button for the *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [fixing the DNS Record for a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-dns-records-on-cloudcannon-dns/#fix-the-dns-record-for-a-subdomain) and [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/).

If your *Custom Domain* uses <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the *DNS* tab of the *Domain* page and the *DNS* section of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain-page.yml">Subdomain page</comp.GlossaryTerm> list the records the new *Subdomain* needs at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-provider.yml">DNS Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm>. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).

To change how visitors reach the *Subdomain*, or how it gets its certificate, please read our documentation on [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/) and [adding an auto-generated SSL Certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/).

## Troubleshoot adding a Subdomain

If CloudCannon doesn't add your *Subdomain*, or the *Add a Subdomain* modal doesn't let you choose what you expect, here are some common causes. If you see an error that isn't listed here, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Organization*, the *Subdomain* you tried to add, and the full error message.

### You do not have permission to edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Add a Subdomain* button. You can view the *Custom Domain*, but you don't have the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for its *Base Domain*. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for this permission.

### You can only select a Site if you have permission to change its hosting.

This message appears below the *Site* dropdown, which disables any *Site* whose hosting you can't change. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for that *Site*.

### This Subdomain is already in use.

The *Subdomain* you entered already exists on your *Custom Domain*. To serve another *Site* on it, add the *Site* to the existing *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/).

### Subdomains can only use letters, numbers, and hyphens.

The *Subdomain* field takes the name in front of your *Base Domain* (e.g., `docs` for `docs.example.com`). Remove any other characters, such as spaces or underscores.

### Cannot select this Subdomain until you configure it on [Base Domain].

The destination you entered in the *Redirect visitors to* field is on a *Custom Domain* connected to your *Organization*, but it isn't a *Subdomain* there yet. Add the destination as a *Subdomain* first, or enter a different destination.
