<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` and `site:settings:hosting:write` permissions, can add *Subdomains*.
</comp.Notice>

Every address you host on a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> in CloudCannon, including the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> itself (e.g., `example.com`). When you add a *Subdomain*, you choose its *Type*:

* **Site** — The *Subdomain* serves one or more <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm>, each at its own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subpath.yml">subpath</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You can add a *Subdomain* with or without a *Site*.
* **Redirect** — The *Subdomain* sends every visitor to another domain, keeping the path and query string.

For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/).

This article covers adding a *Subdomain* from the *Domain* page. You can also add a *Subdomain* while you [add a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/) from *Site Settings*.

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/).

## Add a Subdomain that serves Sites

To serve a *Site* on the *Subdomain*, the *Site* must be in *Hosted Mode*. A *Site* in *Headless Mode* can't have a *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Site Building Mode is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-site-building-mode/).

To add a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites*:

1. Log in to CloudCannon.
2. Select the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> your *Custom Domain* is connected to.
3. Click the *Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
4. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the domain you want to add a *Subdomain* to. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
5. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains* on your *Custom Domain*.
6. Click the *Add subdomain* button. CloudCannon will open the *Add subdomain* modal.
7. Enter the *Subdomain* name in the *Subdomain* field (e.g., `docs`). Leave this field empty to add the *Base Domain* itself.
8. Select the *Site* option from the *Type* dropdown. CloudCannon will show the *Site to serve* dropdown and the *Path* field.
9. (Optional.) Select the *Site* you want the *Subdomain* to serve from the *Site to serve* dropdown.
10. (Optional.) Enter a path in the *Path* field to serve the *Site* at that subpath (e.g., `/docs/`). Leave this field empty to serve the *Site* at the root of the *Subdomain*.
11. Click the *Add subdomain* button.

CloudCannon will add the *Subdomain* to the list on the *Subdomains* tab.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domains-Network-Tab-Add-Subdomain-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add subdomain modal shows the Subdomain field, the Type dropdown set to Site, the Site to serve dropdown and the Path field, and a note that the Careers site moves from jobs.example.org to jobs.example.com/openings/." title="The Add subdomain modal for a Site Subdomain" type="screenshot"/>

You can also click the *Add subdomain* button on the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.

If you leave the *Site to serve* dropdown empty, CloudCannon sets the *Subdomain* up with no *Site* yet, and sends its visitors to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">fallback redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm> until you add a *Site*.

When you add the *Base Domain* (e.g., `example.com`) as a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites*, CloudCannon also adds `www.example.com` as a redirect to it, unless `www.example.com` already exists. When you add `www.example.com`, CloudCannon adds `example.com` as a redirect to it in the same way. The *Add subdomain* modal shows this redirect below the *Subdomain* field before you add the *Subdomain*.

If the *Site* you select already has a *Custom Domain*, the *Add subdomain* modal shows where the *Site* moves from and to. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/).

If you select a *Site* and the new *Subdomain* takes your *Organization* over the number of *Custom Domains* in its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, CloudCannon opens the *Confirm Billing Change* modal. Click the *Accept Billing Increase* button to continue. *Subdomains* with no *Site* yet do not count toward this number, so CloudCannon asks when you add the first *Site* to them instead. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).

In a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> on its extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, serving a *Site* on a *Subdomain* that had no *Sites* ends the trial five days later. CloudCannon opens the *Confirm End of Trial* modal first. Click the *End extended trial* button to continue. For more information, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).

## Add a Redirect Subdomain

To add a *Redirect Subdomain*:

1. Log in to CloudCannon.
2. Select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to.
3. Click the *Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
4. Click the *Card* for the domain you want to add a *Subdomain* to. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
5. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains* on your *Custom Domain*.
6. Click the *Add subdomain* button. CloudCannon will open the *Add subdomain* modal.
7. Enter the *Subdomain* name in the *Subdomain* field (e.g., `promo`). Leave this field empty to add the *Base Domain* itself.
8. Select the *Redirect* option from the *Type* dropdown. CloudCannon will show the *Send visitors to* field and the *Status code* dropdown.
9. Enter the domain to which you want to send visitors in the *Send visitors to* field.
10. (Optional.) Select a status code from the *Status code* dropdown. Leave it on *301 Permanent* for a permanent redirect.
11. Click the *Add subdomain* button.

CloudCannon will add the *Redirect Subdomain* to the list on the *Subdomains* tab.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domains-Network-Tab-Add-Redirect-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add subdomain modal shows promo in the Subdomain field, the Type dropdown set to Redirect, blog.example.com in the Send visitors to field, the redirect 301 promo.example.com/* to blog.example.com/$1, and the Cancel and Add subdomain buttons." title="The Add subdomain modal for a Redirect Subdomain" type="screenshot"/>

The destination can be a *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain* or any other domain. If the destination is on a *Custom Domain* connected to your *Organization*, it must already be a *Subdomain* there. CloudCannon won't add a redirect to the *Subdomain* itself, or a redirect that sends visitors around in a loop, and shows why below the *Send visitors to* field.

*Redirect Subdomains* do not count toward the number of *Custom Domains* in your *Subscription Plan*. For more information on status codes, and on redirecting a whole domain to another domain, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/).

## After you add a Subdomain

Every new *Subdomain* starts on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-hosting.yml">CloudCannon Hosting</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Once the *Subdomain's* DNS points at CloudCannon, visitors can reach it, and CloudCannon issues its SSL certificate. If your *Custom Domain* uses external DNS and the *Subdomain* is already live elsewhere, you can get the certificate before you switch DNS, using TXT records. This isn't available for your *Base Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [getting HTTPS ready before you switch DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#get-https-ready-before-you-switch-dns).

If your *Custom Domain* has no fallback redirect yet, the new *Subdomain* becomes your fallback redirect, whichever *Type* you chose. If it is a *Site Subdomain* with no *Site* yet, its visitors see a not found page until you add a *Site*.

On <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the wildcard record CloudCannon adds for your *Base Domain* covers new *Subdomains* one level below it (e.g., `docs.example.com`) on *CloudCannon Hosting*. A *Subdomain* nested more than one level deep (e.g., `api.docs.example.com`) needs its own record. On <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the *DNS* tab of the *Domain* page and the *DNS* section of the *Subdomain* page list the records the new *Subdomain* needs at your DNS provider. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/) and [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).

To change how visitors reach the *Subdomain*, or how it gets its certificate, please read our documentation on [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/) and [adding an auto-generated SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/).
