<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `site:settings:form:write` permission, can unlink an *Inbox* from a *Site*.
</comp.Notice>

Unlinking an *Inbox* disconnects it from a single *Site*. Forms on that *Site* stop sending submissions to the *Inbox*, and the *Inbox* no longer appears under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inboxes</comp.GlossaryTerm> in that *Site's* *Site Navigation*.

Unlinking does not delete the *Inbox* or any of its submissions. The *Inbox* stays in your *Org Settings*, keeps its targets, and continues to receive submissions from any other *Site* still linked to it. To delete an *Inbox* and its submissions outright, please read our documentation on [removing an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/remove-an-inbox/).

To unlink an *Inbox* from your *Site*:

1. Navigate to the *Forms* page under *Site Settings*.
2. Open the *Context Menu* for the *Inbox* you want to disconnect.
3. Click the *Unlink* option.
4. Confirm your choice in the *Delete inbox from site* modal.

CloudCannon will disconnect the *Inbox* from your *Site*, and submissions from that *Site* will no longer reach it.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Forms-Unlink-Inbox-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Delete inbox from site modal shows the Inbox being unlinked, a warning, and Cancel and Unlink buttons." title="The unlink Inbox modal" type="screenshot" />

If the *Inbox* you unlink is the default *Inbox* for that *Site*, forms that submit without an `inbox_key` input will no longer be processed. Set another *Inbox* as the default, or add the `inbox_key` input to those forms. For more information, please read our documentation on [setting a default Inbox for your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/set-a-default-inbox-for-your-site/).
