<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for the *Site* and the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for the *Base Domain*, can remove a *Custom Domain* from a *Site*.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a unique address used to identify a website (e.g., <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/">cloudcannon.com</a>). You can connect a *Custom Domain* to your CloudCannon *Organization* and add it to a *Site* to host your website content on that domain.

You may want to remove a *Custom Domain* from a CloudCannon *Site* to change the domain hosting your content, move a *Custom Domain* to a different CloudCannon *Site*, or to [disconnect a Custom Domain from your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/disconnect-a-custom-domain-from-your-organization/).

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Removing a *Custom Domain* from your *Site* stops visitors from seeing your *Site* on that domain. Your *Site* keeps its [Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/).
</comp.Notice>

If your *Site* is the last *Site* on its subdomain, CloudCannon asks whether to leave the subdomain set up with no *Site*, turn it into a redirect, or delete it. Leaving the subdomain set up keeps its hosting and certificate, which suits a temporary take-down. If you leave the subdomain set up or delete it, CloudCannon also deletes any redirects that point at the subdomain, unless you uncheck the checkbox for them. If you turn it into a redirect, those redirects send visitors to the new destination instead. If you redirect the subdomain, CloudCannon refuses a destination that would send visitors in a loop, or back to the subdomain itself. If you delete the subdomain and it was your fallback, another subdomain becomes the fallback. You can choose which one under *Fallback* on the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page. For more information on each option, please read our documentation on [managing the subdomains on your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-subdomains-on-your-custom-domain/#remove-a-site-from-a-subdomain).

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/) and [added a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

The *Remove custom domain* button is disabled unless you can both change the hosting of your *Site* and edit the settings of its *Base Domain*. If it's disabled, ask someone in your *Organization* who has both permissions.

To remove a *Custom Domain* from your *Site*:

1. Click the *Sites* button in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Sites Browser*.
2. Identify the *Site* from which you want to remove a *Custom Domain* and click the *Card* for the *Site*. CloudCannon will open the *Dashboard* for that *Site*.
3. Navigate to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*.
4. Click the *Remove custom domain* button in the *Danger zone* section. CloudCannon will open the *Remove [Subdomain] from [Site Name]?* modal.
5. If your *Site* is the last *Site* on the subdomain, select the *Leave it set up*, *Redirect*, or *Delete it* option under *What happens to [Subdomain]*. If you select *Redirect*, enter the domain to which you want to send visitors in the *Send visitors to* field.
6. (Optional.) If you selected *Leave it set up* or *Delete it*, uncheck the *Delete this redirect too* or *Delete these redirects too* checkbox to keep the subdomains that redirect to this one.
7. Click the *Remove from [Subdomain]* button. If your *Site* is the last *Site* on the subdomain, click the *Remove domain*, *Remove and redirect*, or *Remove and delete* button instead.

CloudCannon will immediately stop hosting your *Site* on the *Custom Domain*.

You can also remove a *Site* from the subdomain page, which you can open from the *Subdomains* tab of your *Domain* page.
