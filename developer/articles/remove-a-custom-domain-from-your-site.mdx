<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `site:settings:hosting:write` and `base-domain:settings:details:write` permissions, can remove a *Custom Domain* from a *Site*.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a domain you own and connect to your CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so visitors reach your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> at your own address (e.g., <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/">cloudcannon.com</a>).

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Removing a *Custom Domain* from your *Site* stops visitors from seeing your *Site* on that domain. Your *Site* keeps its [Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/).
</comp.Notice>

If you want to keep sending visitors somewhere, you can change the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> into a redirect instead, with the *Change Subdomain Type* button next to *Change Subdomain type to Redirect* in the *Danger zone* section under *Subdomain settings* on the *Custom Domain* page. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/#change-a-subdomain-that-serves-sites-to-a-redirect).

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/) and [added a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/). If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* that owns the *Site* first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

There are two ways to remove a *Custom Domain* from your *Site*: from the *Custom Domain* page under your *Site Settings*, or from the page for its *Subdomain* on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

If your *Site* is the last *Site* on its *Subdomain*, CloudCannon asks what happens to the *Subdomain*:

* **Leave it set up** — The *Subdomain* stays set up with no *Site*. Its hosting and certificate stay connected, so you can add a *Site* back without waiting for DNS or a new certificate. Until then, visitors see a 404 page. This is the default. Choose this option to take your website down temporarily.
* **Redirect visitors** — The *Subdomain* becomes a permanent (301) redirect that sends every visitor to another domain, keeping the path and query string. If CloudCannon can't use the destination you enter in the *Redirect visitors to* field, it shows why below the field. For each message, please read our documentation on [troubleshooting your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/#troubleshoot-managing-your-redirect-subdomains).
* **Delete the Subdomain** — CloudCannon deletes the *Subdomain* and its certificate, and sends its visitors to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">Fallback Redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm>. If the *Subdomain* was your *Fallback Redirect*, CloudCannon will make another *Subdomain* your *Fallback Redirect*, which you can change on the *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page.

For more information on each option, please read our documentation on [removing a Site from a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/#remove-a-site-from-a-subdomain).

## Remove a Custom Domain from your Site Settings

To remove a *Custom Domain* from your *Site*:

1. Click the *Sites* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/sites-browser.yml">Sites Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Site* you want to remove a *Custom Domain* from. CloudCannon will open the *Dashboard* for that *Site*.
3. Navigate to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*. CloudCannon will open the *Custom Domain* page.
4. Click the *Remove custom domain* button in the *Danger zone* section above the *Subdomain settings* heading. CloudCannon will open the *Remove [Subdomain] from [Site Name]?* modal.
5. If your *Site* is the last *Site* on the *Subdomain*, select the *Leave it set up*, *Redirect visitors*, or *Delete the Subdomain* option. If you select *Redirect visitors*, enter the domain to which you want to send visitors in the *Redirect visitors to* field.
6. (Optional.) If you selected *Leave it set up* or *Delete the Subdomain*, uncheck the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* or *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox to keep the *Subdomains* that redirect to this one.
7. Click the *Remove from [Subdomain]* button. If you selected *Redirect visitors* or *Delete the Subdomain*, click the *Remove and redirect* or *Remove and delete* button instead.

CloudCannon will immediately stop hosting your *Site* on the *Custom Domain* and show the form for adding a *Custom Domain* on the *Custom Domain* page. If you deleted the *Subdomain*, CloudCannon shows *[Subdomain] has been deleted* and opens the *[Site Name] is no longer on [Subdomain]* modal, with the *Stay here* and *Go to subdomains* buttons. Otherwise, CloudCannon shows *[Subdomain] has been removed from [Site Name]*.

## Remove your Site from the Subdomain page

You can remove your *Site* from the page for its *Subdomain*, using the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your *Site* in the *Sites* section.

To remove your *Site* from the *Subdomain* page:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Context Menu* for your *Site* in the *Sites* section.
6. Select the *Remove from [Subdomain]* option. CloudCannon will open the *Remove [Subdomain] from [Site Name]?* modal.
7. If your *Site* is the last *Site* on the *Subdomain*, select the *Leave it set up*, *Redirect visitors*, or *Delete the Subdomain* option. If you select *Redirect visitors*, enter the domain to which you want to send visitors in the *Redirect visitors to* field.
8. (Optional.) If you selected *Leave it set up* or *Delete the Subdomain*, uncheck the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* or *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox to keep the *Subdomains* that redirect to this one.
9. Click the *Remove from [Subdomain]* button. If you selected *Redirect visitors* or *Delete the Subdomain*, click the *Remove and redirect* or *Remove and delete* button instead.

CloudCannon will stop serving your *Site* on the *Subdomain*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Remove-Last-Site-Leave-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Remove blog.example.com from Engineering blog? modal shows the Leave it set up, Redirect visitors, and Delete the Subdomain options, a Delete this other Redirect Subdomain checkbox for news.example.com, and the Remove from blog.example.com button." title="The Remove modal on the Subdomain page" type="screenshot"/>

The same *Sites* section appears under *Subdomain settings* on the *Custom Domain* page. If you remove your *Site* from there, CloudCannon opens the *[Site Name] is no longer on [Subdomain]* modal, with the *Stay here* and *Go to [Subdomain] settings* buttons. For more information, please read our documentation on [removing a Site from a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/#remove-a-site-from-a-subdomain).

## What happens to other Redirect Subdomains

If other *Subdomains* redirect to the *Subdomain* you are emptying, CloudCannon lists them in the *Other Redirect Subdomains* notice and deletes them too when you leave the *Subdomain* set up or delete it, unless you uncheck the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* or *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox. The checkbox only covers redirects on domains whose settings you can edit. CloudCannon keeps redirects on other domains.

If you redirect the *Subdomain*, the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomains</comp.GlossaryTerm> on the same <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> that pointed at it send visitors to the new destination instead. Redirects on your other domains keep pointing at the *Subdomain*.

## Emails after you remove a Custom Domain

If removing your *Site* makes a *Subdomain* stop serving *Sites* while your *Organization* had more *Subdomains* serving *Sites* than its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> includes, CloudCannon will email everyone else in your *Organization* who has permission to view its billing. This includes members of the *Owners* and *Billing* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), members of the *Partner* group in a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and members of [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `org:billing:read` permission. CloudCannon doesn't send this email on the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/enterprise-plan.yml">Enterprise Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

### [Name] removed an extra Subdomain from [Organization]'s Subscription Plan

The subject names the person who made the change. When no person made the change, the subject is *An extra Subdomain was removed from [Organization]'s Subscription Plan* instead.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Mailer-Pricing-Domain-Removed" alt="A screenshot of the extra Subdomain removed email shows the headline Heather Scott removed an extra Subdomain from Jetstream's Subscription Plan, the counts of Subdomains serving Sites, included, and billed as Add-Ons, and the Open the Subscription page button." title="The extra Subdomain removed email" type="screenshot"/>

This email names the *Subdomain* that stopped serving a *Site*, how many *Subdomains* your *Subscription Plan* includes, and the price of each extra one. It also shows how many *Subdomains* serve *Sites* and how many are billed as <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm>. The email is for your information, and you don't need to do anything. To review the change, click the *Open the Subscription page for [Organization]* button to open the *Subscription* page under *Org Settings*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).

To stop receiving this email, click the *Unsubscribe from this list* link at the bottom of the email. The same list includes other emails, such as when CloudCannon adds more <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm> or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or when your *Subscription Plan* changes. To choose which lists you receive, click the *Manage email preferences* link instead.

## Troubleshoot removing a Custom Domain

If CloudCannon doesn't remove your *Custom Domain*, or the *Custom Domain* page doesn't show the controls you expect, here are some common causes. If you see an error that isn't listed here, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Organization* and *Site*, the *Subdomain* you tried to remove, and the full error message.

### The Remove custom domain button is disabled

The *Remove custom domain* button is disabled unless you can both change the hosting of your *Site* and edit the settings of its *Base Domain*. If it's disabled, hover over it to see which permission you're missing, and ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for the `site:settings:hosting:write` or `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission.

### The Remove modal has no options for the Subdomain

If you don't have the `base-domain:details:read` permission for the *Custom Domain*, the *Custom Domain* page shows the *Remove custom domain* button in the *Danger zone* section on its own. It opens a *Remove [Subdomain] from [Site Name]?* modal with a *Remove domain* button and no options for the *Subdomain*. Ask someone who can edit the settings of the *Base Domain* if you want to redirect or delete the *Subdomain* instead.

### Billing issue

If your *Organization's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> is inactive, or your *Organization* doesn't have enough <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the *Custom Domain* page shows a *Billing issue* page with only the *Danger zone* section. You can still remove the *Custom Domain* from the *Danger zone* section.
