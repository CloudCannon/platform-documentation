<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `site:settings:hosting:write` and `base-domain:settings:details:write` permissions, can remove a *Custom Domain* from a *Site*.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a domain you own and connect to your CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so visitors reach your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> at your own address (e.g., <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/">cloudcannon.com</a>). You can connect a *Custom Domain* to your CloudCannon *Organization* and add it to a *Site* to host your website content on that domain.

You may want to remove a *Custom Domain* from a CloudCannon *Site* to change the domain hosting your content, move a *Custom Domain* to a different CloudCannon *Site*, or to [disconnect a Custom Domain from your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/disconnect-a-custom-domain-from-your-organization/).

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Removing a *Custom Domain* from your *Site* stops visitors from seeing your *Site* on that domain. Your *Site* keeps its [Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/).
</comp.Notice>

If you want to keep sending visitors somewhere, you can change the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> into a redirect instead, with the *Change to redirect* button next to *Redirect instead* in the *Danger zone* section of the *Custom Domain* page. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/#change-a-subdomain-that-serves-sites-to-a-redirect).

If your *Site* is the last *Site* on its *Subdomain*, CloudCannon asks what happens to the *Subdomain*:

* **Leave it set up** — The *Subdomain* stays set up with no *Site*. Its hosting and certificate stay connected, so you can add a *Site* back without waiting for DNS or a new certificate. Until then, CloudCannon sends visitors to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">fallback redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or shows a not found page if the *Subdomain* is the fallback. This is the default. Choose this option to take your website down temporarily.
* **Redirect** — The *Subdomain* becomes a *301 Permanent* redirect that sends every visitor to another domain, keeping the path and query string.
* **Delete it** — CloudCannon deletes the *Subdomain* and its certificate, and sends its visitors to your fallback redirect. If the *Subdomain* was your fallback, another *Subdomain* becomes the fallback. You can choose which one under *Fallback* on the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.

If other *Subdomains* redirect to the *Subdomain* you are emptying, CloudCannon lists them and deletes them too when you leave the *Subdomain* set up or delete it, unless you uncheck the *Delete this redirect too*, *Delete these redirects too*, or *Delete [Subdomains] too* checkbox. If you leave the *Subdomain* set up and your fallback sends visitors back to it, CloudCannon refuses, because visitors would go around in a loop, and suggests deleting the redirect or choosing *Redirect* or *Delete it* instead.

If you redirect the *Subdomain*, the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomains</comp.GlossaryTerm> on the same <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> that pointed at it send visitors to the new destination instead. Redirects on your other domains keep pointing at the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon refuses a destination that would send visitors in a loop, the *Subdomain* itself, or a destination on one of your *Custom Domains* that isn't a *Subdomain* yet.

For more information on each option, please read our documentation on [removing a Site from a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/#remove-a-site-from-a-subdomain).

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/) and [added a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

The *Remove custom domain* button is disabled unless you can both change the hosting of your *Site* and edit the settings of its *Base Domain*. If it's disabled, ask someone in your *Organization* who has both permissions.

To remove a *Custom Domain* from your *Site*:

1. Log in to CloudCannon.
2. Select the *Organization* with the *Site* you want to remove a *Custom Domain* from.
3. Click the *Sites* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/sites-browser.yml">Sites Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
4. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Site* you want to remove a *Custom Domain* from. CloudCannon will open the *Dashboard* for that *Site*.
5. Navigate to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*. CloudCannon will open the *Custom Domain* page.
6. Click the *Remove custom domain* button in the *Danger zone* section. CloudCannon will open the *Remove [Subdomain] from [Site Name]?* modal.
7. If your *Site* is the last *Site* on the *Subdomain*, select the *Leave it set up*, *Redirect*, or *Delete it* option. If you select *Redirect*, enter the domain to which you want to send visitors in the *Send visitors to* field.
8. (Optional.) If you selected *Leave it set up* or *Delete it*, uncheck the *Delete this redirect too*, *Delete these redirects too*, or *Delete [Subdomains] too* checkbox to keep the *Subdomains* that redirect to this one.
9. Click the *Remove from [Subdomain]* button. If your *Site* is the last *Site* on the *Subdomain*, click the *Remove domain*, *Remove and redirect*, or *Remove and delete* button instead.

CloudCannon will immediately stop hosting your *Site* on the *Custom Domain* and show the form for adding a *Custom Domain* on the *Custom Domain* page. Unless you deleted the *Subdomain*, CloudCannon also shows *[Subdomain] has been removed from [Site Name]*.

You can also remove a *Site* from the *Subdomain* page, which you can open from the *Subdomains* tab of your *Domain* page. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Site* and select the *Remove from [Subdomain]* option.
