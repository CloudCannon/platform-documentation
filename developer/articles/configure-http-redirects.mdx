<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  This feature is only available for Sites [hosted through CloudCannon](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-web-hosting/#hosting-on-cloudcannon). If you host your Site externally, or use CloudCannon in [Headless Mode](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-headless-mode/), this feature will not work.
</comp.Notice>

Once your [SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-an-ssl-certificate/) is enabled, users can visit your *Site* on the more secure [HTTPS](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-an-ssl-certificate/#http-vs-https) URL. We highly recommend redirecting all your HTTP URLs to the more secure HTTPS URLs.

The HTTPS redirect is a setting of the subdomain your *Site* is on, so it applies to every *Site* on that subdomain. New subdomains redirect HTTP visitors to HTTPS by default. You change the setting in the *HTTPS* section of the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*, or in the same section of the subdomain page, which you can open from the *Subdomains* tab of your *Domain* page. Changing it requires the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission on the *Base Domain*. If the *Configure* button in the *HTTPS* section is disabled, ask someone in your *Organization* who has that permission. Redirect subdomains always redirect HTTP traffic to HTTPS. Your *Site's* [Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/) always uses HTTPS, so this setting only applies to *Sites* with a *Custom Domain*.

The *Routing* page under *Site Settings* doesn't have this setting. For a *Site* with a *Custom Domain*, the *Routing* page shows *The HTTP to HTTPS redirect for [Subdomain] is in Custom Domain settings, under HTTPS.*, with a link to the *Custom Domain* page.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  If you turn the HTTPS redirect on yourself, do so once the *HTTPS* section shows *HTTPS is on*. CloudCannon issues a certificate for each subdomain automatically, or you can upload your own. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding an auto-generated SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/) or [adding a custom SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-ssl-certificate/).
</comp.Notice>

To redirect visitors to HTTPS:

1. Navigate to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*.
2. Click the *Configure* button in the *HTTPS* section. CloudCannon will open the *HTTPS for [Subdomain]* modal.
3. Check the *Redirect http:// visitors to https://* checkbox.
4. Click the *Save HTTPS* button.

CloudCannon will now reroute all visitors using the HTTP URL address to the correct HTTPS URL address, for every *Site* on the subdomain.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-HTTPS-Modal" title="The HTTPS modal" alt="A screenshot of the HTTPS for blog.example.com modal shows the SSL certificate options with Let CloudCannon issue and renew it selected, the Redirect http:// visitors to https:// checkbox checked under HTTP, and the Cancel and Save HTTPS buttons." type="screenshot" />

In the same modal, you can also choose between an SSL certificate CloudCannon issues and one you upload.
