Direct Upload lets you create a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> from files on your computer, without connecting a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-provider.yml">Git Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You can use Direct Upload to try CloudCannon with an existing website. For ongoing work, CloudCannon recommends syncing your *Site* with a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm> so you can edit files locally and keep a history of every change.

You can upload your files as a folder or as a zip file. The *Upload a zip file* option also accepts `.tar`, `.gz`, and `.bz` archives. The total size of your folder or archive must be 524.29 MB or less.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  The *Upload a folder* and *Upload a zip file* options are only available when your browser window is at least 650 pixels wide.
</comp.Notice>

## Create a Site from a folder

When you upload a folder, CloudCannon lets you choose which files inside it to include before it creates your *Site*. CloudCannon selects every file except hidden files and the contents of `_site`, `dist`, `node_modules`, `.sass-cache`, and `.idea` folders, and never uploads your `.git` folder.

To create a *Site* from a folder:

1. Navigate to the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/sites-browser.yml">Sites Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm> by clicking the *Sites* button in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the *Create a Site* button. CloudCannon will open the *Create a Site* page.
3. Select the *Upload a folder* option from the *File source* dropdown.
4. Click the *Select your folder* button.
5. Choose the folder that contains your website files. CloudCannon will open the *Confirm your files* modal.
6. Deselect any files you don't want to upload, or select any excluded files you need.
7. Click the *Confirm selection* button.
8. Enter a name for your *Site* in the *Site name* field.
9. Click the *Create Site and start syncing* button.

CloudCannon will create your *Site*, upload your files, and open the *Site Dashboard*. Follow the *Getting started* steps there to configure your *Site*.

## Create a Site from a zip file

If your zip file contains a single top-level folder, CloudCannon uses the contents of that folder as the root of your *Site*.

To create a *Site* from a zip file:

1. Navigate to the *Sites Browser* by clicking the *Sites* button in the *App Sidebar*.
2. Click the *Create a Site* button. CloudCannon will open the *Create a Site* page.
3. Select the *Upload a zip file* option from the *File source* dropdown.
4. Click the *Select your zip file* button.
5. Choose the zip file that contains your website files. CloudCannon will fill in the *Site name* field from the name of your zip file.
6. Change the name in the *Site name* field if you want a different name.
7. Click the *Create Site and start syncing* button.

CloudCannon will create your *Site*, upload your files, and open the *Site Dashboard*. Follow the *Getting started* steps there to configure your *Site*.

## Add more files later

Once your *Site* exists, you can upload more files or folders from the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/file-browser.yml">File Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or drag and drop them into it. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a file](/documentation/user-articles/add-a-file/#upload-from-the-file-browser).

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  If you later connect your *Site* to a *Git Provider*, CloudCannon backs up your current files and replaces them with the files from your *Git Repository*.
</comp.Notice>
