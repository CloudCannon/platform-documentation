<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `site:settings:form:write` permission, can link an *Inbox* to a *Site*.
</comp.Notice>

Before a form on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> can receive submissions, you have to link an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inbox</comp.GlossaryTerm> to that *Site* and point the form at it. Linking makes the *Inbox* available to the *Site*; the form's `inbox_key` input decides which linked *Inbox* a submission goes to. If you do not have an *Inbox* yet, please read our documentation on [creating an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/create-an-inbox/).

Two options are worth setting while you link. *Display Inbox* controls whether the *Inbox* appears in this *Site's* *Site Navigation*, and *Require CAPTCHA* rejects submissions from this *Site* that arrive without a valid CAPTCHA response. Requiring a CAPTCHA also needs provider keys on the *Inbox* and a widget on your form. For more information, please read our documentation on [reducing spam with a CAPTCHA](/documentation/developer-articles/reduce-spam-with-a-captcha/).

To link an *Inbox* to your *Site*:

1. Navigate to the *Forms* page under *Site Settings*.
2. Select the *Inbox* you want to link from the *Link Inbox* dropdown.
3. Set the *Display Inbox* and *Require CAPTCHA* options as you want them.
4. Click the *Link Inbox* button.

CloudCannon will link the *Inbox* to your *Site*, ready for a form to submit to it.

## Next steps

A linked *Inbox* receives nothing until a form on your *Site* names its key. For more information, please read our documentation on [creating a Form](/documentation/developer-articles/create-a-form/).

You can link more than one *Inbox* to a *Site*, up to the number your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> allows. For more information, please read our documentation on [how Inbox limits work](/documentation/developer-articles/how-do-inbox-limits-work/).

To disconnect an *Inbox* from this *Site* later without deleting its submissions, please read our documentation on [unlinking an Inbox from your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/unlink-an-inbox-from-your-site/).
