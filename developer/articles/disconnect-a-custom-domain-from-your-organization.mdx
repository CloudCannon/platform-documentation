<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:delete:write` permission, can disconnect Custom Domains from an Organization.
</comp.Notice>

A Custom Domain is a unique address used to identify a website (e.g., <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/">cloudcannon.com</a>). If you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/) and [added the Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/), CloudCannon will host your website content at that web address.

Disconnecting a Custom Domain from your Organization will allow you to add the domain to a different CloudCannon Organization.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  Disconnecting a Custom Domain from your CloudCannon Organization does not delete your domain, or affect any of the settings managed by your Domain Registrar.
</comp.Notice>

To protect you from accidentally taking your website down by disconnecting your Custom Domain, CloudCannon prevents you from disconnecting the domain from your Organization while:

* Any *Site* still uses the domain (e.g., *1 site still uses it.*).
* Any redirect subdomain on the domain sends visitors to another domain (e.g., *promo.example.com redirects elsewhere.*).
* Any redirect subdomain on another domain in your *Organization* sends visitors to this domain (e.g., *shop.example.net redirects here from other domains.*).

The *Danger* section lists each reason, and the *Disconnect [Base Domain]* button stays disabled until you resolve them all. Remove the domain from your *Sites*, and delete or change the redirects.

When you disconnect the domain, CloudCannon also removes the subdomains that have no *Site*, and the redirect subdomains that send visitors to another subdomain on the same domain. The *Danger* section lists these subdomains before you disconnect, or shows that nothing else is removed with the domain.

Once you disconnect the domain, another CloudCannon account can connect it. If the domain uses CloudCannon DNS, its nameservers change the next time it is connected. You cannot undo disconnecting a Custom Domain.

The *Danger* section only appears on the *Domain Settings* tab if you have the `base-domain:delete:write` permission. If you can't see it, ask someone in your *Organization* who has that permission.

These instructions assume you have already [removed the Custom Domain from all CloudCannon Sites](/documentation/developer-articles/remove-a-custom-domain-from-your-site/) and [deleted any redirect subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-redirect-subdomains/#delete-a-redirect-subdomain) that send visitors to another domain, or to this domain from another domain.

To disconnect a Custom Domain from your Organization:

1. Log in to CloudCannon and select the Organization from which you want to disconnect the Custom Domain.
2. Click the *Domains* button in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
3. Identify the domain you want to disconnect and click on the *Domain* card. CloudCannon will open the *Domain* page for this Custom Domain.
4. Navigate to the *Danger* section under the *Domain Settings* tab.
5. Click the *Disconnect [Base Domain]* button. CloudCannon will open the *Disconnect [Base Domain]?* modal.
6. Click the *Disconnect [Base Domain]* button in the modal to confirm.

CloudCannon will disconnect the Custom Domain from your Organization.
