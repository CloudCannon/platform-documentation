<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  *Forms* only work for *Sites* hosted through CloudCannon. If you host your *Site* elsewhere, or use CloudCannon in [Headless Mode](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-headless-mode/), your forms will not reach an *Inbox*.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/form.yml">Form</comp.GlossaryTerm> is an ordinary HTML form on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> that posts its submissions to CloudCannon rather than to a backend server of your own. CloudCannon accepts the submission, stores it, and redirects the visitor to the page you nominate.

There is nothing CloudCannon-specific about the markup beyond one input. A form becomes a CloudCannon *Form* when it posts to your *Site* and names an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inbox</comp.GlossaryTerm> in an `inbox_key` input, which is how CloudCannon knows where to file the submission. For the markup and the fields CloudCannon recognizes, please read our documentation on [connecting your Site to an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-your-site-to-an-inbox/).

## What you can do with a submission

Every submission is stored in the *Inbox* it names, where you can read, filter, and export it. For more information, please read our documentation on [viewing your form submissions](/documentation/user-articles/view-your-form-submissions/) and [exporting your form submissions](/documentation/user-articles/export-your-form-submissions/).

An *Inbox* can also forward a copy of each submission on, to an email address or to an external service such as Slack, Zapier, IFTTT, or Make. For more information, please read our documentation on [what an Inbox Target is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-an-inbox-target/).

## Handling spam

A form open to the internet attracts spam. CloudCannon offers two defenses, and you can use both:

* The `_gotcha` honeypot field, which CloudCannon rejects any submission for if it has a value. It needs no configuration beyond adding the hidden field.
* A CAPTCHA provider on the *Inbox*, which checks each submission before CloudCannon accepts it. For more information, please read our documentation on [reducing spam with a CAPTCHA](/documentation/developer-articles/reduce-spam-with-a-captcha/).

Submissions CloudCannon identifies as spam go to the *Spam* category of the *Inbox* rather than being discarded. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing spam form submissions](/documentation/user-articles/manage-spam-form-submissions/).

## Where forms live in CloudCannon

An *Inbox* belongs to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> and is linked to the *Sites* whose forms submit to it, so several *Sites* can share one *Inbox*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what an Inbox is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-an-inbox/).

If your *Site* still uses the form configuration from before *Inboxes*, please read our documentation on [migrating to the new CloudCannon forms](/documentation/developer-articles/legacy-forms-migration-guide/).
