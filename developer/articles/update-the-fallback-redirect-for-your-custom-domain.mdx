<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, can update the *Fallback Redirect* for a *Custom Domain*.
</comp.Notice>

The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">Fallback Redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm> is the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> to which CloudCannon sends visitors when they visit a subdomain on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> that is not set up, including a *Subdomain* you have deleted. The *Fallback Redirect* can be any *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain*, including a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm>. If the *Fallback Redirect* is a *Site Subdomain* with no <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> yet, its visitors see a 404 page. Other *Site Subdomains* with no *Site* yet show their own visitors a 404 page, rather than sending them to the *Fallback Redirect*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/).

Until you add your first *Subdomain*, your *Custom Domain* has no *Fallback Redirect*, so visitors to a subdomain that isn't set up see a 404 page. The first *Subdomain* you add becomes your *Fallback Redirect* automatically, whichever <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain-type.yml">Type</comp.GlossaryTerm> you choose.

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected the Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/) and added the *Subdomain* you want to use as your *Fallback Redirect*. If you are a member of multiple <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm>, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

## Update your Fallback Redirect

The *Fallback Redirect* uses a *307 temporary redirect, keeping the method* status code by default, so search engines don't treat a subdomain that isn't set up as moved for good. You can choose *301 permanent redirect*, *302 temporary redirect*, *307 temporary redirect, keeping the method*, or *308 permanent redirect, keeping the method* in the *Change Fallback* modal. Search engines treat a permanent redirect as a permanent move, so if you might later set up one of those subdomains, keep a temporary status code.

To update your *Fallback Redirect*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the domain you want to update. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm> for this *Custom Domain*.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains* on your *Custom Domain*.
4. Click the *Change Fallback* button in the *Fallback Redirect* section. CloudCannon will open the *Change Fallback* modal.
5. Select the *Subdomain* you want to use as your *Fallback Redirect* from the *Subdomain* dropdown.
6. (Optional.) Select a status code from the *Status code* dropdown.
7. Click the *Save Fallback Redirect* button.

CloudCannon will update your *Fallback Redirect* and show *[Subdomain] is now the Fallback Redirect*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Subdomains-Tab-Fallback-Redirect-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Change Fallback modal shows the Subdomain dropdown set to docs.example.com, the Status code dropdown set to 307 temporary redirect, keeping the method, and the Save Fallback Redirect button." title="The Change Fallback modal" type="screenshot"/>

You can also click the *Change Fallback* button on the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page to open the *Change Fallback* modal, or select the *Use as Fallback Redirect* option in a *Subdomain's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm> on the *Subdomains* tab, which opens the *Change the Fallback Redirect?* modal. Click the *Use as Fallback Redirect* button to confirm.

## How visitors reach your Fallback Redirect

The *Fallback Redirect* section on the *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page for your *Custom Domain* shows the redirect as a tree from `*.example.com`, with a description of its status code, to your current *Fallback Redirect*. CloudCannon labels the *Fallback Redirect* with *Current Fallback* in the list of *Subdomains* and on its *Subdomain* page. The *Summary* tab also names your current *Fallback Redirect*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Domain-Subdomains-Tab" alt="A screenshot of the Subdomains tab on the Domain page shows the list of Subdomains with example.com labeled Current Fallback, and the Fallback Redirect section with the Change Fallback button and the redirect from *.example.com to example.com." title="The Subdomains tab on the Domain page" type="screenshot"/>

The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/w/wildcard-certificate.yml">wildcard SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> for your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> covers subdomains that are not set up, so visitors reach your *Fallback Redirect* over HTTPS. On <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the wildcard *SSL Certificate* needs records at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-provider.yml">DNS Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm> to be issued and renewed, and without them HTTPS stops working for subdomains you haven't set up. For more information, please read our documentation on [how CloudCannon validates your certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#how-cloudcannon-validates-your-certificate) and [renewing the wildcard SSL Certificate on external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#renew-the-wildcard-ssl-certificate-on-external-dns).

If your *Custom Domain* uses an external *DNS Provider*, visitors only reach your *Fallback Redirect* from a subdomain that isn't set up if you add a wildcard record (e.g., `*.example.com`) at your *DNS Provider*. The *DNS* tab shows this record in the *Wildcard record (optional)* table, where you click the *I've updated it* button once you have added it, and CloudCannon shows *Configured* once it finds the record. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#review-the-records-on-the-dns-tab).

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  If you have configured your [DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-dns/) to point a specific subdomain at a service outside of CloudCannon, CloudCannon can't send visitors to that subdomain to your *Fallback Redirect*.
</comp.Notice>

If visitors use a particular subdomain, such as `www.example.com`, add it as a *Redirect Subdomain* rather than relying on the *Fallback Redirect*. Each *Redirect Subdomain* has its own certificate and status code, and works on external DNS without the wildcard *SSL Certificate*. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/).

If you delete the *Subdomain* that is your *Fallback Redirect*, CloudCannon will make another *Subdomain* your *Fallback Redirect*, which you can change on the *Subdomains* tab. If no *Subdomain* is left, your *Custom Domain* has no *Fallback Redirect*, and visitors to a subdomain that isn't set up see a 404 page.

## Troubleshoot updating the Fallback Redirect

If CloudCannon doesn't update your *Fallback Redirect*, or the *Domain* page doesn't show the controls you expect, here are some common causes. If you see an error that isn't listed here, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Organization*, the *Custom Domain*, the *Subdomain* you selected, and the full error message.

### You do not have permission to edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Change Fallback* button or *Use as Fallback Redirect* option. You don't have the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for the *Base Domain*. Ask someone who manages <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> in your *Organization* for this permission.

### This is the Fallback Redirect. To change it, use another Subdomain as the Fallback Redirect.

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Current Fallback Redirect* option in the *Context Menu* of the *Subdomain* that is already your *Fallback Redirect*. Select the *Use as Fallback Redirect* option on another *Subdomain* instead, or click the *Change Fallback* button.

### You cannot select [Subdomain] as your Fallback Redirect, because website visitors would go around in a loop ([Subdomain] → [Subdomain] → [Subdomain]). Select a different Subdomain.

The *Subdomain* you selected redirects visitors, directly or through other redirects, to a subdomain that isn't set up, which would send them back to the *Fallback Redirect*. The message lists the *Subdomains* in the loop. Select a different *Subdomain*, or change one of the redirects in the loop first. For how loops form and how to fix one, please read our documentation on [fixing a redirect loop](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/#fix-a-redirect-loop).
