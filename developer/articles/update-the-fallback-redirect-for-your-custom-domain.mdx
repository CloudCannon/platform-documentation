<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, can update the fallback redirect for a *Custom Domain*.
</comp.Notice>

The <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">fallback redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm> is the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> to which CloudCannon sends visitors when they visit a subdomain on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> that is not set up, or a *Subdomain* with no <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> yet. If the fallback itself has no *Site*, its visitors see a not found page. For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/).

The fallback redirect prevents your visitors from encountering errors if they enter an incorrect subdomain in their browser (e.g., `typo.example.com`). The fallback can be any *Subdomain* on your *Custom Domain*, including a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm>. The wildcard certificate for your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> covers subdomains that are not set up, so visitors reach your fallback redirect over HTTPS. On <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the wildcard certificate needs records at your DNS provider to be issued and renewed, and without them HTTPS stops working for subdomains you haven't set up. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding an auto-generated SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#how-cloudcannon-validates-your-certificate) and [renewing the wildcard certificate on external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/#renew-the-wildcard-certificate-on-external-dns).

The *Fallback* section on the *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page for your *Custom Domain* names your current fallback and shows the redirect as a tree from `*.example.com`, with a description of its status code, to your fallback. CloudCannon labels the fallback *Subdomain* with *Fallback* in the list of *Subdomains*. The *Summary* tab also names your current fallback.

The fallback redirect uses a *307 Temporary, keeps the method* status code by default, so search engines don't treat a subdomain that isn't set up as moved for good. You can choose *301 Permanent*, *302 Temporary*, *307 Temporary, keeps the method*, or *308 Permanent, keeps the method* in the *Change fallback* modal. Search engines treat a permanent redirect as a permanent move, so if you might later set up one of those subdomains, keep a temporary status code.

If visitors use a particular subdomain, such as `www.example.com`, add it as a *Redirect Subdomain* rather than relying on the fallback. Each *Redirect Subdomain* has its own certificate and status code, and works on external DNS without the wildcard certificate. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/).

If your *Custom Domain* uses an external DNS provider, visitors only reach your fallback from a subdomain that isn't set up if you add a wildcard record (e.g., `*.example.com`) at your DNS provider. The *DNS* tab shows this record in the *Wildcard record (optional)* table, where you click the *I've updated it* button once you have added it, and CloudCannon shows *Connected* once it finds the record. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#review-the-records-on-the-dns-tab).

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  If you have configured your [DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-dns/) to point a specific subdomain at a service outside of CloudCannon, CloudCannon can't send visitors to that subdomain to your fallback redirect.
</comp.Notice>

If you delete the *Subdomain* that is your fallback, another *Subdomain* becomes the fallback. You can choose which one under *Fallback* on the *Summary* tab.

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected the Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/) and added the *Subdomain* you want to use as your fallback redirect.

To update your fallback redirect:

1. Log in to CloudCannon.
2. Select the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> with the *Custom Domain* you want to update.
3. Click the *Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
4. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the domain you want to update. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page for this *Custom Domain*.
5. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains* on your *Custom Domain*.
6. Click the *Change fallback* button in the *Fallback* section. CloudCannon will open the *Change fallback* modal.
7. Select the *Subdomain* you want to use as your fallback redirect from the *Fallback subdomain* dropdown.
8. (Optional.) Select a status code from the *Status code* dropdown.
9. Click the *Save fallback* button.

CloudCannon will update your fallback redirect.

You can also click the *Change fallback* button on the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page to open the *Change fallback* modal, or select the *Use as fallback* option in a *Subdomain's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm> on the *Subdomains* tab, which opens the *Change the fallback?* modal.
