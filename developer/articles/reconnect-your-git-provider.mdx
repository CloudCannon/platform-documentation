<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `org:settings:git:provider:write` permission, can reconnect a *Git Provider*.
</comp.Notice>

CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/syncing.yml">syncs</comp.GlossaryTerm> your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> using the account connected to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-provider.yml">Git Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm>. If your *Git Provider* revokes CloudCannon's access, or CloudCannon cannot fetch information about your connected account, CloudCannon may not be able to sync the *Sites* that use that *Git Provider*. The *Authentication* tab on the *Git Provider's* page under <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/org-settings.yml">Org Settings</comp.GlossaryTerm> shows an error message and a *Reconnect* button.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-GitHub-Account-Fetch-Error" alt="A screenshot of the Authentication tab on the GitHub page under Org Settings shows an error message and a Reconnect button." title="A GitHub account that needs reconnecting" type="screenshot"/>

Access to a *Git Provider* applies to your whole <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so retrying an individual *Site* will not resolve the problem. You can reconnect your *Git Provider* without unauthenticating it first.

To reconnect your *Git Provider*:

1. Click the *Org Settings* button in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the *Details* page.
2. Navigate to the page for your *Git Provider* under the *Files* heading.
3. Click the *Reconnect* button on the *Authentication* tab.
4. Sign in to your *Git Provider* as the account already connected to your *Organization*.
5. Follow your *Git Provider's* instructions to authenticate access.

CloudCannon will return you to the *Authentication* tab and show your connected account. Any *Sites* that failed to sync while CloudCannon could not access your *Git Provider* will sync again when you retry them. For more information, please read our documentation on [resolving Syncing errors](/documentation/developer-articles/resolve-syncing-errors/).

## Change the connected account or token

For GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket, CloudCannon will show an error message instead of reconnecting if you sign in as a different account. To connect a different account, unauthenticate your *Git Provider* first, then authenticate it with the new account. For more information, please read our documentation on [unauthenticating your Git Provider](/documentation/developer-articles/authenticate-your-git-provider/#unauthenticate-your-git-provider).

For GitHub Enterprise Server and self-hosted GitLab, CloudCannon reconnects with whichever account you sign in as, and that account replaces the one already connected.

If you authenticated GitLab or self-hosted GitLab with a group access token, you cannot renew your token with the *Reconnect* button. For GitLab, CloudCannon treats the GitLab user account you sign in with as a different account and will not reconnect it. For self-hosted GitLab, reconnecting replaces your token with the user account you sign in with. To replace an expired or revoked token, unauthenticate your *Git Provider*, then authenticate again with a new token.
