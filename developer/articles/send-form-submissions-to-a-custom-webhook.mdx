You can forward the submissions an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inbox</comp.GlossaryTerm> receives to an endpoint you host yourself, using a *Custom webhook* target. *Custom webhook* is the only type with no fixed host, so the *Webhook URL* field accepts any http or https URL on port 80 or 443.

It is also the only type that needs domain verification. CloudCannon sends nothing to the target until you prove you own the domain in its URL by adding a TXT record to your DNS.

Add the target in CloudCannon first, because the record you need is in the validation instructions CloudCannon shows for it. Select *Custom webhook* in the *Choose your target type* dropdown and enter your URL in the *Webhook URL* field. For the full steps, please read our documentation on [adding a target to an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-target-to-an-inbox/).

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Org-Settings-Forms-Add-Target-Custom-Webhook-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Add target modal shows Custom webhook chosen as the target type and a URL in the Webhook URL field." title="The Add target modal" type="screenshot" />

CloudCannon asks for a TXT record named `_cloudcannon-challenge.` followed by the full hostname in your webhook URL, with your *Organization's* UUID as its content. A webhook URL of `https://hooks.example.com/forms` needs the record at `_cloudcannon-challenge.hooks.example.com`, not at `_cloudcannon-challenge.example.com`. One record verifies every *Custom webhook* target on that hostname in your *Organization*, so you only add it once per hostname.

CloudCannon opens the *Validation instructions* modal as soon as you add the target, so the record you need is already on screen. The *View validation instructions* option in that target's *Context Menu* opens the same modal again later.

To verify the hostname in your webhook URL:

1. Add the TXT record from the *Validation instructions* modal to your DNS.
2. Wait for your DNS change to propagate, which can take up to 48 hours.
3. Select the *Revalidate now* option in your target's *Context Menu*.

CloudCannon will check your DNS for the record, and start forwarding new submissions to your webhook once it matches. CloudCannon re-checks the record periodically after that, and stops sending to the target if the record is no longer there.

CloudCannon posts each submission as raw JSON by default, and *Custom webhook* is the only type where you can choose a different payload format, such as a Slack message or form encoded values. For that setting, and the *Spam protection* and *Links to attached files* options a target can carry, please read our documentation on [adding a target to an Inbox](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-target-to-an-inbox/).
