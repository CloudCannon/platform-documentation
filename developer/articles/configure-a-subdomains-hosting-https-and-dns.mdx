<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, can change a *Subdomain's* hosting and HTTPS settings and check its DNS.
</comp.Notice>

Every <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> has its own hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings, whether it serves <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> or redirects visitors to another domain. The settings belong to the *Subdomain*, not to each *Site*, so any change applies to every *Site* on the *Subdomain*. For more information on the hosting options and when to choose each one, please read our documentation on [what the hosting options for a Subdomain are](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-the-hosting-options-for-a-subdomain/).

This article covers how to change the hosting option of a *Subdomain*, change how it gets its certificate, and check its DNS. For what the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain-page.yml">Subdomain page</comp.GlossaryTerm> shows and how to open it, please read our documentation on [what the Subdomain page is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-the-subdomain-page/).

This article doesn't cover which *Sites* a *Subdomain* serves, or where a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> sends visitors. For those, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/) and [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/).

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [added a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/). If you are a member of multiple <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm>, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

## Change the hosting option for a Subdomain

Every new *Subdomain* starts on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-hosting.yml">CloudCannon Hosting</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You can switch it to <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudflare-proxy.yml">Cloudflare Proxy</comp.GlossaryTerm> or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/single-origin.yml">Single Origin</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You can't switch to *Cloudflare Proxy* while your domain uses <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

The hosting option decides which <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-records.yml">DNS Record</comp.GlossaryTerm> the *Subdomain* needs. On <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, changing the hosting option can mean updating the record at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-provider.yml">DNS Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm>. On *CloudCannon DNS*, once your domain's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/n/nameserver.yml">nameservers</comp.GlossaryTerm> point at CloudCannon DNS, CloudCannon updates the record for you when you save.

To change the hosting option for a *Subdomain*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Subdomain* page.
5. Click the *Configure hosting* button in the *Hosting* section. CloudCannon will open the *Hosting for [Subdomain]* modal.
6. Select the hosting option you want. CloudCannon will show the DNS Record the option needs, and say whether the record changes.
7. Click the *Save hosting* button.

CloudCannon will change how visitors reach the *Subdomain*. On external DNS, if the record has to change, update it at your *DNS Provider*. For the records each hosting option needs, please read our documentation on [what the hosting options for a Subdomain are](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-the-hosting-options-for-a-subdomain/). For setting up *Cloudflare Proxy*, please read our documentation on [configuring Cloudflare Proxy](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#configure-cloudflare-proxy).

## Change how a Subdomain gets its certificate

Each *Subdomain* has its own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon can issue and renew it for you, or you can use one you upload. You also choose whether http:// visitors go to https://, and the minimum TLS version the *Subdomain* accepts.

To change how a *Subdomain* gets its certificate:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Subdomain* page.
5. Click the *Configure HTTPS* button in the *HTTPS* section. CloudCannon will open the *HTTPS for [Subdomain]* modal.
6. Under *SSL certificate*, select how the *Subdomain* gets its certificate:
   * *Let CloudCannon issue and renew it* — CloudCannon issues the certificate and renews it for you. This option is labeled *Recommended*.
   * *Use an SSL certificate you upload* — CloudCannon will show the *Uploaded SSL certificate* dropdown. Select a certificate that covers the *Subdomain*, or select *New custom SSL certificate* to open the *Add SSL Certificate* modal and upload one.
7. (Optional.) Under *HTTP*, check or uncheck the *Redirect http:// visitors to https://* checkbox. This checkbox is disabled for a *Redirect Subdomain*.
8. (Optional.) Select a version in the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown.
9. Click the *Save HTTPS* button.

CloudCannon will save the HTTPS settings for the *Subdomain* and show *HTTPS for [Subdomain] has been saved*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-HTTPS-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the HTTPS for blog.example.com modal shows the SSL certificate options with Let CloudCannon issue and renew it selected, the Redirect http:// visitors to https:// checkbox checked under HTTP, and the Cancel and Save HTTPS buttons." title="The HTTPS modal" type="screenshot"/>

The *HTTPS* section also explains the state of the certificate, and what to do if it needs attention.

For more information, please read our documentation on [adding an auto-generated SSL Certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/), [adding a Custom SSL Certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-ssl-certificate/), [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/), and [resolving SSL Certificate errors](/documentation/developer-articles/resolve-ssl-certificate-errors/).

## Check the DNS for a Subdomain

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Fixing a DNS Record from the *DNS* section on *CloudCannon DNS* also requires the `base-domain:settings:dns:write` permission for the *Base Domain*.
</comp.Notice>

The *DNS* section of the *Subdomain* page says whether the *Subdomain* points at CloudCannon, and shows a table with *Subdomain*, *Record*, and *Status* columns. The *Record* column shows the record the *Subdomain* needs, then any records CloudCannon found at that name. The *Status* column shows what CloudCannon found, such as *Configured*, *Not pointing at CloudCannon*, or *No DNS Record*.

The footer of the table says when CloudCannon last checked, with the *View DNS check history* icon and the *Check now* button. For what each status means, what CloudCannon checks, and how often, please read our documentation on [how CloudCannon checks your DNS Records](/documentation/developer-articles/how-does-cloudcannon-check-your-dns-records/).

If the *Subdomain* isn't connected, the sentence above the record says what to do:

* **CloudCannon DNS** — Once your nameservers point at CloudCannon DNS, click the *Fix* button, and CloudCannon replaces or removes the records that stop the *Subdomain* from reaching CloudCannon. For more information, please read our documentation on [fixing the DNS Record for a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-dns-records-on-cloudcannon-dns/#fix-the-dns-record-for-a-subdomain).
* **External DNS** — Add the record at your *DNS Provider*, using the *Copy* icon next to its value, then click the *I've updated it* button. The *Copy* icon shows *Copied!* once it has copied the value. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding the records at your DNS Provider](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#add-the-records).

On external DNS, CloudCannon checks hourly for up to seven days after you click the *I've updated it* button. Once it finds the record and issues the *SSL Certificate*, CloudCannon emails everyone who can edit the settings of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> that the *Subdomain* is live. If CloudCannon still can't find the record 48 hours after you marked it as updated, the *DNS* section lists what usually fixes it. For these emails, please read our documentation on [the emails about your Subdomain's DNS Records](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#emails-about-your-subdomain-s-dns-records).

When the *Subdomain* has TXT Records to add for its certificate, the *DNS* section points you to them under *HTTPS*. On external DNS, while the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/w/wildcard-certificate.yml">wildcard SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> of your *Base Domain* is waiting to be renewed, it points you to *Wildcard certificate records* on the *DNS* tab instead.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-DNS-External-Records" alt="A screenshot of the DNS section for www.example.com shows the CNAME Record it needs, the A Record found marked Not pointing at CloudCannon, and a footer that says how often CloudCannon checks, with the Check now button." title="The DNS section on a Subdomain page" type="screenshot"/>

To check the DNS for a *Subdomain*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Subdomain* page.
5. Click the *Check now* button in the footer of the table in the *DNS* section.

CloudCannon will check the *Subdomain's* DNS and update the *DNS* section. To see earlier checks, click the *View DNS check history* icon next to the *Check now* button. CloudCannon will open the *DNS checks for [Subdomain]* modal. For what the modal shows, please read our documentation on [viewing the DNS check history](/documentation/developer-articles/how-does-cloudcannon-check-your-dns-records/#view-the-dns-check-history). You can check the DNS for each *Subdomain* at most once a minute.

### Check a connection in the hosting diagram

The *Hosting* section of the *Subdomain* page shows how visitors reach the *Subdomain* as a diagram, which depends on its hosting option. For what each diagram shows, please read our documentation on [what the hosting options for a Subdomain are](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-the-hosting-options-for-a-subdomain/).

Each connection in the diagram is labeled with its state, such as *Reaching CloudCannon*, *Not reaching CloudCannon*, *Waiting for DNS to update*, *Not checked yet*, or *Connected securely*. A green line means the connection works, and a red line with an exclamation mark shows where visitors stop reaching CloudCannon.

Click a connection to see what CloudCannon found, including the DNS Records, the result of its test request, and when it last checked, along with what to change if the connection isn't working. Depending on the state of the connection, the details show some of these buttons:

* *View the DNS record* (*View the DNS records* when there is more than one) — Scrolls to the *DNS* section.
* *Check DNS now* — Runs a new check.
* *View check history* — Opens the *DNS checks for [Subdomain]* modal.
* *Configure hosting* — Opens the *Hosting for [Subdomain]* modal. Only shown for a broken *Cloudflare Proxy* connection.

To close the details, press Escape or click the *Close details* icon.

## Troubleshoot configuring a Subdomain

If CloudCannon doesn't make the change you expect, or the *Subdomain* page doesn't show the controls you expect, here are some common causes. If you see an error that isn't listed here, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Organization*, the *Subdomain*, and the full error message.

### You do not have permission to edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Configure hosting*, *Configure HTTPS*, *Check DNS now*, *Check now*, or *I've updated it* button. You don't have the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for the *Base Domain*. Ask someone who manages <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> in your *Organization* for this permission.

### You do not have permission to edit the DNS for [Base Domain].

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Fix* button on *CloudCannon DNS*. You don't have the `base-domain:settings:dns:write` permission for the *Base Domain*. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for this permission.

### Checked less than a minute ago. You can check again in a minute.

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Check now* or *Check DNS now* button. You can check the DNS for each *Subdomain* at most once a minute. Wait a minute, then check again.
