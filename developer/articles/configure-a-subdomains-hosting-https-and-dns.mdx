<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, can change a *Subdomain's* hosting and HTTPS settings and check its DNS.
</comp.Notice>

Every <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> has its own hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings, whether it serves <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Sites</comp.GlossaryTerm> or redirects visitors to another domain. The settings belong to the *Subdomain*, not to each *Site*, so any change applies to every *Site* on the *Subdomain*. For more information on the hosting options and when to choose each one, please read our documentation on [what the hosting options for a Subdomain are](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-the-hosting-options-for-a-subdomain/).

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [added a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/).

## Open the page for a Subdomain

Each *Subdomain* has its own page, with a *Hosting*, *HTTPS*, and *DNS* section.

If you are a member of multiple <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organizations</comp.GlossaryTerm>, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To open the page for a *Subdomain*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the domain you want to configure. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains* on your *Custom Domain*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain* in the list.

CloudCannon will open the page for the *Subdomain*.

You can also reach the same settings from the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm> of each *Subdomain* in the list, which has the *Configure hosting*, *Configure HTTPS*, *Check DNS now*, and *View DNS history* options. In the same menu, the *Open Subdomain page* option opens these settings, and the *Visit live Subdomain* option opens the *Subdomain* itself in a new browser tab. For a *Subdomain* that serves a *Site*, the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* for that *Site* also shows the page for the *Subdomain*, under the *Subdomain settings* heading, if you can edit the settings of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Otherwise, it shows *You do not have permission to edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].* instead.

## Change the hosting option for a Subdomain

Every new *Subdomain* starts on <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-hosting.yml">CloudCannon Hosting</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You can switch it to <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudflare-proxy.yml">Cloudflare Proxy</comp.GlossaryTerm> or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/single-origin.yml">Single Origin</comp.GlossaryTerm>. *Cloudflare Proxy* isn't available while your domain uses <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so the *Hosting for [Subdomain]* modal doesn't offer it there, unless the *Subdomain* already uses it.

The hosting option decides which <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-records.yml">DNS Record</comp.GlossaryTerm> the *Subdomain* needs. On <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, changing the hosting option can mean updating the record at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-provider.yml">DNS Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm>. On *CloudCannon DNS*, once your domain's <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/n/nameserver.yml">nameservers</comp.GlossaryTerm> point at CloudCannon DNS, CloudCannon updates the record for you when you save.

If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To change the hosting option for a *Subdomain*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Configure hosting* button in the *Hosting* section. CloudCannon will open the *Hosting for [Subdomain]* modal.
6. Select the hosting option you want. CloudCannon will show the DNS Record the option needs, and say whether the record changes.
7. Click the *Save hosting* button.

CloudCannon will change how visitors reach the *Subdomain*. On external DNS, if the record has to change, update it at your *DNS Provider*. For the records each hosting option needs, please read our documentation on [what the hosting options for a Subdomain are](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-the-hosting-options-for-a-subdomain/). For setting up *Cloudflare Proxy*, please read our documentation on [configuring Cloudflare Proxy](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#configure-cloudflare-proxy).

### The hosting diagram

The *Hosting* section shows how visitors reach the *Subdomain* as a diagram. The diagram depends on the hosting option:

* **CloudCannon Hosting** — *Visitors*, the *CloudCannon CDN*, and the *CloudCannon Origin*.
* **Cloudflare Proxy** — *Visitors*, *Your Cloudflare*, and the *CloudCannon Origin*.
* **Single Origin** — *Visitors* and the *CloudCannon Origin*. Your *Base Domain* on external DNS uses this diagram too, because its A Records point straight at the *CloudCannon Origin*.

Each connection in the diagram is labeled with its state, such as *Reaching CloudCannon*, *Not reaching CloudCannon*, *Waiting for DNS to update*, *Not checked yet*, or *Connected securely*. A green line means the connection works, and a red line with an exclamation mark shows where visitors stop reaching CloudCannon. Click a connection to see what CloudCannon found, including the DNS Records, the result of its test request, and when it last checked, along with what to change if the connection isn't working. The *View the DNS record* button (*View the DNS records* when there is more than one) scrolls to the *DNS* section, the *Configure hosting* button opens the *Hosting for [Subdomain]* modal, the *Check DNS now* button runs a new check, and the *View check history* button opens the history of checks. For a minute after a check, the *Check DNS now* button is disabled, with the tooltip *Checked less than a minute ago. You can check again in a minute.* Press Escape or click the *Close details* icon to close the details.

## Change how a Subdomain gets its certificate

Each *Subdomain* has its own <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm>. The *Configure HTTPS* button in the *HTTPS* section opens the *HTTPS for [Subdomain]* modal. Under *SSL certificate*, you choose *Let CloudCannon issue and renew it*, which is recommended, or *Use an SSL certificate you upload*. With the second option, you select the certificate in the *Uploaded SSL certificate* dropdown below the options. Under *HTTP*, you choose whether to redirect http:// visitors to https://, and the minimum TLS version the *Subdomain* accepts in the *Minimum TLS version* dropdown.

The *HTTPS* section also explains the state of the certificate, and what to do if it needs attention.

For instructions, please read our documentation on [adding an auto-generated SSL Certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/), [adding a Custom SSL Certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-ssl-certificate/), [increasing the minimum TLS version for your domains](/documentation/developer-articles/increase-the-minimum-tls-version-for-your-domains/), and [resolving SSL Certificate errors](/documentation/developer-articles/resolve-ssl-certificate-errors/).

## Check the DNS for a Subdomain

The *DNS* section of the *Subdomain* page says whether the *Subdomain* points at CloudCannon, and shows a table with *Subdomain*, *Record*, and *Status* columns. The *Record* column shows the record the *Subdomain* needs, then any records CloudCannon found at that name. The *Status* column shows what CloudCannon found, such as *Configured*, *Not pointing at CloudCannon*, or *No DNS Record*. The footer of the table says when CloudCannon last checked, with the *View DNS check history* icon and the *Check now* button. For what each status means, what CloudCannon checks, and how often, please read our documentation on [how CloudCannon checks your DNS Records](/documentation/developer-articles/how-does-cloudcannon-check-your-dns-records/).

If the *Subdomain* isn't connected, the sentence above the record says what to do:

* **CloudCannon DNS** — Once your nameservers point at CloudCannon DNS, click the *Fix* button, and CloudCannon replaces or removes the records that stop the *Subdomain* from reaching CloudCannon. Fixing the record also requires the `base-domain:settings:dns:write` permission. Without it, the *Fix* button is disabled, and hovering over it shows which permission you need. For more information, please read our documentation on [fixing the DNS Record for a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-dns-records-on-cloudcannon-dns/#fix-the-dns-record-for-a-subdomain).
* **External DNS** — Add the record at your *DNS Provider*, using the *Copy* icon next to its value, then click the *I've updated it* button. The *Copy* icon shows *Copied!* once it has copied the value. CloudCannon checks hourly for up to seven days. Once it finds the record and issues the *SSL Certificate*, CloudCannon emails everyone who can edit the settings of the *Base Domain* that the *Subdomain* is live. For this email, please read our documentation on [the emails about your Subdomain's DNS Records](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#emails-about-your-subdomain-s-dns-records). If CloudCannon still can't find the record 48 hours after you marked it as updated, the section lists what usually fixes it. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding the records](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/#add-the-records).

When the *Subdomain* has TXT Records to add for its certificate, the *DNS* section points you to them under *HTTPS*. On external DNS, while the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/w/wildcard-certificate.yml">wildcard SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> of your *Base Domain* is waiting to be renewed, it points you to *Wildcard certificate records* on the *DNS* tab instead.

If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To check the DNS for a *Subdomain*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Check now* button in the footer of the table in the *DNS* section.

CloudCannon will check the *Subdomain's* DNS and update the *DNS* section. To see earlier checks, click the *View DNS check history* icon next to the *Check now* button. CloudCannon will open the *DNS checks for [Subdomain]* modal. For what the modal shows, please read our documentation on [viewing the DNS check history](/documentation/developer-articles/how-does-cloudcannon-check-your-dns-records/#view-the-dns-check-history).

You can check the DNS for each *Subdomain* at most once a minute. For a minute after a check, the *Check now* button is disabled, with the tooltip *Checked less than a minute ago. You can check again in a minute.* Checking requires the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, and without it the *Check now* and *I've updated it* buttons are disabled. Hovering over a disabled button shows the permission it needs.

## Troubleshoot configuring a Subdomain

If CloudCannon doesn't make the change you expect, or the *Subdomain* page doesn't show the controls you expect, here are some common causes. If you see an error that isn't listed here, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Organization*, the *Subdomain*, and the full error message.

### You do not have permission to edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Configure hosting*, *Configure HTTPS*, *Check DNS now*, *Check now*, or *I've updated it* button. You don't have the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for the *Base Domain*. Ask someone who manages <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> in your *Organization* for this permission.

### You do not have permission to edit the DNS for [Base Domain].

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Fix* button on *CloudCannon DNS*. You don't have the `base-domain:settings:dns:write` permission for the *Base Domain*. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for this permission.

### Checked less than a minute ago. You can check again in a minute.

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Check now* or *Check DNS now* button. You can check the DNS for each *Subdomain* at most once a minute. Wait a minute, then check again.
