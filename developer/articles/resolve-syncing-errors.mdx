A Syncing error occurs when CloudCannon cannot sync your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> with your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm>. For example, a recent push or pull failed, your branch or *Git Repository* no longer exists, or CloudCannon cannot authenticate with your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-provider.yml">Git Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm>. You cannot save any files until you resolve the Syncing error.

When a Syncing error occurs, CloudCannon shows a *Sync failed* message on the *Syncing* page under *Site Settings* and on your *Site Dashboard*. The message explains the cause and lists the options that can resolve it.

If your *Site* shows a *Syncing is paused* message instead, incoming changes conflict with unsaved changes on your *Site*. For more information, please read our documentation on [resuming Syncing](/documentation/user-articles/resume-syncing/).

The resolution options depend on the cause of the Syncing error:

* **Retry**: Push the changes saved in CloudCannon and sync again. Use this option when the cause was temporary, such as downtime at your *Git Provider*.
* **Discard changes and retry**: Discard the changes saved in CloudCannon that have not synced, then sync your *Site* from your *Git Repository*.
* **Push changes to a new branch**: Move the changes from your *Site* to a new branch, and connect your *Site* to that branch. You can then merge the changes in your local development environment.
* **Switch branch**: Connect your *Site* to a different branch and sync the files from it.

The *Push changes to a new branch* and *Switch branch* options only appear when a recent push or pull failed.

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  The resolution buttons are disabled while your *Site* has unsaved changes, and if you do not have permission to change the *Site's* Git settings.
</comp.Notice>

## Reconnect your Git Provider first

If the *Sync failed* message says CloudCannon could not authenticate with your *Git Provider*, retrying your *Site* will not resolve the Syncing error. CloudCannon's access to a *Git Provider* applies to your whole *Organization*, so you need to reconnect the *Git Provider* before any *Site* can sync with it. For more information, please read our documentation on [reconnecting your Git Provider](/documentation/developer-articles/reconnect-your-git-provider/).

Once your *Git Provider* is reconnected, click the *Retry* button on the *Syncing* page under *Site Settings*.

## Retry syncing

To retry syncing your *Site*:

1. Navigate to the *Syncing* page under *Site Settings*.
2. Click the *Retry* button under the *Retry syncing to your source provider* heading in the *Sync failed* message.

CloudCannon will push the changes saved in CloudCannon to your *Git Repository* and sync your *Site* again.

## Discard your changes and retry

To discard your changes and retry syncing:

1. Navigate to the *Syncing* page under *Site Settings*.
2. Click the *Discard changes and retry* button under the *Retry syncing to your source provider* heading in the *Sync failed* message. CloudCannon will open the *Clear and reconnect?* modal.
3. Click the *Clear and reconnect* button.

CloudCannon will save your unsynced changes as a backup, then sync your *Site* from your *Git Repository*. You can retrieve the discarded changes from the *Backups* page under *Site Settings*.

## Push your changes to a new branch

To push your changes to a new branch:

1. Navigate to the *Syncing* page under *Site Settings*.
2. Click the *Push changes to a new branch* button under the *Create a new branch from CloudCannon changes* heading in the *Sync failed* message. CloudCannon will open the *Create a new branch* modal.
3. Enter a name for your new branch in the *New branch name* field.
4. Click the *Create branch* button.

CloudCannon will create the new branch, push the changes from your *Site* to it, and connect your *Site* to the new branch. You can then merge the new branch into your original branch in your local development environment.

## Switch to a different branch

To switch your *Site* to a different branch:

1. Navigate to the *Syncing* page under *Site Settings*.
2. Click the *Switch branch* button under the *Switch this Site to a different branch* heading in the *Sync failed* message. CloudCannon will open the *Switch branch* modal.
3. Select the branch to which you want to switch your *Site* from the *Branch* dropdown.
4. Click the *Switch branch* button.

CloudCannon will sync the files from your chosen branch, without deleting any files previously deployed from the old branch.
