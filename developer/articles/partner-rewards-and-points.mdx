If you're in the [CloudCannon Partner Program](/documentation/developer-articles/cloudcannon-partner-program/), your partnership is measured in *Partner Points*. The more client work you run on CloudCannon, the more you earn, and points move you through partner tiers that unlock benefits.

## How you earn points

You earn *Partner Points* for each active client you bring onto CloudCannon. Both ways of structuring client work count toward your total: client *Sites* kept in your Partner *Organization*, and separate [Client Organizations](/documentation/developer-articles/create-a-client-organization/). The larger a client's plan, the more points that client earns you.

You can also earn extra points by contributing to the CloudCannon blog or community, or by teaming up on co-marketing. CloudCannon may award additional points for milestones and campaigns.

## Partner tiers and benefits

As your points grow, your partnership moves up through four tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum. Each tier unlocks more benefits, such as:

* Increasing discounts on your plans as you climb the tiers, up to 20% off.
* Perks such as priority support, a dedicated Slack channel, sales referrals, and a listing on the CloudCannon Experts page.
* The ability to request features and view CloudCannon's product roadmap.

Your current tier, your running points total, and the points needed to reach the next tier are all shown on the *Partner Rewards* page.

## Your partner badge

Each tier comes with a partner badge you can download and display on your own site to show your CloudCannon partnership.

## Track your rewards

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Partner-Rewards-Page" title="The Partner Rewards page" alt="A screenshot of the Partner Rewards page in CloudCannon, showing the partner points total, the current tier, unlocked benefits, and the next tier." type="screenshot" />

The *Partner Rewards* page in your Partner *Organization* is the home base for everything above. From there you can:

* See your running points total and a breakdown by client.
* View your current tier and how many points remain until the next one.
* Download your current partner badge.
* Review the benefits you've unlocked and what's coming next.
