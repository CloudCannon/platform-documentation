A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is the web address you host your website on, purchased through a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. When you host your websites through CloudCannon, you connect your *Custom Domain* to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> and choose which <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subpath.yml">subpath</comp.GlossaryTerm> each <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> is served on. You can manage all the *Custom Domains* connected to your *Organization* through the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>, from the *Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

Articles in this section are for developers and anyone responsible for the domains, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns.yml">DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL certificates</comp.GlossaryTerm> of an *Organization*. <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/testing-domain.yml">Testing Domains</comp.GlossaryTerm>, routing, and external hosting are covered in the Hosting section of our Developer documentation. Hosting your *Site* through CloudCannon is optional. If another provider hosts your *Site*, you set up its domain and SSL certificate with that provider, but you can still manage your domain's DNS records with <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

In the Domains section of our Developer documentation, we cover:

* How hosting works — Learn how visitors reach your *Site*, from your domain registrar to the *Site* CloudCannon serves
* Custom Domains — Learn how to connect a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization* and add it to your *Sites*
* Subdomains — Learn how to manage the subdomains, redirects, and fallback redirect on your *Custom Domain*
* DNS — Learn how to configure *CloudCannon DNS* or external DNS
* SSL — Learn how to add an auto-generated or custom SSL certificate, and resolve SSL certificate errors

Let's briefly introduce these topics.

## How hosting works

When a visitor enters your web address in their internet browser, the nameservers and DNS records for your domain send them to CloudCannon, which serves the *Site* on that address. Each address uses one of three hosting options: <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-hosting.yml">CloudCannon Hosting</comp.GlossaryTerm>, <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudflare-proxy.yml">Cloudflare Proxy</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/single-origin.yml">Single Origin</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

For a more in-depth explanation of how visitors reach your *Site*, please read our documentation:

* [How does hosting work on CloudCannon?](/documentation/developer-articles/how-does-hosting-work-on-cloudcannon/)

## Custom Domains

To manage your *Custom Domains* through CloudCannon, you must connect each one to your *Organization*, choose *CloudCannon DNS* or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and add the subdomain and path you want to host on to a *Site*. For each *Custom Domain* you connect, CloudCannon creates a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm> in the *Domains Browser*, where you can see whether the domain is set up correctly and manage its subdomains, DNS, and settings.

## Subdomains

A subdomain is a name in front of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> (e.g., www.example.com or docs.example.com). Every address on a *Custom Domain* is a subdomain in CloudCannon, including the *Base Domain* itself (e.g., example.com). After you connect a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*, you choose whether each subdomain serves one or more *Sites*, each at its own path, or redirects visitors to another domain. Your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">fallback redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm> decides where CloudCannon sends visitors to a subdomain you haven't set up.

## DNS

The Domain Name System (DNS) translates a domain name into the address of the server that hosts it. When you connect a *Custom Domain*, you choose its DNS provider. With *CloudCannon DNS*, you set CloudCannon's nameservers at your domain registrar, and CloudCannon adds the records your subdomains need. With external DNS, you keep your current DNS provider and add the records CloudCannon lists for each subdomain.

For a more in-depth explanation of DNS, including how to configure CloudCannon or external DNS, please read our documentation:

* [What is DNS?](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-dns/)
* [Configure CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/)
* [Configure external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/)

## SSL

An SSL certificate lets browsers load your *Site* over HTTPS. CloudCannon issues and renews an SSL certificate for each subdomain on your *Custom Domain*, or you can upload your own. You can also set the minimum <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/tls.yml">TLS</comp.GlossaryTerm> version that browsers must use for every subdomain on your *Custom Domain* that uses *CloudCannon Hosting* or *Cloudflare Proxy*.
