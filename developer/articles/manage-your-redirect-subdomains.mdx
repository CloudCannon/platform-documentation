<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, can add, change, and delete *Redirect Subdomains*.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/r/redirect-subdomain.yml">Redirect Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> sends every visitor to another domain. The redirect keeps the path and query string, so a visitor to `www.example.com/pricing?ref=ad` arrives at `example.com/pricing?ref=ad`. Redirects send visitors to the target over HTTPS when the target has an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm>.

You can redirect to another <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> on your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> or to any other domain, including a domain you have moved away from (e.g., an old brand name that now redirects to your new domain). For the steps, please read our documentation on [redirecting a domain to another domain](/documentation/developer-articles/redirect-a-domain-to-another-domain/). If the destination is on a *Custom Domain* connected to your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, it must already be a *Subdomain* there. For example, to redirect to `shop.example.com` when `example.com` is connected to your *Organization*, add `shop.example.com` as a *Subdomain* first. Destinations on domains that aren't connected to your *Organization* have no such requirement. *Redirect Subdomains* do not count toward the number of *Subdomains* serving *Sites* that your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> includes.

You can redirect to a *Subdomain* that is itself a redirect, everywhere you enter a destination. Visitors are then redirected twice, so CloudCannon shows the final destination below the *Redirect visitors to* field (e.g., *Avoid a double redirect by entering example.com instead.*), but still lets you save. CloudCannon refuses the *Subdomain* itself, a subdomain that isn't set up yet on one of the *Custom Domains* connected to your *Organization*, and any change that would send visitors in a loop, and shows why below the *Redirect visitors to* field. For these messages, please read the section on [troubleshooting your Redirect Subdomains](#troubleshoot-managing-your-redirect-subdomains). For how loops form and how to fix one, please read the section on [fixing a redirect loop](#fix-a-redirect-loop).

When you add the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> (e.g., `example.com`) as a *Site Subdomain*, or change it from a redirect to serve *Sites*, CloudCannon also adds `www.example.com` as a redirect to it, unless `www.example.com` already exists. Adding `www.example.com` as a *Site Subdomain* adds `example.com` as a redirect in the same way. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/).

For more information on how *Subdomains* work, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/).

You can also find the *Change redirect target*, *Change type to Site*, *Change type to Redirect*, and *Delete Subdomain* options in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm> for each *Subdomain* on the *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page.

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/).

## Redirect status codes

Each *Redirect Subdomain* has its own status code, which you choose in the *Status code* dropdown when you add or change the redirect. New *Redirect Subdomains* use *301 permanent redirect*, including the redirect CloudCannon adds for `www.example.com` when you add `example.com`.

* **301 permanent redirect** — Search engines move to the target. Browsers can change a form POST to a GET.
* **302 temporary redirect** — Search engines keep the original address. Browsers can change a form POST to a GET.
* **307 temporary redirect, keeping the method** — Search engines keep the original address. Browsers repeat the same method, such as a form POST.
* **308 permanent redirect, keeping the method** — Search engines move to the target. Browsers repeat the same method, such as a form POST.

Search engines treat a permanent redirect (301 or 308) as a permanent move. If you might later serve a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> on the *Subdomain*, or swap which of `example.com` and `www.example.com` redirects to the other, choose a temporary redirect (302 or 307) until you are sure.

The status code of your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/fallback-redirect.yml">Fallback Redirect</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a separate setting. For more information, please read our documentation on [updating the Fallback Redirect for your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/update-the-fallback-redirect-for-your-custom-domain/).

## Add a Redirect Subdomain

To add a *Redirect Subdomain*, please read our documentation on [adding a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/).

## Redirect a whole domain to another domain

To send every visitor on one domain to another domain (e.g., from an old brand name, `old-example.com`, to `example.com`), add *Redirect Subdomains* for the old domain's *Base Domain* and `www`. You don't need a *Site*. For the steps, including DNS and HTTPS for the old domain, please read our documentation on [redirecting a domain to another domain](/documentation/developer-articles/redirect-a-domain-to-another-domain/).

## Change where a redirect goes

You can change the destination of a *Redirect Subdomain*, its status code, or both. Browsers that have already followed a redirect may keep using the old target for up to an hour after you change it.

If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To change where a *Redirect Subdomain* sends visitors:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the domain that has the *Redirect Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-page.yml">Domain page</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Redirect Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Change Target* button in the *Redirect* section. CloudCannon will open the *Change [Subdomain]'s Target* modal.
6. Enter the new domain in the *Redirect visitors to* field.
7. (Optional.) Select a different status code from the *Status code* dropdown.
8. Click the *Save Redirect* button.

CloudCannon will redirect visitors to the new domain with the status code you selected.

The *Redirect* section of the *Subdomain* page shows the redirect as a tree: the *Subdomain* with a description of its status code (e.g., *301 permanent redirect, keeping the path and query string.*), then the destination and, if the destination is on the same *Custom Domain*, the *Sites* it serves or the domain to which it redirects.

## Change a Subdomain that serves Sites to a redirect

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Changing a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites* to a redirect also requires the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for each of those *Sites*.
</comp.Notice>

When you change a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites* into a redirect:

* The *Sites* on the *Subdomain* lose their *Custom Domain*. Each *Site* keeps its [Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/).
* Any redirects on the same *Custom Domain* that pointed at the *Subdomain* now point at the new target.

If you redirect a *Subdomain* to another *Subdomain* that already redirects back to it, the two *Subdomains* swap. For example, if `www.example.com` redirects to `example.com` and you redirect `example.com` to `www.example.com`, `www.example.com` takes the *Sites* from `example.com` at the same paths. If `example.com` was your *Fallback Redirect*, `www.example.com` also becomes the fallback.

If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To change a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites* to a redirect:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Change Subdomain Type* button next to *Change Subdomain type to Redirect* in the *Danger zone* section. CloudCannon will open the *Change [Subdomain] to a Redirect Subdomain* modal.
6. Enter the domain to which you want to send visitors in the *Redirect visitors to* field.
7. (Optional.) Select a status code from the *Status code* dropdown.
8. Click the *Change type to Redirect* button.

CloudCannon will redirect every visitor on the *Subdomain* to the domain you entered.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Subdomain-Page-Change-To-Redirect-Modal" alt="A screenshot of the Change example.com to a Redirect Subdomain modal shows www.example.com in the Redirect visitors to field, a note that CloudCannon will swap the two, 301 permanent redirect under Status code, and the Change type to Redirect button." title="The Change to a Redirect Subdomain modal" type="screenshot"/>

You can also change your *Site's* *Subdomain* to a redirect from the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*, using the *Change Subdomain Type* button next to *Change Subdomain type to Redirect* in the *Danger zone* section under *Subdomain settings*. Your *Site* then leaves the *Subdomain*, and CloudCannon opens the *[Site Name] is no longer on [Subdomain]* modal, with the *Stay here* and *Go to [Subdomain] settings* buttons.

## Change a redirect to serve a Site

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Changing a *Redirect Subdomain* to serve a *Site* also requires the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for that *Site*.
</comp.Notice>

When you change a *Redirect Subdomain* to serve a *Site*, the *Subdomain* stops redirecting. Its hosting and certificate stay as they are.

Once the *Subdomain* serves a *Site*, it counts toward the number of *Subdomains* serving *Sites* that your *Subscription Plan* includes. If serving a *Site* takes your *Organization* over that number, CloudCannon will ask you to confirm the extra cost first. After you accept, if your *Organization* has no payment method, CloudCannon opens the *Add your Credit Card* modal. Moving a *Site* that was the only one on its old *Subdomain* doesn't change the count, so CloudCannon doesn't ask. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).

In a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> on its extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, serving a *Site* on a *Subdomain* that had no *Sites* shortens the trial so that it ends five days later, whether or not the *Client Organization* is still within the limits of its *Subscription Plan*. This applies on every *Subscription Plan* except the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/enterprise-plan.yml">Enterprise Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and not when you move a *Site* that was the only one on its old *Subdomain*. CloudCannon asks you to confirm before it shortens the trial. For more information, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).

If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To change a *Redirect Subdomain* to serve a *Site*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Redirect Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Redirect Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Change Subdomain Type* button next to *Change Subdomain type to Site* in the *Danger zone* section. CloudCannon will open the *Change [Subdomain] to a Site Subdomain* modal.
6. (Optional.) Select the *Site* you want to serve from the *Site* dropdown. Leave it empty to set up the *Subdomain* with no *Site* yet.
7. (Optional.) If you selected a *Site*, enter a path in the *Subpath* field to serve the *Site* under that path, so other *Sites* can share the *Subdomain*.
8. Click the *Change type to Site* button.

CloudCannon will stop redirecting the *Subdomain* and serve the *Site* you selected. If you did not select a *Site*, visitors will see a 404 page until you add one.

If the *Site* you select is the last *Site* on another *Subdomain*, the modal also asks what happens to that *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [moving a Site from another Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/#move-a-site-from-another-subdomain).

## Delete a Redirect Subdomain

When you delete a *Redirect Subdomain*, CloudCannon stops redirecting it and removes its certificate. Visitors to the deleted *Subdomain* go to your *Fallback Redirect*. If the *Redirect Subdomain* was your *Fallback Redirect*, CloudCannon will make another *Subdomain* your *Fallback Redirect*, which you can change on the *Subdomains* tab. If no *Fallback Redirect* is left, visitors see a 404 page.

If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* your *Custom Domain* is connected to first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To delete a *Redirect Subdomain*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Redirect Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Redirect Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Delete Subdomain* button in the *Danger zone* section. CloudCannon will open the *Delete [Subdomain]?* modal.
6. (Optional.) If other *Subdomains* redirect to this one, uncheck the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* or *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox to keep them.
7. Type the *Subdomain* in the *Type [Subdomain] to confirm* field.
8. Click the *Delete Subdomain* button.

CloudCannon will delete the *Redirect Subdomain*.

For more information on deleting *Subdomains* that serve *Sites*, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/#delete-a-subdomain).

## Fix a redirect loop

A redirect loop sends visitors from one *Subdomain* through a chain of redirects and back to where they started, so their browser never reaches a page. CloudCannon checks for loops whenever you add, change, or delete a *Subdomain*, and refuses any change that would create one.

To work out where visitors end up, CloudCannon follows each step of the chain:

* **Redirect Subdomain** — Visitors go to its destination.
* **Site Subdomain** — The chain ends, whether or not the *Site Subdomain* has a *Site* yet.
* **Unconfigured subdomain** — A subdomain you haven't configured on one of the *Custom Domains* connected to your *Organization*. Visitors go to the *Fallback Redirect* for that *Custom Domain*.
* **Any other domain** — The chain ends, because CloudCannon doesn't follow redirects on domains that aren't connected to your *Organization*.

A loop forms in one of two ways:

* **A redirect points back** — A *Redirect Subdomain* sends visitors to another *Redirect Subdomain* that leads back to it, directly or through other redirects. For example, `a.example.com` redirects to `b.example.com`, and `b.example.com` redirects to `a.example.com`.
* **The Fallback Redirect points back** — The chain reaches an unconfigured subdomain, which sends visitors to your *Fallback Redirect*, and the *Fallback Redirect* leads back. For example, your *Fallback Redirect* is `blog.example.com`, which redirects to `news.example.com`. If you delete `news.example.com` and uncheck the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* checkbox to keep `blog.example.com`, visitors to `news.example.com` would go to `blog.example.com`, which sends them back to `news.example.com`. The *Delete [Subdomain]?* modal then shows *Cannot select this Subdomain as it would create a redirect loop.*

When a destination you enter in the *Redirect visitors to* field would create a loop, CloudCannon shows *Cannot select this Subdomain as it would create a redirect loop.* below the field. If you enter the *Subdomain* itself, CloudCannon shows *Cannot select this Subdomain as it would redirect to itself.* instead. The *Redirect visitors to* field appears in:

* The *Add a Subdomain* modal, when you select the *Redirect* type.
* The *Change [Subdomain]'s Target* modal.
* The *Change [Subdomain] to a Redirect Subdomain* modal. If the destination already redirects back to the *Subdomain*, this modal doesn't show the error, because CloudCannon swaps the two *Subdomains* instead. For more information, please read the section on [changing a Subdomain that serves Sites to a redirect](#change-a-subdomain-that-serves-sites-to-a-redirect).
* The *Redirect visitors* option, when you remove or move the last *Site* on a *Subdomain*.

When you change your *Fallback Redirect* to a *Subdomain* that would lead back to itself, the *Change Fallback* modal, and the *Change the Fallback Redirect?* modal that the *Use as Fallback Redirect* option opens, show *You cannot select [Subdomain] as your Fallback Redirect, because website visitors would go around in a loop ([Subdomain] → [Subdomain] → [Subdomain]). Select a different Subdomain.* The message lists the *Subdomains* in the loop.

To fix a loop, find the redirect in the chain that points back. On the *Subdomains* tab of the *Domain* page, each *Redirect Subdomain* shows its destination (e.g., *Redirects visitors to news.example.com.*). Start from the destination you entered, and follow each redirect until you reach the *Subdomain* you were changing. Then do one of the following before you try your change again:

* [Change where the redirect goes](#change-where-a-redirect-goes).
* [Change the redirect to serve a Site](#change-a-redirect-to-serve-a-site).
* [Delete the redirect](#delete-a-redirect-subdomain).
* For a loop through your *Fallback Redirect*, [make a different Subdomain your Fallback Redirect](/documentation/developer-articles/update-the-fallback-redirect-for-your-custom-domain/), or, when you delete a *Subdomain*, leave the *Delete this other Redirect Subdomain* or *Delete these other Redirect Subdomains* checkbox checked so the redirects that point at it are deleted too.

## Emails after you change a Subdomain's type

CloudCannon emails everyone else in your *Organization* who has permission to view its billing when a *Subdomain* starts or stops serving *Sites* while your *Organization* has more *Subdomains* serving *Sites* than its *Subscription Plan* includes. Changing a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites* to a redirect can send the *[Name] removed an extra Subdomain from [Organization]'s Subscription Plan* email, unless the two *Subdomains* swap. Changing a *Redirect Subdomain* to serve a *Site* can send the *[Name] added an extra Subdomain to [Organization]'s Subscription Plan* email. CloudCannon doesn't send these emails on the *Enterprise Plan*.

For these emails, please read our documentation on [the emails after you add a Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/#emails-after-you-add-a-custom-domain) and [the emails after you remove a Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/remove-a-custom-domain-from-your-site/#emails-after-you-remove-a-custom-domain).

## Troubleshoot managing your Redirect Subdomains

If CloudCannon doesn't make the change you expect, or the *Subdomain* page doesn't show the controls you expect, here are some common causes. If you see an error that isn't listed here, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Organization*, the *Subdomain*, the destination you entered, and the full error message.

### You do not have permission to edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Change Target*, *Change Subdomain Type*, or *Delete Subdomain* button, or over a disabled option in a *Subdomain's* *Context Menu*. You don't have the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for the *Base Domain*. Ask someone who manages <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/p/permission-group.yml">Permission Groups</comp.GlossaryTerm> in your *Organization* for this permission.

### You do not have permission to change the hosting of any Site on this Subdomain.

This message appears when you hover over the disabled *Change Subdomain Type* button on a *Subdomain* that serves *Sites*. Changing it to a redirect requires the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for each *Site* it serves. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for this permission for each *Site*.

### You can only select a Site if you have permission to change its hosting.

This message appears below the *Site* dropdown in the *Change [Subdomain] to a Site Subdomain* modal, which disables any *Site* whose hosting you can't change. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for that *Site*.

### Cannot select this Subdomain as it would create a redirect loop.

The destination you entered in the *Redirect visitors to* field would send visitors back to where they started. Enter a different destination. For how loops form and how to fix one, please read the section on [fixing a redirect loop](#fix-a-redirect-loop).

### Cannot select this Subdomain as it would redirect to itself.

The destination you entered in the *Redirect visitors to* field is the *Subdomain* you are changing. Enter a different destination.

### Cannot select this Subdomain until you configure it on [Custom Domain].

The destination you entered is on one of your *Custom Domains*, but it isn't a *Subdomain* there yet. Add the *Subdomain* to that *Custom Domain* first, or enter a different destination. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/).
