<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for the *Site* and the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for the *Base Domain*, can add a *Custom Domain* to a *Site*. This applies to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* and the subdomain page alike. Adding a domain your *Organization* hasn't connected yet requires the `base-domain:create` permission in place of the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a unique address used to identify a website (e.g., <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/">cloudcannon.com</a>). If you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/), you can host your website content at that web address by adding the *Custom Domain* to your *Site*.

You can host a CloudCannon *Site* on the *Base Domain* (e.g., example.com), a subdomain (e.g., docs.example.com), or a path on either (e.g., example.com/staff/). Several *Sites* can share a subdomain, each at its own path. Every *Site* on a subdomain shares that subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings. A *Site* at a path needs to build into a folder that matches the path. For more information, please read the section on [building your Site for a path](#build-your-site-for-a-path).

<comp.DocsImage path="assets/diagrams/CloudCannon-Documentation-URL-Structure-Diagram.png" alt="A diagram of a URL shows labels for protocol, subdomain, domain, top-level domain, subdirectory, slug, and path." title="The parts of a URL" type="ui-snippet" />

When you add the *Base Domain* (e.g., example.com), CloudCannon also adds www.example.com as a redirect to it, if www.example.com does not already exist. When you add www.example.com, CloudCannon adds example.com as a redirect to www.example.com in the same way.

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/).

## Add a Custom Domain from Site Settings

To add a *Custom Domain* to your *Site*:

1. Click the *Sites* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Sites Browser*.
2. Identify the *Site* to which you want to add a *Custom Domain* and click the *Card* for the *Site*. CloudCannon will open the *Dashboard* for that *Site*.
3. Navigate to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*.
4. Select the *Custom Domain* you want to add using the *Base Domain* dropdown.
5. (Optional.) Enter a subdomain in the *Subdomain* text field. Leave this field empty to host your *Site* on the *Base Domain* itself.
6. (Optional.) Enter a path in the *Path (optional)* text field. Leave this field empty to host your *Site* at the root of the subdomain.
7. Click the *Add custom domain* button.

CloudCannon will add your *Site* to the subdomain. Visitors reach your *Site* on your *Custom Domain* once the DNS for the subdomain points at CloudCannon. Until then, your *Site* stays on its [Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/), and the *Custom Domain* page shows a setup guide:

* **CloudCannon DNS** — If the nameservers of your domain don't point at CloudCannon yet, the guide shows the nameservers to set at your domain registrar. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/).
* **External DNS** — The guide shows how far along setup is, and its *Show the record* button scrolls to the *DNS* section, which lists the record the subdomain needs at your DNS provider, with *Copy* and *I've updated it* buttons. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).

The *Base Domain* dropdown disables any domain whose settings you can't edit and labels it *No permission*. If you have the `base-domain:create` permission, you can also connect a new domain from the dropdown. For more information, please read our documentation on [connecting a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/).

If the subdomain has no other *Sites* and takes your *Organization* over the number of *Custom Domains* in its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, CloudCannon will ask you to confirm the extra cost before adding it. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).

In a *Client Organization* on its extended six-month *Free Trial*, serving a *Site* on a subdomain that had no *Sites* shortens the trial so that it ends five days later, whether or not the *Client Organization* is still within the limits of its *Subscription Plan*. CloudCannon asks you to confirm before it shortens the trial. For more information, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).

You can also add a *Site* to a subdomain from the subdomain page, which you can open from the *Subdomains* tab of your *Domain* page. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/).

## Build your Site for a path

If you host your *Site* at a path (e.g., example.com/docs/), your build output needs a folder that matches the path. CloudCannon doesn't remove the path from a request before it looks for the file, so it serves `example.com/docs/about/` from `docs/about/index.html` in your build output. Your *Site's* links also need to include the path, so that a link to `/about/` becomes `/docs/about/`.

Many SSGs have a setting that adds a path to your links, but not all of them also build your files into a matching folder. After you add a path, check that your build output has a folder with that name and that every page of your *Site* is inside it.

If your Jekyll *Site* sets `baseurl` in its `_config.yml` file, CloudCannon serves the build output under that `baseurl` instead. Set `baseurl` to the same path (e.g., `/docs`) rather than moving your files.

### Use Reseed to build for a path

<a href="https://github.com/CloudCannon/reseed" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Reseed</a> is an open-source tool that copies your build output into a folder named after the path and rewrites the links in your pages to include it. You can run Reseed after your build in a postbuild script:

<comp.CodeBlock language="shell" source="/.cloudcannon/postbuild">
```
#!/bin/bash

npx reseed -s _site -d _reseed -b docs
rm -rf _site
mv _reseed _site
```
</comp.CodeBlock>

Replace `_site` with the *Output Path* of your *Site*, and `docs` with your path. CloudCannon uploads the files in your *Output Path* once the postbuild script finishes, so the script must leave the rewritten files there. For more information, please read our documentation on [extending your build process with hooks](/documentation/developer-articles/extending-your-build-process-with-hooks/) and [configuring your command line options](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-your-command-line-options/).

CloudCannon can also run Reseed as a build step for you.

To run Reseed as a build step:

1. Contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Site* and the path.
2. Enter the path in the *Baseurl* field on the *Reseed* page under *Site Settings*.
3. Click the *Update Reseed Details* button.

CloudCannon will rebuild your *Site* with the new path.

## Review your Site's Custom Domain

Once your *Site* has a *Custom Domain*, the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* shows the subdomain that hosts your *Site* as a heading, with a *Fallback* label if it is the fallback for the domain. The *Sites* section below lists every *Site* the subdomain serves, with the path at which each *Site* is served. CloudCannon labels your *Site* with *This site*. The page also shows the same *Hosting*, *HTTPS*, and *DNS* sections as the subdomain page, because these settings belong to the subdomain and apply to every *Site* on it. Until the subdomain is live, the setup guide appears above these sections.

From the *Custom Domain* page, you can:

* Click the *Context Menu* on the *Card* labeled *This site* in the *Sites* section and select the *Change path* option to change the path at which the subdomain serves your *Site*. For more information, please read our documentation on [changing the path of a Site](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/#change-the-path-of-a-site).
* Click the *Open [Subdomain]* button next to the *Sites* heading to open the subdomain page.
* Click the *Change to redirect* button next to *Redirect instead* in the *Danger zone* section to change the subdomain into a redirect to another domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/#change-a-subdomain-that-serves-sites-to-a-redirect).
* Click the *Remove custom domain* button in the *Danger zone* section to stop hosting your *Site* on the subdomain. For more information, please read our documentation on [removing a Custom Domain from your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/remove-a-custom-domain-from-your-site/).

If you can change the hosting of your *Site* but can't view the *Custom Domain*, the *Custom Domain* page shows only your *Site's* address, with a *Path for [Site Name]* field and the *Remove custom domain* button. Both are disabled, with a line that explains which permission you need, and the page doesn't show the *Hosting*, *HTTPS*, or *DNS* sections. If you can view the *Custom Domain* but can't edit its settings, the controls for the subdomain are disabled in the same way. To change the path or remove the *Custom Domain*, ask a member of your *Organization* who can edit the settings of the *Base Domain* (the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission), such as a member of the *Owners* or *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), to make the change.

You should also check the [SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-an-ssl-certificate/) for your *Site's* subdomain. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding an auto-generated SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/) or [adding a custom SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-ssl-certificate/).
