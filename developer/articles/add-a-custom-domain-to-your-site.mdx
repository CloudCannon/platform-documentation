<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `site:settings:hosting:write` and `base-domain:settings:details:write` permissions, can add a *Custom Domain* to a *Site*.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a domain you own and connect to your CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so visitors reach your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> at your own address (e.g., <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/">cloudcannon.com</a>). Adding the *Custom Domain* to your *Site* tells CloudCannon to serve the *Site* at that address.

You can host a *Site* on the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> (e.g., `example.com`), another <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> (e.g., `docs.example.com`), or a path on either (e.g., `example.com/staff/`). A *Site* at a path needs to build into a folder that matches the path. For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/) and [building your Site for a subpath](/documentation/developer-articles/build-your-site-for-a-subpath/).

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/), and that your *Site* is in <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosted-mode.yml">Hosted Mode</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon hides the *Custom Domain* page for a *Site* in <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/headless-mode.yml">Headless Mode</comp.GlossaryTerm>. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Site Building Mode is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-site-building-mode/). If you have the `base-domain:create` permission, you can also connect a new domain from the *Base Domain* dropdown.

There are two ways to add a *Custom Domain* to your *Site*: from the *Custom Domain* page under your *Site Settings*, or from the page for a *Subdomain* that already exists on your *Domain* page.

Before adding your *Site*, CloudCannon may ask you to confirm:

* **An extra cost** — If the *Subdomain* has no other *Sites* and takes your *Organization* over the number of *Subdomains* serving *Sites* that its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> includes. If your *Organization* has no payment method, CloudCannon opens the *Add your Credit Card* modal instead. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).
* **A shorter Free Trial** — In a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> on its extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, serving a *Site* on a *Subdomain* that had no *Sites* shortens the trial so that it ends five days later, on every *Subscription Plan* except the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/enterprise-plan.yml">Enterprise Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>. For more information, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).

## Add a Custom Domain from Site Settings

To add a *Custom Domain* to your *Site*:

1. Click the *Sites* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/sites-browser.yml">Sites Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Site* you want to add a *Custom Domain* to. CloudCannon will open the *Dashboard* for that *Site*.
3. Navigate to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*. CloudCannon will open the *Custom Domain* page.
4. Select the *Custom Domain* you want to add using the *Base Domain* dropdown.
5. (Optional.) Enter a *Subdomain* in the *Subdomain* text field. Leave this field empty to host your *Site* on the *Base Domain* itself.
6. (Optional.) Enter a path in the *Subpath* text field (e.g., `/jobs/`), using lowercase letters, numbers, `_`, and `-`, with a `/` between folders (e.g., `/docs/v2/`). Leave this field empty to host your *Site* at the root of the *Subdomain*.
7. Click the *Add custom domain* button.

CloudCannon will add your *Site* to the *Subdomain*. 

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Custom-Domain-Add-Domain" alt="A screenshot of the Custom Domain page under Site Settings shows example.com selected in the Base Domain dropdown, www in the Subdomain field with a note that example.com is added as a redirect to www.example.com, the Subpath field, and the Add custom domain button." title="Adding a Custom Domain on the Custom Domain page" type="screenshot"/>

Visitors reach your *Site* on your *Custom Domain* once the DNS for the *Subdomain* points at CloudCannon. Until then, your *Site* stays on its [Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/), and the *Custom Domain* page shows a setup guide:

* **CloudCannon DNS** — If the nameservers of your domain don't point at <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm> yet, the guide shows the nameservers to set at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Without the `base-domain:settings:dns:read` permission, the guide says that *CloudCannon DNS* isn't configured for your domain and that you don't have permission to edit its DNS, and doesn't list the nameservers. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/).
* **External DNS** — On <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the guide shows how far along setup is, and its *Scroll to DNS Records* button scrolls to the *DNS* section, which lists the record the *Subdomain* needs at your *DNS Provider*, with a *Copy* icon next to each value and the *I've updated it* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  When you add the *Base Domain* (e.g., `example.com`) as a new *Subdomain*, CloudCannon also adds `www.example.com` as a redirect to it, if `www.example.com` does not already exist. When you add `www.example.com` as a new *Subdomain*, CloudCannon adds `example.com` as a redirect to `www.example.com` in the same way.
</comp.Notice>

If the *Subdomain* you enter already exists, CloudCannon adds your *Site* to it:

* **The Subdomain has no Sites yet** — Your *Site* becomes the first *Site* on it and uses its hosting and HTTPS settings. Before you add it, the *Custom Domain* page tells you (e.g., *jobs.example.com is set up and waiting for a site.*).
* **The Subdomain already serves Sites** — Your *Site* joins it and uses its hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings. The path you choose must not already be in use on the *Subdomain*.
* **The Subdomain is a redirect** — Adding your *Site* stops the redirect and serves your *Site* on the *Subdomain* instead. Before you add it, the *Custom Domain* page warns you (e.g., *www.example.com redirects to example.com now. Adding Marketing site stops the redirect and serves Marketing site there.*).

## Add your Site from the Subdomain page

You can add your *Site* to a *Subdomain* that already exists from the page for that *Subdomain*. To add your *Site* to a new *Subdomain*, use the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* instead.

If your *Site* already has a *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon moves your *Site* to the new address.

If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* that owns the *Site* first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To add your *Site* from the *Subdomain* page:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Add a Site* button in the *Sites* section. CloudCannon will open the *Add a Site to [Subdomain]* modal.
6. Select your *Site* from the *Site* dropdown.
7. (Optional.) Enter a path in the *Subpath* field. Leave this field empty to serve your *Site* at the root of the *Subdomain*.
8. Click the *Add Site* button.

CloudCannon will serve your *Site* on the *Subdomain* at the path you entered.

## Review your Site's Custom Domain

Once your *Site* has a *Custom Domain*, the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* shows your *Site's* address on the *Subdomain*, a *Danger zone* section, and the *Subdomain settings*.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Custom-Domain-Success" alt="A screenshot of the Custom Domain page under Site Settings shows that Sendit is live at https://example.com/, the Danger zone section with the Remove custom domain button, and, under the Subdomain settings heading, a link to the page for example.com and the Sites section with the Add a Site button and Sendit labeled This site at the root path." title="The Custom Domain page for a Site with a Custom Domain" type="screenshot"/>

### Your Site's address

On the *Custom Domain* page, a sentence above your *Site's* full address (e.g., `https://example.com/docs/`) tells you whether visitors can reach your *Site* there yet:

* **[Site Name] goes live at this address once [Subdomain] points at CloudCannon** — The DNS Record for the *Subdomain* doesn't point at CloudCannon yet.
* **[Site Name] is live at** — Visitors reach your *Site* at this address over HTTPS.
* **[Site Name] is live at this address, and CloudCannon is turning HTTPS on** — Visitors reach your *Site*, and its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> is on its way.
* **[Site Name] is live at this address, but HTTPS is not working, so visitors see a security warning** — The *HTTPS* section under *Subdomain settings* says why.

Until the *Subdomain* is live, the setup guide appears below the address.

### Danger zone

The *Danger zone* section above the *Subdomain settings* heading on the *Custom Domain* page has the *Remove custom domain* button, which stops hosting your *Site* on the *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [removing a Custom Domain from your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/remove-a-custom-domain-from-your-site/).

### Subdomain settings

Under the *Subdomain settings* heading, the *Custom Domain* page shows the same sections as the page for the *Subdomain* on your *Domain* page: *Sites*, *Hosting*, *HTTPS*, *DNS*, and *Danger zone*. You can see all the *Sites* these settings apply to under the *Sites* section. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/), [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/), and [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/).

To check or change the *SSL Certificate* for your *Subdomain*, click the *Configure HTTPS* button in the *HTTPS* section under *Subdomain settings*. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding an auto-generated SSL Certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/) or [adding a Custom SSL Certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-ssl-certificate/).

## Emails after you add a Custom Domain

If adding your *Site* makes a *Subdomain* start serving *Sites* while your *Organization* has more *Subdomains* serving *Sites* than its *Subscription Plan* includes, CloudCannon will email everyone else in your *Organization* who has permission to view its billing. This includes members of the *Owners* and *Billing* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), members of the *Partner* group in a *Client Organization*, and members of [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `org:billing:read` permission. CloudCannon doesn't send this email on the *Enterprise Plan*.

### [Name] added an extra Subdomain to [Organization]'s Subscription Plan

The subject names the person who made the change. When no person made the change, the subject is *An extra Subdomain was added to [Organization]'s Subscription Plan* instead.

This email names the *Subdomain* that started serving a *Site*, how many *Subdomains* your *Subscription Plan* includes, and the price of each extra one. It also shows how many *Subdomains* serve *Sites* and how many are billed as <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/add-on.yml">Add-Ons</comp.GlossaryTerm>. The email is for your information, and you don't need to do anything. To review the extra cost, click the *Open the Subscription page for [Organization]* button to open the *Subscription* page under *Org Settings*. CloudCannon will email again if a *Subdomain* stops serving *Sites* while your *Organization* is over the number its *Subscription Plan* includes. To stop receiving this email, click the *Unsubscribe from this list* link at the bottom of the email. The same list includes other emails, such as when CloudCannon adds more <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosting-bandwidth.yml">Hosting Bandwidth</comp.GlossaryTerm> or <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/build-time.yml">Build Time</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or when your *Subscription Plan* changes. To choose which lists you receive, click the *Manage email preferences* link instead.

## Troubleshoot adding a Custom Domain

If CloudCannon doesn't add your *Custom Domain*, or the *Custom Domain* page doesn't show the controls you expect, here are some common causes. If you see an error that isn't listed here, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Organization* and *Site*, the address you tried to add, and the full error message.

### There is no Custom Domain page

CloudCannon hides the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* for a *Site* in *Headless Mode*. Switch your *Site* to *Hosted Mode* to add a *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Site Building Mode is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-site-building-mode/).

### Billing issue

If your *Organization's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> is inactive, or your *Organization* doesn't have enough <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the *Custom Domain* page shows a *Billing issue* page instead of the *Base Domain* dropdown. If your *Site* already has a *Custom Domain*, the page shows only the *Remove custom domain* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [what happens when a payment fails](/documentation/user-articles/what-happens-when-a-payment-fails/).

### No permission

The *Base Domain* dropdown disables any domain whose settings you can't edit and labels it *No permission*. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for that domain.

### You do not have permission to edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].

This message appears below the *Subdomain settings* heading in place of the settings when you can view the *Custom Domain* but don't have the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for its *Base Domain*. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for this permission.

### [Site Name] is served on [address].

If you don't have the `base-domain:details:read` permission for the *Custom Domain*, the *Custom Domain* page shows the name of the *Subdomain*, *[Site Name] is served on [address].*, a *Subpath for [Site Name]* field, an *Update Subpath* button, and the *Remove custom domain* button. If you also lack the `site:settings:hosting:write` or `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, all three controls are disabled, and hovering over each one shows a tooltip that names the permission you need. To change the path or remove the *Custom Domain*, ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for the permission the tooltip names, or ask someone who can edit the settings of the *Base Domain* to make the change for you.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Custom-Domain-Site-Only" alt="A screenshot of the Custom Domain page under Site Settings shows blog.example.com, a note that Engineering blog is served on blog.example.com/, the disabled Subpath for Engineering blog field and Update Subpath button, and the Danger zone section with the disabled Remove custom domain button." title="The Custom Domain page without permission to view the Custom Domain" type="screenshot"/>

### Domain already in use. Add a subpath to have two sites on the same domain

Another *Site* is already served at the root of the *Subdomain*. Enter a path in the *Subpath* field to serve your *Site* alongside it, or enter a different *Subdomain*.

### Domain can only contain letters, numbers, dots and hyphens

The *Subdomain* field contains a character that a domain name can't use. Remove it, then click the *Add custom domain* button again.

### Subpaths can only use lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens, and underscores, with a slash at the start and end.

Change the path in the *Subpath* field to use only those characters, with a `/` at the start and end (e.g., `/jobs/`).

### This Subpath is already in use.

Another *Site* on the *Subdomain* is already served at that path. Enter a different path in the *Subpath* field.
