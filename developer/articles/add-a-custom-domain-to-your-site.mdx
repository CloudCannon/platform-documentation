<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `site:settings:hosting:write` and `base-domain:settings:details:write` permissions, can add a *Custom Domain* to a *Site*. Adding a domain your *Organization* hasn't connected yet requires the `base-domain:create` permission instead of `base-domain:settings:details:write`.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a domain you own and connect to your CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so visitors reach your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> at your own address (e.g., <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/">cloudcannon.com</a>). If you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/), you can host your website content at that web address by adding the *Custom Domain* to your *Site*.

You can host a CloudCannon *Site* on the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> (e.g., `example.com`), another <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> (e.g., `docs.example.com`), or a path on either (e.g., `example.com/staff/`). Several *Sites* can share a *Subdomain*, each at its own path. Every *Site* on a *Subdomain* shares that *Subdomain's* hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings. A *Site* at a path needs to build into a folder that matches the path. For more information, please read the section on [building your Site for a path](#build-your-site-for-a-path).

<comp.DocsImage path="assets/diagrams/CloudCannon-Documentation-URL-Structure-Diagram.png" alt="A diagram of a URL shows labels for protocol, subdomain, domain, top-level domain, subdirectory, slug, and path." title="The parts of a URL" type="ui-snippet" />

When you add the *Base Domain* (e.g., `example.com`) as a new *Subdomain*, CloudCannon also adds `www.example.com` as a redirect to it, if `www.example.com` does not already exist. When you add `www.example.com` as a new *Subdomain*, CloudCannon adds `example.com` as a redirect to `www.example.com` in the same way.

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/), and that your *Site* is in *Hosted Mode*. CloudCannon hides the *Custom Domain* page for a *Site* in *Headless Mode*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Site Building Mode is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-site-building-mode/).

## Add a Custom Domain from Site Settings

To add a *Custom Domain* to your *Site*:

1. Log in to CloudCannon.
2. Select the *Organization* with the *Site* you want to add a *Custom Domain* to.
3. Click the *Sites* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/sites-browser.yml">Sites Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
4. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Site* you want to add a *Custom Domain* to. CloudCannon will open the *Dashboard* for that *Site*.
5. Navigate to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*. CloudCannon will open the *Custom Domain* page.
6. Select the *Custom Domain* you want to add using the *Base Domain* dropdown.
7. (Optional.) Enter a *Subdomain* in the *Subdomain* text field. Leave this field empty to host your *Site* on the *Base Domain* itself.
8. (Optional.) Enter a path in the *Path (optional)* text field (e.g., `/jobs/`), using lowercase letters, numbers, `_`, and `-`. Leave this field empty to host your *Site* at the root of the *Subdomain*.
9. Click the *Add custom domain* button.

CloudCannon will add your *Site* to the *Subdomain*. Visitors reach your *Site* on your *Custom Domain* once the DNS for the *Subdomain* points at CloudCannon. Until then, your *Site* stays on its [Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/), and the *Custom Domain* page shows a setup guide:

* **CloudCannon DNS** — If the nameservers of your domain don't point at <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm> yet, the guide shows the nameservers to set at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Without the `base-domain:settings:dns:read` permission, the guide asks you to finish setting up DNS but doesn't list the nameservers. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/).
* **External DNS** — On <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the guide shows how far along setup is, and its *Show the record* button scrolls to the *DNS* section, which lists the record the *Subdomain* needs at your DNS provider, with a *Copy* icon next to each value and the *I've updated it* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).

If the *Subdomain* you enter already exists, CloudCannon adds your *Site* to it:

* **The Subdomain already serves Sites** — Your *Site* joins it and uses its hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings. The path you choose must not already be in use on the *Subdomain*.
* **The Subdomain is a redirect** — Adding your *Site* stops the redirect and serves your *Site* on the *Subdomain* instead. Before you add it, the *Custom Domain* page warns you (e.g., *www.example.com redirects to example.com now. Adding Marketing site stops the redirect and serves Marketing site there.*).

The *Base Domain* dropdown disables any domain whose settings you can't edit and labels it *No permission*. If you have the `base-domain:create` permission, you can also connect a new domain from the dropdown. For more information, please read our documentation on [connecting a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/).

If the *Subdomain* has no other *Sites* and takes your *Organization* over the number of *Custom Domains* in its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, CloudCannon will ask you to confirm the extra cost before adding it. If your *Organization* has no payment method, CloudCannon opens the *Add your Credit Card* modal instead. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).

In a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> on its extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, serving a *Site* on a *Subdomain* that had no *Sites* shortens the trial so that it ends five days later, whether or not the *Client Organization* is still within the limits of its *Subscription Plan*. CloudCannon asks you to confirm before it shortens the trial. For more information, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).

You can also add a *Site* to a *Subdomain* from the *Subdomain* page, which you can open from the *Subdomains* tab of your *Domain* page. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/).

## Build your Site for a path

If you host your *Site* at a path (e.g., `example.com/docs/`), your build output needs a folder that matches the path. CloudCannon doesn't remove the path from a request before it looks for the file, so it serves `example.com/docs/about/` from `docs/about/index.html` in your build output. Your *Site's* links also need to include the path, so that a link to `/about/` becomes `/docs/about/`.

Many SSGs have a setting that adds a path to your links, but not all of them also build your files into a matching folder. After you add a path, check that your build output has a folder with that name and that every page of your *Site* is inside it.

If your Jekyll *Site* sets `baseurl` in its `_config.yml` file, CloudCannon serves the build output under that `baseurl` instead. Set `baseurl` to the same path (e.g., `/docs`) rather than moving your files.

### Use Reseed to build for a path

<a href="https://github.com/CloudCannon/reseed" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Reseed</a> is an open-source tool that copies your build output into a folder named after the path and rewrites the links in your pages to include it. You can run Reseed after your build in a postbuild script:

<comp.CodeBlock language="shell" source="/.cloudcannon/postbuild">
```
#!/bin/bash

npx reseed -s _site -d _reseed -b docs
rm -rf _site
mv _reseed _site
```
</comp.CodeBlock>

Replace `_site` with the *Output Path* of your *Site*, and `docs` with your path. CloudCannon uploads the files in your *Output Path* once the postbuild script finishes, so the script must leave the rewritten files there. For more information, please read our documentation on [extending your build process with hooks](/documentation/developer-articles/extending-your-build-process-with-hooks/) and [configuring your command line options](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-your-command-line-options/).

CloudCannon can also run Reseed as a build step for you.

To run Reseed as a build step:

1. Contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Site* and the path.
2. Enter the path in the *Baseurl* field on the *Reseed* page under *Site Settings*.
3. Click the *Update Reseed Details* button.

CloudCannon will rebuild your *Site* with the new path.

## Review your Site's Custom Domain

Once your *Site* has a *Custom Domain*, the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* shows the address of your *Site* on the *Subdomain*, the same way the *Testing Domain* page shows its *Testing Domain*. The line above the address says which state it is in:

* **[Site Name] goes live at this address once [Subdomain] points at CloudCannon** - The DNS record for the *Subdomain* doesn't point at CloudCannon yet.
* **[Site Name] is live at** - Visitors reach your *Site* at this address over HTTPS.
* **[Site Name] is live at this address, and CloudCannon is turning HTTPS on** - Visitors reach your *Site*, and its SSL certificate is on its way.
* **[Site Name] is live at this address, but HTTPS is not working, so visitors see a security warning** - The *HTTPS* section under *Subdomain settings* says why.

Until the *Subdomain* is live, the setup guide appears below the address. The *Danger zone* section above the *Subdomain settings* heading has the *Remove custom domain* button, which stops hosting your *Site* on the *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [removing a Custom Domain from your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/remove-a-custom-domain-from-your-site/).

Below the *Subdomain settings* heading, a line links to the page for the *Subdomain* on your *Domain* page, and the page for the *Subdomain* follows, without its heading. It has the same *Sites*, *Hosting*, *HTTPS*, *DNS*, and *Danger zone* sections as the *Subdomain* page you open from the *Subdomains* tab of your *Domain* page, because these settings belong to the *Subdomain* and apply to every *Site* on it. The *Sites* section lists every *Site* the *Subdomain* serves, with the path at which each *Site* is served, and labels your *Site* with *This site*.

From the *Subdomain settings*, you can:

* Click the *Add site* button in the *Sites* section to serve another *Site* on the *Subdomain*.
* Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm> for a *Site* in the *Sites* section and select the *Open site*, *Swap for another site*, *Change path*, or *Remove from [Subdomain]* option. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/).
* Click the *Configure* button in the *Hosting* or *HTTPS* section, or check the DNS in the *DNS* section. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/).
* Click the *Change to redirect* button next to *Redirect instead* in the *Danger zone* section to change the *Subdomain* into a redirect to another domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/#change-a-subdomain-that-serves-sites-to-a-redirect).
* Click the *Delete subdomain* button in the *Danger zone* section to delete the *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [deleting a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/#delete-a-subdomain).

If a change in the *Subdomain settings* takes your *Site* off the *Subdomain*, such as removing or swapping your *Site*, changing the *Subdomain* to a redirect, or deleting the *Subdomain*, CloudCannon opens the *[Site Name] is no longer on [Subdomain]* modal. The modal explains what happened and that your *Site* keeps its *Testing Domain*. Click the *Stay here* button to stay on the *Custom Domain* page, or the *Go to [Subdomain] settings* button to open the page for the *Subdomain*. If you deleted the *Subdomain*, the second button is *Go to subdomains*, and it opens the *Subdomains* tab instead.

If you can view the *Custom Domain* but don't have the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for its *Base Domain*, a sentence that explains which permission you need replaces the *Subdomain settings*. If you don't have the `base-domain:details:read` permission for the *Custom Domain*, the *Custom Domain* page shows only your *Site's* address, with a *Path for [Site Name]* field, an *Update path* button, and the *Remove custom domain* button. All three are disabled, with a line that explains which permission you need. To change the path or remove the *Custom Domain*, ask a member of your *Organization* who can edit the settings of the *Base Domain* (the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission), such as a member of the *Owners*, *Developers*, or *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), to make the change.

You should also check the [SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-an-ssl-certificate/) for your *Site's* *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding an auto-generated SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-an-auto-generated-ssl-certificate/) or [adding a custom SSL certificate](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-ssl-certificate/).
