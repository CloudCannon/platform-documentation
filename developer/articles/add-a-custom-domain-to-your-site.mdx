<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `site:settings:hosting:write` and `base-domain:settings:details:write` permissions, can add a *Custom Domain* to a *Site*.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a domain you own and connect to your CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so visitors reach your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> at your own address (e.g., <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/">cloudcannon.com</a>). Adding the *Custom Domain* to your *Site* tells CloudCannon to serve the *Site* at that address.

You can host a *Site* on the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/b/base-domain.yml">Base Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> (e.g., `example.com`), another <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subdomain.yml">Subdomain</comp.GlossaryTerm> (e.g., `docs.example.com`), or a path on either (e.g., `example.com/staff/`). A *Site* at a path needs to build into a folder that matches the path. For more information, please read our documentation on [what a Subdomain is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-a-subdomain/) and [building your Site for a subpath](/documentation/developer-articles/build-your-site-for-a-subpath/).

Before we begin, this article assumes you have [connected a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/), and that your *Site* is in <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosted-mode.yml">Hosted Mode</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon hides the *Custom Domain* page for a *Site* in <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/headless-mode.yml">Headless Mode</comp.GlossaryTerm>. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Site Building Mode is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-site-building-mode/).

There are two ways to add a *Custom Domain* to your *Site*: from the *Custom Domain* page under your *Site Settings*, or from the page for a *Subdomain* that already exists on your *Domain* page.

## Add a Custom Domain from Site Settings

To add a *Custom Domain* to your *Site*:

1. Click the *Sites* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/sites-browser.yml">Sites Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Site* you want to add a *Custom Domain* to. CloudCannon will open the *Dashboard* for that *Site*.
3. Navigate to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*. CloudCannon will open the *Custom Domain* page.
4. Select the *Custom Domain* you want to add using the *Base Domain* dropdown.
5. (Optional.) Enter a *Subdomain* in the *Subdomain* text field. Leave this field empty to host your *Site* on the *Base Domain* itself.
6. (Optional.) Enter a path in the *Subpath* text field (e.g., `/jobs/`), using lowercase letters, numbers, `_`, and `-`, with a `/` between folders (e.g., `/docs/v2/`). Leave this field empty to host your *Site* at the root of the *Subdomain*.
7. Click the *Add custom domain* button.

CloudCannon will add your *Site* to the *Subdomain*. Visitors reach your *Site* on your *Custom Domain* once the DNS for the *Subdomain* points at CloudCannon. Until then, your *Site* stays on its [Testing Domain](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-a-testing-domain/), and the *Custom Domain* page shows a setup guide:

* **CloudCannon DNS** — If the nameservers of your domain don't point at <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cloudcannon-dns.yml">CloudCannon DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm> yet, the guide shows the nameservers to set at your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. Without the `base-domain:settings:dns:read` permission, the guide says that *CloudCannon DNS* isn't configured for your domain and that you don't have permission to edit its DNS, and doesn't list the nameservers. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/).
* **External DNS** — On <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/external-dns.yml">external DNS</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the guide shows how far along setup is, and its *Scroll to DNS Records* button scrolls to the *DNS* section, which lists the record the *Subdomain* needs at your *DNS Provider*, with a *Copy* icon next to each value and the *I've updated it* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).

When you add the *Base Domain* (e.g., `example.com`) as a new *Subdomain*, CloudCannon also adds `www.example.com` as a redirect to it, if `www.example.com` does not already exist. When you add `www.example.com` as a new *Subdomain*, CloudCannon adds `example.com` as a redirect to `www.example.com` in the same way.

If the *Subdomain* you enter already exists, CloudCannon adds your *Site* to it:

* **The Subdomain has no Sites yet** — Your *Site* becomes the first *Site* on it and uses its hosting and HTTPS settings. Before you add it, the *Custom Domain* page tells you (e.g., *jobs.example.com is set up and waiting for a site.*).
* **The Subdomain already serves Sites** — Your *Site* joins it and uses its hosting, HTTPS, and DNS settings. The path you choose must not already be in use on the *Subdomain*.
* **The Subdomain is a redirect** — Adding your *Site* stops the redirect and serves your *Site* on the *Subdomain* instead. Before you add it, the *Custom Domain* page warns you (e.g., *www.example.com redirects to example.com now. Adding Marketing site stops the redirect and serves Marketing site there.*).

If you have the `base-domain:create` permission, you can also connect a new domain from the *Base Domain* dropdown. For more information, please read our documentation on [connecting a Custom Domain to your Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/connect-a-custom-domain-to-your-organization/).

If the *Subdomain* has no other *Sites* and takes your *Organization* over the number of *Subdomains* serving *Sites* that its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription-plan.yml">Subscription Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm> includes, CloudCannon will ask you to confirm the extra cost before adding it. If your *Organization* has no payment method, CloudCannon opens the *Add your Credit Card* modal instead. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).

In a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm> on its extended six-month <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/f/free-trial.yml">Free Trial</comp.GlossaryTerm>, serving a *Site* on a *Subdomain* that had no *Sites* shortens the trial so that it ends five days later. This applies on every *Subscription Plan* that limits *Subdomains* serving *Sites*, which is every *Subscription Plan* except the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/e/enterprise-plan.yml">Enterprise Plan</comp.GlossaryTerm>, whether or not the *Client Organization* is still within that limit. CloudCannon asks you to confirm before it shortens the trial. For more information, please read our documentation on [how billing works in the Partner Program](/documentation/partner-articles/how-does-billing-work-in-the-partner-program/).

## Add your Site from the Subdomain page

You can add your *Site* to a *Subdomain* that already exists from the page for that *Subdomain*. To add your *Site* to a new *Subdomain*, use the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* instead.

If your *Site* already has a *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon moves your *Site* to the new address. If the *Subdomain* has no other *Sites* and adding yours takes your *Organization* over the number of *Subdomains* serving *Sites* that its *Subscription Plan* includes, CloudCannon will ask you to confirm the extra cost first.

If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* that owns the *Site* first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To add your *Site* from the *Subdomain* page:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the *Card* for the domain that has the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page.
3. Click the *Subdomains* tab. CloudCannon will open the list of *Subdomains*.
4. Click the *Card* for the *Subdomain*. CloudCannon will open the page for that *Subdomain*.
5. Click the *Add a Site* button in the *Sites* section. CloudCannon will open the *Add a Site to [Subdomain]* modal.
6. Select your *Site* from the *Site* dropdown.
7. (Optional.) Enter a path in the *Subpath* field. Leave this field empty to serve your *Site* at the root of the *Subdomain*.
8. Click the *Add Site* button.

CloudCannon will serve your *Site* on the *Subdomain* at the path you entered.

## Review your Site's Custom Domain

Once your *Site* has a *Custom Domain*, the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* shows the address of your *Site* on the *Subdomain*, the same way the *Testing Domain* page shows its *Testing Domain*. The line above the address says which state it is in:

* **[Site Name] goes live at this address once [Subdomain] points at CloudCannon** — The DNS Record for the *Subdomain* doesn't point at CloudCannon yet.
* **[Site Name] is live at** — Visitors reach your *Site* at this address over HTTPS.
* **[Site Name] is live at this address, and CloudCannon is turning HTTPS on** — Visitors reach your *Site*, and its <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/ssl-certificate.yml">SSL Certificate</comp.GlossaryTerm> is on its way.
* **[Site Name] is live at this address, but HTTPS is not working, so visitors see a security warning** — The *HTTPS* section under *Subdomain settings* says why.

Until the *Subdomain* is live, the setup guide appears below the address. The *Danger zone* section above the *Subdomain settings* heading has the *Remove custom domain* button, which stops hosting your *Site* on the *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [removing a Custom Domain from your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/remove-a-custom-domain-from-your-site/).

Below the *Subdomain settings* heading, a line links to the page for the *Subdomain* on your *Domain* page, and the page for the *Subdomain* follows, without its heading. It has the same *Sites*, *Hosting*, *HTTPS*, *DNS*, and *Danger zone* sections as the *Subdomain* page you open from the *Subdomains* tab of your *Domain* page, because these settings belong to the *Subdomain* and apply to every *Site* on it. The *Sites* section lists every *Site* the *Subdomain* serves, with the path at which each *Site* is served, and labels your *Site* with *This site*.

From the *Subdomain settings*, you can:

* Click the *Add a Site* button in the *Sites* section to serve another *Site* on the *Subdomain*.
* Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/context-menu.yml">Context Menu</comp.GlossaryTerm> for a *Site* in the *Sites* section and select the *Open Site*, *Swap for another Site*, *Change Subpath*, or *Remove from [Subdomain]* option. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing the Sites on a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/).
* Click the *Configure hosting* button in the *Hosting* section or the *Configure HTTPS* button in the *HTTPS* section, or check the DNS in the *DNS* section. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring a Subdomain's hosting, HTTPS, and DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-a-subdomains-hosting-https-and-dns/).
* Click the *Change Subdomain Type* button next to *Change Subdomain type to Redirect* in the *Danger zone* section to change the *Subdomain* into a redirect to another domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [managing your Redirect Subdomains](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-your-redirect-subdomains/#change-a-subdomain-that-serves-sites-to-a-redirect).
* Click the *Delete Subdomain* button in the *Danger zone* section to delete the *Subdomain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [deleting a Subdomain](/documentation/developer-articles/manage-the-sites-on-a-subdomain/#delete-a-subdomain).

If a change in the *Subdomain settings* takes your *Site* off the *Subdomain*, such as removing or swapping your *Site*, changing the *Subdomain* to a redirect, or deleting the *Subdomain*, CloudCannon opens the *[Site Name] is no longer on [Subdomain]* modal. The modal explains what happened and that your *Site* keeps its *Testing Domain*. Click the *Stay here* button to stay on the *Custom Domain* page, or the *Go to [Subdomain] settings* button to open the page for the *Subdomain*. If you deleted the *Subdomain*, the second button is *Go to subdomains*, and it opens the *Subdomains* tab instead.

If you can view the *Custom Domain* but don't have the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for its *Base Domain*, *You do not have permission to edit the Domain Settings for [Base Domain].* appears below the *Subdomain settings* heading in place of the settings.

If you don't have the `base-domain:details:read` permission for the *Custom Domain*, the *Custom Domain* page shows the name of the *Subdomain*, *[Site Name] is served on [address].*, a *Subpath for [Site Name]* field, an *Update Subpath* button, and the *Remove custom domain* button. If you also lack the `site:settings:hosting:write` or `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission, all three controls are disabled, and hovering over each one shows a tooltip that names the permission you need. To change the path or remove the *Custom Domain*, ask a member of your *Organization* who can edit the settings of the *Base Domain* (the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission), such as a member of the *Owners*, *Developers*, or *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), to make the change.

## Troubleshoot adding a Custom Domain

If CloudCannon doesn't add your *Custom Domain*, here are some common causes. If you see an error that isn't listed here, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Organization* and *Site*, the address you tried to add, and the full error message.

### There is no Custom Domain page

CloudCannon hides the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* for a *Site* in *Headless Mode*. Switch your *Site* to *Hosted Mode* to add a *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Site Building Mode is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-site-building-mode/).

### Billing issue

If your *Organization's* <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/subscription.yml">Subscription</comp.GlossaryTerm> is inactive, or your *Organization* doesn't have enough <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm>, the *Custom Domain* page shows a *Billing issue* page instead of the *Base Domain* dropdown. If your *Site* already has a *Custom Domain*, the page shows only the *Remove custom domain* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [what happens when a payment fails](/documentation/user-articles/what-happens-when-a-payment-fails/).

### No permission

The *Base Domain* dropdown disables any domain whose settings you can't edit and labels it *No permission*. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission for that domain.

### Domain already in use. Add a subpath to have two sites on the same domain

Another *Site* is already served at the root of the *Subdomain*. Enter a path in the *Subpath* field to serve your *Site* alongside it, or enter a different *Subdomain*.

### Domain can only contain letters, numbers, dots and hyphens

The *Subdomain* field contains a character that a domain name can't use. Remove it, then click the *Add custom domain* button again.

### Subpaths can only use lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens, and underscores, with a slash at the start and end.

Change the path in the *Subpath* field to use only those characters, with a `/` at the start and end (e.g., `/jobs/`).

### This Subpath is already in use.

Another *Site* on the *Subdomain* is already served at that path. Enter a different path in the *Subpath* field.
