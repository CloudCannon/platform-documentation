<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:create` permission, can connect *Custom Domains* to an *Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a domain you own and connect to your CloudCannon <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization.yml">Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, so visitors reach your <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm> at your own address (e.g., <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/">cloudcannon.com</a>). Once you have purchased a domain from a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domain-registrar.yml">domain registrar</comp.GlossaryTerm>, you can connect that *Custom Domain* to CloudCannon and use it on any *Site* in your *Organization*, provided that *Site* is in <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/h/hosted-mode.yml">Hosted Mode</comp.GlossaryTerm>. After you have connected a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*, you can manage it from its [Domain page](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/) and [add your Custom Domain to a Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

Before we begin, this article assumes that you want to host your website through CloudCannon and know which <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/dns-provider.yml">DNS Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm> is right for your *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what web hosting is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-web-hosting/) and [choosing your DNS Provider](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-dns/#which-dns-provider-is-right-for-your-site).

There are two ways to connect a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*: from the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or from the *Custom Domain* page under your *Site Settings*.

## Connect a Custom Domain from your Domains Browser

The *Custom Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm> opens the *Domains Browser*, which lists the *Custom Domains* connected to your *Organization*. If you can't view any *Custom Domain* yet, such as when your *Organization* hasn't connected one, CloudCannon will open the *Connect a Custom Domain* page directly instead.

To connect a *Custom Domain* from the *Domains Browser*:

1. Click the *Custom Domains* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
2. Click the *Connect a Custom Domain* button in the top right of the *Domains Browser*. CloudCannon will open the *Connect a Custom Domain* page.
3. Enter the domain you registered, without `www` (e.g., `example.com`), in the *Base Domain* field.
4. Select the *CloudCannon DNS (recommended)* or *External DNS* option under *DNS Provider*.
5. Click the *Connect Custom Domain* button.

CloudCannon will connect the *Custom Domain* to your *Organization* and open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page for this *Custom Domain*. A setup guide at the top of the *Summary* tab shows what to do next, depending on the *DNS Provider* you selected.

If you selected *CloudCannon DNS*, the guide prompts you to add your existing records and shows the nameservers to set at your domain registrar, once CloudCannon has created the DNS zone for your domain. Until you add a *Subdomain*, the *Summary* tab shows *No subdomains on [Base Domain] yet* with an *Add subdomain* button. The TXT Records for the optional wildcard *SSL Certificate* of your domain are on the *DNS* tab. ## Connect a Custom Domain from your Site Settings

<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Connecting a *Custom Domain* from *Site Settings* also requires the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for the *Site*.
</comp.Notice>

If you are a member of multiple *Organizations*, make sure you select the *Organization* that owns the *Site* first. For more information, please read our documentation on [switching between your Organizations](/documentation/user-articles/switch-between-your-organizations/).

To connect a *Custom Domain* from the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*:

1. Click the *Sites* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/sites-browser.yml">Sites Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
2. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Site* you want to add the *Custom Domain* to. CloudCannon will open the *Dashboard* for that *Site*.
3. Navigate to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*. CloudCannon will open the *Custom Domain* page.
4. Select the *Connect a new domain* option in the *Base Domain* dropdown. CloudCannon will open the *Connect a Custom Domain* modal.
5. Enter the domain you registered, without `www` (e.g., `example.com`), in the *Domain* field.
6. Select the *CloudCannon DNS (recommended)* or *External DNS* option under *DNS Provider*.
7. Click the *Connect Custom Domain* button.

CloudCannon will connect the *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*, close the modal, and select the new domain in the *Base Domain* dropdown in place of the *Connect a new domain* option, with a *Just connected* label. You can then choose the *Subdomain* and *Subpath* for your *Site* and click the *Add custom domain* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

## Emails after you connect a Custom Domain

After you connect a *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon will email everyone else in your *Organization* who has permission to edit the settings of that *Custom Domain*. This includes members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), members of the *Partner* group in a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/client-organization.yml">Client Organization</comp.GlossaryTerm>, and members of [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission.

### [Name] added the Custom Domain [Base Domain] to [Organization]

CloudCannon sends this email shortly after the *Custom Domain* is connected. The subject names the person who connected the domain. If no person connected it, such as when an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/api-key.yml">API Key</comp.GlossaryTerm> connects it, the subject is *The Custom Domain [Base Domain] was added to [Organization]* instead.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Mailer-Dns-Connected" alt="A screenshot of the domain connected email shows the headline CloudCannon DNS is ready for example.com, who added the Custom Domain, a three-step tracker, the nameservers required and the nameservers found, the Next actions, and the Open the Domain page for example.com button." title="The domain connected email for CloudCannon DNS" type="screenshot"/>

This email shows who added the domain, its *DNS Provider*, and a progress tracker. For *CloudCannon DNS*, it lists the nameservers to set at your domain registrar next to the nameservers CloudCannon found, and its headline is *CloudCannon DNS is ready for [Base Domain]* once the DNS zone exists. For external DNS, the tracker shows the domain added, the DNS Records to add at your *DNS Provider*, and the *Subdomains* going live.

Unless CloudCannon is still creating the DNS zone, the email lists what to do next under *Next actions*: copy your other DNS Records to the *DNS* tab and update your nameservers, retry creating the DNS zone, or add a *Subdomain* and its DNS Record. Click the *Open the Domain page for [Base Domain]* button to open the *Domain* page. The person who connected the domain may have already done these. CloudCannon will email you again when the domain uses *CloudCannon DNS*, or as each *Subdomain* goes live.

To stop receiving this email, click the *Unsubscribe from this list* link at the bottom of the email. The same list includes other emails, such as when someone creates a *Site* or *Project* in your *Organization*. To choose which lists you receive, click the *Manage email preferences* link instead.

## Troubleshoot connecting a Custom Domain

If CloudCannon doesn't connect your domain, here are some common causes. If you see an error that isn't listed here, contact our <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/support/">support team</a> with the name of your *Organization*, the domain you entered, and the full error message.

### Permission denied

Connecting a *Custom Domain* requires the `base-domain:create` permission. Without it, the *Domains Browser* doesn't show the *Connect a Custom Domain* button, and opening the *Connect a Custom Domain* page directly shows a *Permission denied* page that says *You do not have permission to connect new Custom Domains.* The *Base Domain* dropdown on the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* doesn't list the *Connect a new domain* option. Ask someone who manages *Permission Groups* in your *Organization* for this permission.

### Billing issue

If your *Organization's* *Subscription* is inactive, or your *Organization* doesn't have enough <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/seat.yml">Seats</comp.GlossaryTerm>, CloudCannon shows a *Billing issue* page instead of the *Connect a Custom Domain* page. The *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings* also shows a *Billing issue* page, with no *Connect a new domain* option. For more information, please read our documentation on [what happens when a payment fails](/documentation/user-articles/what-happens-when-a-payment-fails/) and [what Add-Ons and Overages are](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-add-ons-and-overages/).

### The domain is already connected

If another *Organization* has already connected the domain, CloudCannon shows an error that the domain is already connected to another CloudCannon *Organization*. For more information, please read our documentation on [resolving ownership over a Custom Domain](/documentation/user-articles/resolve-ownership-over-a-custom-domain/).

If your own *Organization* has already connected the domain, CloudCannon shows a message that the domain is already connected to your *Organization*, then opens its *Domain* page. From the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*, CloudCannon selects the domain in the *Base Domain* dropdown instead. If you don't have permission to view that *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon shows an error that the domain is already connected to this *Organization*.

### The domain must not include a subdomain

Enter the domain you registered, such as `example.com`, not an address on it such as `www.example.com` or `blog.example.com`. After you connect the domain, you can [add a Subdomain to your Custom Domain](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-subdomain-to-your-custom-domain/) for each address you want to use.

### The domain is a cloudvent.net address

You can't connect a `cloudvent.net` address, such as the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/t/testing-domain.yml">Testing Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> of a *Site*, as a *Custom Domain*. CloudCannon shows an error that the domain cannot be a `cloudvent.net` address.
