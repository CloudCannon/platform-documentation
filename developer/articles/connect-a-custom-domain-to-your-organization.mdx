<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:create` permission, can connect *Custom Domains* to an *Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a unique address used to identify a website (e.g., <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/">cloudcannon.com</a>). Once you have purchased a domain from a domain registrar, you can connect that *Custom Domain* to CloudCannon and use it on any *Site* in your *Organization*.

After you have connected a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*, you can manage your *Custom Domain* settings and [add your Custom Domain to a Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

Before we begin, this article assumes that you want to host your website through CloudCannon and know which DNS provider is right for your *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what web hosting is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-web-hosting/) and [what DNS is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-dns/).

There are two ways to connect a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*: from the *Domains Browser*, or from the *Custom Domain* page under your *Site Settings*.

## Connect a domain from your Domains Browser

To connect a *Custom Domain* from the *Domains Browser*:

1. Log in to CloudCannon and select the *Organization* to which you want to connect the *Custom Domain*.
2. Click the *Domains* button in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>.
3. Click the *Connect a Domain* button in the top right of the *Domains Browser*. CloudCannon will open the *Connect a Domain* page.
4. Enter your *Custom Domain* name in the *Base Domain* text input.
5. Select the *CloudCannon DNS (recommended)* or *External DNS* option under *DNS provider*.
6. Click the *Connect Domain* button.

CloudCannon will connect the *Custom Domain* to your *Organization* and open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page for this *Custom Domain*. The *What happens next* box below the *DNS provider* options lists the next steps for the provider you selected, and a setup guide at the top of the *Summary* tab shows what to do next:

* **CloudCannon DNS**: The guide shows the nameservers to set at your domain registrar. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/).
* **External DNS**: The guide lists the records to add at your DNS provider, starting with the TXT records for the wildcard certificate of your domain. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).

If you do not have at least one *Custom Domain* connected to your *Organization*, the *Domains* button in the *App Sidebar* opens the *Connect a Domain* page directly, without the *Domains Browser*.

Each subdomain gets its own certificate. The domain's wildcard certificate covers subdomains you haven't set up yet.

## Connect a domain from your Site Settings

To connect a *Custom Domain* from the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*:

1. Log in to CloudCannon and select the *Organization* to which you want to connect the *Custom Domain*.
2. Click the *Sites* button in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Sites Browser*.
3. Identify the *Site* to which you want to add the *Custom Domain* and click the *Card* for the *Site*. CloudCannon will open the *Dashboard* for that *Site*.
4. Navigate to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*.
5. Select the *Connect a new domain* option in the *Base Domain* dropdown. CloudCannon will open the *Connect a Domain* modal.
6. Enter your *Custom Domain* name in the *Domain* text input.
7. Select the *CloudCannon DNS (recommended)* or *External DNS* option under *DNS provider*.
8. Click the *Connect Domain* button.

CloudCannon will connect the *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*, close the modal, and select the new domain in the *Base Domain* dropdown with a *Just connected* label. You can then choose the subdomain and path for your *Site* and click the *Add custom domain* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).
