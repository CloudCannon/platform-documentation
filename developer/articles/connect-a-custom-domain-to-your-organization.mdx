<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners*, *Developers*, and *Partner* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `base-domain:create` permission, can connect *Custom Domains* to an *Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

A <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/custom-domain.yml">Custom Domain</comp.GlossaryTerm> is a domain you own and connect to your CloudCannon *Organization*, so visitors reach your *Site* at your own address (e.g., <a href="https://cloudcannon.com/">cloudcannon.com</a>) instead of its *Testing Domain*. Once you have purchased a domain from a domain registrar, you can connect that *Custom Domain* to CloudCannon and use it on any *Site* in your *Organization*.

After you have connected a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*, you can manage it from its [Domain page](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-the-domain-page/) and [add your Custom Domain to a Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

Before we begin, this article assumes that you want to host your website through CloudCannon and know which DNS provider is right for your *Custom Domain*. For more information, please read our documentation on [what web hosting is](/documentation/user-articles/what-is-web-hosting/) and [what DNS is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-dns/).

There are two ways to connect a *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*: from the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/d/domains-browser.yml">Domains Browser</comp.GlossaryTerm>, or from the *Custom Domain* page under your *Site Settings*.

## Connect a domain from your Domains Browser

If you do not have at least one *Custom Domain* connected to your *Organization*, the *Domains* link in the *App Sidebar* opens the *Connect a Domain* page directly, without the *Domains Browser*. If you don't have the `base-domain:create` permission, CloudCannon shows a *Permission denied* page instead.

To connect a *Custom Domain* from the *Domains Browser*:

1. Log in to CloudCannon.
2. Select the *Organization* to which you want to connect the *Custom Domain*.
3. Click the *Domains* link in the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/a/app-sidebar.yml">App Sidebar</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon will open the *Domains Browser*.
4. Click the *Connect a Domain* button in the top right of the *Domains Browser*. CloudCannon will open the *Connect a Domain* page.
5. Enter your *Custom Domain* name in the *Base Domain* field.
6. Select the *CloudCannon DNS (recommended)* or *External DNS* option under *DNS provider*. The *What happens next* box below the options lists the next steps for the DNS provider you select.
7. Click the *Connect Domain* button.

CloudCannon will connect the *Custom Domain* to your *Organization* and open the *Summary* tab of the *Domain* page for this *Custom Domain*. A setup guide at the top of the *Summary* tab shows what to do next:

* **CloudCannon DNS** — Once CloudCannon has created the DNS zone for your domain, usually in a moment, the guide prompts you to add your existing records and shows the nameservers to set at your domain registrar. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring CloudCannon DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-cloudcannon-dns/).
* **External DNS** — Once you add a *Subdomain*, the guide lists the records it needs at your DNS provider. Until then, the *Summary* tab shows *No subdomains on [Base Domain] yet* with an *Add subdomain* button. The TXT records for the optional wildcard certificate of your domain are on the *DNS* tab. For more information, please read our documentation on [configuring external DNS](/documentation/developer-articles/configure-external-dns/).

If another *Organization* has already connected the domain, CloudCannon shows an error that the domain is already connected to another CloudCannon *Organization*. For more information, please read our documentation on [resolving ownership over a Custom Domain](/documentation/user-articles/resolve-ownership-over-a-custom-domain/).

## Connect a domain from your Site Settings

Connecting a *Custom Domain* from *Site Settings* also requires the `site:settings:hosting:write` permission for the *Site*.

To connect a *Custom Domain* from the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*:

1. Log in to CloudCannon.
2. Select the *Organization* to which you want to connect the *Custom Domain*.
3. Click the *Sites* link in the *App Sidebar*. CloudCannon will open the *Sites Browser*.
4. Click the <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/card.yml">Card</comp.GlossaryTerm> for the *Site* you want to add the *Custom Domain* to. CloudCannon will open the *Dashboard* for that *Site*.
5. Navigate to the *Custom Domain* page under *Site Settings*.
6. Select the *Connect a new domain* option in the *Base Domain* dropdown. CloudCannon will open the *Connect a Domain* modal.
7. Enter your *Custom Domain* name in the *Domain* field.
8. Select the *CloudCannon DNS (recommended)* or *External DNS* option under *DNS provider*.
9. Click the *Connect Domain* button.

CloudCannon will connect the *Custom Domain* to your *Organization*, close the modal, and select the new domain in the *Base Domain* dropdown with a *Just connected* label. You can then choose the *Subdomain* and path for your *Site* and click the *Add custom domain* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [adding a Custom Domain to your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/add-a-custom-domain-to-your-site/).

## Domain connected email

When you connect a *Custom Domain*, CloudCannon emails everyone else in your *Organization* who can edit the settings of the domain (the `base-domain:settings:details:write` permission).

### [Organization]: [Name] added [Base Domain]

CloudCannon sends this email to everyone who can edit the settings of the new domain except the person who connected it. The subject names that person. If no person connected the domain, such as when an API key connects it, the subject is *[Base Domain] was added to [Organization]* instead. The email shows the DNS provider of the domain and, for CloudCannon DNS, the nameservers to set at your domain registrar, with what to do next and an *Open [Base Domain]* button.
