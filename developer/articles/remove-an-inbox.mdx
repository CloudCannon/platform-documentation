<comp.Notice info_type="permissions">
  **Permissions required**

  Members of the *Owners* and *Developers* [Default Permission Groups](/documentation/user-articles/what-are-default-permission-groups/), or [Custom Permission Groups](/documentation/developer-articles/what-are-custom-permission-groups/) with the `org:settings:inbox:write` permission, can remove an *Inbox* from an *Organization*.
</comp.Notice>

<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Removing an *Inbox* is permanent. CloudCannon removes the *Inbox* from every *Site* it is linked to and deletes all submissions it has received. This cannot be undone.
</comp.Notice>

Removing an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/inbox.yml">Inbox</comp.GlossaryTerm> deletes it from your *Organization* entirely, along with its targets and every submission stored against it. If you only want to disconnect an *Inbox* from one *Site* and keep its data, unlink it instead. For more information, please read our documentation on [unlinking an Inbox from your Site](/documentation/developer-articles/unlink-an-inbox-from-your-site/).

Before you remove an *Inbox*, export any submissions you want to keep. For more information, please read our documentation on [exporting your form submissions](/documentation/user-articles/export-your-form-submissions/).

To remove an *Inbox*:

1. Navigate to the *Forms* page under *Org Settings*.
2. Select the *Inbox* you want to remove.
3. Open the *Danger Zone* tab.
4. Click the *Remove Inbox* button.
5. Confirm your choice in the *Remove Inbox* modal.

CloudCannon will delete the *Inbox* and return you to the *Forms* page.
