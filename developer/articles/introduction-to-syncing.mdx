CloudCannon is a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git.yml">Git-backed</comp.GlossaryTerm> <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/c/cms.yml">Content Management System</comp.GlossaryTerm>, allowing you to store your website files on a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-provider.yml">Git Provider</comp.GlossaryTerm>. CloudCannon automatically <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/syncing.yml">syncs</comp.GlossaryTerm> your files when you connect a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-repository.yml">Git Repository</comp.GlossaryTerm> to a <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/s/site.yml">Site</comp.GlossaryTerm>, when you save changes in CloudCannon, and when someone pushes changes to your *Git Repository*, for example from an <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/i/ide.yml">IDE</comp.GlossaryTerm>. This means that no matter what software your team uses, everyone has access to the latest version of your files.

Articles in this section are for <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/o/organization-member.yml">Team Members</comp.GlossaryTerm> responsible for setting up *Sites* in CloudCannon and managing website files.

<comp.Notice info_type="info">
  You can upload your website files directly from your computer, but CloudCannon recommends using a *Git Provider* such as GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket.
</comp.Notice>

In the Syncing section of our Developer documentation, we cover:

* Git: Learn how to get your website files from your *Git Repository* onto CloudCannon
* Direct Upload: Learn how to create a *Site* from files on your computer
* Syncing Errors: Learn how to resolve syncing errors when CloudCannon cannot push changes to, or pull changes from, your *Git Repository*

Let's briefly introduce these topics.

## Git

CloudCannon supports several cloud and self-hosted *Git Providers*. You can authenticate and reconnect your *Git Provider*, control which *Git Repository* and branch a *Site* syncs with, and enable <comp.GlossaryTerm term="/user/glossary/g/git-lfs.yml">Git LFS</comp.GlossaryTerm> so CloudCannon includes your Git LFS files when syncing.

For a more in-depth explanation of Git in CloudCannon, please read our documentation:

* [Reconnect your Git Provider](/documentation/developer-articles/reconnect-your-git-provider/)

* [Connecting a GitLab repository as your source](/documentation/developer-articles/connecting-a-gitlab-respository-as-your-source/)
* [Connecting a Bitbucket repository as your source](/documentation/developer-articles/connecting-a-bitbucket-respository-as-your-source/)

* [Enable Git LFS](/documentation/developer-articles/enable-git-lfs/)

## Direct Upload

Although not technically syncing, you can upload your website files directly from your computer when you create a *Site*. You can still use the majority of CloudCannon's features, except for syncing and publishing.

For a more in-depth explanation of Direct Upload, please read our documentation:

* [Create a Site with Direct Upload](/documentation/developer-articles/create-a-site-with-direct-upload/)

## Syncing Errors

CloudCannon pauses syncing when files with unsaved changes on your *Site* also change in your *Git Repository*. Saving or discarding those changes resumes syncing. If CloudCannon still cannot push changes to, or pull changes from, your *Git Repository*, you can resolve the syncing error in one of several ways.

For a more in-depth explanation of syncing errors, please read our documentation:

* [Resolve Syncing errors](/documentation/developer-articles/resolve-syncing-errors/)
