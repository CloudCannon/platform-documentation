<comp.Notice info_type="important">
  Headless Mode is only available for Sites using Unified Configuration. For more information, please read our documentation on [migrating to Unified Configuration](/documentation/developer-guides/unified-configuration-migration-guide/).
</comp.Notice>

Headless Mode disables hosting and building for your Site.

To enable Headless Mode on your Site:

1. Navigate to the *Details* page under *Site Settings*.
2. Select the *Headless Mode* option under *Site Building Mode*.
3. Click the *Update Details* button. CloudCannon will open the *Switch to Headless Mode?* modal.
4. Click the *Turn off hosting and builds* button.

CloudCannon will immediately take down your live Site and prevents any further builds.

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Site-Settings-Details" title="The Details page" alt="A screenshot of the Details page under Site Settings shows the Site Name field and the Site Building Mode options, with Hosted Mode selected and Headless Mode below it." type="screenshot" />

When your Site is in Headless Mode, CloudCannon will display a reduced set of pages in your *Site Settings*.

You can turn off *Headless Mode* at any time by selecting the *Hosted Mode* option on the *Details* page under your *Site Settings* and clicking the *Update Details* button. For more information, please read our documentation on [what Site Building Mode is](/documentation/developer-articles/what-is-site-building-mode/).
