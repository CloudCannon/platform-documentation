The *Client Organizations Browser* in your Partner *Organization* is where you see and manage every [Client Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/create-a-client-organization/) you run. Open it from the *App Sidebar* in your Partner *Organization*.

## View your Client Organizations

The *Client Organizations Browser* lists each *Client Organization* as a card showing its *Site* and domain counts, its plan, and whether it has any issues that need attention. Use the filter controls to narrow the list when you manage a large number of clients.

## Open and manage a Client Organization

Click a *Client Organization* to switch into it. From there you manage it like any other *Organization*: create and configure *Sites*, invite team members, and adjust its settings. To switch back to your Partner *Organization*, use the organization switcher in the *App Sidebar* (see Switch between your Organizations).

<comp.DocShot docshot_key="CloudCannon-Documentation-Organization-Home-Client-Org" alt="A screenshot of a Client Organization Home shows a Partner Organization card above the current Client Organization card in the App Sidebar." title="A Client Organization Home" type="screenshot"/>

## Your team comes for free

Members of your Partner *Organization* can be added to any *Client Organization* at no extra cost, so you never pay twice for the same seat. Add your team to a *Client Organization* the same way you add any team member.

## Add or hand over a Client Organization

* To create a new one, click *New client* and follow [Create a Client Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/create-a-client-organization/).
* To hand a *Client Organization* over to a client, or detach it so it becomes independent, read [Hand over or detach a Client Organization](/documentation/developer-articles/hand-over-or-detach-a-client-organization/).
